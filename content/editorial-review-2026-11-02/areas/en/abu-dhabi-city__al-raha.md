# Construction and maintenance services in Al Raha, Abu Dhabi City

**Start with the actual property and scope:** Mixed residential property, façade maintenance and building approvals. The area name alone does not define the required permits, the contractor's licence or whether a supplier covers the jobsite. ## A practical decision in Al Raha

Al Raha includes different residential property types. Confirm whether the unit belongs to a managed development before estimating external alterations.

Specify façade access, glass type, seals, waterproofing details and any required management approvals.

## Permits and professional checks

For a new structure or a material alteration, determine the exact permitted work through Abu Dhabi's competent municipal service, working with a qualified consultant where required. DMT has a simplified process for certain minor works, but that is not a blanket waiver for all maintenance and building activity.

Check the contractor's economic licence and any engineering classification separately. Self-reported project photos, Google ratings, actual issued permits and successfully completed projects are different types of evidence. Only display provider listings that match the job specialty, are published and have confirmed service coverage for Al Raha.

## Quotation details to prepare

**Area-specific scope:** Property and development type, façade access, glazing and sealant system, waterproofing details and approvals. Provide the site location privately in the request, relevant drawings, safe photo attachments, quantities, materials and desired schedule. Compare inclusions, exclusions, delivery, installation, inspection and warranty; a price per square metre does not replace a complete specification.

## When to use the existing tender workflow

Projects with multiple trades or competing offers need a consistent package: scope of work, drawing revisions, BOQ, supplier qualifications, clarification deadline and bidding deadline. Compare technical compliance, exclusions, materials and programme before total price. ## Questions and answers

### Does an Al Ain or Abu Dhabi provider automatically cover Al Raha?
No. Confirm the actual geographic coverage, matching service and any travel arrangements.

### When do I need a permit?
The answer depends on the work, property and competent authority. Confirm requirements before beginning any structural alteration.
