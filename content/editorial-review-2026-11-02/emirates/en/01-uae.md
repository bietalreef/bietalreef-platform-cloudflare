# UAE Construction, Contracting and Maintenance Directory

**From a project idea to a documented decision.** Choose the emirate and the service you need, explore published contractors and suppliers, and learn what a clear scope and comparable quotation should contain. Biet Al Reef helps organise discovery and requests; it does not replace permits, independent engineering assessment or legally required approvals.

## Start with the emirate and the scope

Select Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah or Fujairah. Local authorities, permit procedures and eligibility standards may differ. A provider's stated coverage area is not proof that it is licensed to undertake any regulated engineering activity. These editorial pathways supplement—not replace—the platform's existing service and location taxonomy.

## Building a home: seven decisions before handover

Define the family brief, land and site conditions, budget assumptions and intended standard of finishing. Engage appropriately qualified designers for architectural, structural and building-services drawings. Check permits with the relevant emirate authority. Prequalify contractors against the actual work, rather than public ratings alone. Price a consistent bill of quantities (BOQ), drawings and material schedule. Record material approvals, hidden-work inspections and written change orders during construction. At handover, obtain relevant test reports, a snag list, equipment documents, applicable completion records and written warranties.

**Compare the work before the price.** A trade licence is not interchangeable with engineering classification or evidence of completed projects. Provider-uploaded photos represent submitted portfolio material unless independently substantiated. The number of building permits is not a count of successfully completed contracts.

## Digital construction and project records

A controlled digital project file tracks drawing revisions, approved materials, quotations, variations, inspections and handover records. Building Information Modelling (BIM) can expose clashes between structural, mechanical and electrical systems; 2D/3D and VR/AR can explain design intent. AI may identify missing documents or inconsistent tender items, but cannot approve structural safety or grant municipal permissions. Abu Dhabi's government Binaa service must never be presented as an established Biet Al Reef integration or partnership without evidence.

## RFQ, RFI and tendering: select the right process

**RFQ** requests a comparable price for a defined scope. **RFI** requests missing technical information or clarification. **EOI** gauges interest and qualification. **RFP** invites proposed technical and financial solutions. **ITT** is a formal invitation to tender under the issuer's terms. The correct process depends on the actual invitation documents.

A credible construction tender includes drawings with revision numbers, quantities and units, technical specifications, exclusions, supplier qualification rules, submission and clarification deadlines, and a transparent evaluation method. Assess technical compliance first and compare prices only for equivalent scopes. A missing item is not a zero-priced item. The project owner or authorised decision-maker, not Weyaak, makes the award.

Federal procurement follows the Ministry of Finance's supplier registration and Digital Procurement Platform rules for opportunities within their applicable scope. Private Biet Al Reef tenders are separate and must never be described as government tenders.

## Bespoke quotation requests

A clear request provides project location, property type, photos, verified measurements where available, drawings, materials, installation requirements, delivery access, target dates, exclusions and warranty expectations. For custom kitchens record wall-to-wall dimensions, doors, windows, electrical and plumbing points, board materials, fittings and an on-site measuring step. For HVAC identify system type, unit quantity, capacity and symptoms. For paint or waterproofing describe surface conditions and potential moisture sources before committing to remedial work. For materials include exact quantity and delivery conditions.

Weyaak should request only the missing information, preserve the user's context, and show a confirmation step. A request is not submitted until the platform returns a valid acknowledgement or tracking number.

## Engineering consultancy and maintenance specialties

Find the appropriate discipline: architecture; interior and exterior design; structural/civil engineering; geotechnics; mechanical engineering; electrical engineering; fire and life safety; urban planning; roads and transportation; water and drainage; landscape; sustainable design; project supervision; quantity surveying and cost management; and specialist engineering studies. Verify the specific licence, qualifications and scope of each firm.

The maintenance directory covers plumbing, leak detection, HVAC, electrical work, waterproofing, thermal insulation, painting, plaster, floors and tiles, gypsum ceilings, carpentry and cabinets, aluminium and glass, façades, water tanks, pumps, swimming pools, fire systems, lifts, smart and security systems, gardens and irrigation, drainage and stormwater, and integrated facilities maintenance. Each specific service page should explain symptoms, safe checks, professional diagnostics, materials, price drivers, testing, acceptance and warranty—never inventing a definitive diagnosis from an image.

## Biet Al Reef Market and customer rights

A relevant educational page can link to a live supplier, a ready-made product or a made-to-measure product in the Market. Each commercial item retains a single source identity across the Market, provider profile and directory. Product pages disclose actual specifications, dimensions, manufacture or delivery expectations, installation and returns according to the seller's real policies—not assumed blanket terms.

Customers should understand scope, price, timing and warranty. Providers should receive accurate project details, access and payments under the agreement. Changes, inspection notes and defects must be documented. A listing badge does not imply government certification.
