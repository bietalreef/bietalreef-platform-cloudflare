# Construction, Permits, Fit-Out and Engineering Services in Dubai

**A design proposal is not a building permit.** Begin by identifying the property, authority, development or building-specific restrictions and type of change. Dubai Municipality publishes different building-permit procedures for villas, industrial developments, multi-storey and public buildings. Work involving alterations may require a different review from minor decorative maintenance.

## Permission and contractor eligibility

Agree who is responsible for preparing and submitting architectural, structural and MEP drawings and responding to authority comments. Dubai Municipality also publishes engineering consultancy and contracting licensing standards, activity classification and technical-staff requirements. A general commercial licence does not establish eligibility to perform every structural or specialist activity. Check the exact authorised scope, personnel requirements and permits before signing the work contract.

## Avoid misleading quotations

Ask for drawings with issue dates, a BOQ, measured quantities and specified materials. The quote must distinguish structural works, MEP, fit-out, façade systems, fire protection, service connections, waste removal and protection of adjoining property where relevant. The lowest total can be misleading when one bid excludes waterproofing, access restrictions, testing or site protection.

For HVAC identify equipment type, capacity, symptoms and building-system ownership. For aluminium and glazing record opening dimensions, profile systems, glass specification, seals and safe access arrangements. For custom joinery and kitchens record wall-to-wall measurements, appliances, doors, windows, material quality, fittings and final site survey. For bathroom waterproofing, establish the leak source and a test plan before laying new tiles.

## Tendering and digital workflows

Private projects involving multiple bidders benefit from a single controlled document issue, clarification deadline, agreed BOQ and technical evaluation before financial comparison. An RFI resolves questions; an RFQ prices a defined scope; an ITT formalises bidding under the issuer's conditions. Weyaak can help collect missing details and guide users to the existing verified transaction path, not issue municipal approvals or select a winner by itself.

## Market, handover and warranty

A product supplier should describe exact technical specifications, quantities, delivery location, installation arrangements and seller-specific return terms. For made-to-measure products, the request should remain linked to the same underlying merchant/product record across Biet Al Reef Market and the directory. At handover confirm work against approved samples, site tests, warranties, operation information and a dated snag list; obtain any required completion documentation from the relevant authority.
