# Building, Maintenance and Contractors in Sharjah

**Start by distinguishing new construction from maintenance.** Sharjah City Municipality publishes a service for an ordinary building permit and another for maintenance permits. The maintenance service describes site inspection and supporting documentation that may include title records, approved drawings, contractor licences, contracts and other approvals depending on the property. Requirements must be checked against the correct live service rather than copied from another emirate.

## Check the authority and nature of the works

A Sharjah City Municipality service description should not automatically be assumed to govern another administrative area in the emirate. Determine the competent authority for the actual property. Structural changes and certain regulated installations may require specialist engineering review, while routine cosmetic work should be described accurately instead of being incorrectly classified as structural construction.

Verify contractor activity, licence and professional responsibility before entering a contract. The provider's business address and areas it advertises serving are separate pieces of information. A contractor's portfolio photographs are not independent evidence of a regulator-approved completed project.

## A quote that can be inspected

For leak repairs or damp walls, establish the source before repainting. The quotation should separate investigation, substrate preparation, remedial work, testing and final coating. For majlis or villa fit-out record the room dimensions, openings, lighting, HVAC positions, joinery and gypsum details as well as furniture protection and cleaning. For aluminium, wood and other factory-made items request exact materials, thicknesses, measurements and delivery/installation obligations.

## Specialist services and private tenders

Use an RFQ when the quantities and installation conditions are clear. Use a private tender with common drawings, BOQ, qualification rules and clarification deadlines when multiple contractors must be compared. Weyaak should request missing data and link to genuine published suppliers. It must not invent provider eligibility or impersonate a municipal official.

Before final payment, document tests, outstanding defects, repairs, warranty coverage and contract-based acceptance. Select a Sharjah city or area and service, then prepare a measurable request.
