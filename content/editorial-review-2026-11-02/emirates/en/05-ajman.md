# Construction, Building Extensions and Maintenance in Ajman

**The status of the existing building determines the first decision.** New work, an extension and routine maintenance should not be treated as interchangeable. The Ajman Government service for an extension to an existing building refers to approved architectural and building plans, relevant service-authority approvals and contractual documentation. Always check the current service details for the exact property and project type.

## Before extending or constructing a property

Engage appropriately qualified designers and identify who prepares drawings, submits applications and resolves technical comments. Changes that affect loads or drainage connections need competent assessment. Compare contractors against the same scope, quantities, specifications and handover criteria. Make explicit who supplies materials, who protects existing rooms and who pays for disposal or delay caused by approvals.

## Completion and handover are not identical

Ajman's official completion certificate service is associated with confirmation that a building meets approved plans and relevant requirements after inspection. Its listed documents include inspection reports, roof-waterproofing warranty and existing building permit, plus project-specific approvals. This is different from a private snag-list acceptance between the owner and contractor. Keep inspection evidence during the build, not only when preparing a completion application.

## Quotes for repairs and custom products

For dampness and paint, identify the moisture source, affected surface and preparation before the finishing coats. For a kitchen, door or majlis installation, include verified measurements, materials, fitting details, on-site surveying, delivery, assembly and warranty. For HVAC repairs describe system type, capacity, problem history and required testing. Biet Al Reef Market should display actual merchant-specific manufacturing, delivery and return policies; do not invent a universal cancellation rule for bespoke products.

A measurable small repair can proceed to a structured RFQ. Multi-contractor building work may require a private tender with a consistent BOQ, drawings and transparent technical comparison. Weyaak may guide the customer, but the authorised owner chooses the contractor and confirms any submission.
