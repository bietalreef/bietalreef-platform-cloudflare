# Building Permits, Consultants and Contractors in Ras Al Khaimah

**A construction project begins with consultant appointment and approved technical documents.** Ras Al Khaimah Municipality's Sanad catalogue identifies services including ZP00 consultant assignment, ZP02 new building permits, drawing approvals and permit renewals. The ZP02 guidance describes applications via an authorised consultant, review of architectural, structural and building-services drawings, final fee and contractor-related stages, and electronic permit issuance.

## Before building or extending

Clarify the plot, demarcation and survey documents, building type and competent consultant. Obtain an up-to-date list of required drawings and approvals from the actual municipal service. Do not assume agreement with a contractor authorises work to begin before the permit is issued. For expired permits or contractor replacement, check the separate permit and account-status requirements and the condition of work already performed.

## Qualify contractors and review bids

Use the same drawings and BOQ for all competitors, including excavation, concrete, waterproofing, building services, testing, waste management and site protection as relevant. A reduced headline price may exclude costly required works. Technical eligibility, material specifications, programme, resources, exceptions and warranty should be assessed before financial comparison. No competitor should access another bidder's private offer.

## Maintenance, suppliers and the Market

For waterproofing record the moisture source and test method. For HVAC record type, capacity and symptoms. For joinery and glazing record exact dimensions, materials, hardware, installation and site survey. For construction materials specify item standard, units and delivery access. In Biet Al Reef Market, made-to-measure products must remain associated with their original merchant and product identity instead of being cloned for a directory landing page.

## Construction records and handover

Maintain drawings with revision identifiers, samples, inspection notes, written variations and test evidence. The owner should receive a documented defects list, operation manuals and relevant warranties. Official completion or other authority records should be verified through the applicable service. Weyaak may support a quotation or tender request but cannot issue permits or award a contract autonomously.
