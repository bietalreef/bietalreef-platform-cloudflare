# Construction, Building Permits and Maintenance in Fujairah

**Site conditions, service approvals and the roles of the consultant and contractor determine the building route in Fujairah.** The municipality's published new-building-permit information describes online submission, the application form, a construction agreement, contractor and consultant trade licences, approved drawings, valid land plans and utility approvals where applicable. Confirm the current requirements for the actual plot and work instead of applying one checklist to every site.

## Municipal e-services and project documentation

Fujairah Municipality's Rukhsati platform provides access to municipal licensing information and digital request workflows. It must not be portrayed as a Biet Al Reef service or partnership. A contractor's offer should identify who is responsible for the architectural and technical drawings, municipal submissions and follow-up with relevant utilities.

Different service operators or approval requirements may apply to different areas. Clarify the project's utility connections and any no-objection certificates needed before fixing a construction schedule or promising a connection price. Approved documents should be kept in a project file with clear revision numbers.

## Selecting contractors, workshops and materials

For building work request a measurable scope covering foundations, concrete, waterproofing, MEP, finishes, equipment and safety. Check each provider's actual licence and capacity rather than relying on portfolio imagery. Workshops and merchants need to specify dimensions, materials, finishes, manufacturing time, delivery, installation and product-specific warranties. Custom kitchens should not be priced as standard inventory without the final site measurement.

## RFQs, tenders and maintenance

A building tender distributes one consistent document set and BOQ to competing firms. Technical compliance and exclusions are reviewed before pricing; tender award stays with the authorised owner. A smaller material purchase or maintenance service may need only an RFQ. Weyaak can organise the information and direct users to relevant published providers, but may not diagnose structural failures remotely or claim a transaction succeeded without confirmation.

For water leakage, HVAC and waterproofing, request an appropriate assessment and a defined test of the completed repair. Handover should include defect records, relevant operation documents and warranty terms. Any official completion certificate or permit remains a matter for the competent authority.
