## Technical brief and quote inputs

A manufacturing enquiry should include dimensioned drawings, quantities, material grades, colour or finish, site access, sample approval and the exact split between production, delivery and installation. When requesting offers for Al Khawaneej, Dubai, explicitly identify whether the request is supply-only, installation, ongoing maintenance or a fixed-scope job. ## Verifying the correct business and service

Do not label an ordinary shop as a certified factory. Verify business activity, actual manufacturing capability, equipment and subcontracting responsibilities from the published provider record. Compare verified activities, real project examples, location coverage, quotation responsiveness and the role each provider can legally perform. Any claimed accreditation or completed project must come from a traceable source with a verification date, not from a promotional label.

## How to compare offers fairly

A price per piece can hide edging, hardware, fitting, transport, on-site adjustment and after-sales service. Compare the same drawings and specification revision; assess sample acceptance, dimensional tolerances, delivery period, workmanship warranty and exclusions. An apparently low price cannot be evaluated until quantities and obligations match.
