## Location and project considerations

Local request context: Design requests should include the intended use, measured survey, desired drawings and approval responsibilities, not a generic design-only price. ## Technical brief and quote inputs

Provide site survey, property type, intended alteration, existing drawings, room dimensions, objectives, required formats (2D/3D/BIM), responsibilities for submissions and design revision allowance. When requesting offers for Al Hamidiyah, Ajman, explicitly identify whether the request is supply-only, installation, ongoing maintenance or a fixed-scope job. ## Verifying the correct business and service

Where regulated consultancy is required, check the applicable professional licence, classification and authorised scope. Artistic visualisations alone do not constitute approved engineering drawings. Compare verified activities, real project examples, location coverage, quotation responsiveness and the role each provider can legally perform. ## How to compare offers fairly

A concept-design fee can omit surveys, shop drawings, coordinated MEP, authority fees and site supervision. Compare drawing packages, approval responsibilities, revision rounds, consultant accountability, milestones and handover format. An apparently low price cannot be evaluated until quantities and obligations match.
