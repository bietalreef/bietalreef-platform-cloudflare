## What to define before selecting a provider

Joinery work should start with the room measurements, load/use requirements, panel composition, board thickness, edge treatment, hardware, finishing and installation responsibility. A custom kitchen, wardrobe and door require separate measurement and fitting schedules.

## Measurements, specification and RFQ

For custom products, ask for a dimensioned elevation or 2D plan, site measurement responsibility, finish sample, hinges/slides, ventilation and moisture exposure where relevant. Compare shop drawings before manufacturing and use the original merchant product identifier.

## Risks and handover checks

Specify the fabrication lead time, design approvals, fit-out works, delivery and snagging visit; no blanket return right or warranty term should be invented.

## Publication and data requirements

This topic is a national category, not a local business or a promise of stock availability. Use it to choose materials and compare the actual suppliers and products on Biet Al Reef. A real product card requires approved product data, seller ID and a live destination. Without these, show the technical selection guide only.
