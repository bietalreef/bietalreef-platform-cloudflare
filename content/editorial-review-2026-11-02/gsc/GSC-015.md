## What to define before selecting a provider

Windows, glazed doors and partitions need accurate openings, profile choice, glazing safety requirements, water/air sealing method, hardware and access conditions. Design drawings are not substitutes for site measurements.

## Measurements, specification and RFQ

Ask for a schedule of openings with rough and final sizes, glazing make-up, hardware, colour/finish, wall interface, tolerances, installation, disposal of old frames and watertightness inspection.

## Risks and handover checks

A product price alone does not prove a compliant installed system. Verify the supplier, installer responsibilities and warranties separately.

## Publication and data requirements

This topic is a national category, not a local business or a promise of stock availability. Use it to choose materials and compare the actual suppliers and products on Biet Al Reef. A real product card requires approved product data, seller ID and a live destination. Without these, show the technical selection guide only.
