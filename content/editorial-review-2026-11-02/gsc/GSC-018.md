## محتوى مفصل للصفحة الحالية — English draft

## Scope of this page

The public support policy should identify the operator, supported channels, contact options, response process and which responsibilities belong to the platform versus independent merchants or providers.

## Requests, quotation and transactions

Explain how users submit a support enquiry and how a request is tracked. Distinguish a service quotation enquiry from an order or payment made through the marketplace. No refund or service-level guarantees may be published without the approved legal policy.

## Data and evidence

Set out the documents users may submit (such as reference number, service description and relevant photos) and direct sensitive payment details to secure channels. Do not request passwords, OTPs or payment-card details by chat.

## Complaints, disputes and revisions

Give the official escalation route and show the current approved policy version, effective date, jurisdiction and links to privacy/terms/merchant policies. This document is a publication checklist until legal and customer-support owners confirm the actual wording.
