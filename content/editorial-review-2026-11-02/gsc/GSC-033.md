## What this page helps you decide

This services and offers page helps a buyer define a real construction and contracting requirement in Al Barsha, Dubai. Match the contractor to the precise scope: new building, extension, structural works, fit-out, refurbishment or specialist trade package. Start with the work needed, not a broad company list. Specify the deliverable, workmanship requirements and what is included or expressly excluded.

## Location and project considerations

Local request context: Clarify whether the client needs structural works, internal modifications or interior design; these can require different consultant deliverables. Project location must be checked against the licensing authority and the actual site address. Dubai Municipality publishes building-permit guidance, but the approval route depends on the activity and plot. The page must distinguish requirements that can be explained generally from facts that depend on the specific property, activity or actual supplier.

## Technical brief and quote inputs

Attach plot/property details, project stage, approved drawings when available, BOQ, specifications, contract boundaries, programme, quality checks, payment milestones and contractor access requirements. When requesting offers for Al Barsha, Dubai, explicitly identify whether the request is supply-only, installation, ongoing maintenance or a fixed-scope job. Ask for written inclusions, exclusions, delivery dates, VAT treatment and an appropriate inspection or acceptance method.

## Verifying the correct business and service

Check trade licence, engineering classification where applicable, licensed activities, supervision arrangements and actual project references. Licences, permits and completed-project counts are separate evidence. Check that any advertised offer is published, current, tied to the right service and available for the requested location. Any claimed accreditation or completed project must come from a traceable source with a verification date, not from a promotional label.

## How to compare offers fairly

The lowest lump-sum offer may exclude provisional sums, permits, testing, waterproofing details, escalation or variations. Assess scope compliance first, then itemised BOQ, programme, milestones, materials, exclusions, defects liability and handover documentation. An apparently low price cannot be evaluated until quantities and obligations match.
