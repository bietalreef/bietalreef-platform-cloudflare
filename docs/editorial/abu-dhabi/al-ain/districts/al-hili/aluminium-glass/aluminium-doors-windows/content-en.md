# Aluminium doors, windows and cabinets — Al Hili

Before ordering an aluminium door or window in Al Hili, do more than provide a single opening measurement. Photograph both sides, note the opening direction and exposure, and insist that profiles, hardware and glazing are identified in the quote.

## 1. Aluminium window fabrication

Mark fixed and opening panels against furniture positions and state noise or thermal performance needs.

**Quotation brief:** Measure openings at multiple points; photograph frames, hinges, thresholds, drainage paths and adjacent finishes. Specify profile system, finish, glazing, screens and hardware, and clarify whether removal, sealant, wall repairs and transport are included. State whether design, fabrication or installation is required: an inclusive total without that breakdown is difficult to compare.

**Handover:** Check sliding or hinged sashes operate without rubbing. Test operation and locks, check frame alignment, drainage and properly sealed junctions. Keep photos, approved samples and warranty details, and record snags before final payment.
