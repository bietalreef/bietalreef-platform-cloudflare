# Glass facades and glazing — Al Hili

Glazing is not just a visual finish. A useful quote needs installation height, usage, safety expectations, cleaning access and whether lifting equipment is necessary. Glass type and fixings must suit the actual opening.

## 1. Glass facades

Specify facade area, sun exposure and glare-control needs; colour alone does not define performance.

**Quotation brief:** Send clear photos from both sides, opening dimensions, height from ground and fixing surfaces. Request the design-specified glass type and thickness, fixings, lifting, sealing and removal of old glazing as separate items. State whether design, fabrication or installation is required: an inclusive total without that breakdown is difficult to compare.

**Handover:** Inspect panel alignment, seals and water drainage. Check the required glazing documents, secure supports, finished joints, safe operation and cleaning access. Keep photos, approved samples and warranty details, and record snags before final payment.
