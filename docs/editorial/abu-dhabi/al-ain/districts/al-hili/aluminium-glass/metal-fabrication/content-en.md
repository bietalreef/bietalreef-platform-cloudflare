# Metal fabrication and welding — Al Hili

For metal gates, rails or shade structures in Al Hili, agree on drawings, section sizes and corrosion protection before accepting a price. Steel thickness, coating and anchoring can matter more than the total figure.

## 1. Steel door fabrication

Confirm gate width, leaf weight, swing space and locking; heavy leaves need suitable hinges and supports.

**Quotation brief:** Provide site photos, opening and height measurements, fixing locations and any known concealed services. Identify metal grade, sections, welds, surface preparation, coating, delivery, lifting and anchoring; distinguish structural design from fabrication when required. State whether design, fabrication or installation is required: an inclusive total without that breakdown is difficult to compare.

**Handover:** Cycle the gate repeatedly and check ground clearance. Check accessible welds, alignment, coating coverage and sound fixings with no hazardous edges. Keep photos, approved samples and warranty details, and record snags before final payment.
