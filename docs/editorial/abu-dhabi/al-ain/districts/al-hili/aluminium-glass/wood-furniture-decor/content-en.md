# Custom furniture and timber interiors — Al Hili

For bespoke joinery in an Al Hili home or workplace, a matching colour is only one part of the brief. Access clearances, intended use and fixing details matter just as much. Get a workable layout and final dimensions before pricing production.

## 1. Custom furniture making

Confirm use and anticipated loads before choosing board material; a study table is not a heavy-duty cupboard.

**Quotation brief:** Share photographs of walls and entrances, width/height/depth measurements, socket and AC positions, and visible damp areas. Itemise timber or board grade, thickness, edging, hardware, transport and installation; electrical relocation or substrate repairs should not be assumed included. State whether design, fabrication or installation is required: an inclusive total without that breakdown is difficult to compare.

**Handover:** Test drawers and shelving under normal intended use. Check secure anchoring, consistent reveals, safe edges and agreement with the approved sample. Keep photos, approved samples and warranty details, and record snags before final payment.
