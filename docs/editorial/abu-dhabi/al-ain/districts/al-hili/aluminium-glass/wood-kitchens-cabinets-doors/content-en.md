# Wood kitchens, wardrobes and doors — Al Hili

Preparatory company/workshop content, not evidence of an available workshop, price or date.

## 1. Wood kitchen design

Before requesting wood kitchen design, prepare site photos, opening dimensions, electrical and plumbing locations, daily-use requirements and the desired finish. Focus the specification on **work triangle, appliance positions and ventilation**: errors here can affect fit, safety and service life.

**Scope and exclusions:** Ask for fabrication drawings and material/finish samples identifying thickness, edging, hinges and fixing methods. Demolition, service relocation, damp treatment and removal of old units are excluded unless written into the offer. Fire-rated doors require documented conformity for the complete assembly under applicable authority rules, not just a trade label.

**Comparison and agreement:** Compare materials, dimensions, fittings, lead time, delivery, installation and warranty. Approve the final survey and drawings before cutting, and agree how dimensional discrepancies, payments and site protection are handled.

**Handover:** Inspect work triangle, appliance positions and ventilation, straight edges and joints, secure fixings and smooth movement where doors or drawers are present. Photograph snags and retain the finish sample. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare a quotation request without guaranteeing a company or appointment.
