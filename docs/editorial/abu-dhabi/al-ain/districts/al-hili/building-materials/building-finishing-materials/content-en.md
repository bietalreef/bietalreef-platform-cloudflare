# Building and finishing materials — Al Hili, Al Ain

An editorial guide to specifications and comparing establishment quotations; it does not claim that a supplier, price or appointment is available.

**Classification:** currently not selectable; this is preparatory information, not an activation notice.


## 1. Cement supply

For cement supply on an Al Hili project, first establish where the material will be used, the quantity needed and the delivery date that fits the work sequence. The distinguishing technical check is **cement type, strength grade, production date and intact bags**; a generic trade name alone is not a sufficient specification.

**Request preparation:** Attach a quantity schedule, reference sample photo or drawing, and record units, sizes, required grade and unloading location. Ask about compatible substitutes when the exact item is unavailable, but approve changes in writing. The scope covers only the listed supply items; transport, offloading, lifting, cutting, installation and waste removal are excluded unless expressly priced.

**Compare and agree:** Compare like-for-like unit rates, product origin and grade, minimum order, lead time, delivery charges, expected breakage or waste and the process for shortages or damage. Confirm quantities, batch details, delivery milestones and rejection rights for nonconforming goods in the agreement.

**Receiving and quotation:** Match product labels, invoice and counts against the order; inspect cement type, strength grade, production date and intact bags where practical before signing. Photograph packaging and any defects. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare a quotation request with these details, without promising supplier availability, price or delivery date.
