# Building operations and maintenance — Al Hili

Repeated faults call for a structured maintenance register, not isolated callouts. Start with an asset list, fault priority and an authorised contact, then compare service scopes, response terms and exclusions.

Company-service guidance only; it does not confirm availability of personnel, businesses, price or a date.
