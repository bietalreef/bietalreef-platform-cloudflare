# Cleaning, disinfection and sanitisation — Al Hili

Disinfection is not a substitute for removing dirt. Ask about cleaning before treatment, suitable products and surface compatibility. Avoid unsupported promises of complete pathogen removal.

Company-service guidance only; it does not confirm availability of personnel, businesses, price or a date.

## 1. Home disinfection service

Identify kitchens, bathrooms, toys and vulnerable furniture and ask for suitable non-spray approaches where needed. For the request, List room sizes, use, frequently touched surfaces, last cleaning and occupancy windows. For sensitive or regulated work, the client should check the contractor's suitability and any applicable approvals before engagement.

**Clear terms:** Specify pre-cleaning, authorised product use, safety controls, recommended contact times, re-entry advice and service records. Compare responsibilities, visits, records, exclusions and supplies; avoid a lump sum that hides how output will be measured.

**Acceptance and follow-up:** Check ventilation and child/pet re-entry instructions. Request a record of procedures and treated surfaces, and check that electronics and soft finishes were protected. Have open issues recorded and resolved rather than accepting a generic completion statement.
