# Facades and site cleaning — Al Hili

Facade and site cleaning may involve specialist access, drainage and permissions rather than ordinary cleaners. Show the facade height, cladding and surrounding pedestrian routes so the method does not damage glass or stone.

This is preparation guidance for company services; it does not guarantee a listed provider, availability or price.

## 1. Glass facade cleaning

Ask how mineral deposits will be removed without abrasive tools that scratch glass or coatings. Before requesting a quotation, Provide elevation photographs, storeys, access areas, contamination type and nearby pedestrians or cars. Distinguish cleaning from repair when the underlying surface is damaged rather than dirty.

**Compare the scope:** Itemise access method, barriers, working window, water/power, cleaning agents and dirty-water collection. Agree on products, protection of furniture and finishes, and drying or shutdown time where relevant. Avoid unverifiable outcome guarantees.

**Handover:** Inspect panes in angled light for streaks or new scratches. Inspect upper edges, joints and sensitive finishes, and check that water has not entered interiors. Photograph outstanding issues and agree any retreatment before sign-off.
