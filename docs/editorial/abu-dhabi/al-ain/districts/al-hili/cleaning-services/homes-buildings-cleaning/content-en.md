# Home and building cleaning — Al Hili

For an Al Hili villa, apartment or office, describe the dirt level and each room's required work. Routine housekeeping and post-fit-out dust removal are different jobs and should not be priced as if identical.

This is preparation guidance for company services; it does not guarantee a listed provider, availability or price.

## 1. Villa cleaning

List majlis, stair, annex and little-used rooms; high ceilings may need special access equipment. Before requesting a quotation, Send room, bathroom, kitchen and floor counts, photographs, approximate area, floor materials and access to water/power. Distinguish cleaning from repair when the underlying surface is damaged rather than dirty.

**Compare the scope:** Separate dusting, interior/exterior glass, appliances, cupboards, floors, waste removal and who supplies consumables and equipment. Agree on products, protection of furniture and finishes, and drying or shutdown time where relevant. Avoid unverifiable outcome guarantees.

**Handover:** Check stair corners, door bottoms and furniture zones. Inspect edges, behind furniture, drains and corners in good light; fragrance or a shiny floor alone is not proof. Photograph outstanding issues and agree any retreatment before sign-off.
