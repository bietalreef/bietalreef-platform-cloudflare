# Pest and rodent control — Al Hili

A pest sighting in a kitchen or garden does not automatically call for indiscriminate spraying. Document where and when pests appear, mention children and pets, and ask for an inspection-led treatment and follow-up plan.

Company-service guidance only; it does not confirm availability of personnel, businesses, price or a date.

## 1. Cockroach control

Check plumbing leaks, cracks behind appliances and accessible food sources before treatment. For the request, Share pest photos, droppings or damage, locations, duration, and any children, pets or exposed water. For sensitive or regulated work, the client should check the contractor's suitability and any applicable approvals before engagement.

**Clear terms:** Ask for the target species, inspection, authorised treatment, follow-up, prevention and re-entry advice from a suitably qualified and licensed contractor as required. Compare responsibilities, visits, records, exclusions and supplies; avoid a lump sum that hides how output will be measured.

**Acceptance and follow-up:** Seek follow-up for hidden harbourages rather than only spraying visible surfaces. Review visit records, safety directions and exclusion recommendations; one sighting alone does not prove overall success or failure. Have open issues recorded and resolved rather than accepting a generic completion statement.
