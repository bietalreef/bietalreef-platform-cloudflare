# Water tank and pool cleaning — Al Hili

Tank and pool cleaning needs a planned shutdown, cleaning and recommissioning process. Before quoting, confirm capacity, access and whether disinfection, testing and documented records are included.

This is preparation guidance for company services; it does not guarantee a listed provider, availability or price.

## 1. Water tank cleaning

Ask for a safe-entry plan for enclosed tanks and responsibility for pumps and water isolation. Before requesting a quotation, Provide site photos, dimensions, known capacity and fill, drainage and electrical points. Distinguish cleaning from repair when the underlying surface is damaged rather than dirty.

**Compare the scope:** Separate drainage, sediment removal, rinsing, permitted disinfection, water-quality tests, recommissioning and service records. Agree on products, protection of furniture and finishes, and drying or shutdown time where relevant. Avoid unverifiable outcome guarantees.

**Handover:** Inspect sediment removal and confirm no tools or debris remain. Obtain a procedure record and check lids, valves and leaks after recommissioning. Photograph outstanding issues and agree any retreatment before sign-off.
