# Upholstery and soft furnishing cleaning — Al Hili

Before booking sofa, majlis or mattress cleaning, photograph the fabric tag, stains and any prior treatment. Not every textile tolerates hot water or steam; the method should be checked against care guidance and trialled discreetly.

This is preparation guidance for company services; it does not guarantee a listed provider, availability or price.

## 1. Steam sofa cleaning

Check the care label permits the proposed method; inappropriate steam can shrink fibres or set marks. Before requesting a quotation, Provide seat count or square metres, fabric composition, stains/odours and when the room can be left to ventilate and dry. Distinguish cleaning from repair when the underlying surface is damaged rather than dirty.

**Compare the scope:** State whether dry extraction, wet extraction or stain treatment is planned and whether moving items and colourfastness testing are included. Agree on products, protection of furniture and finishes, and drying or shutdown time where relevant. Avoid unverifiable outcome guarantees.

**Handover:** Check there is no excessive moisture under cushions. Check for shade change, rings, residual damp and odours after drying; the final result may not be visible immediately. Photograph outstanding issues and agree any retreatment before sign-off.
