# Staffing and operations services — Al Hili

Staff supply is more than a headcount. Define tasks, location, working hours, supervision and skills, and establish responsibility for safe work and compliance with applicable labour requirements.

Company-service guidance only; it does not confirm availability of personnel, businesses, price or a date.

## 1. Cleaning staff supply

Set cleaning tasks and room counts and whether consumables/equipment are provided. For the request, Give headcount, tasks, shift length, site conditions, available equipment and who provides instructions. For sensitive or regulated work, the client should check the contractor's suitability and any applicable approvals before engagement.

**Clear terms:** Specify staffing arrangement, supervision, transport, PPE, absence cover, legal responsibilities, insurance and extra charges. Compare responsibilities, visits, records, exclusions and supplies; avoid a lump sum that hides how output will be measured.

**Acceptance and follow-up:** Inspect completed shift checklists. Require attendance, daily tasks and safe-working records rather than only a headcount. Have open issues recorded and resolved rather than accepting a generic completion statement.
