# Aluminium and glass factories — Al Hili

For an Al Hili project, assess the task, equipment capacity, site access and safety arrangements before comparing a headline hire rate or factory quote. Operational and delivery responsibilities must be set out in writing.

> Taxonomy status: currently non-selectable. This guide does not advertise an available supplier or activate a restricted category.

## 1. Aluminium profile manufacture

Specify section geometry, wall thickness, thermal requirements and anodising or coating by specification. To request a realistic quote, Send fabrication drawings, door/profile schedules, quantities and panel dimensions. Also check there is safe unloading, working and storage space where needed.

**Terms and comparison:** Identify profile system, finish, glass specification, compliance documents, sample approval, final sizes and shipping. Agree how extras, downtime, delays and damage are treated. A cheaper headline may exclude the operator, logistics or essential accessories.

**Acceptance:** Inspect profile straightness and coating consistency. Compare part tags, batches, dimensions and transport condition against approved drawings. Record dates, item references and photographic snags before signing delivery notes.
