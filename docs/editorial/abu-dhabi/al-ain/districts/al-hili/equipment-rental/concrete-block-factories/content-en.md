# Concrete, block and cement-product plants — Al Hili

For concrete and blockwork, the design specification and bill of quantities should lead the order. A cheaper unit rate can be misleading if strength, delivery and testing do not match the project.

> Source taxonomy status: proposed, not active. Editorial preparation does not establish supplier availability or category activation.

## 1. Ready-mix concrete production

Specify concrete grade, workability, admixtures and pour timing from the engineer's approved design. When explaining the site, Provide technical specifications, quantity schedules, casting/storage locations and site access. Identify fragile items and access restrictions so the supplier can price the actual work.

**Written terms:** Define grade, dimensions, agreed tests, batch sizes, haulage, discharge and quotation validity. Compare offers on equivalent scope, confirm feasible timings, acceptance method, damage responsibility and exclusions. Establish whether rates are per unit, journey or day.

**Handover:** Check batch tickets, dispatch/arrival times and specified tests. Check batch labels and specified conformity/test records, with project sampling as agreed. Record discrepancies before signing delivery acceptance.
