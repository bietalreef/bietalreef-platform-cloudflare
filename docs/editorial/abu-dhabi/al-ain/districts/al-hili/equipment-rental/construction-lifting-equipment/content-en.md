# Construction and lifting equipment rental — Al Hili

For an Al Hili project, assess the task, equipment capacity, site access and safety arrangements before comparing a headline hire rate or factory quote. Operational and delivery responsibilities must be set out in writing.

> Taxonomy status: listed in taxonomy. This guide does not advertise an available supplier or activate a restricted category.

## 1. Scaffolding rental

Provide facade height/length, ground conditions and public walkway proximity; safe erection and dismantling must be planned. To request a realistic quote, Provide site location, duration, movement area, working height, indicative load and truck-access photos. Also check there is safe unloading, working and storage space where needed.

**Terms and comparison:** The quote should separate machine class, rated capacity, delivery period, operator, fuel, transport, maintenance, insurance and site-damage responsibility. Agree how extras, downtime, delays and damage are treated. A cheaper headline may exclude the operator, logistics or essential accessories.

**Acceptance:** Check guardrails, base support, anchoring and competent inspection before use. Do not accept equipment until identity, condition and required checks are confirmed, with pre-existing damage photographed. Record dates, item references and photographic snags before signing delivery notes.
