# Light tools and equipment rental — Al Hili

For an Al Hili project, assess the task, equipment capacity, site access and safety arrangements before comparing a headline hire rate or factory quote. Operational and delivery responsibilities must be set out in writing.

> Taxonomy status: listed in taxonomy. This guide does not advertise an available supplier or activate a restricted category.

## 1. Generator rental

Calculate continuous and start-up loads, connections and ventilation; a small set may trip when AC starts. To request a realistic quote, State task, hire duration and available electrical/water connections, with work-area photos. Also check there is safe unloading, working and storage space where needed.

**Terms and comparison:** Confirm rating, accessories, consumables, delivery, chargeable rental period and deposit or insurance terms. Agree how extras, downtime, delays and damage are treated. A cheaper headline may exclude the operator, logistics or essential accessories.

**Acceptance:** Check voltage stability, fuel arrangement and appropriate earthing. Perform a handover test with the supplier and record machine and accessory condition. Record dates, item references and photographic snags before signing delivery notes.
