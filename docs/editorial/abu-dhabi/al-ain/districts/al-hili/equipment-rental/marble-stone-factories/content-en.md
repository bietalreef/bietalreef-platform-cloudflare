# Marble and stone factories — Al Hili

For an Al Hili project, assess the task, equipment capacity, site access and safety arrangements before comparing a headline hire rate or factory quote. Operational and delivery responsibilities must be set out in writing.

> Taxonomy status: currently non-selectable. This guide does not advertise an available supplier or activate a restricted category.

## 1. Marble and stone cutting

Confirm vein direction and joint locations before cutting to avoid unnecessary slab waste. To request a realistic quote, Provide detailed measurements, site photos, layout and cut-out positions. Also check there is safe unloading, working and storage space where needed.

**Terms and comparison:** Identify stone type, approved sample, thickness, finish, cutting, delivery, lifting, wastage and installation scope. Agree how extras, downtime, delays and damage are treated. A cheaper headline may exclude the operator, logistics or essential accessories.

**Acceptance:** Check final dimensions, stone selection and protected corners. Inspect veining, shade, thickness, edge finishing and slab labels before acceptance. Record dates, item references and photographic snags before signing delivery notes.
