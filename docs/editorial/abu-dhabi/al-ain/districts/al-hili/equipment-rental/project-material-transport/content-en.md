# Transport of construction materials and equipment — Al Hili

Stone, steel and machinery need different restraints and unloading methods from household furniture. For an Al Hili site, state dimensions, weights, loading windows, truck access and who provides unloading equipment.

> Source taxonomy status: listed in taxonomy. Editorial preparation does not establish supplier availability or category activation.

## 1. Construction materials transport

Count bricks or cement on suitable pallets and protect moisture-sensitive loads. When explaining the site, Attach a list with indicative weights/lengths and photographs of loading and unloading access. Identify fragile items and access restrictions so the supplier can price the actual work.

**Written terms:** Specify vehicle class, restraints, any required lifting, appropriate route permissions, waiting and loading/unloading. Compare offers on equivalent scope, confirm feasible timings, acceptance method, damage responsibility and exclusions. Establish whether rates are per unit, journey or day.

**Handover:** Check breakage, moisture and counts when unloaded. Check quantity, load protection and condition at pickup and delivery, recording damage before sign-off. Record discrepancies before signing delivery acceptance.
