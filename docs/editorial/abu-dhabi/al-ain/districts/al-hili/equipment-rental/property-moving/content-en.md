# Moving furniture and belongings — Al Hili

A household or office move needs an inventory and an access plan before selecting a truck. Record bulky items, lift restrictions and loading distances, then compare packing, disassembly and damage terms.

> Source taxonomy status: listed in taxonomy. Editorial preparation does not establish supplier availability or category activation.

## 1. Home furniture moving

Count rooms, kitchen appliances, heavy glazing and pieces that may not pass doorways. When explaining the site, Share a photo inventory, bulky dimensions, both addresses, floors, lift access and loading arrangements. Identify fragile items and access restrictions so the supplier can price the actual work.

**Written terms:** Separate vehicles, crew, packing, dismantling, assembly, storage, insurance and additional trips. Compare offers on equivalent scope, confirm feasible timings, acceptance method, damage responsibility and exclusions. Establish whether rates are per unit, journey or day.

**Handover:** Reconcile arrivals against inventory and inspect delicate furniture. Use before/after inventory and photos of vulnerable corners, recording loss or scratches promptly. Record discrepancies before signing delivery acceptance.
