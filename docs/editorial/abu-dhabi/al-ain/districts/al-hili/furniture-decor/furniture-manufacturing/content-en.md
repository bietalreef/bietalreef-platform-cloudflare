# Furniture manufacturing — Al Hili

A useful furnishing request starts with verified measurements and an approved sample. A reference picture will not identify construction, installation or delivery requirements. Compare what is included in each quote.

> Taxonomy status: non-selectable. Editorial guidance does not activate a listing.

## 1. Home furniture fabrication

Measure doorways and clearance around beds and cabinets before signing off dimensions. For the quotation, prepare space and access photos, dimensions, users and intended function; make clear whether the request is for a new item, repair or supply only. Made-to-measure work should not rely on approximate dimensions.

**What the quote should cover:** Ask for a breakdown of board or frame construction, thickness, finish, fittings, fabrication and installation. Identify extras such as disposal of old items, wall repairs and access to upper floors. Agree quantities, samples, payment stages, timing and warranty terms so offers are comparable.

**At handover:** Try the item under normal use. Check finish, dimensions, edges, stability and cleaning access. Photograph any defects before signing off.
