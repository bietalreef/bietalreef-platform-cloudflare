# Furniture and furnishing stores — Al Hili

A useful furnishing request starts with verified measurements and an approved sample. A reference picture will not identify construction, installation or delivery requirements. Compare what is included in each quote.

> Taxonomy status: non-selectable. Editorial guidance does not activate a listing.

## 1. Home furniture retail

Verify actual product dimensions and access through stairs and doors. For the quotation, prepare site photos, door clearances and item counts; make clear whether the request is for a new item, repair or supply only. Made-to-measure work should not rely on approximate dimensions.

**What the quote should cover:** Ask for a breakdown of models, specifications, counts, delivery, assembly and warranty. Identify extras such as disposal of old items, wall repairs and access to upper floors. Agree quantities, samples, payment stages, timing and warranty terms so offers are comparable.

**At handover:** Try the item under normal use. Check finish, dimensions, edges, stability and cleaning access. Photograph any defects before signing off.
