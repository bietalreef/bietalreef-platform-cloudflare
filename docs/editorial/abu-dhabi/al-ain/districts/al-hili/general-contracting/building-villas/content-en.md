# Al Hili — Building and villa construction

For a project in Al Hili, establish the scope, drawings and site photographs before requesting quotations. This guide does not confirm that a contractor is listed or available. Permits and supervision requirements depend on the work and must be verified with the relevant authorities.

## Villa construction

Provide the intended floor count, rooms, annexes and built-up area, with approved drawings if available. Request a programme separating foundations, structure and finishes; compare bids only against matching specifications.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Residential building construction

State the number of units, entrances, lifts, parking spaces and shared services. Agree the scope of building systems, fire provisions and testing with the consultant before accepting the bill of quantities.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Commercial building construction

Describe the commercial use, load requirements, façade and delivery access. Separate shell construction from tenant fit-out, shared systems and permit responsibilities.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## General building works

Request quantities for blockwork, mortar, lintels and openings, and identify who provides scaffolding and temporary utilities. Check alignment and opening positions before plastering.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Concrete works

Identify the elements to be poured, specified concrete grade, testing and curing arrangements. Clarify whether formwork, reinforcement and pumping are included.
