# Al Hili — Demolition and concrete works

For a project in Al Hili, establish the scope, drawings and site photographs before requesting quotations. This guide does not confirm that a contractor is listed or available. Permits and supervision requirements depend on the work and must be verified with the relevant authorities.

## Demolition works

Supply the building plan, demolition limits and existing services. Require utility isolation, temporary support, neighbour protection and lawful waste disposal.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Removal of buildings and annexes

Record footprint, height, foundation type and proximity to fences and utilities. Clarify permits, haulage and site levelling after removal.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Concrete cutting and coring

Mark each opening's diameter, depth and position relative to reinforcement and concealed services. Obtain structural approval where required and control slurry, dust and finish damage.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Footing and foundation pours

Confirm excavation level, soil conditions, reinforcement and concrete cover before pouring. Check delivery tickets, tests, curing and water protection.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Concrete slab pours

Inspect formwork, props, MEP sleeves and pour locations before trucks arrive. Require curing and formwork-stripping procedures approved by the responsible professional.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Concrete wall construction

Specify wall thickness, height, openings, embedded services and required finish. Inspect formwork, reinforcement, joints and curing before acceptance.

## Demolition waste removal
