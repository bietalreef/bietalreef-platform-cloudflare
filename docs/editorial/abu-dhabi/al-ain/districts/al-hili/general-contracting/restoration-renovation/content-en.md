# Al Hili — Restoration and renovation

For a project in Al Hili, establish the scope, drawings and site photographs before requesting quotations. This guide does not confirm that a contractor is listed or available. Permits and supervision requirements depend on the work and must be verified with the relevant authorities.

## Older building restoration

Commission a condition survey of concrete, steel and leaks before choosing repair products. Record what will be removed or retained and any temporary propping.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Villa renovation

Identify rooms occupied during works and whether plumbing and wiring are included or only finishes. Require protection of furniture and floors and a room-by-room schedule.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Apartment renovation

Check building rules on working hours, lifts and waste. Send a layout and kitchen/bathroom photos so bids can itemise the work.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Building rehabilitation

Separate structural safety repairs, service reinstatement and cosmetic improvements. Specify electrical, drainage and waterproofing tests before reoccupation.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Structural crack remediation

Photograph the crack with a scale and record its location and progression. Do not accept filler or injection without an engineer's diagnosis and monitoring/repair method.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Façade refurbishment

Record façade height, cladding type, cracks and water-entry points. Check safe access, joint treatment and material compatibility before approving the finish.

**Quotation and handover:** Request written quantities, material specifications, testing, exclusions and payment milestones. Record defects with photographs before signing off the work.

## Preparing a building for reuse

Specify the intended use, occupancy, exits, ventilation and service condition. Request an approvals checklist and commissioning tests before handover.
