# Al Hili — General maintenance and finishes — Electrical and lighting

This practical guide is for villa, apartment and property owners in Al Hili. Before requesting a quote, document site conditions, dimensions and anything that must remain untouched. Preparation and repair scope can affect costs as much as material selection.

## Building electrical rough-in

Provide plans, room count and anticipated equipment loads before walls are closed. Require conduit routes, circuits, earthing, tests and necessary approvals; verify outlet positions and photograph concealed works. Request a written breakdown of materials, labour, preparation, clean-up, exclusions and workmanship warranty.
