# Al Hili — General maintenance and finishes — Flooring and wall cladding

This practical guide is for villa, apartment and property owners in Al Hili. Before requesting a quote, document site conditions, dimensions and anything that must remain untouched. Preparation and repair scope can affect costs as much as material selection.

## Ceramic tile installation

Measure the net area, tile size and laying direction; photograph the existing floor and drains. Confirm removal, substrate levelling, adhesive, grout and movement joints. Check aligned courses, sound bonding and drainage before handover. Request a written breakdown of materials, labour, preparation, clean-up, exclusions and workmanship warranty.
