# Al Hili — General maintenance and finishes — General maintenance

For a property in Al Hili, explain whether the site is occupied or vacant and share clear photos and approximate measurements before requesting a quote. Ask suppliers to separate materials, labour, protection, clean-up and warranty. These notes explain each service without assuming a provider is available.

## Routine villa maintenance

Separate preventive inspections of doors, plumbing, electrical circuits and AC from chargeable repairs; request a checklist, visit frequency and defect report. Require the quotation to specify inclusions, exclusions, materials and timeframe; inspect the completed work on site before acceptance.

## Routine building maintenance
