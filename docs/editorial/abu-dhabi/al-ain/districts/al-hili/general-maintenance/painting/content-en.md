# Al Hili — General maintenance and finishes — Painting and coatings

For a villa, flat or business premises in Al Hili, a service name alone is not enough for a useful quotation. Describe the existing condition and location, attach overview and close-up photos, and share available measurements. The guidance below helps scope work without implying a listed contractor is available locally.

## دهان الفلل

List rooms, wall and ceiling areas and filler condition; itemise preparation, primer, coats, protection and cleanup. Ask the company to itemise materials and models, enabling or remedial works, exclusions, duration and warranty in writing. Compare quantities and specifications rather than headline prices alone; inspect the finished result or test its operation and record defects before sign-off.
