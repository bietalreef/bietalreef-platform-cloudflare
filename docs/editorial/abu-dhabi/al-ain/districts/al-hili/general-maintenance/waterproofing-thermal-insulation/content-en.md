# Al Hili — General maintenance and finishes — Waterproofing and thermal insulation

For a villa, flat or business premises in Al Hili, a service name alone is not enough for a useful quotation. Describe the existing condition and location, attach overview and close-up photos, and share available measurements. The guidance below helps scope work without implying a listed contractor is available locally.

## عزل الأسطح

Photograph ponding, drains and joints and measure roof area; specify drainage falls, upstands and a suitable water test. Ask the company to itemise materials and models, enabling or remedial works, exclusions, duration and warranty in writing. Compare quantities and specifications rather than headline prices alone; inspect the finished result or test its operation and record defects before sign-off.
