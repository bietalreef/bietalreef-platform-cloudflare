# Metal fabrication in Al Jimi

This is preparatory information for business and workshop quotations, not a claim that a provider is available. Final dimensions require site verification; structural and safety-critical work needs suitably qualified review and applicable approvals.

## 1. Steel door fabrication

**When needed and scope:** For a service-room door, external entrance or gate, state security needs, exposure and swing direction. State whether the proposal covers fabrication only or supply and installation; removal, transport and repairs to adjoining finishes must be explicit.

**Photos and measurements:** Photograph both sides of the opening; record clear width, height, wall depth and finished floor level. Final workshop measurements must be verified on site.

**Materials and quotation comparison:** Compare sheet and frame thickness, hinge and lock specification, anchors, anti-corrosion treatment and finish. Demolition and wall repairs must be separately priced. Compare itemized quantities, schedule, change provisions and warranty in writing.

**Handover checks:** Cycle the door and lock; inspect clearances, weld edges, hinge fixings and complete paint coverage.
