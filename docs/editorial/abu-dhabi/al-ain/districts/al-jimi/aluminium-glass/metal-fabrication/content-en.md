# Metal fabrication in Al Jimi

A workshop quotation should identify materials, shop drawings, finishing and fixings, not merely a total. For work in Al Jimi, document actual openings, approved samples and delivery access, and obtain professional design or approvals where the job requires them.

## 1. Steel door fabrication

**Design decision:** Steel door fabrication. Provide site photos, opening and fixing dimensions and desired metal grade; indicate opening direction, intended use and exposure to water or heat where relevant.

**Scope and quotation:** Specify sections, thickness, welds, anchors, corrosion protection, coating and lifting, with structural design for load-bearing work where applicable. Compare thickness, hardware, samples, warranty and fabrication/fitting stages. Agree how site-size discrepancies and transport damage are handled; moving services or repairing adjoining walls is not automatically included.

**Handover:** Inspect weld quality, safe edges, alignment, coating coverage and sound anchors, photograph joints and defects, and test movement or operation. Safety-critical installations need appropriate conformity evidence, not just a good appearance.
