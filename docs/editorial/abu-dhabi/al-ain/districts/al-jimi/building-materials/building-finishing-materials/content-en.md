# Building and finishing materials in Al Jimi — procurement guide

This is preparatory information for a company/establishment specialty that is currently non-selectable. It does not imply a listed supplier, stock, price or delivery slot. Obtain the responsible engineer's approval for structural materials before purchase.

## 1. Cement supply

**When this is needed and what to send:** For mortar, plaster or concrete work, provide the cement type and grade from the approved specification, bag count and weight, intended use and delivery sequence. Photograph the pallet unloading access and identify whether a crane or manual handling is needed. Specify a dry, sheltered storage area.

**Scope and quote comparison:** Request manufacturer, type, grade, pack weight, batch identification, production date and any project-required compliance documents. Compare equivalent total weights rather than bag counts alone. Separate transport, lifting and unloading charges. Mixing, concrete placement and on-site suitability testing are not part of cement supply unless expressly contracted.

**Agreement and acceptance:** Agree delivery batches, product-specific storage limits and who replaces torn or moisture-damaged bags. On arrival match grade, bag weight, batch markings and invoice, checking for damp packaging and visible lumps. Segregate suspect stock pending a competent acceptance decision; visual inspection alone cannot certify technical compliance.

**Requesting quotes:** Send the approved specification, quantity by weight or bag count and size, and unloading/storage photographs. Ask for itemised delivery, batch dates and damaged-bag replacement terms. Biet Al Reef customer service may help prepare the request without guaranteeing a supplier, price or delivery date.

## 2. Sand supply

**When and what to provide:** For sand supply to an Al Jimi project, supply plaster, concrete or backfill use, grading, cleanliness and cubic-metre load, plus relevant site or sample photos and drawings. Distinguish washed sand from fill sand and verify grading and cleanliness.

**Quotation scope and comparison:** Compare net cubic-metre volume, sand type, washing and grading, plus tipper waiting charges; agree how delivered volume will be checked. Separate sand haulage from waiting and unloading charges, and assign responsibility for rejecting a load that differs from the agreed sample or grading. Spreading, compaction and engineering suitability tests are excluded unless expressly included.

**Agreement and acceptance:** Specify sand type, unloading point and how delivered volume will be verified on arrival. At receipt, verify no visible debris or contamination and delivered volume agrees. Photograph discrepancies on the delivery record before use; obtain project approval for material substitutions where required.

**Requesting quotes:** State whether sand is for plaster, concrete or fill, the load volume and unloading point; request grading, washing, measurement basis, waiting charges and quantity verification. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare a comparable request without guaranteeing a supplier, price or date.

## 4. Block and brick supply

**When this is needed and what to send:** For internal or external masonry walls, provide wall drawings, the block schedule, dimensions, thicknesses and specified strength. Identify solid, hollow or insulating blocks as applicable. Include an approved sample photo, pallet unloading location and safe storage area.

**Scope and quote comparison:** Compare unit prices only for matching dimensions, strength and pieces per pallet. Ask for project-required test evidence and clarify breakage allowance, replacement, pallet charges, lifting and transport. **Agreement and acceptance:** Record block type, delivery lots and how nonconforming dimensions or damaged pieces will be rejected. Check representative pieces from multiple pallets, count units and compare dimensions, edges and labels with the purchase order. Photograph and record breakage before distributing pallets. Structural material approval remains with the responsible professional.

**Requesting quotes:** Send block dimensions, types, quantities and specified strength. Ask for piece and pallet prices, lifting, damage/replacement terms and available technical evidence. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare the request without guaranteeing availability, price or timing.

## 7. Construction material supply for projects

**When and what to provide:** For a project requiring several materials over successive construction stages, prepare an approved item schedule showing each product, unit, quantity, specification and planned use date. Attach access photos, an unloading plan and realistic storage capacity; flag products vulnerable to moisture or heat. A generic request for “structural and finishing materials” is not enough for comparable quotations.

**Quotation scope and comparison:** Require a separate rate for each specified item, including its grade or manufacturer, unit of measure, quotation validity and any alternative subject to approval. Compare offers using identical quantities, specifications and delivery stages. Separate freight, lifting, unloading and waiting charges. A consolidated supply contract does not automatically include installation, engineering verification or on-site inventory management.

**Agreement and acceptance:** Link purchase orders to construction stages and require the responsible technical party to approve substitutions. Define order notice periods, responsibility for late critical items, shortages, damaged goods and returns according to the product type. For each delivery, match quantities, labels and batch references where applicable to the purchase order; photograph discrepancies before materials are distributed. Visual checks alone cannot establish structural-material compliance.

**Requesting quotes:** Share an itemised, reviewable schedule with quantities, specifications, staged delivery dates and unloading location. Ask for item-by-item prices, lead times, substitution approval rules, replacement conditions and separate delivery invoices. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare the request without guaranteeing a supplier, price or delivery date.

## 8. Gypsum material supply

**When this is needed and what to provide:** For gypsum board ceilings or partitions, send the area drawings and board schedule showing dimensions, thickness, edge type and any specified moisture or fire rating by location. If gypsum powder is required instead, give its intended use, bag weight and specification; it is not interchangeable with board. Photograph delivery access and a dry, flat storage location.

**Scope and quote comparison:** Compare equivalent square-metre board costs for the same thickness and rating, with pack quantities, edge protection and freight. Request the project-required rating documentation and clarify whether screws, tape, joint compound and framing channels are included or separate. Supply excludes framing installation, joint finishing and certification of the completed fire-rated assembly.

**Agreement and acceptance:** Record product codes, board ratings and dimensions, breakage allowances and who lifts packs to the required floor. Check pack labels and representative board edges and faces before distribution; document wet, bowed or broken sheets under the agreed rejection procedure. A rated board alone does not establish the fire rating of an installed wall or ceiling assembly; the complete system requires professional approval.

**Requesting quotes:** Separate ordinary, moisture-rated and fire-rated boards, and any gypsum powder, in the quantity schedule. Specify unloading and storage arrangements; request accessory prices, lifting charges and damage remedies. Biet Al Reef customer service may help structure the request without guaranteeing a supplier, price or delivery date.

**Requesting quotes:** Send a surface-and-area schedule, coating system and colour codes. Ask for estimated quantities by product, pack rates, transport and remedies for damaged or incorrect cans.
