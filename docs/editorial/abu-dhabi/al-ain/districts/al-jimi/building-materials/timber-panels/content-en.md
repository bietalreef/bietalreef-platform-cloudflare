# Timber and boards in Al Jimi — company procurement guide

Joinery boards should be chosen for their application, dimensions and assembly method. This is guidance, not confirmation of a listed supplier, stock or price.

## 1. MDF board supply

**Use case and preparation:** For this order, send a drawing stating board thickness, density, sheet size and moisture-resistance grade appropriate to the room, alongside site photos and quantities. Specify whether you need raw material or workshop preparation; installation is not assumed.

**Additional survey:** Inspect MDF sheet edges and damp storage risks; document required thickness, density and quantities.

**Comparing quotations:** Specify MDF grade, density, thickness and stated moisture-resistance classification with a product reference or approved sample; visually similar boards can have different cores. Compare full-sheet dimensions and rates, separating cutting, edging and delivery. Agree rejection criteria for swollen or bowed sheets and dry storage on arrival.

**Acceptance check:** Measure actual thickness on a sample from each bundle and check sheet flatness, edge swelling and water marks before signing. Reconcile sheet counts, dimensions and MDF grade with the purchase order; photograph damage and segregate rejected boards.

**Request a quote:** Send sheet counts, MDF dimensions, thickness, density and required moisture-resistance grade; state whether cutting, edging and delivery are included. Biet Al Reef customer service can help structure the enquiry without guaranteeing stock or a delivery date.

## 2. Solid timber supply

**Use case and preparation:** For this order, send a drawing stating wood species, section sizes, lengths, acceptable moisture content and finish, alongside site photos and quantities. Specify whether you need raw material or workshop preparation; installation is not assumed.

**Additional survey:** Photograph timber sections, lengths and visible knots; state the application and required drying condition.

**Comparing quotations:** Identify timber species, grade, cross-section, length and drying condition, since knots, splits and straightness affect value. Compare volume or linear-metre rates against the stated section, with machining, sanding, finishing and transport separately priced. Record allowable waste and rejection criteria for warping or unacceptable moisture.

**Acceptance check:** Check each section for straightness, length, knots and splits against the agreed timber grade. Where moisture content is contractual, request a documented sample measurement; record rejected pieces and reasons before final unloading.

**Request a quote:** Provide timber species, section sizes, lengths, quantities and drying requirements, with photos of the acceptable grading sample. Ask for machining and transport as separate items. Customer service can help prepare the request without guaranteeing a supplier or date.

## 3. Plywood supply

**Use case and preparation:** For this order, send a drawing stating ply count, thickness, bonding grade and suitability for dry or damp conditions, alongside site photos and quantities. Specify whether you need raw material or workshop preparation; installation is not assumed.

**Additional survey:** Provide a plywood edge photograph, intended wet or dry use and the required bonding grade.

**Comparing quotations:** Compare plywood by ply count, face grade, bonding classification and stated service conditions; do not accept a vague 'waterproof' label without a specification. Inspect a sample cut edge for voids and layers, and price cutting, packaging and delivery separately. Agree remedies for delamination or distortion and edge protection in transit.

**Acceptance check:** Inspect exposed cut edges for voids or delamination, then verify thickness, flatness and face quality against the sample. Record transit swelling or damage, and confirm the specified bonding grade rather than relying on a generic moisture-resistant label.

**Request a quote:** Specify plywood dimensions, thickness, face and bonding grades, service environment and quantity, attaching an edge photo or reference sample. Biet Al Reef customer service can help compare requests without guaranteeing stock or timing.

## 4. Melamine-faced board supply

**Use case and preparation:** For this order, send a drawing stating colour and texture code, core thickness, grain direction and matching edging, alongside site photos and quantities. Specify whether you need raw material or workshop preparation; installation is not assumed.

**Additional survey:** Specify the melamine finish code, grain direction, exposed edges and an actual finish sample.

**Comparing quotations:** Approve melamine colour code, texture, grain direction, core thickness and finish on both faces using a sample from the proposed batch. Compare usable sheets and grain-matching waste, with edge tape, cutting and drilling separate. Allocate responsibility for batch mismatch, scratches and chipped corners.

**Acceptance check:** Compare finish codes, texture and grain direction across sheets, particularly when batches are mixed. Inspect both faces for scratches and corners for chips, and separate mismatched boards before cutting or edge banding.

**Request a quote:** Supply the melamine finish code, physical colour sample, grain direction, core thickness, sheet count and edging requirements; state whether cutting and drilling are needed. Customer service can assist without guaranteeing availability.

## 5. HPL laminate supply

**Use case and preparation:** For this order, send a drawing stating laminate thickness, application, colour, texture and intended substrate, alongside site photos and quantities. Specify whether you need raw material or workshop preparation; installation is not assumed.

**Additional survey:** Show whether the order is raw HPL laminate or a faced board, with substrate and bonding method.

**Comparing quotations:** Distinguish raw HPL laminate sheets from pre-laminated boards; their pricing and fabrication differ. Request laminate thickness, finish code, substrate and intended adhesive, separating pressing or bonding labour from supply. Approve a sample and agree remedies for bubbles, delamination or shade mismatch.

**Acceptance check:** Confirm whether the delivery contains raw HPL sheets or pre-faced boards, then check thickness, finish code and quantities. Inspect faces and edges; where bonding to a substrate is included, test an agreed sample for adhesion and bubbles before accepting the batch.

**Request a quote:** State whether you need raw HPL laminate or faced boards, with finish code, thickness, substrate, quantities and any pressing service. Biet Al Reef customer service can structure the enquiry without guaranteeing supply or timing.

## 6. Joinery hardware supply

**Use case and supply scope:** Joinery hardware is needed for new cabinetry or when existing hinges and drawer runners cannot safely support a door or drawer. Photograph the existing fitting and its model number if available; measure board thickness, cabinet depth, door or drawer width and opening clearance. Provide a schedule of hinge, runner and handle quantities with required load ratings. The quotation covers only the specified components and accessories; drilling, installation, front alignment and on-site testing require separate agreement.

**Additional survey:** Provide a hardware schedule with door and drawer sizes, hinge types, runner lengths and load requirements.

**Comparing quotations:** Provide a hardware schedule with hinge counts, opening angles, runner lengths, drawer load ratings, handle dimensions and board thickness. Compare complete set prices including mounting plates, screws and written warranty rather than isolated components. Test a compatible sample before ordering the full quantity.

**Acceptance check:** Match hinge, runner and handle models to the hardware schedule, counting plates, screws and other fittings. Test a mounted sample for smooth operation under an appropriate load before accepting the full order, recording shortages and board-thickness incompatibilities.

**Request a quote:** Attach a hardware schedule with model references, quantities, hinge angles, runner lengths and ratings, and board thickness; request a compatibility sample before bulk supply. Customer service can assist without guaranteeing stock.

## 7. Board cutting and preparation

**Use case, scope and exclusions:** This service turns customer-supplied or workshop-procured boards into numbered parts ready for assembly. Send a millimetre cut list specifying each part's length, width, quantity, board grade and thickness, grain direction, edge-banding sides and hole positions. Agree whether the workshop supplies the boards or works on customer-owned sheets, and whether cutting, edging, drilling, labelling and protective packing are included. On-site assembly and fitting, correction of unapproved drawings, and changes after cutting starts are separate unless expressly priced.

**Additional survey:** Provide a numbered millimetre cutting list showing edge banding, drilling and grain direction.

**Comparing quotations:** Compare bids against a numbered millimetre cut list specifying board type, grain direction, edge-banding sides and drill locations. Separate sheet cost from cutting, edging, boring and packing, and agree dimensional tolerances and responsibility for recutting incorrect parts. Require labelled parts matching the assembly drawing.

**Acceptance check:** Receive parts by numbered cut-list item, not only total count. Sample-check length, width, squareness, grain direction, drilling positions and edge banding; segregate incorrect pieces and agree recutting or compensation before assembly.

**Request a quote:** Send a numbered millimetre cut list identifying board type, grain direction, edge treatment, holes, dimensional tolerances, quantities and requested delivery window. Ask for separate cutting, edging and packaging prices. Customer service can assist without guaranteeing a date.
