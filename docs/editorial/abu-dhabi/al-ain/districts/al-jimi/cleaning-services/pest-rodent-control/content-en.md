# Pest and rodent control in Al Jimi

This is preparatory information for company and establishment services, not a listing of an available or licensed provider. Clarify site access and requirements before requesting a quote.

## 1. Cockroach control

**When it is needed:** night sightings around kitchens or drains.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide pest photos, hiding areas and moisture.

**Scope and exclusions:** inspect harbourage, use permitted bait or treatment and agreed sealing; plumbing repair excluded.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check visit count and food or child safety precautions; record treatment points, safe re-entry instructions, follow-up timing and any additional visit charge in writing.

**Acceptance:** Verify treatment locations and monitoring plan. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Ask for inspection of moisture, cracks and appliances and identification of the pest before selecting bait or other suitable treatment; blanket spraying is not a substitute for diagnosis.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Record who removes food residue and addresses drainage issues, and specify re-entry precautions for children and pets based on product instructions.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Obtain a treatment-location record and a follow-up date rather than treating same-day disappearance as proof of eradication.

## 2. Ant control

**When it is needed:** recurrent trails near gaps and sinks.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide trail and entry-point photos.

**Scope and exclusions:** trace trails and apply suitable approved control; demolition excluded.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check bait checks and responsibility for sealing; specify who monitors baits, seals entry gaps and checks the affected trails after treatment.

**Acceptance:** Verify activity along recorded trails. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Distinguish kitchen, garden and wall-cavity activity. Photographs of repeated trails are more useful than a general estimate of numbers.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Compare colony tracing and bait monitoring with treatment of visible trails alone; agree who seals identified gaps.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Monitor the photographed trails over the agreed period and report newly emerging routes.

## 3. Rodent control

**When it is needed:** droppings or gnawing near stores and service ducts.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide gnawing, pipe penetrations and food storage photos.

**Scope and exclusions:** survey entry points and secure monitoring stations; major building works excluded.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check station map, checks and safe disposal; document secured station locations, inspection frequency, carcass disposal and unresolved access gaps.

**Acceptance:** Verify station log and unresolved openings. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Photograph droppings and gnaw marks without handling them, and disclose nearby children, pets and exposed food.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Ask for a map of secured monitoring stations, an exclusion plan and safe disposal arrangements. Baits must not be accessible to untrained occupants.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Review inspection logs and closure of entry points; fewer sightings do not replace sanitation and exclusion.

## 4. Bedbug control

**When it is needed:** signs along mattress seams and bed frames.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide specimen and sleeping-area photos.

**Scope and exclusions:** inspect harbourage and plan suitable treatments and follow-up; furniture disposal excluded unless agreed.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check laundry preparation and repeat visits; agree laundry and bedding preparation, repeat inspections and how persistent signs will be reported.

**Acceptance:** Verify reinspection of original hotspots. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Where possible, photograph specimens, mattress seams and bed frames; avoid moving affected furniture between rooms before specialist advice.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Compare laundry preparation, treatment of sensitive belongings and planned follow-up visits. A single spray does not guarantee eradication.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Reinspect original hiding areas at the agreed follow-up and document fresh signs without promising immediate success.

## 5. Recurring pest-control contracts

**When it is needed:** repeated pest activity in occupied buildings.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide floor plan, incident log and sensitive zones.

**Scope and exclusions:** scheduled inspection, monitoring, reports and escalation; structural cleaning excluded.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check emergency callouts, response targets and permitted products; specify routine inspection frequency, incident escalation triggers, reporting format and building-management duties.

**Acceptance:** Verify trend reports and unresolved recommendations. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Provide a floor plan highlighting kitchens, stores, plant rooms, public areas and previous incidents.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Compare scheduled and emergency visits, documentation and escalation thresholds; separate housekeeping and building repairs from pest treatment.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Review monitoring trends, station records and outstanding corrective actions at every service cycle.
