# Tank and pool cleaning in Al Jimi

This is preparatory information for company and establishment services, not a listing of an available or licensed provider. Clarify site access and requirements before requesting a quote.

## 1. Water tank cleaning

**When it is needed:** sediment and internal tank condition.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide access hatch, capacity, drainage and access photos.

**Scope and exclusions:** safe isolation, sediment removal, surface cleaning and rinsing; leak repair and pump replacement excluded.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check safe access, wastewater handling and water outage; confirm duration, responsibilities, materials, equipment, access restrictions and extras in writing.

**Acceptance:** Verify visible sediment, cover and valve integrity and dated evidence. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Agree on isolating the water supply and a wastewater disposal route that will not damage the property. Tank entry requires a confined-space risk assessment, suitable equipment and supervision; external cleaning methods may be preferable where practicable.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Clarify whether hatch, rim and exposed fitting cleaning are included, and how clean surfaces and outlets will be protected from recontamination.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Inspect visible sediment at the floor and corners, secure closure of openings, and a separate defect list for any repairs.

## 2. Water tank disinfection

**When it is needed:** disinfection does not replace prior physical cleaning.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide tank material, capacity, water source and cleaning record.

**Scope and exclusions:** specified suitable product, concentration, contact time and agreed rinsing or tests; laboratory analysis only if contracted.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check product documentation, safe return-to-use and testing scope; confirm duration, responsibilities, materials, equipment, access restrictions and extras in writing.

**Acceptance:** Verify treatment log and agreed test evidence. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** Request documentation showing that the selected disinfectant is suitable for potable-water systems, together with handling, dilution and disposal precautions. Do not assume a universal dose or mix products.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Specify who performs any required tests and the conditions for returning the tank to service. A cleaning certificate is not automatically a water-quality laboratory certificate.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Check the treatment record, timings and contracted test results, with a defined response if return-to-service criteria are not met.

## 3. Swimming pool cleaning

**When it is needed:** algae and deposits along waterline and floor.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide pool length, depth, tile, filter and pump-room photos.

**Scope and exclusions:** skimming, brushing, vacuuming and basket cleaning; structural leak repair and filter replacement excluded.

**Compare quotations and agree terms:** Check water tests, filter service and treatment chemicals; confirm duration, responsibilities, materials, equipment, access restrictions and extras in writing.

**Acceptance:** Verify waterline and floor condition and any agreed readings. Record outstanding issues with dated photos.

**Execution and safety details:** State whether the pool is filled, its finish and the wash-water disposal point. Draining is not necessary for every cleaning job and may require separate assessment.

**Quotation checkpoints:** Separate brushing and vacuuming from filter servicing and water-chemistry adjustment, and identify who supplies chemicals or repairs faulty pumps.

**Additional acceptance checks:** Inspect the waterline and floor, review agreed test readings and confirm the pool is suitable for reopening.
