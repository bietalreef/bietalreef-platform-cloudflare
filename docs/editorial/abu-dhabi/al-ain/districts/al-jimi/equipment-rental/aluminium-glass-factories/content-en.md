# Aluminium and glass factories in Al Jimi

Practical preparation guidance for company and establishment enquiries in Al Jimi, not a listing of available factories. **This category is currently non-selectable; the information is preparatory only.**

## 1. Aluminium profile manufacture

For frames or repeat joinery, send the section drawing, cut lengths, quantities, wall thickness, alloy and finish. Cutting, drilling and fitting are not automatically included.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Compare the specified alloy, temper and wall thickness against the approved section drawing, coating sample, cut waste, packaging and lead time. Approve a sample section before mass production and distinguish stock length from net cut length. Specify dimensional tolerances and transport responsibility; inspect straightness, scratches, wall thickness and quantities.

**Profile quotation brief:** Attach a section drawing and a cut list showing quantities and finishes; ask whether pricing is by weight or length and whether dies, samples, cutting and delivery are included.

## 2. Door and window manufacture

For replacement openings or a new project, photograph each opening and record swing, sill, width, height and reveal depth; attach a unit schedule, glazing and hardware choices.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Compare drainage paths, weather gaskets, glazing specification, hinges, locks, finishes and warranty. Request a frame corner sample and glazing retention detail rather than relying on marketing descriptions. State whether lifting, installation, sealant and old-frame removal are included; test operation, closure and drainage.

**Door and window quotation brief:** Send a numbered opening schedule, interior and exterior photographs, swing direction and sill levels; request separate prices for frames, glass, hardware, fitting and removal.

## 3. Tempered glass manufacture

For doors or partitions needing heat-treated glass, provide final pane dimensions, thickness, cut-outs, hole positions and edge finish before tempering; later machining is generally unsuitable.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Request glass type, thickness, applicable performance specification and marking, and approve all holes and sizes before the tempering cycle. Compare edge work, packing, transport and breakage terms; inspect holes, dimensions and visible edge damage on receipt.

**Tempered glass quotation brief:** Supply a final pane-by-pane drawing with edge treatments, holes and corner clearances; ask about drawing approval lead time and replacement terms for breakage before handover.

## 4. Facade glazing preparation

For multi-panel facades, provide grid elevations, pane IDs, dimensions, site access and lifting plan. Distinguish fabrication-only orders from supply-and-install packages.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Compare compatibility with the approved facade system, wind and movement requirements, safety, fixings and sequence as determined by the responsible designer; do not substitute glass or anchors without design approval. Allocate lifting, storage and testing responsibilities; verify pane labels, edge protection and required product records.

**Facade quotation brief:** Provide numbered elevations, approved system details, pane sizes and lifting/access restrictions; separate fabrication, transport, lifting, installation and testing and identify design responsibility.

## 5. Partition fabrication

For an office or reception partition, provide layout, ceiling height, door positions, power and air-conditioning interfaces, and visual or acoustic privacy needs.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Compare fixings, panel or glass thickness, seals and hardware; identify safety glazing requirements at doors and impact-prone areas. Agree floor and ceiling making-good exclusions; inspect alignment, stability and door operation.

**Partition quotation brief:** Include room layouts, door positions, ceiling heights, floor finishes and privacy requirements; request frame and glazing samples and clarify making-good around services.

## 6. Glass supply

For workshop or project supply, send a pane schedule with sizes, thickness, glass type, tint, edge finish, unloading point and truck-access limits.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Compare pane cost alongside cutting, racks, loading, delivery and breakage liability. Agree who provides safe unloading equipment and when damage liability transfers. Require pane labels; count units and inspect corners and surfaces before signing the delivery note.

**Glass supply quotation brief:** Send a pane schedule, types, thicknesses, unloading location and access photographs; confirm racks, loading, unloading, breakage liability and shortage documentation.

## 7. Project order fulfilment

For staged projects, submit numbered quantities, approved drawings, floor priorities, storage zones and receiving contacts.

**Quotation, agreement and acceptance:** Compare batch identification linked to drawing revisions, rejection replacement and sequencing. Agree variation, delay and storage terms; reconcile each shipment with its packing list, drawings and snag log.

## Requesting a quotation

Share photos, dimensions, drawings, quantities, location and target dates with Biet Al Reef customer service to prepare a clear enquiry. No factory availability, price or date is guaranteed, and this guidance does not activate the category.

**Project order quotation brief:** Provide a phased, floor-by-floor quantity schedule with drawing revision IDs; request a batch plan, production sample approvals, variation control, replacement and storage arrangements.
