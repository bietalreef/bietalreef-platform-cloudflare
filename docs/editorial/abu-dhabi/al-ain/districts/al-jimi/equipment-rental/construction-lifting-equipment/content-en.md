# Construction and lifting equipment rental in Al Jimi

Guidance for company and establishment quotation enquiries, not a claim that equipment or suppliers are available. Site conditions, loads, permits and safety checks vary; do not operate lifting, excavation or cutting equipment before applicable safety requirements are met.

## 1. Scaffolding rental

For facade finishing or elevated maintenance, a generic request for scaffolding is insufficient. Photograph the facade, access points and obstructions; give platform height, working length, intended load and crew size. Ask for a suitable system, an erection plan where needed and inspection by a competent person before use.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Clarify whether transport, erection, dismantling, ladders, guardrails, base plates and periodic inspections are included; allocate permit and exclusion-zone responsibilities. Finishing labour is not automatically included. Compare identical platform sizes and hire periods; at handover check ties, level, inspection records and component inventory.

## 2. Crane rental

For lifting an AC unit, steel or materials to an upper floor, provide load weight and dimensions, pick-up and landing points and lift radius—not just height. Share road, overhead-line, ground and outrigger-position photos; request suitability assessment and a competent lift plan.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Separate operator, banksman, rigging, mats, permits, road closure, standby and overtime costs. Installation of the lifted item is excluded unless agreed. Verify crane identity, inspection records, lifting accessories and the secured lifting zone against the agreed plan.

## 3. Excavation equipment rental

For trenches, foundations or earth removal, specify excavation depth, trench width, soil conditions, swing clearance and spoil-loading location. Photograph and measure the entrance; identify buried utilities before selecting the machine and bucket.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Compare machine class, operating hours, fuel, operator, attachments, haulage and idle-time terms. Shoring, dewatering and spoil disposal are not automatically included. Check tracks, leaks, alarms and bucket, and record hour-meter reading and site condition.

## 4. Compaction equipment rental

After backfilling or preparing a paving sub-base, choose a rammer, plate or roller according to soil type, lift thickness, passage width and nearby walls or utilities. Provide area dimensions, fill type and any compaction-test requirement; heavy rollers may be unsuitable in narrow spaces.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Specify water, fuel, transport, operator and pass requirements; density testing and fill supply are separate unless included. Compare compaction capability and hire period, not just machine names. Inspect vibration operation, guards and plate or drum condition; renting equipment alone does not guarantee soil-test results.

## 5. Concrete mixer rental

For small concrete or mortar batches where ready-mix access is impractical, a site mixer may help. State batch capacity, daily output, power or fuel source, water supply, standing space and a controlled washout area away from drains.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Ask about usable drum capacity, delivery, loading accessories, cleaning, fuel and breakdown response. Cement, aggregates, labour and mix-design control are excluded unless contracted. Test drum rotation, stop switch and guards; document hardened residue at delivery.

## 6. Concrete-cutting equipment rental

For an opening or slab cut, state cut length and depth, member thickness and possible reinforcement, cables or pipes. Provide drawings, photos, noise restrictions and slurry or dust-control arrangements. Structural assessment and applicable approvals may be needed before cutting load-bearing elements.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Distinguish bare saw rental from operated cutting; agree blade type and wear charges, water, power, slurry collection and breakdown handling. Structural approval and block removal are not implicit. Inspect blade guard, connections, cooling and switches and record blade condition.

## 7. General project equipment rental

For projects needing several machines in phases, prepare an equipment schedule tied to tasks, expected use and on/off-hire dates rather than an open-ended list. Share site traffic plans, operating hours, storage points and load or height limits for each phase.

**Quotation, exclusions and acceptance:** Request itemised pricing for each machine, swaps, haulage, fuel, operator, insurance, downtime and early return, with a breakdown replacement procedure. Work performed with the machine is separate unless specified. Use numbered inventories, photos and meter readings at delivery and signed off-hire records at return.
