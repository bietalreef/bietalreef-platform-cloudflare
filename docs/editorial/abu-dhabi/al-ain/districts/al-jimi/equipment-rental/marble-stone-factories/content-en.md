# Marble and stone factories in Al Jimi

Preparatory guidance for establishment enquiries, not evidence of available factories. **This category is currently non-selectable.**

## 1. Marble and stone cutting

Send cutting drawings, dimensions, thickness, vein direction and slab photos with cut-out positions.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare cutting method, waste, edge finish, labelling and transport; check dimensions, vein alignment and chipped corners.

**Quotation detail:** State whether slabs are client-supplied, the number of repeated cuts and dimensional tolerances. Request a cutting schedule with acceptable wastage and responsibility for remaking rejected pieces.

**Scope boundary and handover:** Slab cutting does not include repairing inherent stone defects or on-site installation unless expressly quoted. Photograph and identify each slab before cutting, then reconcile piece labels against the cutting schedule and check individual edge dimensions at delivery.

## 2. Kitchen worktop fabrication

Provide an accurate cabinet template, sink and hob positions, uneven walls, stone type, thickness and edge profile.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare cut-outs, seams, supports, sink sealing and installation scope; check level and opening alignment.

**Quotation detail:** Separate fabrication-only from supply-and-install offers, identify who verifies final templates after cabinets are fitted, and allocate sink removal and reconnection. Confirm appliance models before approving cut-outs.

**Additional acceptance check:** For worktops with sink or hob cut-outs, inspect internal edges and corners for hairline cracking or weak points. Do not load the surface or fit appliances until the specified supports and bonding system have been completed and checked.

**Scope boundary and handover:** Cabinet alteration, plumbing and electrical work are not automatically included in worktop fabrication. Agree seam locations in relation to sinks and hobs; after installation, check wet-area falls and whether water collects along the edges.

## 3. Stair stone fabrication

Measure each tread and riser, landings and existing substrate; specify nosing and slip-resistance requirements.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare finish, slip performance, colour consistency and special cuts; allocate installation and substrate correction, then inspect tread and riser consistency.

**Quotation detail:** Request a separate schedule for each flight and landing, with vein orientation and edge finish. Confirm site-specific slip requirements and have the supporting substrate assessed before approving stone thickness.

**Scope boundary and handover:** Fabricating stone steps is separate from structural stair repair or handrail installation. Record variations between existing steps, agree fixing and edge protection during transport, and approve a finish sample under site lighting before production.

## 4. Facade marble preparation

Provide elevations, cladding sizes, specified anchoring, lifting and storage access; do not assume every stone is suitable outdoors.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare suitability records, anchors, joints and transport protection; inspect thickness, fixing holes and pane IDs.

**Quotation detail:** Ask for approved shop drawings identifying fixing systems, joints and competent structural review. Stone preparation does not automatically include facade engineering or statutory approvals.

**Scope boundary and handover:** Pre-drilled fixing holes do not establish that a facade fixing system is safe; load and anchor design require competent approval. Match panel numbers to drawings, obtain edge and hole photographs before dispatch, and check for damage around fixings on unloading.

## 5. Decorative stone carving

Send a scaled motif, reference image, carving depth, stone type and display position.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare machine or hand carving, detail level and rear/edge finish; approve a sample and inspect symmetry and fine edges.

**Quotation detail:** Request a representative sample showing corners and carving depth, and agree variation charges after approval. Specify whether edge treatment, packaging and installation are included.

**Scope boundary and handover:** Decorative carving does not automatically include fixing heavy pieces to walls or remedying weak stone. Define viewing direction, lighting and colour sample; compare carving depth with the approved sample and inspect fine edges after removing fabrication dust.

## 6. Marble polishing and treatment

Photograph scratches, stains and dull areas; identify stone and floor area, and test a small patch first.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare grinding stages, polishing compounds and furniture protection; do not promise deep-stain removal without a trial. Inspect sheen consistency and slip risk.

**Quotation detail:** Record stain type and previously used cleaners. Compare local treatment with full resurfacing, including downtime and protection of adjacent joints.

**Scope boundary and handover:** Polishing may not resolve cracks, crumbling or moisture rising through the stone. Identify proposed cleaners and sealers, test in an inconspicuous area, and inspect the finish after drying rather than only while wet.

## 7. Contractor order preparation

Send a floor-by-floor numbered bill of quantities, approved cutting drawings, batch schedule and unloading locations.

**Quote comparison, contract scope and acceptance:** Compare part tracking, packing, change logs and breakage replacement; reconcile batches with packing lists before acceptance.

**Quotation detail:** Request a delivery register linking every piece to drawing, floor and room, with responsibility for lifting, site storage and rejection of damaged or nonconforming pieces.

## Quotation request

Prepare photos, measurements, drawings, quantities and delivery location. Biet Al Reef customer service can help structure an enquiry without guaranteeing a supplier, price or date.
