# Furniture and belongings moving in Al Jimi

These notes help a customer prepare a company enquiry for Al Jimi, not identify available vendors. Transport, permits, insurance and checks depend on the actual load or equipment. Agree the specifications and responsibilities before accepting a quote.

## 1. Home furniture moving

Label boxes by room and count kitchen goods and mattresses. Before requesting prices, supply photo inventory, bulky item sizes, pickup/delivery addresses and loading routes; state whether machines, workforce or goods fit the site and when delivery or return is needed.

**Quote and scope:** Specify trucks, labour, packing, dismantling, assembly, floors, lift access, protection, insurance and damage procedure. Agree damage, downtime, operating windows, setup and cleanup responsibilities. Ask for applicable suitability or compliance records rather than assuming credentials or permits are in place.

**Acceptance:** Check box inventory, fragile corners and furniture operation after reassembly. Retain photos, part inventories and operational or delivery logs and record differences before signing off.
