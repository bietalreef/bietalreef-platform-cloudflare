# Furniture and belongings moving in Al Jimi

Practical preparation for company enquiries, without asserting that a mover, rate or appointment is available. Biet Al Reef customer support can help prepare a quotation request; availability must be confirmed.

## 1. Home furniture moving

**Need and scope:** Inventory large furniture, appliances and boxes room by room, identifying items staying behind. Only services expressly listed in the quotation are included.

**Photos and dimensions:** Photograph entrances, stairs, lifts and furniture condition; measure doorways and large cabinets.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare crew size, vehicle capacity, trips, protection and dismantling; electrical and plumbing disconnections are separate. Record responsibilities, damage handling, payment and indicative arrival arrangements in writing.

**Acceptance:** Reconcile the inventory and inspect finishes, glass and appliances before signing.

**Request a quote:** Supply inventory, photos, access details and a preferred date subject to confirmation; ask for an itemised proposal.

## 2. Office furniture moving

**Acceptance:** Check desk IDs, placement, drawers and locks, logging any missing items.
