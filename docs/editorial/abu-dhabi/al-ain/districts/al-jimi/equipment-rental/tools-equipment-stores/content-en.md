## 1. Hand tools sales

Wrench sizes and grip materials should fit the task, not only a toolbox appearance. Before requesting prices, supply task, material, existing machine photo or part number; state whether machines, workforce or goods fit the site and when delivery or return is needed.

**Quote and scope:** Require exact model, rating, power source, warranty, consumables, safety features and returns policy. Agree damage, downtime, operating windows, setup and cleanup responsibilities. Ask for applicable suitability or compliance records rather than assuming credentials or permits are in place.
