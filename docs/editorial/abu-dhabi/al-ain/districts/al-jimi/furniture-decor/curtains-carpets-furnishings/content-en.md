# Curtains, carpets and soft furnishings in Al Jimi

For Al Jimi premises, take real measurements before choosing or commissioning furniture; “medium size” is not a specification. Confirm finish, stability, cleaning, transport and assembly responsibilities before comparing costs.

> **Category status:** proposed, not active; this preparatory content does not activate a category or prove a provider is available.

## 1. Curtain tailoring

Curtain tailoring. Send window, wall and floor photographs, width/drop dimensions and track locations and clarify whether the request is new fabrication, refurbishment or ready-made supply; floor levels and access dimensions may affect fit.

**Exclusions and agreement:** Specify material, lining, stitch style, tracks, cutting, installation, delivery and shade tolerances. Compare approved materials, item counts, delivery, defects policy and the written treatment of wall repairs or removal of old goods if required.

**Handover:** Inspect even pleats, finished edges, free movement and no door obstruction. Try seating, door/drawer operation or fabric movement as relevant, and photograph shortages or scratches before signing.
