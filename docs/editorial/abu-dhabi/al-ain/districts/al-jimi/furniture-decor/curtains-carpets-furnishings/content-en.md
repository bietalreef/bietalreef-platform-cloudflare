# Curtains, carpets and soft furnishings in Al Jimi

This guide helps residents and premises in Al Jimi prepare a clear enquiry for curtains, carpets and furnishings. Actual measurements and material samples matter more than generic size descriptions.

> **Category status:** Newly proposed under company and establishment specialties. This is preparatory information only; it does not activate the category or establish provider availability.

## 1. Curtain tailoring

**When and what is included:** Made-to-measure curtains suit unusually wide or tall windows and rooms requiring specific privacy or light control. Fabric cutting, hemming and pleating should follow an approved finished drop and fullness ratio; installation is not automatically included.

**Photos and specifications:** Photograph the entire window, ceiling, floor, recess and any air-conditioning or furniture obstruction. Record track width, mounting height, finished drop, panel count, fabric direction and a sample or reference for the intended colour.

**Compare and agree:** Ask for the fabric composition, lining and blackout specification, pleat style, allowance for shrinkage, stitching, sample approval before cutting, delivery and fitting responsibilities. Check whether track hardware and wall repair are excluded.

**Acceptance:** Inspect matching lengths, straight hems, regular pleats, panel overlap and appearance in daylight and when drawn.

**Quotation request:** Send dimensions, photos, quantities and Al Jimi delivery location to Biet Al Reef customer service for a possible quotation enquiry. No company, price or date is guaranteed.

## 2. Curtain installation

**When and what is included:** Fitting ready-made curtains or replacing tracks requires sound fixing points and care around concealed electrical or plumbing services. The service may involve brackets, rails, hooks and alignment, not fabric tailoring.

**Photos and specifications:** Supply close-ups of the wall or ceiling substrate, track type, window width, curtain weight and nearby power outlets, doors or air-conditioning units. Note ladder access and any unusually high mounting point.

**Compare and agree:** Specify whether rails, anchors, brackets, drilling, safe access equipment, removal of old fittings and filling old holes are included. Ask how the installer checks the substrate before fixing; hidden service detection and making-good must be agreed explicitly.

**Acceptance:** Open and close every panel end-to-end, test the stops and supports, and confirm that rails remain secure and curtains clear doors and appliances.

**Quotation request:** Send photographs, quantities and access details to Biet Al Reef customer service for an enquiry; availability and timing remain unconfirmed.

## 3. Upholstery fabric supply

**When and what is included:** Fabric for sofas, chairs or cushions should be chosen for expected wear, cleaning needs and colour stability rather than appearance alone. Fabric supply does not automatically include cutting patterns or upholstery labour.

**Photos and specifications:** Photograph each furniture item, count cushions and seats, note approximate dimensions and required running metres, fabric width, pattern repeat and desired sample. Ask an upholsterer to confirm the final cutting allowance.

**Compare and agree:** Compare fibre composition, abrasion or care information where documented, roll width, dye-lot consistency, pattern wastage, lead time and returns policy for cut fabric. Agree whether swatches, transport or sewing are included.

**Acceptance:** Check delivered length, colour and batch identification against the sample; inspect the weave for defects before any cutting.

**Quotation request:** Share the item list, measurements and sample requirements with Biet Al Reef customer service. A supplier, price and delivery date cannot be promised.

## 4. Rugs and fitted carpet supply

**When and what is included:** Fitted carpet needs a room-by-room take-off and seam layout; loose rugs should be selected against furniture clearances and circulation. Supply, underlay and installation are distinct cost items.

**Photos and specifications:** Provide a room sketch with lengths and widths in metres, doorway positions, corners, floor condition and intended use. State whether the request is for cut carpet, carpet tiles or separate rugs.

**Compare and agree:** Compare pile type, backing, maintenance requirements, stain resistance where documented, underlay, adhesive, edge binding, seams, fitting, removal of old flooring and disposal. Require a clear waste allowance and written quantities.

**Acceptance:** Verify pile direction, flat seams, secure edges, unobstructed doors and that the delivered design and dimensions match the approved sample.

**Quotation request:** Send the sketch, photos and requested area to Biet Al Reef customer service for a possible quotation enquiry without any guarantee of provider availability or price.

## 5. Mattress supply

**When and what is included:** Choosing a mattress requires matching the bed base, preferred support and ventilation conditions; marketing labels alone do not establish suitability. Delivery and removal of an old mattress are separate unless stated.

**Photos and specifications:** Measure the bed base length and width, allowable mattress height, and photograph the frame, lift, staircase and access route. State quantity, firmness preference and any manufacturer specifications needed.

**Compare and agree:** Ask for layer construction, support type, cover and care instructions, documented warranty, trial or return conditions, packaging, delivery to the room and old-mattress collection. Compare like-for-like sizes and stated models.

**Acceptance:** Check the size label, model, packaging integrity, surface condition and fit on the bed frame. Record visible defects before accepting delivery.

**Quotation request:** Send the bed dimensions, access photos and model requirements to Biet Al Reef customer service. This proposed category is not automatically active, and no supplier, price or date is guaranteed.
