# Furniture and furnishing stores in Al Jimi

For Al Jimi premises, take real measurements before choosing or commissioning furniture; “medium size” is not a specification. Confirm finish, stability, cleaning, transport and assembly responsibilities before comparing costs.

> **Category status:** non-selectable category; this preparatory content does not activate a category or prove a provider is available.

## 1. Home furniture retail

Home furniture retail. Send room/door measurements, site photos and required item count and clarify whether the request is new fabrication, refurbishment or ready-made supply; floor levels and access dimensions may affect fit.

**Exclusions and agreement:** Ask for product model, exact dimensions, material, alternatives, returns policy, installation warranty and delivery/lifting charges. Compare approved materials, item counts, delivery, defects policy and the written treatment of wall repairs or removal of old goods if required.

**Handover:** Inspect packaging, correct model, complete fittings and no transport damage. Try seating, door/drawer operation or fabric movement as relevant, and photograph shortages or scratches before signing.
