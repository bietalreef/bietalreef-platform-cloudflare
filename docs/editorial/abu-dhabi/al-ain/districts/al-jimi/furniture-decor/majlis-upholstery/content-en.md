# Majlis and sofa tailoring in Al Jimi

For Al Jimi premises, take real measurements before choosing or commissioning furniture; “medium size” is not a specification. Confirm finish, stability, cleaning, transport and assembly responsibilities before comparing costs.

> **Category status:** proposed, not active; this preparatory content does not activate a category or prove a provider is available.

## 1. Arabic majlis tailoring

Arabic majlis fabrication. Send all-side photos, seat lengths, cushion count and fabric information and clarify whether the request is new fabrication, refurbishment or ready-made supply; floor levels and access dimensions may affect fit.

**Exclusions and agreement:** Specify frame, padding, foam density, fabric, stitching, dismantling, packing, transport and exclusions. Compare approved materials, item counts, delivery, defects policy and the written treatment of wall repairs or removal of old goods if required.

**Handover:** Inspect even padding, seams, seating support and fabric tension in normal use. Try seating, door/drawer operation or fabric movement as relevant, and photograph shortages or scratches before signing.
