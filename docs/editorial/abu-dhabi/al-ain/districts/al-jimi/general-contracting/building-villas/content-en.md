# Building and villa construction in Al Jimi — company service guide

A useful enquiry starts with the actual site rather than a vague request for a contractor. Existing properties and new projects in Al Jimi can need different scopes; use photos, drawings and quantities rather than assumptions. This guide is for preparing requests, not an offer by any named company.

## 1. Villa construction

For **villa construction**, map the intended work and explain the existing condition before discussing a quote. Attach approved drawings if available, access photos and measurable quantities. Make the quantities and limitations explicit.

**Quotation and agreement:** Separate design approval, structure, finishing, utilities and professional fees, including explicit exclusions. Compare bills of quantities with approved drawings and samples, tying payments to inspected milestones. Include the programme, protection duties and inspection milestones.

**Acceptance:** Review design loads, dimensions and phase-specific checks with the project consultant, then insist on written inspection hold points. Confirm the agreed result on site and record photographs, tests and any snag items before final payment.
