# Irrigation networks in Al Jimi — company service guide

This category is **currently non-selectable**. Information is preparatory and does not establish supplier availability. Verify buried services and safety requirements before excavation or connections. Where irrigation uses non-potable water or shares a supply connection, ask a qualified specialist to specify suitable backflow protection under the relevant authority requirements; never assume a direct connection to potable water is acceptable.

## 1. Irrigation network design

**Purpose and scope:** Divide trees and lawn into distinct zones and size flow and pressure rather than drawing pipe routes by appearance.

**Photos and measurements:** Supply planting plan, water source, available pressure and flow readings, levels and buried services.

**Pre-work decision:** Verify the source of flow and pressure measurements under representative operation and separate planting with different water demands; do not finalise pipe sizes before checking losses and available pressure.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare hydraulic calculations, pipe diameters, valves, filters and maintenance access; excavation and installation are separate unless included.

**Handover:** Check coverage of remote zones, valve access and independent isolation. Match final zone drawings to the flow schedule and available pressure; confirm isolation valves, backflow protection and zone identifiers are documented before accepting the design.

**Detailed contract terms:** Approve a numbered zone plan showing each zone's area and plant type, design flow, pressure at the furthest outlet, and valve, filter, flushing and isolation locations. Agree how source pressure and flow will be measured, the limits of those readings and who revises the design if actual supply differs. Do not authorise excavation until buried utilities and valve locations have been checked.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide each zone's area, planting types, water-source position and any measured pressure and flow. Request separate prices for zone drawings, hydraulic calculations and equipment schedules versus excavation and installation; agree on revision rounds and editable drawing handover.

## 2. Drip irrigation installation

**Purpose and scope:** Map plant spacing and root depth so emitters wet roots without soaking trunks or leaving bed edges dry.

**Photos and measurements:** Photograph beds; measure row lengths, plant counts and locate supply, filter and regulator.

**Pre-work decision:** Check water quality, filtration, flushing and emitter placement relative to roots; trial one representative zone before burying pipework.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare emitter flow rates, filtration, line fixing and flushing method; main supply repairs are separate.

**Handover:** Run the zone and check end emitters, leaks and wetting near each plant. Flush the line end under competent instructions, check soil wetting at the first and last emitter, and record blocked outlets before burying lines.

**Detailed contract terms:** Specify pipe depth, emitters and paving reinstatement. Agree on emitter-clogging prevention, filter cleaning and who replaces damaged components during the handover period.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Send planting-row lengths, tree counts, spacing and supply photos. Ask for emitter quantity and flow, filter and regulator positions, flushing, uniformity testing and whether disturbed soil will be reinstated.

## 3. Sprinkler installation

**Purpose and scope:** Plan overlapping coverage with wind exposure and avoid spraying walls or pedestrian routes.

**Photos and measurements:** Measure lawn widths, paving edges, obstacles and operating pressure; photograph boundaries.

**Pre-work decision:** Check sprinkler overlap and direct spray away from walls, paths and pedestrians; test coverage under representative wind conditions before acceptance.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare nozzle range, heads, zones and control valves; paving reinstatement must be specified separately.

**Handover:** Observe a full watering cycle for dry strips, overspray and ponding. Test coverage along paving edges under representative wind conditions and log overspray and any settlement around sprinkler heads.

**Detailed contract terms:** Test sprinkler coverage and avoid wetting facades. Agree on nozzle and head adjustments after a site trial and re-levelling any heads that settle after backfilling.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide lawn area, obstacles, paths and photographs of spray boundaries. Ask for head counts, nozzle specifications, overlap and actual coverage tests, plus responsibility for damaged paving or planting.

## 4. Irrigation tanks and pumps

**Purpose and scope:** Estimate storage demand, flow and elevation before selecting a pump or tank.

**Photos and measurements:** Provide tank-space dimensions, electrical supply, suction/discharge route and highest outlet elevation.

**Pre-work decision:** Review tank capacity, dry-run protection, isolation, drainage, ventilation and safe access, with electrical inspection by a qualified person.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare pump curve, tank, valves, dry-run protection and mounting base; electrical and civil works require explicit scope.

**Handover:** Have a qualified technician test start-stop control, dry-run protection, leakage and unusual vibration. Do not energise the pump before appropriate earthing and electrical protection have been checked, and isolate power safely for inspection; record operating results and pump details. Record operating pressure and flow with a representative zone running; check access to isolation valves, filter servicing and pump dry-run protection.

**Detailed contract terms:** Price pump, tank and electrical work separately; test pressure. Require connection and isolation-point diagrams, dry-run protection details, and explicit responsibility for later tank cleaning and pump maintenance.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Share tank-space dimensions, electrical supply location, furthest sprinkler elevation and required flow. Ask for tank capacity, pump curve, protection, connections, foundation and commissioning as separate priced items, with electrical/civil exclusions and warranty recorded.

## 5. Irrigation network maintenance

**Purpose and scope:** Diagnose low pressure, clogging or stuck valves before recommending wholesale replacement.

**Photos and measurements:** Provide fault locations, zone IDs, recurrence and any pressure readings.

**Pre-work decision:** Determine whether poor irrigation results from blocked filters, pressure loss or leakage, and isolate the affected zone before replacing sound components.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare diagnosis, parts, excavation, reinstatement and retesting; controllers and pumps are separate unless listed.

**Handover:** Run each affected zone and record post-repair pressure, leaks and reinstated surfaces. Compare pressure readings before and after the repair in the affected zone, and inspect reinstatement and access to every repaired valve or joint.

**Detailed contract terms:** Define replacement parts, repair limits and recommissioning tests. Request a photographic list and locations of replaced components and specify whether controller reprogramming is included.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Photograph the fault, controller and zone identifiers; note when low pressure or blockage occurs. Separate diagnosis from parts, excavation and reinstatement, and require written approval before unforeseen repairs.

## 6. Automatic irrigation programming

**Purpose and scope:** Set schedules for plant types, seasons and water limits, avoiding incompatible simultaneous zones.

**Photos and measurements:** Photograph controller model and display; list zone names, current schedules and plant concerns.

**Pre-work decision:** Record existing schedules, permitted watering windows, seasonal settings and shade zones; test manual override and restoration after power interruption.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare programming, sensor checks, saved settings and user handover; new wiring and valves are separate.

**Handover:** Test manual and scheduled cycles, stop functions and any rain-delay feature. Receive a written start time and duration for each zone, and test cancellation using the stop command or rain sensor where fitted. Follow the controller manual to check what happens to the schedule and clock after power interruption and restoration, recording any reset required. Identify who can change schedules and how account access is transferred if the controller is internet-connected. Do not assume seasonal settings suit every plant without reviewing actual water use.

**Detailed contract terms:** Hand over a numbered zone schedule identifying planting types, watering days, start times, run durations and seasonal adjustment limits, with a settings backup and recovery procedure. Where an app-connected controller is present, assign account ownership, credential transfer and removal of the technician's access after handover. Identify responsibility for calibrating installed rain or moisture sensors. Record manual and scheduled-cycle trials and controller behaviour after power loss; wiring, valve repairs and internet provision are separate unless explicitly priced.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Share controller model, display photographs, zone/plant list and current watering times. Request a written seasonal schedule, backed-up settings, recovery instructions and practical user training; specify whether sensor or wiring replacement is excluded.

## 7. Irrigation network leak detection

**Purpose and scope:** Distinguish normal watering from leakage by checking the meter with zones off and testing zones individually.

**Photos and measurements:** Provide damp-spot locations, timing, meter readings and any pipe layout.

**Pre-work decision:** Isolate zones and compare pressure and flow before excavation, documenting buried services; surface dampness alone cannot pinpoint a leak.

**Quotation, contract and exclusions:** Compare tracing method, accuracy limits, exploratory excavation and reporting; detection does not automatically include repair or reinstatement.

**Handover:** Obtain documented suspected locations and test findings; commission and verify repairs separately if needed. Record meter and pressure-test results zone by zone, stating whether the leak location is confirmed or remains a suspected point needing further investigation.

**Detailed contract terms:** Price leak detection separately from excavation and repairs. Agree on the permitted number of trial excavations, protection of existing plants and utilities, and who authorises further investigation if the suspected leak is not confirmed.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide meter readings while irrigation is off, timing of damp patches and any pipe plan. Request the detection method, accuracy limits, trial excavation charges and a written suspected-location report; price repair and reinstatement separately.
