# Shades and outdoor works in Al Jimi — company enquiry guide

This is preparatory information for the companies and establishments taxonomy, not a listing of available suppliers. **The category is currently non-selectable.** Existing homes and new sites require different surveys; quantities, design and permissions depend on site conditions and approved drawings, not the district name alone.

## 1. Car shade installation

**Detailed contract terms:** Specify foundations, anchors and buried-utility checks.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Attach a parking sketch showing vehicle count, entrance levels and proposed posts. Ask for separate prices for underground-service checks, foundations, frame, covering, drainage and paving reinstatement, and identify who handles design and permits.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** Specify the number and size of vehicles, door-opening space and entry clearance. Photograph the parking area, gate, proposed posts and drainage; locate buried services before excavation.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Compare approved drawings, the designed steel or aluminium structure, canopy fabric or panel, corrosion protection, foundations, anchors and runoff. Separate permits, excavation, lifting, paving reinstatement and lighting where excluded. Approve post locations, clearances and foundation drawings before excavation; assign responsibility for buried-service checks and reinstating paving.

**Handover inspection:** Check vehicle clearance, post positions, foundation fixings, runoff and safe edges; compare anchor locations and quantities with the approved design, request foundation photos before concealment and a fixing-inspection report from a qualified specialist against the approved design, and obtain material and finish warranties. Such a report does not replace any required statutory inspection or approval.

**Pre-contract decision:** If parking is next to an existing wall or utility entrance, do not assume the wall can support a canopy. Ask the designer to confirm whether the shade is freestanding or fixed to verified structural elements, and show where roof runoff reaches neighbouring areas and walkways.

## 2. Pergola construction

**Detailed contract terms:** Approve pergola structure and drainage fall; separate lighting.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Send seating dimensions, fixing-point photos and sun orientation. Request distinct open-slat and roofed options specifying member and anchor materials, finish, load verification and excluded electrical works.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** Send seating-area dimensions, sun direction and photos of fixing walls or ground. Clarify open slats versus weatherproof covering, lighting and climbing plants.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Compare spans, sections, fixings, materials, finishes and weather resistance; obtain competent design checks for wind and structural loading where required. Exclude furniture, electrical works and flooring unless itemised. Record pergola spans, anchor locations and required structural checks; approve finish and covering samples, and price lighting connections separately.

**Handover inspection:** Compare clear span, height, foundation and anchor positions with the approved drawing; inspect alignment, secure joints and safe edges. For a rainproof cover, test runoff away from doors and walls and inspect the flashing where it meets the building rather than assuming sealant alone is permanent waterproofing. Request photographs of concealed fixings and any required structural inspection report, and confirm future access for anchor maintenance.

**Pre-contract decision:** For pergolas that may later carry climbing plants or additional panels, ask for the intended design load and permitted future additions. A visual rendering or catalogue photo does not replace site-specific fixing and loading details.

## 3. Outdoor seating construction

**Detailed contract terms:** List included furniture, waterproofing and service connections.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Specify the seating footprint, fixed-seat count and circulation width. Request a bill separating foundations, paving, fixed benches, shade and lighting, and identify excluded furniture and water/power connections.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** State occupancy, intended use, entrances and privacy needs; sketch seating and circulation. Photograph floor conditions and any required power or water points.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Ask for separate line items for groundworks, structure, fixed seating, shade and lighting, with materials suitable for sun and moisture. Do not assume air-conditioning, loose furniture or utility extensions are included. Specify fixed-seat quantities, circulation space and each built element; identify who connects any power or water services.

**Handover inspection:** Test safe circulation, fixed seating, switch locations and cleanability; check that runoff does not discharge onto seating or walkways, and that exits remain unobstructed.

**Seating usability check:** Before approving fixed benches, trace access, serving and cleaning routes on the drawing; specify seat height, depth and exposed edges. For timber or composite decking, request joint, under-deck ventilation and removable access details. An indoor-looking sample is not evidence that a finish is suitable outdoors.

**Pre-contract decision:** If the seating scheme includes a fire feature or outdoor kitchen, separate it from paving and bench works. Have relevant specialists assess ventilation, clearances and service routes; never assume flame-producing equipment is included in the seating contractor's scope.

## 4. External fence installation

**Detailed contract terms:** Document boundaries, posts, gates and permit responsibility.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Attach the approved plot-boundary plan, fence lengths, gate positions and level changes. Ask for separate allowances for boundary verification, permissions, foundations, gates, removal of the old fence and waste disposal.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** Provide an approved plot-boundary drawing where available, measure lengths, heights and gates, and photograph level changes and adjacent services. Never estimate legal boundaries by eye.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Compare fence material, foundations, posts, corrosion protection, gate hinges and coating. Specify who checks boundaries and permissions, removes the old fence and disposes of waste. Tie the fence works to the approved boundary survey, and assign permits, old-fence removal, gate testing and key handover.

**Handover inspection:** Check alignment, post spacing, gate and lock operation and safe edges; verify the approved boundary against the accepted survey rather than relying on visual alignment alone.

**Gate and privacy check:** Record each gate's swing, vehicle or pedestrian clearance and lock position. Compare leaf weight, hinges and bracing with the fabricator's detail. Where ground levels differ, obtain a drawing showing transitions and runoff; do not obstruct drains or maintenance access.

**Pre-contract decision:** For a fence next to a street, neighbour or service corridor, establish the verified site boundary and responsibility for temporary protection before dismantling the old fence. Do not excavate or alter levels before the necessary boundary, utility and approval checks.

## 5. Garden pathway construction

**Detailed contract terms:** Specify sub-base layers, path falls and drainage.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide the pathway route, usable width, threshold levels and photos of roots and irrigation points. Request the sub-base, compaction, edging, falls, root protection, irrigation diversion and final drainage test as distinct items.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** Sketch the walking route from gate to entrances, noting usable width, levels, irrigation and existing trees. Photograph ponding and thresholds.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Compare excavation, sub-base, compaction, edge restraint, surface finish, slip resistance and falls. Clarify tree-root protection and irrigation diversion; exclude planting and lighting unless priced. Approve path levels, falls, sub-base and tree-root protection, with a procedure for irrigation conflicts discovered on site.

**Handover inspection:** Walk the route to check trip points, joints, gradients, drainage and unobstructed doors; record any ponding observed during testing for correction before acceptance.

**Access and levels check:** Distinguish clear walking width from planting and edging widths, and photograph door thresholds before work. Where accessible use is needed, have gradients, widths, joints and slip performance checked against applicable site requirements rather than accepting a vague claim that the path suits everyone.

**Pre-contract decision:** Where the pathway passes near established tree trunks, request a reroute or root-protection detail before approving excavation. The quotation should describe how irrigation and soil levels will be restored, not just the paving appearance.

## 6. Interlock paving

**Detailed contract terms:** Approve interlock sample, joints and settlement remedy.

**Service-specific quotation request:** State the paved area and whether pedestrians or vehicles will use it; photograph settlement and service covers. Request specified paver thickness, base layers, compaction, removal, cover adjustment, edge restraints and a settlement-remedy warranty.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** Measure the area in square metres, state pedestrian or vehicle loading, and photograph soil, previous settlement, service covers and door thresholds.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Compare paver type and thickness, base layers, compaction, bedding, joints, edge restraints and drainage. Specify old-paver removal, waste transport and cover adjustments. Specify paver thickness and base build-up for pedestrian or vehicle loads, including manhole adjustments and post-handover settlement remedies.

**Handover inspection:** Check consistent joints, stable edges, no movement under expected loads and no standing water after an appropriate test; check that service covers finish flush without preventing maintenance access.

**Pavement build-up check:** Do not compare only the rate per square metre. Ask each bidder for base and bedding thicknesses, compaction approach and edge restraints for the intended loading, plus inspection before laying pavers. Photograph service covers before covering work and allocate responsibility for settlement where new trenches meet old paving.

**Pre-contract decision:** Where interlock paving crosses recent fill or a utility trench, request records of backfill layers, compaction and pre-paving inspection. Distinguish sub-base settlement from defective pavers in the warranty and specify the remedy.

## 7. Water features and fountains

**Detailed contract terms:** Separate pumps, waterproofing and lighting; test electrical safety.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Supply basin dimensions, site photos, utility locations and child-access information. Request a water-circulation plan, pump, filtration, waterproofing and electrical safeguards, with separate excavation, commissioning, leak testing and maintenance instructions.

**When needed and evidence for pricing:** Define basin size, depth, location and operating mode. Photograph water, power and drainage points and child-access areas; request a circulation and maintenance layout.

**Scope, exclusions and quotation comparison:** Compare waterproofing and testing, pump, filtration, suitable electrical protection near water, isolation and cleaning access. Itemise water supply, lighting and excavation; qualified specialists must review electrical work. Approve pump, waterproofing, electrical protection and service-access drawings; require documented leak and operating tests before final payment.

**Handover inspection:** Require leak and pump-operation tests and safe shutdown; check noise, overspray, maintenance access and operating instructions and a filter-cleaning and pump-isolation schedule, together with documented electrical-protection testing by a qualified specialist.

**Operation and maintenance check:** Request a drawing showing suction and return lines, cleaning openings, strainers and a way to remove the pump without breaking finishes. Agree on low-water shutdown, filter-cleaning responsibility and measures to restrict unsupervised child access appropriate to the site design.

**Pre-contract decision:** For open-basin fountains, ask the designer to assess slip risks, unsupervised access, suction points and electrical interfaces, and identify who tests and signs off safeguards. A trial run alone is not proof of safety.

## Preparing a quotation request

Send the service name, site location, overview and detail photos, dimensions, intended use and available drawings. Request separate material, labour, permit, transport and warranty items. Biet Al Reef customer service may help structure an enquiry even if no company is listed, **without guaranteeing a supplier, price or date**.
