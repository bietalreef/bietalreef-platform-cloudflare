# Flooring and cladding in Al Jimi

Explain the actual condition and expected outcome before seeking a company quotation for an Al Jimi property. This buyer's guide does not establish a listed business, price or appointment.

## 1. Ceramic tile installation

**When this matters:** Tile size and drain location influence cutting and bathroom floor falls. For the site survey, bring net dimensions, substrate photos, door and drain points, damp condition and a measured description or count of the affected locations.

**Scope and exclusions:** Document substrate, drainage falls, adhesive, joints and skirting, and who removes/disposes of the old finish. Specify the exact material or replacement, samples, warranty and visit timing in writing. Ask who removes debris and makes good adjacent finishes. Compare equivalent deliverables, not headline totals alone.

**At handover:** Check levels, joints, hollow spots and hazardous lippage; then operate or inspect the repaired component under normal use. Photograph defects and record open snags before approving payment.
