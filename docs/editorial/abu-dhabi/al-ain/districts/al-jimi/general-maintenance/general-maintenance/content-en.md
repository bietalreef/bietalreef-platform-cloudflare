# General maintenance in Al Jimi

Explain the actual condition and expected outcome before seeking a company quotation for an Al Jimi property. This buyer's guide does not establish a listed business, price or appointment.

## 1. Routine villa maintenance

**When this matters:** Recurring bathroom leaks, hinges and switches call for a maintenance schedule, not isolated callouts. For the site survey, bring photos of the defect showing its size, location and timing and a measured description or count of the affected locations.

**Scope and exclusions:** Clarify diagnostic visit, replacement parts, sundries, transport and repeat-fault responsibilities. Specify the exact material or replacement, samples, warranty and visit timing in writing. Ask who removes debris and makes good adjacent finishes. Compare equivalent deliverables, not headline totals alone.

**At handover:** Check functioning of the repaired item without recurrent leaks, looseness or damage; then operate or inspect the repaired component under normal use. Photograph defects and record open snags before approving payment.
