# Gypsum and decorative works in Al Jimi — company quotation guide

This guide helps customers define scope, compare quotations and inspect delivery. It does not confirm a listed contractor, approval, price or appointment. Safety and permit matters must be checked with qualified parties and relevant authorities.

## 1. Gypsum ceiling installation

**When it is needed:** A suspended ceiling can conceal services or reshape lighting, but maintenance hatches must remain accessible.

**Site information:** Photograph the whole ceiling, measure clear height and room area, and mark AC grilles, sprinklers and detectors.

**Materials and specification:** Specify board type for the environment, metal framing, hangers, joint treatment and access panels.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Compare board thickness, framing specification, removal, painting and electrical work as separate inclusions. Record the finished ceiling level, suspension and framing system, access-hatch positions and coordination with AC and fire devices. Price demolition, painting and service relocation separately, and inspect framing alignment before boarding.

**Handover check:** Before boarding, request photos of hangers, framing and reinforced fixing points. Heavy luminaires and building services must not be suspended from gypsum board without independently designed supports. After finishing, check levels, wall junctions, access panels and signs of sagging or cracks; detectors, sprinklers and AC outlets must remain functional and serviceable.

**Request a quotation:** Provide existing and proposed ceiling heights, room areas and AC/fire-device photos; request framing and access-hatch details with separate electrical and paint prices.

## 2. Gypsum partition construction

**When it is needed:** Partitions divide rooms but must not be treated as structural walls.

**Site information:** Provide dimensions, door positions, socket locations, planned wall-mounted loads and acoustic requirements.

**Materials and specification:** Specify studs, tracks, board layers, insulation and local reinforcement for doors or cabinetry.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Compare fire/acoustic specifications where required and explicitly exclude structural alterations or service relocation unless priced. Attach a partition layout showing doors, reinforcement and permitted wall-mounted loads. Define board layers, acoustic infill and any required fire performance; price socket relocation and floor or ceiling repairs separately.

**Handover check:** Before closing the second face, record reinforcement, insulation and concealed services in photographs. Check partition alignment, door-frame stability, door operation and socket finishing. Do not accept a claimed fire or acoustic rating without a documented compatible assembly and installation appropriate to the project requirements.

**Request a quotation:** Share room division drawings, door sizes, suspended loads and acoustic expectations; request a partition build-up, reinforcement detail and separately priced service moves.

## 3. Recessed cove lighting construction

**When it is needed:** Indirect cove lighting needs a serviceable driver and a planned light path.

**Site information:** Share perimeter dimensions, recess depth, electrical feed location and nearby AC routes.

**Materials and specification:** Define the gypsum recess profile, straight edges, LED mounting and suitable component ventilation.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Separate gypsum fabrication from strips, drivers, wiring, controls and commissioning; agree light temperature. Agree a cove section, recess dimensions and accessible driver position. Identify who supplies and tests strips, drivers and wiring, approve a working sample before closing the gypsum, and separate lighting warranties from finish defects.

**Handover check:** Test in a darkened room for even light, exposed wiring and visible LED hotspots. Confirm the driver can be reached and replaced without breaking the gypsum. A qualified electrical specialist should inspect and commission connections; drivers and joints must not be buried in inaccessible voids.

**Request a quotation:** Provide room perimeter, intended cove profile and electrical feed location; request separate gypsum and lighting prices plus driver-access and maintenance details.

## 4. Decorative gypsum detailing

**When it is needed:** Cornices and gypsum motifs need consistent proportions and carefully matched corners.

**Site information:** Provide wall elevations, lengths, heights, a sample motif and photos of openings.

**Materials and specification:** Agree moulding profile, fixing method, joint finishing and a sample section.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Compare linear metres, corner pieces, paint, access equipment and removal of old details. Approve a drawing or sample cornice with lengths, corners and special pieces. State who handles uneven substrates, old moulding removal and painting; sign off a trial section before repeating the pattern.

**Handover check:** Compare installed profiles with the approved sample at doorways and corners; inspect repeated spacing, straight lines, sound fixings and visible joints under side lighting. Ensure mouldings do not obstruct doors or service-access covers.

**Request a quotation:** Send a reference motif, wall lengths and corner photographs; ask for linear-metre and special-piece rates and a sample section for approval.

## 5. Wood-look panel installation

**When it is needed:** Wood-look cladding must suit the intended interior or exposed environment.

**Site information:** Measure wall area, switches and door openings; identify panel direction and colour sample.

**Materials and specification:** Specify product grade, thickness, compatible fixings and edge trims; obtain evidence for claimed moisture or fire resistance.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Compare brand, fixing method, joints, corners, waste and whether substrate repairs are included. Identify the panel product, colour code, installation direction, joints and edge trims. Verify fixing compatibility with the substrate and separately allocate existing damp repairs and socket reinstatement.

**Handover check:** Inspect groove alignment, batch-to-batch colour consistency, firm joints and edges, and any swelling or separation from the substrate. Keep outlets and service openings accessible, and do not conceal unresolved damp patches behind panels.

**Request a quotation:** Share wall dimensions, groove direction, colour sample and outlet positions; ask for product data, fixing details, edge trims and waste allowance.

## 6. Marble-look panel installation

**When it is needed:** Marble-look cladding is decorative and does not replace waterproofing.

**Site information:** Measure the wall, photograph wet and heated areas, and agree a vein-matching layout.

**Materials and specification:** Specify panel type, substrate preparation, adhesive or mechanical fixing and corner treatment.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Compare panel quantities, offcuts, joint sealant and substrate repairs; price waterproofing separately if required. Attach a panel cutting and vein layout, joint locations, adhesive specification and substrate preparation. Treat leak repairs and waterproofing as separate prerequisite work rather than implied cladding inclusions.

**Handover check:** Compare the installed vein pattern with the approved cutting layout; inspect flatness, joints, edges and any loose areas. Near wet zones, verify that the moisture source and any required waterproofing were dealt with as separate works; silicone and decorative cladding are not proof of waterproofing.

**Request a quotation:** Provide wall dimensions, vein-layout preference and wet/heat exposure photos; request a panel schedule, adhesive details and separate substrate-preparation costs.

## 7. Gypsum repair and maintenance

**When it is needed:** Recurring stains or cracks require diagnosis before cosmetic patching.

**Site information:** Photograph the defect close up and from a distance; note dimensions, when it appeared and pipes or AC above.

**Materials and specification:** Assess framing and moisture source, replace unsound board, tape joints, finish and touch up paint.

**Quotation comparison and exclusions:** Distinguish leak remediation from cosmetic repairs, and agree patch area, colour matching and disposal. Document the cause of damage, cut-out area and moisture readings before reinstatement. Separate leak rectification from gypsum patching, and agree paint blending limits and a follow-up inspection.

**Handover check:** Request evidence that the leak source was addressed and the substrate was dry before patch closure. Check board stability, joint flatness under side lighting and any paint mismatch after drying. Record a follow-up inspection if recurrent damp or building movement may have caused the defect.

**Request a quotation:** Send dated defect photographs, damaged area and leak indicators; ask for a diagnosis and separate prices for root-cause repair, gypsum reinstatement and painting.
