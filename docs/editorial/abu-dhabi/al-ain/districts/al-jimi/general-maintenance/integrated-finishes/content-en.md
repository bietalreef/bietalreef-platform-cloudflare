# Integrated finishing in Al Jimi

A preparation guide for company quotations, not confirmation of a listed contractor, price or appointment. Regulated work requires appropriately qualified parties and applicable approvals.

## 1. Villa finishing

**When needed:** Majlis, bedrooms and wet rooms need different specifications; begin with a floor-by-floor plan showing completed structure and services.

**Photos and measurements:** Provide room photos, floor areas, ceiling heights, outlet locations and proposed flooring and paint samples.

**Scope and exclusions:** Separate flooring, gypsum, paint, doors, electrical and plumbing; assign material supply and testing, and exclude furniture or appliances unless stated.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare measured quantities, waste allowances, product grades, work sequence and waterproofing cure periods; link payments to inspected milestones.

**Acceptance check:** Check floor levels, joints and wet-area falls, operate lights and water fixtures, and log defects room by room.

## 2. Apartment finishing

**When needed:** Occupied apartments and shared buildings require lift booking, noise control and common-area protection.

**Photos and measurements:** Photograph entrances and retained furniture; record room dimensions, existing tiles and service condition, plus building rules.

**Scope and exclusions:** Specify stripping, debris removal, lift protection, work windows and water shutdowns; structural changes and main-service alterations need separate assessment.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare protection and disposal costs, material specifications, duration and kitchen or bathroom downtime rather than lump sums.

**Acceptance check:** Inspect common-area damage, floor edges, sealant, sanitary operation and final cleaning.

## 3. Retail shop finishing

**When needed:** A retail unit needs coordinated frontage, customer circulation, display fixtures and lighting before surface finishes.

**Photos and measurements:** Provide a layout, storefront dimensions, power and data points, ceiling and floor photographs and landlord requirements.

**Scope and exclusions:** Separate partitions, flooring, counter, lighting and signage; permits, life-safety systems and external signage are not automatically included.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare display loads, floor wear ratings, fixture specifications, sample approval stages and frontage lead times.

**Acceptance check:** Walk the customer route, test door clearance and power points, assess merchandise lighting and display stability.

## 4. Office finishing

**When needed:** Office layouts depend on workstations, meeting rooms, acoustic privacy and network routes.

**Photos and measurements:** Provide furniture layouts, headcount, data outlet locations, ceiling heights and HVAC or sprinkler constraints.

**Scope and exclusions:** Separate glazed and gypsum partitions, flooring, service penetrations and data cabling; active networking and fire-system changes are not assumed.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare partition acoustic performance, floor durability, service access and downtime; agree a coordinated trade drawing.

**Acceptance check:** Check door swings, task lighting, data outlets, access panels and finishes around services.

## 5. Interior refurbishment

**When needed:** Before replacing damaged finishes, establish whether moisture, substrate movement or ordinary wear caused failure.

**Photos and measurements:** Photograph damage and its history, measure affected areas, inspect substrate condition and list items to retain.

**Scope and exclusions:** Specify investigation, removal, treatment, reinstatement and waste disposal; concealed leaks or structural faults require separate scope.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare targeted repair against full refurbishment, disturbance to adjacent work and matching of old and new materials.

**Acceptance check:** Inspect the dry repaired substrate, colour, levels and edges; document the cause before concealing it.

## 6. Decorative fit-out works

**When needed:** Decorative work may combine wall cladding, light coves and fixed joinery; clarify boundaries between finisher, carpenter and electrician.

**Photos and measurements:** Supply wall photographs, elevations, socket and AC locations, finish samples and detailed drawings.

**Scope and exclusions:** Specify fixings, panel edges, joints, lighting and painting; distinguish movable furniture and appliances from fixed work.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare corner mock-ups, joint treatment, moisture suitability and access to lighting drivers.

**Acceptance check:** Inspect line alignment, colour consistency, secure fixing and maintenance access without breaking the feature.

## 7. Integrated finishing handover

**When needed:** Handover is more than cleaning: each item must be evidenced and outstanding defects closed before acceptance.

**Photos and measurements:** Collect as-built drawings, agreed quantities, concealed-work photos, product invoices, warranties and a snag list.

**Scope and exclusions:** Name the party responsible for electrical, water and waterproofing tests, keys and manuals; statutory certificates are not assumed.

**Comparing quotes and contract:** Compare document schedules, defect rectification periods and final payment retention; list exclusions expressly.

**Acceptance check:** Walk each room, operate lights, switches, drains and doors, check falls, and sign a dated outstanding-items record.
