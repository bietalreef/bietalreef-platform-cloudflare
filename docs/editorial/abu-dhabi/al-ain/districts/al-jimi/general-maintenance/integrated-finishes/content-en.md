# Integrated finishing in Al Jimi

Explain the actual condition and expected outcome before seeking a company quotation for an Al Jimi property. This buyer's guide does not establish a listed business, price or appointment.

## 1. Villa fit-out

**When this matters:** Bedrooms, majlis and bathrooms need different floor finishes; specify room-by-room performance. For the site survey, bring room-by-room photos, available layouts and a list of retained items and a measured description or count of the affected locations.

**Scope and exclusions:** Request an itemised bill separating flooring, paint, gypsum, electrical, plumbing, materials and mobilisation, with exclusions. Specify the exact material or replacement, samples, warranty and visit timing in writing. Ask who removes debris and makes good adjacent finishes. Compare equivalent deliverables, not headline totals alone.

**At handover:** Check room-by-room snag closure, functional system checks and waste clearance; then operate or inspect the repaired component under normal use. Photograph defects and record open snags before approving payment.
