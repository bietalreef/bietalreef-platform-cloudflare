# Smart systems and surveillance in Al Jimi — company quotation guide

This is preparation guidance for company quotations, not a listing of available providers or a claim of approval or fixed pricing. Check applicable qualifications and permissions for electrical, surveillance and alarm work.

## 1. Smart lighting control

**When it helps:** rooms needing scheduled lighting scenes at an Al Jimi property. Define the outcome before selecting hardware.

**Photos, measurements and specifications:** Record circuit count, switch photos and dimmable-lamp compatibility. Share location and cable-route photos without exposing passwords or unrelated people's footage.

**Scope, materials and equipment:** Ask the company to itemise compatible load-rated modules and scene setup, including model, quantity, power, connection and making good. **Not automatically included:** replacement of incompatible lamps or old wiring unless separately priced.

**Comparing quotations and agreement:** compare protocol, local control and number of circuits rather than switch price. Agree equipment, labour, programming, tests, warranty, dates and variation approval in writing.

**Acceptance checks:** test dimming, physical switches and offline behaviour. Obtain operating instructions, owner-held administrator credentials and a list of outstanding defects.

**Request a quotation:** Send the intended use, photographs, measurements, point counts and existing system details; ask for separate hardware, installation, subscription and maintenance costs.
