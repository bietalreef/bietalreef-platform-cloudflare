# Architectural design and plans — Al Jimi, Al Ain

A practical briefing guide for company services, not a listing of available firms or an assurance of approval. Authority requirements depend on the project.

## 1. Villa design (تصميم الفلل)

**When it is needed:** Separate guest access from family circulation and relate the majlis to the kitchen, bedrooms and garden.

**Survey and brief:** Plot boundaries and orientation, required rooms, site photographs and available setback requirements. Attach the available plot boundary survey and mark separate family and guest entries on a legible plan.

**Scope and exclusions:** Alternative layouts, circulation diagrams and preliminary elevations; structural and MEP design only if contracted.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide the plot survey, north orientation, household brief and guest-entry requirements. Ask for two priced layout options with room areas, parking provision and explicit exclusions for structural and MEP design.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare the number of workable floor-plan and elevation options, how guest/family privacy and parking are tested, and not merely the number of renderings. Contract for revision rounds, responsibility for setback checks, editable drawing delivery and whether structural and MEP coordination is included.

**Acceptance check:** Check privacy, furniture clearances, door swings and parking arrangement. Check door swings, circulation widths and guest privacy against the furnished layout before sign-off.

## 2. Residential building design (تصميم المباني السكنية)

**When it is needed:** Establish unit mix, shared circulation, waste handling and service routes.

**Survey and brief:** Site area, entrances, parking demand and intended storey count. State unit and floor counts, lift cores and parking, distinguishing shared service areas.

**Scope and exclusions:** Unit plans, vertical cores and shared areas; detailed lift engineering excluded unless specified.

**Service-specific quotation request:** State unit count, floors, parking and service access. Request priced unit-mix alternatives showing circulation, lift-core allowances and the boundary of fire-egress and ventilation coordination.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare usable apartment area separately from shared corridors, stairs and plant spaces, along with unit-mix and parking alternatives. Allocate responsibility for lift cores, ventilation and egress coordination, specialist review stages and excluded detailed engineering studies.

**Acceptance check:** Review access separation, service ventilation and escape arrangements with qualified designers. Reconcile unit and parking counts and service routes against the area schedule and plans.

## 3. Commercial building design (تصميم المباني التجارية)

**When it is needed:** Start from intended use, customer flow and deliveries rather than facade appearance.

**Survey and brief:** Business use, storage and loading needs, access requirements and site plan. Mark delivery routes and loading bays separately from visitor entrances, with access-opening dimensions.

**Scope and exclusions:** Retail, service, storage and entrance layouts; specialist fit-out is separately scoped.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Supply the proposed business use, operating hours, loading and storage brief. Price customer and goods-flow plans separately from tenant-specific equipment design and facade works.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Require a schedule distinguishing lettable floor area from storage, loading and circulation, with customer and goods-flow scenarios. Compare base-building design against tenant fit-out, signage and trade-specific systems; identify who checks safety and accessibility requirements.

**Acceptance check:** Test delivery/customer conflicts, entrance placement and sanitary provision. Check that loading does not conflict with visitor exits and review service locations with the designer.

## 4. Architectural drawings preparation (إعداد المخططات المعمارية)

**When it is needed:** Translate the agreed concept into dimensioned plans, levels and openings for coordination.

**Survey and brief:** Approved concept, site dimensions, datum and required drawing detail. Record the project datum, drawing scale and revision of every sheet before agreeing deliverables.

**Scope and exclusions:** Agreed plans, sections, elevations and opening schedules; no automatic certification or permit.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Identify the design stage and approved drawing revision. Request a priced sheet register for plans, sections, elevations and opening schedules, with drawing scales, delivery formats and coordination boundaries.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare a numbered drawing register, scales, opening schedules and level references rather than an undefined sheet count. Require revision tracking across plans, sections and elevations, and assign coordination of structural, electrical and plumbing clashes and deliverable formats.

**Acceptance check:** Cross-check dimensions between views and drawing revisions. Cross-check levels, sheet revisions and door schedules between plans and sections.

## 5. Facade development (تطوير الواجهات)

**When it is needed:** Balance shading, window proportions, maintenance and material appearance.

**Survey and brief:** Facade photographs, solar orientation, opening sizes and finish samples. Photograph each facade by orientation and record opening heights, shading zones and obstructions.

**Scope and exclusions:** Material and colour options, openings and concept details; glazing engineering separately scoped.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Send facade photos, opening dimensions and solar orientation. Request material and shading options, fixing and maintenance concepts, and clear exclusions for thermal or glazing engineering.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare cladding and shading options against opening dimensions and safe cleaning access. Specify who develops fixing details, who reviews facade loads and glazing performance, and whether physical samples, thermal studies and construction drawings are separate.

**Acceptance check:** Check plan alignment and safe cleaning and maintenance access. Reconcile facade options with actual openings, maintenance access and agreed detail deliverables.

## 6. 3D model preparation (إعداد نماذج ثلاثية الأبعاد)

**When it is needed:** Decide whether the model serves visual presentation or technical coordination.

**Survey and brief:** Revision-controlled drawings, finish references and requested camera views. Supply coordinated plan revisions and specify view count, image resolution and required model format.

**Scope and exclusions:** Agreed model detail, images and editable file if specified; renders are not construction drawings.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Specify whether the model is for marketing or engineering coordination. Price camera views, image resolution, level of detail, editable model delivery, usage rights and revision rounds separately.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare view count, camera angles, output resolution, model detail and editable source-file delivery. Separate image-use rights and lighting/material revision rounds from obligations to match construction drawings; visualisations are not engineering approvals.

**Acceptance check:** Check geometry against plans; rendered colours are not approved material samples. Check camera views, image count and file formats against the delivery register; renders are not construction plans.

## 7. Existing drawing revisions (تعديل المخططات القائمة)

**When it is needed:** Identify requested changes and their effect on circulation, doors and services.

**Survey and brief:** Original drawing revision, site photographs and marked-up change list. Number each markup against the original drawing and identify rooms and services affected.

**Scope and exclusions:** Revised sheets and change register; redesign of every discipline is not implicit.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Supply editable source drawings and a numbered markup register. Request pricing by affected drawing group, stating whether structural, electrical and mechanical redesign is included.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Price revisions by affected sheets and disciplines, not building area alone. Identify the baseline revision, change register, structural and MEP impact checks and the party responsible for seeking approval where altered plans require it.

**Acceptance check:** Verify revision clouds and eliminate conflicting superseded sheets. Verify every change against the revision register and withdraw superseded drawing versions.

## 8. Permit drawing preparation (إعداد مخططات الترخيص)

**When it is needed:** Confirm the application type and competent authority's document requirements first.

**Survey and brief:** Available ownership or authorization documents, site plans and project details. Attach the available authority checklist and identify missing documents rather than assuming a complete application.

**Scope and exclusions:** Drawing package against verified checklist; filing, fees and tracking excluded unless agreed.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide application type, available plans and the authority checklist where known. Request a deliverable-by-deliverable drawing and form schedule, with submission and statutory fees priced separately.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare the submission drawing and document register, professional sign-off responsibilities and responses to authority comments; separate official fees from design fees. Define revision rounds and submission-version records without promising approval.

**Acceptance check:** Reconcile drawing index with application checklist; preparation is not approval. Check the submission document index and required professional sign-offs; preparation is not a permit.

## 9. Construction application submission (تقديم معاملات البناء)

**When it is needed:** Clarify who is authorised to submit, sign and respond to authority comments.

**Survey and brief:** Valid authorisation, applicant details, supporting documents and existing case reference. Record the application reference, authorisation and access rights for the submitting account.

**Scope and exclusions:** Submission and agreed follow-up; statutory fees and authority decisions remain outside the consultant's control.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide the application reference, valid authorisation and current attachments. Agree the number of comment-response cycles, submission evidence and progress updates; separate official charges.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare application preparation, data entry, document upload and follow-up responsibilities, including the authorised submitting account. Separate administrative charges, authority fees and drawing revisions; require application references and status notices.

**Acceptance check:** Request submission receipt, reference number, correspondence and actual status. Collect the filing receipt, application reference and comment log; submission is not approval.

## 10. Changes to approved drawings (تعديل المخططات المعتمدة)

**When it is needed:** An amended drawing is not approved until impacts and required formal steps are addressed.

**Survey and brief:** Previously approved sheets, proposed changes and affected dimensions. Compare the approved drawing revision with proposed changes and mark affected openings and services.

**Scope and exclusions:** Change drawings and impact coordination; reapproval is separately defined.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Attach the previously approved revision and a marked change drawing. Ask for an impact review covering structure and services, then separate fees for change sheets and any necessary reapproval.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Require a side-by-side change register against approved drawings, listing affected sheets and structural or MEP interfaces. Allocate redesign, resubmission and fee responsibilities and prohibit treating unapproved alterations as authorised construction.

**Acceptance check:** Check change register and approval references before construction. Confirm the revised drawing version and approval status before any affected work begins.

## 11. Maintenance permits (استخراج تصاريح الصيانة)

**When it is needed:** Distinguish cosmetic maintenance from work affecting systems or regulated elements.

**Survey and brief:** Defect photographs, repair method, location and previous documents. Photograph defects before repair and describe the proposed method to establish permit requirements.

**Scope and exclusions:** Prepare and track a permit application where required; physical repairs excluded.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Send defect photos and the proposed repair method. Request an assessment of whether a permit is needed, with documents, filing fees and follow-up itemised; exclude physical repairs.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Describe proposed maintenance works by location and photograph, then compare whether permit screening, supporting drawings or reports and application submission are included. Separate contractor work and authority fees from consultant fees and require decision records.

**Acceptance check:** Verify permit scope, conditions and validity before work starts. Match the permit category, validity and conditions to the stated maintenance work before starting.

## 12. Extension permits (استخراج تصاريح الإضافات)

**When it is needed:** Relate the extension to boundaries, setbacks, structure and utility connections.

**Survey and brief:** Plot plan, existing building drawings, proposed dimensions and service locations. Dimension the proposed extension against plot limits and show links to the existing structure and utilities.

**Scope and exclusions:** Study, drawings and submission as contracted; acceptance is not guaranteed.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide plot and existing-building plans with proposed extension dimensions. Ask for a scoped setbacks, structural and utilities review, drawings and filing, without assuming approval.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare extension studies by added area, connections to the existing building and any required structural assessment. Allocate annex drawings, utility alterations, permit submission and comment responses without assuming permission will be granted.

**Acceptance check:** Compare submitted extension area with drawings and approval conditions. Compare extension area and boundaries against submitted drawings and any issued conditions.

## 13. As-built condition drawings (إعداد مخططات الوضع القائم)

**When it is needed:** Use when built conditions differ from old plans or before renovation.

**Survey and brief:** Access to rooms, site measurements, photographs, visible openings and services. Identify accessible rooms and distinguish measured site dimensions from those copied from old drawings.

**Scope and exclusions:** Measured survey and drawings of accessible conditions; hidden services and safety certification excluded.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Identify accessible spaces and survey visit windows. Request a measured-survey quotation specifying tolerance, floor count, opening schedules and exclusions for concealed elements.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Compare site-survey method, measured points, levels, openings and visible service locations, as well as required CAD/PDF outputs. Require a discrepancy register against old drawings and clear exclusions for concealed elements not verifiable without specialist investigation.

**Acceptance check:** Spot-check dimensions and record inaccessible areas. Spot-check site dimensions and openings and mark inaccessible or unverified areas on the drawings.

## 14. Authority approvals coordination (تنسيق الاعتمادات الحكومية)

**When it is needed:** Separate authority sequence and comments from any promise of approval.

**Survey and brief:** Application references, authority correspondence, current drawings and authorisation. List each relevant authority, application reference and correspondence, separating pending from received approvals.

**Scope and exclusions:** Submission tracker, response coordination and document register; no guarantee of decision or timeframe.

**Service-specific quotation request:** Provide case references and correspondence. Ask for an authority-by-authority tracking plan identifying owner, document, target response and status reports, without any approval or timing guarantee.

**Compare quotes and agree terms:** Request an approval matrix showing relevant authorities, supporting documents, submitting parties and comment-response stages. Separate official charges from coordination and engineering revisions; require correspondence and decision records without guaranteeing dates or outcomes.

**Acceptance check:** Receive an authority-by-authority log of filings, comments, replies and status. Receive a separate status register for each authority, with latest correspondence and decision evidence.
