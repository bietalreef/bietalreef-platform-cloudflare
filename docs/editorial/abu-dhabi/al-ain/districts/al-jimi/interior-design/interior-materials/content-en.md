# Interior design and materials in Al Jimi

Practical preparation guidance for company-led design enquiries. No available firm or official approval is implied.

## 1. Majlis design

**When it is useful:** when the majlis layout obstructs guest movement, leaves seating exposed to the entrance or cannot accommodate the usual gathering. State the expected guest count, hospitality arrangements, privacy needs and furniture to retain.

**Photographs, dimensions and specifications:** Supply an existing plan if available; measure entrance widths, door and window openings and clear wall lengths. Photograph hospitality-serving areas, AC outlets and light switches, and record retained seating dimensions.

**Scope and exclusions:** The principal deliverable is a seating plan showing guest circulation, sockets and lighting. The design fee covers the agreed drawings and schedules only. Material purchasing, physical installation, professional site surveys and authority submissions require separately defined scope, responsibility and fees where applicable. The majlis plan does not authorise wall alterations or service-point relocation.

**Comparing quotes and agreeing terms:** Ask each designer for the number of seating alternatives and a drawing showing the entrance, serving route and spacing between seats. Compare sightlines from the doorway; agree on drawing scale, revision allowance and responsibility for verifying site dimensions before sign-off.

**Acceptance and quotation enquiry:** Cross-check the majlis circulation widths against the supplied measurements, door swings against seating, and the hospitality-serving route against guest movement. Review entrance sightlines and switch locations with the client before sign-off; a drawing alone is not site-execution approval. For a comparable design quotation, provide guest capacity, hospitality locations, privacy requirements and the requested majlis plan; request stated deliverables, revision rounds and design fees separately from execution. Biet Al Reef customer service can help structure the enquiry without guaranteeing a firm, price or date.

## 2. Living room design

**When it is useful:** when screen viewing, children's play and everyday circulation compete for the same space. Specify household use, screen viewing habits, storage needs and furniture that must remain.

**Photographs, dimensions and specifications:** Photograph the proposed screen wall and windows under typical daylight; measure sofa width, viewing distance and routes to doors. Mark existing sockets and devices to be retained.

**Scope and exclusions:** The principal deliverable is a furniture arrangement with storage and device outlets. The design fee covers the agreed drawings and schedules only. Material purchasing, physical installation, professional site surveys and authority submissions require separately defined scope, responsibility and fees where applicable. Screen purchase and concealed cable installation are outside this design scope.

**Comparing quotes and agreeing terms:** Compare screen glare, seating distances and storage arrangements; require a dimensioned furniture plan and device connection locations.

**Acceptance and quotation enquiry:** Review the plan using the actual dimensions of the sofa, tables and storage units rather than their appearance in a 3D rendering. Check full room-door and drawer operation, access to power outlets, and whether the entry route crosses the screen-viewing or children's play zone. Compare seating orientation with window glare at typical viewing times, recording any clashes for correction before approving the layout. For comparable quotations, send room photographs and measurements, retained-furniture sizes, screen and device positions, and household usage. Request a dimensioned plan, clearly identified layout alternatives, a defined revision allowance, and design fees separate from furniture purchasing, electrical works and installation. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare the enquiry without guaranteeing a firm, price or date.

## 3. Bedroom design

**When it is useful:** When wardrobe doors clash with the bed, storage is insufficient, or a desk or child's cot must share the bedroom. State who uses the room, the actual mattress size, retained furniture and whether a work or nursery zone is needed.

**Photographs, dimensions and specifications:** Photograph every wall with the room door open. Measure clear wall lengths, ceiling height, doorway, window, AC and socket positions. Record bed and wardrobe dimensions, door swing and drawer extension, including walking clearance around the bed.

**Scope and exclusions:** Request a dimensioned arrangement of the bed, wardrobes, bedside units and reading lights. The design does not include wardrobe fabrication, mattress purchase, AC relocation or electrical alterations. Specialist site surveys, approvals and physical works need separately assigned scope and fees.

**Comparing quotes and agreeing terms:** Compare usable passages beside the bed, wardrobe and drawer opening space, and access to switches and sockets. Agree on a scaled furniture plan, options and revision allowance, bespoke-unit details if requested, and who verifies dimensions before manufacture.

**Acceptance and quotation enquiry:** Check the mattress footprint, entrance route, door swings and reading-light locations against the final dimensioned plan; a 3D image alone is insufficient. Submit wall photographs, bed and wardrobe sizes, existing outlets and retained furniture. Request design deliverables and fees separately from supply and installation. Biet Al Reef customer service can help prepare the enquiry without guaranteeing a firm, price or date.

**Detailed coordination check:** Match the mattress, bed frame and headboard dimensions against the measured wall. Show wardrobe-door swings, drawer pull-outs and access to sockets and air-conditioning. Request a schedule distinguishing retained furniture from proposed purchases and naming who verifies product dimensions before ordering. At acceptance, inspect dimensioned clearances around the bed; a visual render alone cannot confirm fit.

Compare bids by the number of elevations, construction details and services coordination drawings, and identify whether electrical, plumbing and HVAC specialist checks are included. Accept the design against the appliance and services schedule rather than renders alone.
