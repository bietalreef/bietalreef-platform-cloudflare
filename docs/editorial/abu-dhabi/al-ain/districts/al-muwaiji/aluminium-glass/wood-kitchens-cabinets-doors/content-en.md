# Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors in Al Muwaiji

For Al Muwaiji joinery or metal and glazing work, prepare an opening-by-opening or unit-by-unit measurement schedule. Distinguish workshop production from on-site fitting and protection of existing finishes. These are company-service instructions, not an available workshop directory.

## 1. Wood kitchen design

**A design or repair choice:** Map appliances, sink and working aisles before cabinet styling. For refurbishment, inspect the substrate and existing part before assuming wholesale replacement is needed.

**Survey and quote:** Photograph openings from both sides or all faces of the unit; measure width, height, depth and operating direction as needed. Compare quoted timber/board grade, thickness, edge finish and hardware, including delivery, fitting, removal, protection and any excluded wall repairs.
