# Ceramic and porcelain tiles — supplies for Al Muwaiji

Before placing a materials order for an Al Muwaiji property, check when the stock is needed, where it can be unloaded and stored, and whether it serves a small repair or a complete build. Equivalent product specifications and delivery responsibilities matter more than headline unit prices. This does not imply stock or a listed supplier.

## 1. Ceramic tile supply

**Specify the correct product:** Select ceramic for floor or wall use according to water and slip performance. A reference photograph needs a model, drawing, sample or measurable specification before substitutes can be assessed.

**Pricing request:** Record approved sample, thickness, net dimensions and unloading access, quantities, intended use and preferred delivery window. Ask for a breakdown of material, batch, allowance, cutting, carriage, unloading and fitting if requested, and make removal, waste disposal or making-good explicit if required.

**Comparing and receiving:** Compare equivalent grades, price units, damaged-goods replacement and invoices. Check breakage, batch shade, size and joints before cutting or using the goods. Photograph nonconforming stock and do not assume delivery until confirmed.
