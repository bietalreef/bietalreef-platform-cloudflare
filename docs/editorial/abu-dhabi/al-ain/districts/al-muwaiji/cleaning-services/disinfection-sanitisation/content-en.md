# Disinfection and sanitisation — Al Muwaiji

For an Al Muwaiji property, begin with the site condition and expected outcome rather than choosing a treatment or crew size without inspection. Surface compatibility, human safety and waste handling depend on the task. This guide does not establish a listed business or available appointment.

## 1. Home disinfection service

**Diagnose before choosing a method:** Avoid indiscriminate spraying of toys or electronics; plan surfaces, ventilation and re-entry. Work that may expose occupants or workers to hazards requires appropriate controls and qualified providers where applicable.

**Quotation brief:** Supply photographs showing surface types, sensitive devices and vacancy windows, work quantities, allowed access hours and surfaces or devices requiring protection. Ask what needs a preliminary survey rather than accepting an unsupported fixed outcome.

**Compare the written scope:** Separate supervision, visits, supplies, records, response arrangements and exclusions. Avoid vague promises of a “complete clean” or “guaranteed disinfection”; identify process, products, return visits, unresolved issues and protection obligations.

**Acceptance:** Inspect the affected areas, document missed work and review agreed measurements or service logs when relevant. Do not assume health or operating outcomes have been proven without suitable evidence.
