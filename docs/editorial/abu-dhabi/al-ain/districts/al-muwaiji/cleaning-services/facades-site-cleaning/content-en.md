# Facade, car park and site cleaning — Al Muwaiji

For an Al Muwaiji property, begin with the site condition and expected outcome rather than choosing a treatment or crew size without inspection. Surface compatibility, human safety and waste handling depend on the task. This guide does not establish a listed business or available appointment.

## 1. Glass facade cleaning

**Diagnose before choosing a method:** Coated glass requires non-abrasive tools and compatible cleaning solutions. Work that may expose occupants or workers to hazards requires appropriate controls and qualified providers where applicable.

**Quotation brief:** Supply photographs showing facade height, access, cladding and pedestrian routes, work quantities, allowed access hours and surfaces or devices requiring protection. Ask what needs a preliminary survey rather than accepting an unsupported fixed outcome.

**Compare the written scope:** Separate equipment, chemicals, waste handling, damage liability and drying or shutdown time. Avoid vague promises of a “complete clean” or “guaranteed disinfection”; identify process, products, return visits, unresolved issues and protection obligations.

**Acceptance:** Inspect the affected areas, document missed work and review agreed measurements or service logs when relevant. Do not assume health or operating outcomes have been proven without suitable evidence.
