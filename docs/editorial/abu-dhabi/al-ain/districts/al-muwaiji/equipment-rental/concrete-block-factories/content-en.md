## 1. Ready-mix concrete production

**The technical decision:** Agree ready-mix strength, delivery time and testing with the engineer. A contractor should confirm capacity, dimensions or load conditions when errors may harm people, machinery or goods.

**Quote information:** Include item schedule, samples, product sizes, shop drawings and production batches, pickup or delivery locations and working windows. Compare grade, size, finish, testing, transport, lifting and rejected-product replacement separately, specifying permission checks, inspection duties, loss/damage liabilities and delays.

**Acceptance and comparison:** Match the machine or goods to the specification, charging unit and operating or inventory record. Photograph condition at receipt and return and retain any required test or delivery documents.
