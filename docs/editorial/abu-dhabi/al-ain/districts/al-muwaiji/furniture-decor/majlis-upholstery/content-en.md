# Majlis tailoring and sofa upholstery for Al Muwaiji

Before ordering furniture for an Al Muwaiji property, decide whether the task is retail supply, bespoke manufacture or repair. Photograph room access and keep approved colour and material samples so fitting issues can be resolved before delivery.

> **Source status:** proposed and not automatically active; informational guidance only.

## 1. Arabic majlis tailoring

**Use and dimensions:** Measure majlis runs, cushion count and coffee-table clearance. Before selecting the model or cutting materials, record width, height, depth and door or circulation clearances as relevant.

**What is included?** Break down materials, frames, foam or fabric, hardware, delivery, lifting and assembly. Identify whether removal of old items, wall repairs or returns are excluded. Approve a finish sample and written specification rather than “exactly like the photo”.

**At delivery:** Operate moving parts, inspect stability, seams, edges and adjoining finishes, and try seating or drawer access as appropriate. Record transport marks and missing fittings before accepting the work.
