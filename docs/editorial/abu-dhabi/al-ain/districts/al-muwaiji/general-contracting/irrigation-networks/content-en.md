# Garden irrigation systems — Al Muwaiji company services

An Al Muwaiji enquiry may involve a new project or an alteration to an occupied property. Record what is to remain, what must change and the measured site constraints. This is quotation-preparation guidance, not a claim of available contractors.

> **Category restriction:** currently non-selectable; this page is preparatory information only.

## 1. Irrigation network design

For **irrigation network design**, establish the site constraints first: Separate trees and lawn into irrigation zones by water need and supply.

**Before the site visit:** Send an overview photo, close-ups of affected elements and verifiable measurements or quantities, together with outdoor dimensions, drainage, buried utilities and maintenance access. Ask whether a professional survey or approval is needed; safety-critical works must be appropriately assessed.

**Comparing company offers:** Request separate prices for ground preparation, supply, irrigation, anchoring, drainage and aftercare. Clarify exclusions such as removing existing materials, protecting occupied areas or repairing newly uncovered defects. Record approved samples, stage dates and variation authorisation in writing.

**Checking completion:** Examine levels, anchors, drainage and agreed aftercare. Test the agreed outcome and document outstanding defects with photos before closing the order.
