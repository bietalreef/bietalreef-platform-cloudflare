# Restoration and refurbishment — Al Muwaiji company services

An Al Muwaiji enquiry may involve a new project or an alteration to an occupied property. Record what is to remain, what must change and the measured site constraints. This is quotation-preparation guidance, not a claim of available contractors.

## 1. Older building restoration

For **older building restoration**, establish the site constraints first: Record cracking and moisture history in old buildings before stripping finishes.

**Before the site visit:** Send an overview photo, close-ups of affected elements and verifiable measurements or quantities, together with drawings, measurable quantities and who signs off concealed works. Ask whether a professional survey or approval is needed; safety-critical works must be appropriately assessed.

**Comparing company offers:** Request separate prices for labour, materials, logistics, approvals, testing and reinstatement. Clarify exclusions such as removing existing materials, protecting occupied areas or repairing newly uncovered defects. Record approved samples, stage dates and variation authorisation in writing.

**Checking completion:** Examine concealed-stage records, operational checks and unresolved snags. Test the agreed outcome and document outstanding defects with photos before closing the order.
