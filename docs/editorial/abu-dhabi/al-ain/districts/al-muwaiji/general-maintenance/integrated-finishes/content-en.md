# Integrated finishing and fit-out in Al Muwaiji

For space-by-space completion at an Al Muwaiji property, describe existing conditions before asking for a fixed price. A visible symptom may start behind the finish, so agree how investigations and follow-on repairs will be treated. This guide does not confirm a listed company or a quotation.

## 1. Villa fit-out

**A practical concern:** Bathrooms, entrances and living rooms require different finishing specifications. If the source of a defect is uncertain, request a survey before accepting wholesale replacement or concealing unfinished work.

**To prepare the enquiry:** Provide room schedule, sizes and services drawings, the number or size of affected points and any occupied-building restrictions or shutdown windows.

**Compare contractor scope:** Separate materials, labour, access, protection and surface reinstatement. Clarify diagnosis, exclusions, consumables, variations, warranty and necessary drying or testing time before authorising work.

**Handover:** Demonstrate the requested function, inspect adjacent finishes and housekeeping, and collect agreed test or service records. Do not close a recurring fault without checking its underlying cause.
