# Paint and decorative coatings in Al Muwaiji

For surface preparation and finish selection at an Al Muwaiji property, describe existing conditions before asking for a fixed price. A visible symptom may start behind the finish, so agree how investigations and follow-on repairs will be treated. This guide does not confirm a listed company or a quotation.

## 1. دهان الفلل

**A practical concern:** List villa rooms, colour schemes and ceiling heights before pricing. If the source of a defect is uncertain, request a survey before accepting wholesale replacement or concealing unfinished work.

**To prepare the enquiry:** Provide wall areas, damage photos and colour samples, the number or size of affected points and any occupied-building restrictions or shutdown windows.

**Compare contractor scope:** Separate substrate preparation, samples, supplies, fitting, cleanup and adjacent repairs. Clarify diagnosis, exclusions, consumables, variations, warranty and necessary drying or testing time before authorising work.
