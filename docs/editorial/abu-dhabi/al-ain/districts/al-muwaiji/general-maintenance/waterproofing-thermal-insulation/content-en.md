# Waterproofing and thermal protection in Al Muwaiji

For water ingress and thermal bridges at an Al Muwaiji property, describe existing conditions before asking for a fixed price. A visible symptom may start behind the finish, so agree how investigations and follow-on repairs will be treated. This guide does not confirm a listed company or a quotation.
