# Architectural planning and permits in Al Muwaiji

For a property in Al Muwaiji, a useful design brief states how the space must work and what existing features cannot change. Distinguish conceptual illustrations from coordinated drawings, technical certification and statutory permission.

## 1. تصميم الفلل — Architectural design and drawings

**The decision:** Room count and guest/service circulation should inform a villa floor plan. Settle this issue before commissioning drawings that may otherwise have to be redone.

**Provide:** record measured site dimensions, intended use, existing services and current drawing revision. Identify retained elements, changes and required deliverables, including the responsibility for extra surveys, approvals and revision cycles.

**Compare offers:** Look beyond drawing count: assess coordination between disciplines, definition level, file formats, response times and exclusions such as official fees or specialist reports.

**Acceptance:** Verify editable source drawings, final sheets and approval responsibilities, then check that the proposal answers the agreed space and usage brief before issuing a final revision to contractors.
