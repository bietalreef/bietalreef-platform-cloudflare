# Iron, Aluminium, Glass and Wood Workshops in Al Dhafra

This sector covers joinery, steel, aluminium, glass and metal workshops serving projects in Al Dhafra. Before requesting a survey, identify the exact project location within the region and provide opening and façade measurements, vehicle access and unloading points for long profiles or glass. Do not assume a workshop or installation crew is close to every site; transport, lifting, assembly and site visits should be itemised.

## Directory scope

Company, workshop and establishment services only; the professionals and tradespeople list remains separate. This content does not confirm that a workshop is listed or that a product has technical approval.


## Wooden Kitchens, Cabinets and Doors


### Wooden kitchen design

Provide wall and corner measurements, water, drainage, power, extraction and appliance positions, with photographs after initial finishing. Request cabinet elevations showing heights, openings, storage, materials and edges, checking maintenance access before fabrication.


### Kitchen fabrication

Approve final drawings and a cabinet schedule defining dimensions, materials, hardware and built-in appliances before cutting. Request colour samples and edge, back-panel and joint details, with each unit labelled to its installation location.


### Kitchen installation

Confirm walls, floors, service outlets and levels are ready before scheduling installation. Agree cabinet anchoring, levelling, worktop and appliance connections, then inspect door and drawer alignment, gaps and access to valves and sockets.


### Wardrobe design

Specify available width, height and depth, door, window and socket positions, user count and storage habits. Request an internal layout for hanging, drawers, shelves, shoes and bags, checking door swing and circulation.


### Wardrobe fabrication

Provide panel and dimension schedules, door fronts, hinges, runners and handles, specifying grain direction and colour. Confirm board, edging, back-panel and reinforcement thicknesses, and inspect labelled parts against the approved sample before delivery.


### Walk-in wardrobe fit-out

Provide room plan, ceiling height, lighting, air-conditioning and door positions, defining hanging, storage and seating zones. Coordinate open and closed cabinets, mirrors, lighting and circulation, preserving ventilation and maintenance access.


### Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

Photograph damaged units and state whether fronts, hinges, worktops or cabinet carcasses need repair or replacement. Inspect internal panels and damp areas before covering them, comparing partial repair with replacement based on carcass condition and parts compatibility.


### Wooden door fabrication

Specify opening size, wall thickness, swing, door type, core, facing and finish, with a photograph of the existing frame. Confirm edges, reinforcement, hinges and lock details, and approve a colour sample and final measurement after floor level is set.


### Door installation

Measure opening and frame, wall thickness, hinges, lock and threshold, noting finished floor condition. Specify clearances, swing, frame anchoring and edge treatment, then test closing, locking and floor clearance.


### Fire-rated door fabrication

Specify the required door rating from project documents, opening size, frame type and hardware; do not infer a rating from appearance. Request product documentation, label, leaf and frame compatibility and self-closing details, verifying project and authority requirements before approval.


### Door frame fabrication

Provide opening dimensions, wall thickness, frame material, door type and threshold, specifying wall anchorage. Confirm corner, joint, fixing and finish details, checking frame plumb and squareness before hanging the door leaf.


### Timber partition construction

Specify partition length, height and thickness, whether fixed or demountable, and door and service locations. Request framing, anchorage, any required insulation and two-sided finish details, checking stability and clearance from circulation and services.


### Door maintenance

Describe sagging, locking difficulty, hinge damage or timber swelling and photograph the edges and frame. Identify the cause and required parts, assessing adjustment before replacement, then test opening, closing and locking after repair.


### Wood finishing and refinishing

Identify timber type, existing coating and desired colour or sheen, with photographs of scratches and peeling. Request a limited sanding and finish test, specifying primer, coats, drying time and protection from dust and odours.


## Wooden Furniture and Décor


### Custom furniture fabrication

Provide a sketch or reference image with dimensions, function, load, materials and colour, and state the installation site within Al Dhafra. Request shop drawings, joint and edge details and samples, separating transport and installation from fabrication and inspecting the item before dispatch.


### TV console fabrication

Specify screen and equipment sizes, cable routes, ventilation and viewing height, providing wall width and socket locations. Confirm storage layout, ventilation openings, top load and anchorage, then test doors and drawers after installation.


### Storage unit fabrication

List items to be stored, their weights and dimensions, and specify unit height, width, depth and access. Confirm shelf and reinforcement design, drawers, hinges and board type, stating whether the unit is wall-fixed or freestanding.


### Timber wall cladding

Specify wall area and height, door, switch and lighting positions, panel direction and joints. Request a sample and section showing framing, rear void, ventilation and fixing, then inspect joint alignment and colour continuity around corners.


### CNC decorative joinery

Provide the design or vector file, material thickness, sheet size and required engraving depth. Request a test cut to check fine details, edges and assembly, and specify finish, fixing and transport protection.


### Counter fabrication

Specify counter length, height and depth, equipment, cable, storage and service openings. Request drawings for top, edges, frame, supports and cladding, coordinating connections and staff movement before fabrication approval.


### Retail and office furniture fabrication

Provide space plan, unit count, function and dimensions of equipment or files each piece will hold. Request part labels, material and edging schedule and assembly details, distinguishing site-fixed items from workshop-assembled pieces.


### Interior space design

Define activity, users, circulation, doors, windows and services, attaching measurements and existing-condition photographs. Request a functional layout for furniture, lighting and materials, linking each element to construction and budget requirements rather than relying on a render alone.


### Joinery fabrication and installation

Compile doors, cabinets, cladding and counters in one schedule with locations, dimensions and finishes. Approve drawings and samples before fabrication, and agree transport and installation methods protecting edges and surfaces with staged handover points.


### Majlis joinery and fit-out

Specify seating capacity, majlis area, guest entrances, curtains, lighting and cooling positions, with wall measurements. Plan furniture, cladding, storage and materials, ensuring joinery does not obstruct doors or service outlets.


### Bedroom joinery and fit-out

Provide room plan, bed size, wardrobes, bedside units, power and lighting locations. Coordinate fixed furniture with doors, drawers and circulation, approving fabric, timber and colour samples before fabrication.


### Office and retail joinery fit-out

Specify workstation or display-unit counts, equipment dimensions, storage and reception areas, and provide the site plan. Request a parts schedule for function, materials, loads, fixing and cables, sequencing installation to limit operational disruption.


### Timber decorative feature installation

Provide reference images and wall or ceiling dimensions, specifying grain direction, joints and integrated lighting. Require framing, ventilation, fixing and edge details, with a physical sample and coordination checks for electrical and air-conditioning services.


### Interior joinery finishing and handover

Prepare an inventory of each joinery item, location, approved sample and hardware, stating whether adjustment, surface treatment or replacement is required. Inspect joints, edges, finish, operation and anchorage, and receive item drawings, warranties and care instructions.


## Aluminium Doors and Windows


### Aluminium window fabrication

Measure openings after finish levels are established and specify profile, colour, glazing, opening type and insect screen. Request shop drawings, profile sections, hardware, gaskets and drainage details, with a colour sample and dimension check before production.


### Aluminium window installation

Confirm opening readiness, squareness and dimensions, providing frame, sill and façade photographs. Specify anchoring, perimeter sealing, drainage and sash adjustment, then test closing, leakage and hinge movement after installation.


### Aluminium door fabrication

Specify opening width and height, door type and swing, leaf count, lock and glazing or infill. Confirm profile, reinforcement, hinges, hardware and threshold, with a drawing showing clearances and movement before fabrication.


### Aluminium door installation

Provide opening dimensions, wall material, floor levels, door type and weight, and site access route. Specify frame anchoring, sealing, threshold and hinge adjustment, then test movement, lock, stability and clearance after installation.


### Insect screen installation

Identify window or door type, opening dimensions, operation and required mesh, with frame photographs. Specify frame and mesh type and removal, cleaning and closing method, then inspect edge sealing and ease of operation.


### Door and window maintenance

Record rubbing, leakage or movement problems, identify profile and hardware and photograph labels if available. Inspect rollers, hinges, gaskets, locks and drainage channels, distinguishing adjustable parts from replacements.


### Hardware and glazing replacement

Identify hardware or glass type, thickness, dimensions and fixing, providing photographs and profile reference if available. Confirm compatibility and remeasure after removing the damaged part, with site protection and safe handling of broken pieces.


### Aluminium kitchen fabrication

Provide kitchen plan, wall measurements, water, drainage, power and appliance positions, specifying panel type, colour and edges. Request cabinet, reinforcement, hinge, drawer and ventilation schedules, approving a sample and finish before fabrication.


### Aluminium kitchen installation

Confirm wall and floor levels and service-point readiness, providing final site measurements. Agree cabinet, worktop, sink and appliance fixing and joint treatment, then inspect alignment, drawer and door operation and corner cleanability.


### Aluminium cabinet fabrication

Specify cabinet dimensions, location, use, load, doors, shelves and ventilation. Confirm profile, panel thickness, reinforcement, hinges, lock and finish, with a drawing showing anchorage and clearances before production.


### Bathroom cabinet fabrication

Provide bathroom dimensions and basin, mirror, supply and waste positions, defining storage and moisture exposure. Specify materials and hardware suited to actual conditions, with protected edges, floor clearance and access to pipework.


### External storage unit fabrication

Specify unit location, dimensions, anchorage, sun, rain and dust exposure and stored load. Confirm profile, coating, locks, ventilation and drainage, and verify the unit can be transported and installed without blocking access routes.


### Aluminium kitchen refurbishment

Photograph units and identify damage to doors, hinges, worktop or carcass, noting dimensions and current colour. Inspect profiles and joints before replacing fronts, comparing hardware repair with replacement of damaged units.


### Kitchen hinge and hardware maintenance

Identify doors or drawers that fail to close or make noise, providing hinge or runner photographs and part numbers if available. Request adjustment and fixing checks first, replacing incompatible parts with hardware suited to door weight and panel thickness.


## Glass Façades and Glazing


### Glass façade construction

Provide elevations, sections, building heights, opening dimensions and proposed system. Request fixing, glazing, joint, sealing, drainage and maintenance-access details, and confirm the project's required technical documents before fabrication.


### Tempered glass installation

Specify application, pane size and thickness, edges and hinge or handle cut-outs, with a site photograph. Request glass specifications and documentation, handling, fixing and clearance details, and inspect edges, identification and stability at handover.


### Shopfront glazing

Provide shopfront width and height, door, signage, equipment and fixing points, defining entry and exit movement. Request profile, glass, lock, threshold and sealing drawings, and coordinate installation and handover to avoid unagreed operational obstruction.


### Glass door installation

Specify door type, weight, dimensions, swing and hinge or pivot positions and finished floor level. Confirm glass, hardware, thresholds and stops, then test closing, balance and clearance from walls and floor.


### Glass partition construction

Provide space plan, partition height, door and equipment positions and fixing points, specifying privacy or acoustic needs. Confirm glazing, profiles, gaskets and joints, then inspect alignment, door stability and seals after installation.


### Glass balustrade installation

Specify balustrade length and height, edge and base type, anchors and user movement around it. Request system, glass, hardware, fixing and project-required test details; do not set dimensions or thickness from visual estimates.


### Glass façade maintenance

Identify damaged panes or joints, their height and access method, providing inside and outside photographs if possible. Inspect gaskets, sealant, anchorage, drainage and glass, with a work and protection plan for areas below and a record of replaced parts.


## Steel and Metal Fabrication


### Steel door fabrication

Specify opening dimensions, door thickness, use, coating, lock and ventilation. Request frame sections, reinforcement, welds, hinges, weight and corrosion protection, with a shop drawing and confirmed swing before cutting.


### Metal handrail fabrication

Provide stair or balcony measurements, post and anchor locations, material and finish. Request drawings of section heights, joints, welds and bases, checking handrail continuity, edge safety and anchorage before fabrication.


### Metal shade fabrication

Specify coverage area, heights, column positions, vehicle route and drainage, providing a site plan and obstacle photographs. Request frame, footing, cover, protection and anchorage details, with a transport and on-site assembly plan for long members.


### Metal structure fabrication and erection

Provide structural drawings, dimensions, loads, use and lifting and assembly location. Request schedules for sections, plates, welds, bolts, coating and protection, with a lifting plan, joint inspection and documented changes before execution.


### Stainless steel works

Specify item type, application, moisture or chemical exposure and finish, providing dimensions and joint drawings. Confirm material grade, thickness, welding, polishing and scratch protection, then inspect surface consistency and edges at handover.


### Welding works

Identify metal type and thickness, joint location and condition and expected loads or function, providing clear pre-work photographs. Specify welding process, edge preparation, consumables and suitable inspection, protecting the surroundings and documenting the cleaned joint.


### Metalwork maintenance

Record corrosion, loose anchors, deformation or coating failure, stating whether the item is structural or decorative. Request an assessment for repair or replacement, specifying rust removal, treatment, coating, re-anchoring and post-work checks.

**Quotation and handover:** Provide the Al Dhafra project location, dimensions, drawings, photographs, required samples, quantities and need-by date. Separate design, survey, fabrication, materials, hardware, transport, lifting, installation, testing and warranty. If no workshop is listed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive the same request; intake does not depend on a visible provider. At handover, compare dimensions, colour, profiles, glass and hardware with approved samples and drawings, test operation and fixing, and record discrepancies before signing.
