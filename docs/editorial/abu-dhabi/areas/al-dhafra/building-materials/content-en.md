# Building Materials and Supply in Al Dhafra

This page covers supply and sale of construction and finishing materials, timber, stone, worktops and tiles for projects across Al Dhafra. The region is extensive, so provide the project location, unloading point, vehicle route and storage capacity before transport is priced or deliveries are scheduled. Attach quantity schedules, specifications, drawings and photographs of existing materials where replacement is involved.

## Directory scope

Company and establishment listings only; the professionals and tradespeople list remains separate. The first specialty is marked in the source as containing a non-selectable category. Editorial content does not activate that category or establish that a supplier is listed.


## Building and Finishing Materials

> Taxonomy note: The source marks this specialty as currently non-selectable.


### Cement supply

Specify cement type and required grade or specification, bag count or bulk quantity and storage point. Provide the quantity schedule and use date, and request brand, batch, packaging, transport, unloading and storage-life details.


### Sand supply

State intended sand use, quantity, unloading point and any specified cleanliness or grading requirements. Request source description, net load, delivery window and unloading method; compare prices only after material type and quantity are aligned.


### Aggregate supply

Specify aggregate type, size, grading, quantity and application, attaching the specification or engineer's request if available. Request load, batch, source and delivery details, and ensure there is safe storage space that prevents mixing with other materials.


### Brick and block supply

Provide unit type, dimensions, required strength or specification, approximate count and unloading point. Compare dimensions, weight, pallet quantities, expected breakage and returns, clarifying who supplies lifting equipment or unloading labour.


### Reinforcement steel supply

Attach the bar bending schedule or list of diameters, lengths, weights and required batches, and identify storage and unloading points. Request product documentation, actual weights, bundling and delivery details, comparing the same grade, diameter and quantity.


### Ready-mix concrete supply

Specify concrete class, pour location, volume, delivery rate, expected time and pumping needs. Request mix details, tests, delivery tickets and truck arrival windows, coordinating supply with formwork and reinforcement readiness and mixer access.


### Construction materials supply for projects

Prepare a consolidated material schedule with item codes, quantities, need-by dates and delivery points within Al Dhafra, separating urgent items from phased stock. Request batch deliveries, approved alternatives, transport, unloading, storage and unavailable items to be stated.


### Gypsum supply

Specify gypsum form—bags, boards or prefabricated products—plus board size, thickness, quantity and storage conditions. Request product data, stacking and moisture and breakage protection, ensuring delivery matches the work stage and unloading location.


### Paint materials supply

Provide colour and surface schedules, areas and required primer and finish system, distinguishing interior from exterior. Request product codes, pack sizes, batch consistency, storage and coverage data; do not substitute a specified product without the technical decision-maker's approval.


### Waterproofing and insulation materials supply

Specify application area, substrate, required system, layer thickness and quantity, attaching the specification or reference product. Request performance and compatibility data, shelf life, transport protection and product documents before approving the shipment.


### Flooring materials supply

Specify flooring type, size, thickness, colour, quantity and laying plan. Request item code, batch, pieces per pack, weight and expected waste, and reserve spare material from the same batch where appropriate.


### Sanitaryware supply

Provide a fixture schedule with model and dimensions, supply and waste positions and accessories. Compare set completeness, spare parts, warranty, transport and breakage protection, checking quantities and models on arrival before signing.


### Electrical supplies

Prepare a schedule of cable, breaker, switch, socket and panel types, quantities and specifications. Request product data, sizes, ratings, compatibility and batches, distinguishing project-specified materials from proposed alternatives before purchase.


### Plumbing supplies

Provide a list of pipes, diameters, fittings, valves, fixtures and sealants, identifying system and application. Confirm compatibility and operating pressure, material data and storage, and schedule phased deliveries around site progress.


### Building materials retail

Identify required materials by name or photograph, quantities and delivery point, separating unit price from transport and unloading. For multiple items, send one list to compare alternatives, pack sizes and total delivered cost rather than a single-item price.


### Sanitaryware retail

Specify fixture type, size, model, colour and installation accessories, with a photograph of the location or item being replaced. Check connection compatibility, matching components and return terms after opening, and inspect the product before transporting it to site.


### Electrical equipment retail

State purpose, rating, voltage, use and operating environment. Request model number, accessories, warranty and operating data, comparing actual specifications with project needs rather than relying on a brand name or product photograph.


### Plumbing materials retail

Provide pipe size, material, required pressure, fitting type and photographs of existing parts for replacements. Confirm compatibility across pipe, fitting and valve dimensions and technical specifications, and record small components to avoid shortages during installation.


### Paint retail

State paint area, substrate, colour, sheen and interior or exterior conditions, noting any existing coating. Request quantities for primer and finish coats, colour codes and pack sizes, and verify batch consistency when buying multiple containers.


### Insulation materials retail

Specify insulation type, application, thickness, area and expected installation method. Request product data, compatibility with adjacent layers and protection and storage requirements, verifying actual quantities before transport to prevent work stoppage.


### Material delivery to site

Send the project pin within Al Dhafra and describe truck access, gate width, unloading point and receiving capacity. Specify vehicle type, trip count, arrival window, waiting charges and lifting or labour needs, and assign responsibility for receiving and checking quantities.


## Timber and Sheet Materials


### MDF board supply

Specify thickness, sheet size, density, raw or faced surface and quantity, noting any moisture exposure. Request board and batch data and flat storage and warping protection, including whether cutting service is required.


### Natural timber supply

State timber species, sections, lengths, grade, required moisture and application. Request batch photographs and drying and defect information, specify whether pricing is by length, piece or volume, and agree transport protection.


### Plywood supply

Specify sheet size, thickness, ply count, adhesive type and interior or exterior use, attaching any specification. Compare flatness, layer consistency, edges and weight, and clarify storage and moisture prevention.


### Melamine board supply

Provide colour code or sample image, thickness, core type, sheet size and quantity, specifying grain direction where relevant. Confirm colour and batch match, surface layer and corner protection, reserving matching spare sheets if needed.


### HPL sheet supply

Specify thickness, finish, colour, dimensions and substrate. Request scratch, heat and moisture performance relevant to the application, plus transport, storage and compatible adhesive details, reviewing the sample under site lighting.


### Joinery hardware supply

Provide a schedule of hinges, runners, handles, brackets and sliding systems with dimensions and expected loads. Confirm models, load ratings, installation and board-thickness compatibility, and check that screws and accessories are complete for each unit.


### Panel cutting and preparation

Provide a cutting file or schedule listing each part's dimensions, grain direction and edged sides, stating saw kerf and acceptable tolerance. Request part labelling, grouping by room or unit and edge protection during transport.


## Marble and Granite


### Natural marble supply

Specify marble type or reference image, finish, thickness, dimensions, quantity and application. Request photographs and identifiers for actual slabs and note vein and colour variation; agree slab selection, numbering and packing to avoid batch mismatch.


### Granite supply

State indoor or outdoor use, colour, finish, thickness and piece sizes, including special openings or edges. Compare actual slabs and natural variation, cutting, packing and weight, and clarify whether lifting and unloading are included.


### Marble cutting to size

Send a numbered cutting schedule with length, width, thickness, vein direction, openings and exposed edges. Confirm dimensions and tolerances before cutting, label each piece and inspect edges, corners and openings physically at handover.


### Marble grinding and polishing

State floor area, surface condition, scratch and stain depth and joint condition, providing raking-light photographs. Request a test patch, grinding stages, gloss level and post-treatment protection, with dust and water control and furniture protection.


### Marble floor installation

Provide the floor setting-out plan, slab sizes, vein direction and door and corridor levels. Specify substrate, adhesive, joints, skirting and cuts, then inspect vein matching, level, joints and stability before polishing or handover.


### Marble wall and façade cladding

State cladding heights, piece weights, substrate, anchors and openings, providing sections and elevations. Specify mechanical or adhesive fixing as designed, joints and movement details, protection and safe access, and inspect anchorage before concealing components.


### Marble maintenance and treatment

Photograph stains, scratches and wear, identifying stone type, location and previous cleaning products. Request a compatibility test in a discreet area and specify cleaning, filling, polishing or sealing, with aftercare instructions.


## Kitchen and Vanity Tops


### Kitchen worktop fabrication

Provide the kitchen cabinet plan, final measurements, sink and hob positions, cut-outs and overhangs. Specify surface material, thickness, edge and upstand, and request a fabrication drawing showing joints and cuts before production approval.


### Kitchen worktop installation

Confirm cabinets are level and secure before final templating, providing photographs of the base, joints and appliance locations. Agree lifting, transport, fixing, seam and silicone details, then inspect level, cut-outs and wall junctions.


### Marble vanity fabrication

Specify vanity width, depth and height, basin and tap type and waste position, providing a drawing or cut-out template. Confirm stone thickness, support for overhangs, edge treatment and sealing around the basin before fabrication.


### Sink and appliance cut-out fabrication

Provide sink and appliance models or original templates, installation dimensions and manufacturer instructions, marking each cut-out and orientation. Check clearances and support around openings, and compare the template with the actual item before final cutting.


### Quartz worktop installation

Specify quartz type, colour, thickness, piece lengths, cut-outs and seams, and request a sample and view of the available slab or batch. Detail support, fixing and seam and edge materials, then inspect colour and joints after installation.


### Porcelain slab worktop installation

Provide worktop dimensions, openings, edges, slab thickness and substrate, and state use and cleaning expectations. Specify support, cutting, transport, lifting and seams, protecting corners and surface from breakage during handling.


### Worktop maintenance and repolishing

Identify worktop material, damaged area and stain or scratch type, with close and raking-light photographs. Request a test to determine whether marks can be removed without changing gloss or colour, and specify suitable protection and aftercare.


## Ceramic and Porcelain Tiles


### Ceramic tile supply

Specify size, colour, finish, application and quantity, providing a sample image or item code. Request pieces per pack, weight, batch and shade variation, and calculate waste and spare stock based on the laying pattern before ordering.


### Porcelain tile supply

State size, thickness, finish, traffic and installation location, noting rectified or standard edges. Request product and batch data and slip performance appropriate to use, inspecting samples from the same cartons when ordering larger quantities.


### Outdoor tile supply

Specify exposed location, use, water and sun exposure and pedestrian or vehicle traffic. Compare surface, finish, thickness, slip suitability and cleaning, requesting product data rather than choosing from photographs alone.


### Tile cutting and preparation

Provide a cutting schedule with corner, opening and exposed-edge dimensions, grain or pattern direction and piece labels. Specify cutting and edge treatment and tolerances, then check pieces against the layout before delivery to site.


### Ceramic tile installation

Provide installation area, tile layout, door and drain levels and substrate condition. Specify adhesive, grout, joints, skirting and cuts, then inspect lippage, joint consistency, falls and tile stability before acceptance.


### Porcelain tile installation

Specify tile size and thickness, substrate, use loads and laying pattern, attaching a batch sample. Request a setting-out plan minimising narrow cuts and detailing joints, edges and adhesive, then inspect bond, level and perimeter cuts.


### Skirting supply and installation

Measure wall lengths and skirting height, specifying material, colour and junctions with floors and doors. Include corners, joints, cuts and spare quantity, then inspect top-line alignment, clean joints and secure fixing.
