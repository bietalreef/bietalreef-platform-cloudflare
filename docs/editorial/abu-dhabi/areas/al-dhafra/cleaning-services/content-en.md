# Cleaning, Disinfection and Pest Control in Al Dhafra

This sector covers cleaning, disinfection, pest control, building operations and support staffing across Al Dhafra. The region's scale makes exact service location, access hours, water and power supply, vehicle routes and site count essential to estimating visits, labour and equipment. Do not assume one provider can cover every location or offer a uniform appointment.

## Directory scope

Company and establishment services only; the professionals and tradespeople list remains separate. A service description does not establish that a company is listed, a permit exists or a treatment result is guaranteed.


## Home and Building Cleaning


### Villa cleaning

State villa area, floors, rooms and occupancy, distinguishing routine, deep and post-construction cleaning. Provide photographs of sensitive areas and external surfaces, and request a room and surface checklist, products used and confirmation of whether windows and stores are included.


### Apartment cleaning

Provide room and bathroom count, unit area and pre-service condition, noting access, parking and lift rules where applicable. Separate kitchen, bathroom, glass, floor and interior cleaning from balcony or storage, and clarify waste removal and cleaning products.


### Office cleaning

State office area, workstation count, shared rooms and available cleaning hours, identifying areas with sensitive equipment or files. Request daily and periodic task schedules, products, key-handling procedures and issue reporting.


### Building cleaning

Provide floor, entrance, corridor, lift, common-area, water-point and service-store details. Define responsibility for stairs, glass, parking and service rooms, visit frequency, staffing, equipment and completion records.


### Post-construction cleaning

Provide site photographs and expected completion date, noting any ongoing work and sensitive surfaces such as stone, glass and aluminium. Separate construction debris, fine dust, paint and adhesive residue and window cleaning, protecting new finishes.


### Pre-move-in cleaning

State furniture move-in date, unit condition and whether cabinets, appliances and windows are ready. Request a sequence for interiors, cabinets and bathrooms followed by floors and glass, testing products on a discreet area where needed.


### Recurring cleaning contracts

Prepare an inventory of areas and facilities, usage points, access hours and required visit frequency, separating daily, weekly and monthly tasks. Compare staffing, hours, products, equipment, supervision, reporting and exclusions, considering the site's location within Al Dhafra.


## Upholstery and Furnishing Cleaning


### Steam sofa cleaning

Identify fabric type, seat count, stains, odours and any manufacturer care instructions, providing a label photograph if available. Request a colourfastness test, cleaning method, moisture level and drying time, protecting timber and floors.


### Carpet cleaning

Provide rug dimensions, fibre type, backing or hand-finished edges, and identify stains or odours. Request a hidden-area test and dust removal, washing and drying method, clarifying whether cleaning is on site or requires transport.


### Fitted carpet cleaning

State fitted-carpet area, pile type, traffic level, stains and fixed furniture. Confirm cleaning method, drying time and room re-entry, protecting furniture, edges and electrical outlets.


### Arabic majlis cleaning

List cushions and mattress pieces, fabric type and removable covers, noting stains and high-use areas. Test fabric and filling, define cleaning of covers and frame and drying, and label pieces for accurate replacement.


### Mattress cleaning

Specify mattress size and type, used sides, stains or odours and any moisture sensitivity. Confirm a material-appropriate treatment, extraction and drying time, and do not reuse the mattress until it is dry according to service instructions.


### Curtain cleaning

Identify fabric and hanging system and whether curtains can be removed, providing dimensions, labels and care instructions. Clarify on-site or off-site cleaning, shrinkage and colour risks and rehanging, labelling each set by room and window.


### Upholstery sanitising

Specify furnishing type, area, use and purpose, noting products previously applied. Request product identity, application method, ventilation time and precautions for children, pets and surfaces; sanitising does not correct the source of soil or moisture.


## Façade and Site Cleaning


### Glass façade cleaning

State glass area and height, access method, water and drainage points, providing façade and obstacle photographs. Confirm access and protection equipment, cleaning product and stain treatment, isolating areas below and managing water runoff.


### Building façade cleaning

Identify façade material, building height, soiled areas and possible access. Test cleaner compatibility on a limited area and select suitable pressure or brush, protecting glass, paint, signage and adjacent areas.


### Parking area cleaning

State parking area, floor type, drains, vehicle locations and obstacles, and available closure windows. Specify oil and dust removal, water and waste collection, work-zone management and vehicle reopening sequence.


### Industrial site cleaning

Provide site plan and identify activity, production, storage and access zones and any residues. Request an assessment of protective equipment, cleaning methods, waste collection and segregation; unknown substances must not be mixed or handled before identification.


## Tank and Swimming Pool Cleaning


### Water tank cleaning

Specify tank type, location, capacity, access opening, isolation and draining method, noting last known cleaning. Confirm sediment removal, cleaning, rinsing, refilling and connection checks, documenting condition before and after service.


### Water tank disinfection

State tank type, water source, recommissioning method and disinfection purpose, and request product data, dosage, contact time and rinsing procedure. Document service steps and appropriate verification before water reuse; do not claim laboratory results that were not performed.


### Swimming pool cleaning

Provide pool dimensions and volume, lining type, water condition, filters and pump details, with equipment photographs. Define cleaning of floor, walls, waterline, baskets and filters, recording water readings, chemicals and operating condition after service.


## Pest and Rodent Control


### Cockroach control

Record sightings, frequency and kitchen, drain and storage locations, providing photographs or size and type descriptions. Request inspection, source assessment, treatment method and product, precautions and follow-up, addressing attractants such as water and food residue.


### Ant control

Map ant trails, entry points and possible nests, noting building type, food and moisture sources. Identify species and treatment points and protect food, surfaces and pets, with follow-up that addresses the colony rather than only the visible trail.


### Rodent control

Record activity signs, droppings or gnawing, entry gaps, stores and food sources, with photographs. Request inspection, exclusion work, trap locations, monitoring and safe disposal, including precautions for children and pets.


### Bed bug control

Identify affected rooms and furniture, onset and any movement of furnishings between sites. Photograph mattress seams and edges, confirm diagnosis and treatment for furniture and clothing, and agree isolation, cleaning and follow-up before reuse.


### Recurring pest-control contracts

Provide building plan, use type, storage, kitchen and waste zones and previous infestation records. Compare visit frequency, monitoring points, products, application logs, follow-up and complaint response, listing the exact Al Dhafra sites covered.


## Disinfection and Sanitisation


### Home disinfection treatment

Specify home area, high-touch and sensitive surfaces and the reason for treatment, with photographs. Request product identity, application and concentration, ventilation and re-entry time and precautions, protecting food, toys and pets.


### Office and facility disinfection

Provide facility plan, vacant hours, shared-use zones and sensitive surfaces and equipment. Request a plan listing areas, product, application method, service record and re-entry time, separating pre-cleaning from disinfection itself.


## Building Operations and Maintenance


### Maintenance management

Provide asset and system registers, locations, recurring faults and existing contracts, defining the maintenance manager's authority. Request fault intake, prioritisation, work orders, closure and monthly reporting, with clear owner, operator and contractor responsibilities.


### Facilities operations

Identify facility types, operating hours, users, common areas and systems requiring daily oversight. Request rounds, logs, emergency procedures, suppliers, stock and reporting, accounting for site separation and team travel time within Al Dhafra.


### Preventive maintenance

Provide equipment inventory and models, operating hours, manufacturer guidance and fault history, defining acceptable downtime. Schedule inspections, cleaning, lubrication, measurements and consumables per asset, recording completion and deviations.


### Electrical systems monitoring

Identify panels, circuits, generators and critical systems, inspection intervals and fault records, attaching available drawings. Request documented rounds and suitable readings, thermal or acoustic observations where useful, and referral of repairs to qualified technicians without exceeding safe operating limits.


### Air-conditioning systems monitoring

Provide unit inventory and locations, operating hours, setpoints and recurring faults. Maintain filter, temperature, pressure, drainage and cleaning records, identifying units needing specialist intervention or separately priced parts.


### Plumbing and drainage monitoring

Identify tanks, pumps, valves, inspection chambers and areas with odours or leaks, attaching network plans. Request checks of pressure, flow, leakage and overflow, recording location, action and materials used.


### Building condition reporting

State report purpose, buildings and areas covered and systems to assess, providing previous records and photographs. Request a photographic report classifying observations by location, priority, impact and action, distinguishing visual observations from technical inspections and tests.


## Staffing and Operational Support


### Cleaning staff provision

Specify work area, facility type, operating hours, shifts and daily tasks, identifying service locations within Al Dhafra. Confirm cleaners and supervisors, uniforms, products, equipment and absence cover, defining training, supervision and attendance records.


### Loading labour provision

State material types, weights, dimensions, trip count, floors and vehicle position, along with hours and handling equipment. Confirm worker and supervisor count, task allocation and protection, clarifying whether vehicles and lifting equipment are included.


### Maintenance technician provision

Specify required trades, systems, site count, shifts, tasks and authority limits, providing expected work or fault lists. Request task-relevant qualifications, experience, work logs, reports and parts, verifying any current professional requirements before assignment.


### Site supervisor provision

State site count and distances, operating hours, team sizes and work types to supervise. Define supervisor authority and attendance, quality, safety and coordination reports, including transport, communication and escalation when access is delayed.


### Residential community operations

Provide building and unit counts, common areas, gates, amenities, systems and service hours. Request operations for complaints, cleaning, maintenance, security and resident and supplier communication, defining operator scope versus owner or property management responsibilities.


### Commercial facility operations

Specify activity, operating hours, tenants, loading and service areas and critical systems, providing a facility plan. Request opening and closing, cleaning, maintenance, emergency and supplier procedures, with performance measures, incident logs and shift handovers.


### Project support services

Provide project team structure, work schedule, site locations and required labour, supervision or support services, stating assignment duration. Define roles, shifts, reporting, site equipment, transport and absence cover, separating fees, materials and expenses.
