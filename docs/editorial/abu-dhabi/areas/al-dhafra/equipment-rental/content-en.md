## Construction and Lifting Equipment Rental


### Scaffolding rental

Specify working height, façade length, hire period, ground conditions and anchorage points, providing site photographs and a plan. Confirm scaffold type, dimensions, load rating, erection, inspection and dismantling, including transport, labour and return responsibilities.


### Crane rental

State load weight and dimensions, lifting radius and height, crane standing area and access route, attaching a lift plan if available. Confirm crane type and capacity at the required configuration, operator and setup needs, exclusion zone, travel and waiting charges.


### Excavation equipment rental

Specify soil conditions, excavation dimensions and depth, available space, buried services and machine access. Confirm excavator and attachments, operator, working hours, transport and fuel, and define responsibility for locating services and protecting excavation limits.


### Compaction equipment rental

State layer area and thickness, material type and specified compaction requirement, and identify path widths. Compare roller or rammer type and operating weight, and clarify which compaction tests are performed by the site team and who supplies them.


### Concrete mixer rental

Specify batch size, production rate, operating period, power or fuel source and mixer location. Confirm usable capacity, drum condition, cleaning and transport, and state who supplies materials and operator and handles residual concrete.


### Concrete cutting equipment rental

Specify member thickness, concrete type, cut location and dimensions, power and water supply, with a section or photograph. Confirm saw or coring equipment, blade and attachments, slurry and dust containment, and verify reinforcement and services before use.


### Project equipment rental

Provide an equipment list and purpose, hire period, site location within Al Dhafra, operating hours and transport route. Confirm condition, maintenance, attachments, operator, fuel, insurance and breakdown and downtime responsibilities, separating hire from mobilisation.


## Light Tools and Equipment Rental


### Generator rental

List simultaneous loads, operating duration, fuel source and generator location relative to buildings. Confirm continuous and standby ratings, connection and protection, maintenance, fuel, cables, earthing and whether an operator is included.


### Cleaning equipment rental

State surface type and area, soil type, water and power supply and hire period, with site photographs. Confirm machine and attachments, compatible chemicals, operation, cleaning and maintenance, and responsibility for damage from misuse.


### Air compressor rental

Specify required air pressure and flow, connected tools, operating duration and compressor location. Confirm tank capacity, power source, accessories, filters and hoses, and check leaks, noise and condensate drainage at handover.


### Power tool rental

State task, material, tool type, accessory diameter or size, hire period and power source. Confirm model, condition, accessories, guards and operating instructions, inspecting cable, blade or bit and safety switch at collection.


### Welding equipment rental

Specify metal type and thickness, welding process, power source and work location, describing the joints. Confirm machine rating, leads, electrode holder or feed accessories and protection, stating whether consumables and operator are included.


### Water pump rental

State required flow and head, water type, solids content and suction and discharge locations. Confirm pump curve, hose diameters, power and dry-run protection, then test flow and connections before operation.


### Garden equipment rental

Specify work area, planting or surface type, task, hire period and storage point. Confirm machine and attachments, fuel or charging, blade condition and protection, including transport, cleaning and return condition.
