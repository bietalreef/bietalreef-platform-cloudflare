### Home furniture manufacturing

### Office furniture manufacturing

Provide office plan, workstation count, monitor and equipment dimensions and storage needs, stating work patterns. Specify worktops, edging, supports and cable management, and check stability and future reconfiguration.


### Hotel furniture manufacturing

Prepare a room and repeated-unit schedule with bed, table and wardrobe dimensions and intensive-use requirements. Approve a first-article sample and standard materials and hardware schedule, with batch production and checks for consistency across rooms.


### Restaurant furniture manufacturing

Specify table and chair quantities and dimensions, layout, service routes, storage and cleaning expectations. Request materials suited to repeated use and repairable components, then inspect seat heights, table stability and surface edges.


### Custom furniture manufacturing

Provide a dimensioned drawing or reference model, specifying materials, function, load, finish and moving parts. Review the design before production and document changes, then inspect the item against dimensions and approved sample before dispatch.


### Furniture repair and refurbishment

Photograph the item from all sides and identify faults in frame, hinges, upholstery or surface, noting approximate age if known. Request a diagnosis distinguishing repairable parts from replacements, and compare repair with full refurbishment before workshop transport.


### Project furniture supply

Provide room and item schedules, quantities, models, required dates and delivery locations within Al Dhafra. Identify stock versus made-to-order items, packing, storage, transport, lifting and installation, and approve a sample before batch acceptance.

### Curtain tailoring

### Curtain installation

### Upholstery fabric supply

Specify furniture type, quantity, colour, pattern, fabric width, wear and cleaning expectations. Request a sample from the actual batch and composition and abrasion and fading data, confirming enough matching fabric before upholstery begins.


### Carpet and fitted carpet supply

### Mattress supply
