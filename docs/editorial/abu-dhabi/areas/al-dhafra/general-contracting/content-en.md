# General Contracting, Building and Construction in Al Dhafra

This guide covers general contracting, building and construction enquiries across Al Dhafra. Al Dhafra is a geographically extensive region; travel distance, mobilisation time and equipment access must not be assumed to be the same for every site. State the relevant locality or settlement within the region and provide the project pin or coordinates, entrance and access photographs, unloading point, property details and available drawings. Inspection, feasibility and scheduling must be assessed against the actual site. This page does not confirm that a provider is listed, or that a price or appointment is available.

## Directory scope

This file covers only the company and establishment services in the source taxonomy. The professionals and tradespeople list remains separate and is not merged into this directory. Specialty and service names follow the source tree; restricted or non-selectable specialties are descriptive content only and are not represented as activated.


## Building and Villa Construction


### Villa construction

A villa enquiry should identify the plot, site plan, permitted building envelope, number of floors and required rooms. Attach available drawings and site photographs, then separate site preparation, foundations, structure, finishes and service connections in the scope.


### Residential building construction

For a residential building, provide plot information, unit and floor counts, parking requirements and available architectural and structural drawings. The quotation should explain quantity measurement, construction sequence and whether external works and utility connections are included.


### Commercial building construction

Commercial building requirements depend on the intended use, operational areas, loading arrangements and building services. Share the activity description, drawings and operator or tenant requirements, and ask each bidder to distinguish base construction, specialist fit-out and exclusions.


### General building works

When the request covers selected building works rather than a complete project, break it into measurable items such as blockwork, plastering, concrete or alterations. Mark each location and quantity, and state who supplies materials and removes waste.


### Concrete works

Identify the concrete element, dimensions, preparation, reinforcement and specified concrete using the project's approved drawings. The quotation should allocate responsibility for formwork, reinforcement, pouring, curing, testing and records rather than hiding them in one vague item.


### Structural frame construction

Pricing a structural frame requires review of structural drawings, reinforcement schedules, member levels and pour stages. Ask the contractor to define responsibility for labour, equipment, formwork, steel fixing, concrete placement and tests, including how variations are approved.


### Turnkey project delivery

For a turnkey project, define the final handover list instead of relying on a broad label. Specify finishes, equipment, lighting, external works, documents and commissioning. Compare bills of quantities, samples, warranties and exclusions, and require staged inspection records before final acceptance.


### New villa builds

For a vacant plot, document boundaries, levels, access points and any actual limits on storage or equipment movement. Request stage-based pricing tied to drawings, quantities and programme; do not treat a photograph or approximate area as a final measurement.


### Residential annex construction

An annex must be assessed in relation to the existing building, ground levels, service routes and connection points. Send a plan showing its position, dimensions and use, and ask which foundation, waterproofing, service and finishing works are included.


### External majlis construction

For an external majlis, state expected occupancy, seating area, washrooms, entrances and any parking provision. Photographs and a preliminary plan help define the scope, while dimensions and levels should be confirmed by site survey and coordination with the plot.


### Villa extensions

Before pricing an extension, identify the existing areas it will connect to and provide available drawings and photographs of junctions. Separate connection, protection, any required strengthening and finishing works; do not assume the existing structure is suitable without professional assessment.


### Residential building alterations

Describe the alteration precisely: wall removal, opening modification, room reconfiguration or entrance change. Attach an as-built plan and clear photographs, and ask the professional to identify any structural assessment or service coordination needed before pricing.


### Villa restoration

For villa restoration, collect photographs, locations, the time damage appeared and any previous repairs. Request an inspection that distinguishes the visible symptom from its cause, remedial work and reinstatement, including proposed materials and checks before surfaces are closed.


### Residential building renovation

State whether the renovation covers the whole residence or selected units and whether the building remains occupied. Provide a room-by-room work schedule and photographs, and request a plan for protecting occupied areas and sequencing demolition, deliveries, construction and cleaning.


## Restoration and Renovation


### Older building restoration

Record the building condition before removing anything: cracks, dampness, deteriorated finishes and visible roof or service defects. Ask for an inspection report distinguishing repairable items from those requiring structural or specialist assessment, then price each item separately.


### Villa renovation

For a villa renovation, list the rooms to be updated and identify doors, floors or fittings that will remain. Photograph each space and provide approximate dimensions; ask for separate removal, protection, supply, installation and recommissioning items, including furniture handling.


### Apartment renovation

For an apartment, include access rules, delivery periods and lift or corridor protection where applicable. Distinguish work inside the unit from any common-area work, and do not assume building-management approvals or arrangements are included in the contractor's price.


### Building rehabilitation

Rehabilitation may combine structural, building-services, façade and finishing repairs, so the condition must be surveyed before pricing. Request a photographic report, priority schedule and work stages, separating confirmed items from those that can only be assessed after opening up.


### Structural crack remediation

Do not price a structural crack from its appearance alone. Record its location, direction, approximate width and whether it changes over time. A qualified engineer should determine the cause and suitable monitoring or repair method, documenting the condition before and after treatment.


### Façade renovation

Provide multi-angle façade photographs, approximate heights, existing materials and the areas to repair or change. Ask about access and protection, substrate preparation, samples, adhesion and finish checks, and how work at different heights is measured.


### Building preparation for reuse

Before reuse, identify the intended activity, current room condition and services to retain or remove. Request checks of partitions, utilities, air-conditioning and service outlets, with a clear defects list rather than assuming the building is suitable for its new purpose.


## Demolition and Concrete Works


### Demolition works

Identify the elements to be removed, their limits and adjacent buildings, services or retained structures. Attach photographs and a plan; request a method for isolating the work area, protecting adjoining elements, sorting and removing waste, and checking any site-specific approvals beforehand.


### Building and annex removal

For a building or annex removal, state whether the work is complete or partial and identify where power, water and other services must be isolated. Request an inventory of items to remove and protect, waste-loading arrangements and the required site condition afterward.


### Concrete cutting and coring

Send a drawing showing the opening location, dimensions, member thickness and purpose. A specialist must review reinforcement and concealed services before work; specify slurry and dust collection, area protection and edge inspection after cutting.


### Foundation and footing pours

Foundation pricing needs structural drawings, levels, available ground information and reinforcement schedules. Separate excavation, replacement fill, formwork, reinforcement, pouring, curing and tests, with measurement and inspection records at each handover stage.


### Concrete roof slab pours

Specify slab type, area, thickness, openings and service zones from the structural drawing. Ask for formwork and propping arrangements, reinforcement, concrete supply, pour and curing plan, and tests; verify quantities against drawings and survey rather than estimates.


### Concrete wall construction

For a concrete wall, state height, thickness, length, openings, finish and relationship to existing elements. The quotation should follow reinforcement, formwork, pouring, curing and joint details, and explain how alignment and dimensions will be checked.


### Demolition waste removal

Estimate waste type and volume, collection point and truck access to the loading area. Clarify trip counts, loading, protection and cleanup after removal, and retain evidence that waste was delivered through the project's approved disposal route without assuming a particular facility.


## Landscape Design and Maintenance

### Home garden design

Start with a plan showing plot boundaries, entrances, seating areas, water and power points, and existing shade. Provide photographs taken at different times if possible, and request a design showing planting, irrigation, circulation and maintenance needs rather than a decorative image alone.


### Villa landscaping

For villa landscaping, divide the garden into use areas and identify trees, paving or fences to retain. Request a planting schedule with quantities and supply sizes, soil preparation and irrigation, and define responsibility for protecting buried services.


### Outdoor space design

Provide dimensions and levels for yards and paths, door positions and vehicle areas, then state the intended use of each zone. The design should address drainage, shade, lighting, materials, access and maintenance through measurable drawings before construction.


### Lawn installation

State lawn area, soil type, surface condition, irrigation source and drainage. Compare quotations by ground preparation, levelling, seed or turf type, soil layer, establishment coverage and initial watering schedule, not simply by price per square metre.


### Tree and shrub planting

Send preferred plant names or reference photographs and mark planting locations, bed dimensions and available soil depth. Ask for supplied plant sizes, root and staking requirements, irrigation and aftercare, with a horticultural specialist checking suitability for the actual site.


### Natural grass installation

Before installing natural turf, inspect soil, levels and drainage and measure the area accurately. The quotation should identify turf type and source, soil preparation, growing layer, joints and initial irrigation, plus how settlement or gaps will be addressed after establishment.


### Artificial grass installation

Measure the area, recording corners, slopes and drains, and request a sample showing pile height, density and texture. Compare base preparation, drainage layer, seam and edge details, fixing method, then inspect surface evenness and joint alignment at handover.


### Planting plants and trees

Attach a planting-location plan with plant counts and sizes, distinguishing new planting from relocated specimens. Specify soil, fertiliser, pits, staking and irrigation, along with an establishment follow-up period and a record of plant condition at handover.


### Tree pruning and trimming

Identify each tree and photograph its canopy, surroundings and nearby obstacles. Clarify the pruning type, achievable height, safety equipment and branch collection; avoid severe cutting and seek specialist assessment for large or hazardous limbs.


### Lawn maintenance

State lawn area, condition, yellowing or waterlogged spots and required service frequency. Separate mowing, edging, waste removal, irrigation checks and fertilising, and clarify whether pest treatment or replacement of damaged sections is included.


### Plant fertilising and treatment

Provide photographs of leaves, stems and soil, noting when symptoms began, watering practices and previous products. Request diagnosis before selecting fertiliser or treatment, with dosage, application method, precautions and indicators for monitoring improvement.


### Garden pest control

Describe the pest or damage, where it appears and how widely it has spread, with close photographs of affected plants. Ask for the control method, proposed products, precautions for children, pets and beneficial plants, and a follow-up plan to assess reduction.


### Garden cleaning

Define the area and waste type—leaves, branches, soil, pruning residues or work debris. State whether cleaning is one-off or recurring, where waste will be collected and how removed, and how plants and outdoor furniture will be protected.


### Garden maintenance contracts

Prepare an inventory of planted areas, irrigation systems, trees and lawns with approximate areas, then set visit frequency and seasonal needs. Compare included tasks, materials, labour, fault response and reporting, keeping extra works separate from the recurring fee.


## Irrigation Networks

### Irrigation system design

Provide the garden plan, planting locations, water source, available pressure and any existing control zones. Request a design showing zones, pipe diameters, filters, valves and watering rates, while allowing maintenance access and avoiding clashes with other works.


### Drip irrigation installation

Measure bed lengths, plant types, tree positions and water supply, then specify emitter layout, flow rates, filters and valves. At handover, test even water delivery and check for blocked emitters or leaking connections.


### Sprinkler installation

Send a plan of irrigation areas, obstacles and level changes, and identify water source and pressure. Compare spray radius, coverage overlap, head and valve types and controls, then test actual coverage to reduce dry patches and overspray beyond planted areas.


### Irrigation tanks and pump installation

State required storage capacity, demand rate, tank and pump location, pipe route and electrical supply. Request pump duty specifications, protection, controls and float switches, and verify tank support, leak testing and operation before acceptance.


### Irrigation network maintenance

Record zones receiving no water, areas with pooling or low pressure, and any known recent system changes. Request checks of valves, filters, emitters, pipes and controls, with a repair list and post-service test results for each zone.


### Automatic irrigation programming

Provide planting types, sun exposure zones, water source, current schedule and site operating windows. Ask for separate programmes for different zones and instructions for manual adjustment, then run a full cycle and confirm settings are saved.


### Irrigation network leak detection

Identify damp areas, pressure loss or unusual consumption and provide the network plan if available. Ask the technician to isolate the affected section and explain testing before excavation or replacement, then document the leak, repair and post-repair pressure or operating test.


## Shades and External Works

### Car shade installation

Provide parking dimensions, vehicle count, entry height, column and gate positions, and any known buried services. Compare frame, fabric or panels, anchorage, durability, maintenance and rainwater drainage, and request a column-setting drawing before fabrication.


### Pergola construction

Specify pergola dimensions, height, attachment to the building or ground and required shade. Request foundation and material details, finish, weather exposure and drainage provisions, plus a sample or connection drawing before fabrication approval.


### Outdoor seating areas

Draw the seating footprint, access paths, seating positions, utilities, shade and lighting, and attach site photographs. The quotation should cover ground preparation, paving, bases, seating, outdoor materials and drainage, distinguishing movable furniture from fixed construction.


### External fence installation

Measure boundary lengths and required height, marking gates, corners, ground levels and junctions. Specify material, thickness, post and footing details and finish, and clarify work limits and responsibility for confirming the property boundary before installation.


### Garden pathway construction

Define pathway route, width, level and connections to entrances and drains, including expected pedestrian or trolley use. Compare sub-base layers, materials, joints, edging and drainage falls, then inspect stability and water pooling at handover.


### Interlock paving installation

Provide paving area, boundary plan, door and drain levels, and expected vehicle loads. Specify paver thickness, sub-base, compaction, bedding sand, edging and laying pattern, then inspect level, unit stability and drainage after installation.


### Waterfalls and fountains

For a water feature, specify dimensions, location, supply and drainage, pump position and control panel. Request waterproofing and circulation details, maintenance access and electrical protection, then test leakage, operation, noise and cleaning access before handover.


## Quotation, comparison and handover

**Preparing a quotation request:** Provide the exact project location within Al Dhafra, property type, work description, drawings or measurements and photographs, available quantities, required materials or finish level, and a preferred start window if flexible. Ask for labour, materials, equipment, transport, waste removal, testing, exclusions and warranties to be itemised. If no suitable company is displayed, the same scope can be sent to Bait Al Reef customer service for completion and routing; receiving a request does not depend on a published company. **Handover:** Check work against drawings, quantities and approved samples, record defects with photographs and test reports, and do not accept hidden items or verbal variations.
