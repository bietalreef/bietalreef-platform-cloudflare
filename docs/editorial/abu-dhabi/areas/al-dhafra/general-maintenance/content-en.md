# General Maintenance and Finishes in Al Dhafra — Partial Draft

This file currently covers only the first two specialties—General Maintenance and Complete Finishing Works—within Al Dhafra's General Maintenance and Finishes sector. The sector is not yet complete; the remaining specialties will be added in subsequent updates. Because Al Dhafra is geographically extensive, provide the exact site location, coordinates and access conditions before a visit, transport or service scope is set.

## Current coverage

This version completes two of ten specialties and 21 of 105 services. It must not be treated as a complete sector directory.


## General Maintenance


### Routine villa maintenance

Prepare an inventory of systems to inspect—air-conditioning, electrical, plumbing, doors and waterproofing—and note previous service dates if known. Provide the villa's location within Al Dhafra and issue photographs, then request a visit schedule separating checks, consumables and works requiring separate approval.


### Routine building maintenance

State unit count, common areas, central systems and site access windows. The service scope should define inspection rounds, fault logging, agreed response windows and excluded spare parts, with actual travel distance to the site considered in service arrangements.


### Internal damage repairs

Photograph the damage from several angles and identify the room, affected area and known cause. Request an inspection separating removal of damaged material, correction of the cause and reinstatement of paint or finishes, including protection of furniture and floors and a record of the result.


### Door and window repairs

Identify door or window type, material, opening size and fault—rubbing, air leakage, broken glass or damaged hinges. Photograph labels or hardware if available and ask whether repair is feasible or partial or full replacement is the more practical option.


### Damp treatment

Record damp location, height, area and onset, noting whether it changes after rain, water use or air-conditioning operation. Ask for source investigation before paint removal or repainting, including cause testing, drying method, materials and post-treatment verification.


### Crack repairs

Photograph the crack with a scale and record its location, direction, approximate length and when it was noticed. If it affects a structural element or is widening, obtain specialist assessment before repair; surface cracks should be priced after identifying the cause, repair compound and finish reinstatement.


### Pre-handover building preparation

State the expected handover date, rooms and systems to inspect, and attach previous snag lists. Request a documented walk-through of doors, windows, leaks, lighting, sanitary fittings and finishes, separating repairs from cleaning and technical testing.


### Minor household maintenance

Combine small tasks in one request with photographs, approximate dimensions and each item's location, such as fixing a fitting, adjusting a hinge or repairing a visible leak. Ask about visit minimums, included materials and whether the listed jobs can be completed in one visit after inspection.


### Curtain installation

Measure each opening's width and height from the proposed fixing points, and specify track or pole type, curtain weight and wall or ceiling substrate. Photograph obstructions and air outlets; request bracket, anchorage and finish details and a movement test after installation.


### Shelf installation

Provide shelf width, depth, expected load, wall material and fixing position, with a photograph showing nearby services or switches. Fixings must suit the substrate and load; check level and bracket stability before placing items.


### TV mounting

Specify screen size and weight, bracket type, viewing height, power outlets and data point. Photograph the wall and identify its material; request a cable-route and drilling check before work, then test bracket security, tilt and movement.


### Fixture and accessory installation

Attach photographs and dimensions of the accessories, marking their positions and heights and identifying whether the substrate is tile, stone, timber or gypsum. Use fixings suited to the surface, protect finishes from cracking and inspect alignment and stability after installation.


### Furniture assembly

Provide the item name or package photograph, model number, unit count and assembly location, noting whether furniture is new or dismantled for moving. Clarify package handling and packaging removal, then check parts, joint stability, drawers and doors.


### Minor fault repairs

Describe the fault, when it occurs and whether it is intermittent or constant; provide a photograph or short recording if useful. Request diagnosis before replacing parts, including inspection cost, likely materials and whether another specialist trade is needed.


## Complete Finishing Works


### Villa finishing

State structural status, completed work, included rooms and required finish level, attaching drawings and door, flooring and lighting schedules. Request a bill of quantities, samples and approval stages, separating external works, kitchens, wardrobes and appliances if excluded.


### Apartment finishing

Provide the unit plan, areas, service condition and finish specifications, noting access and lift or corridor protection rules where applicable. The quotation should distinguish in-unit work from common areas and state material delivery windows and waste removal arrangements.


### Shop fit-out

Define shop activity, frontage, sales and storage areas, services, signage and equipment. Provide plans and site photographs; separate partitions, flooring, lighting, joinery and fixtures, identifying items that may require review or approval by site management or the competent authority.


### Office fit-out

Provide staff numbers, department layout, meeting rooms, data, power and cooling needs, identifying furniture and systems to reuse. Request coordinated construction drawings and a materials schedule covering acoustics, lighting, cable routes and maintenance access.


### Interior space renovation

Identify rooms to change and elements to retain, providing condition photographs and a dimensioned plan. Request a sequence protecting occupied areas and defining removal, alteration, finishing and cleaning, including whether phased work will reduce disruption.


### Decorative works execution

Gather detail drawings, samples, wall and ceiling dimensions, lighting and outlet locations before pricing. Specify materials, finishes, profiles, joints and fixing methods, and request a mock-up for items where the visual concept may differ significantly from the built result.


### Complete finishing handover

Prepare a room-by-room handover checklist for surfaces, doors, windows, sanitaryware, lighting, equipment and commissioning. Require defect closure and retesting, material and warranty documents and final drawings, recording any items outside the contract scope.


## Request, comparison and handover

**Quotation and handover:** Provide the property's location within Al Dhafra, condition photographs, measurements, item list and preferred work window. Ask for labour, materials, transport, visit, testing and exclusions to be itemised. If no company is listed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive the same request; a published company is not a prerequisite. At handover, inspect completed items, record defects and photographs, and test operation before closing the request.
