# General Maintenance and Finishes in Al Dhafra — Partial Draft

This file currently covers only the first two specialties—General Maintenance and Complete Finishing Works—within Al Dhafra's General Maintenance and Finishes sector. The sector is not yet complete; the remaining specialties will be added in subsequent updates. Because Al Dhafra is geographically extensive, provide the exact site location, coordinates and access conditions before a visit, transport or service scope is set.

## Current coverage

This version completes seven of ten specialties and 77 of 105 services. It must not be treated as a complete sector directory.


## General Maintenance


### Routine villa maintenance

Prepare an inventory of systems to inspect—air-conditioning, electrical, plumbing, doors and waterproofing—and note previous service dates if known. Provide the villa's location within Al Dhafra and issue photographs, then request a visit schedule separating checks, consumables and works requiring separate approval.


### Routine building maintenance

State unit count, common areas, central systems and site access windows. The service scope should define inspection rounds, fault logging, agreed response windows and excluded spare parts, with actual travel distance to the site considered in service arrangements.


### Internal damage repairs

Photograph the damage from several angles and identify the room, affected area and known cause. Request an inspection separating removal of damaged material, correction of the cause and reinstatement of paint or finishes, including protection of furniture and floors and a record of the result.


### Door and window repairs

Identify door or window type, material, opening size and fault—rubbing, air leakage, broken glass or damaged hinges. Photograph labels or hardware if available and ask whether repair is feasible or partial or full replacement is the more practical option.


### Damp treatment

Record damp location, height, area and onset, noting whether it changes after rain, water use or air-conditioning operation. Ask for source investigation before paint removal or repainting, including cause testing, drying method, materials and post-treatment verification.


### Crack repairs

Photograph the crack with a scale and record its location, direction, approximate length and when it was noticed. If it affects a structural element or is widening, obtain specialist assessment before repair; surface cracks should be priced after identifying the cause, repair compound and finish reinstatement.


### Pre-handover building preparation

State the expected handover date, rooms and systems to inspect, and attach previous snag lists. Request a documented walk-through of doors, windows, leaks, lighting, sanitary fittings and finishes, separating repairs from cleaning and technical testing.


### Minor household maintenance

Combine small tasks in one request with photographs, approximate dimensions and each item's location, such as fixing a fitting, adjusting a hinge or repairing a visible leak. Ask about visit minimums, included materials and whether the listed jobs can be completed in one visit after inspection.


### Curtain installation

Measure each opening's width and height from the proposed fixing points, and specify track or pole type, curtain weight and wall or ceiling substrate. Photograph obstructions and air outlets; request bracket, anchorage and finish details and a movement test after installation.


### Shelf installation

Provide shelf width, depth, expected load, wall material and fixing position, with a photograph showing nearby services or switches. Fixings must suit the substrate and load; check level and bracket stability before placing items.


### TV mounting

Specify screen size and weight, bracket type, viewing height, power outlets and data point. Photograph the wall and identify its material; request a cable-route and drilling check before work, then test bracket security, tilt and movement.


### Fixture and accessory installation

Attach photographs and dimensions of the accessories, marking their positions and heights and identifying whether the substrate is tile, stone, timber or gypsum. Use fixings suited to the surface, protect finishes from cracking and inspect alignment and stability after installation.


### Furniture assembly

Provide the item name or package photograph, model number, unit count and assembly location, noting whether furniture is new or dismantled for moving. Clarify package handling and packaging removal, then check parts, joint stability, drawers and doors.


### Minor fault repairs

Describe the fault, when it occurs and whether it is intermittent or constant; provide a photograph or short recording if useful. Request diagnosis before replacing parts, including inspection cost, likely materials and whether another specialist trade is needed.


## Complete Finishing Works


### Villa finishing

State structural status, completed work, included rooms and required finish level, attaching drawings and door, flooring and lighting schedules. Request a bill of quantities, samples and approval stages, separating external works, kitchens, wardrobes and appliances if excluded.


### Apartment finishing

Provide the unit plan, areas, service condition and finish specifications, noting access and lift or corridor protection rules where applicable. The quotation should distinguish in-unit work from common areas and state material delivery windows and waste removal arrangements.


### Shop fit-out

Define shop activity, frontage, sales and storage areas, services, signage and equipment. Provide plans and site photographs; separate partitions, flooring, lighting, joinery and fixtures, identifying items that may require review or approval by site management or the competent authority.


### Office fit-out

Provide staff numbers, department layout, meeting rooms, data, power and cooling needs, identifying furniture and systems to reuse. Request coordinated construction drawings and a materials schedule covering acoustics, lighting, cable routes and maintenance access.


### Interior space renovation

Identify rooms to change and elements to retain, providing condition photographs and a dimensioned plan. Request a sequence protecting occupied areas and defining removal, alteration, finishing and cleaning, including whether phased work will reduce disruption.


### Decorative works execution

Gather detail drawings, samples, wall and ceiling dimensions, lighting and outlet locations before pricing. Specify materials, finishes, profiles, joints and fixing methods, and request a mock-up for items where the visual concept may differ significantly from the built result.


### Complete finishing handover

Prepare a room-by-room handover checklist for surfaces, doors, windows, sanitaryware, lighting, equipment and commissioning. Require defect closure and retesting, material and warranty documents and final drawings, recording any items outside the contract scope.



## Gypsum and Decorative Works


### Gypsum ceiling installation

Specify ceiling area, existing height and positions of lights, air-conditioning, sprinklers or access panels. Provide a reflected ceiling plan and obstacle photographs; itemise framing, suspension, boards, joints and access openings, then inspect level and joint stability before painting.


### Gypsum partition construction

State partition height, length and thickness, and whether it will support doors, cabinets or equipment. Specify framing, board type, internal insulation, reinforcements and service routes; inspect edge anchorage, alignment and joint treatment before finishing.


### Cove and concealed lighting detail

Provide a section showing cove depth and height, light source position and maintenance route. Coordinate gypsum dimensions with the fixture, driver and ventilation, and test light distribution and component access before closing the recess.


### Gypsum decorative features

Define the feature's shape, dimensions and position, attaching a reference image or detail drawing and stating whether it connects to the ceiling or wall. Request a finish sample and fixing and joint details; inspect symmetry and surface smoothness before painting.


### Wood-look panel installation

Measure the cladding area and define grain direction, joints, edges and socket or door positions. Specify panel type, thickness, moisture performance, fixing and corner treatment, and review a colour sample under site lighting before confirming quantities.


### Marble-look panel installation

Provide wall and height measurements, openings and corners, and specify pattern and vein direction. Compare panel thickness, surface, weight and adhesive or mechanical fixing, requiring clear joint, edge and wet-area details.


### Gypsum repair and maintenance

Photograph the damaged area and identify whether the cause may be moisture, movement, impact or failed suspension. Inspect surrounding sections before closing, and define replacement limits, cause correction, joint tape, compound and repainting so the repair does more than conceal the symptom.


## Flooring and Wall Cladding


### Ceramic tile installation

State floor or wall area, tile size, laying pattern and joint width, and provide substrate photographs and door and drain levels. Specify surface preparation, adhesive, grout, cuts and skirting; inspect tile lippage, joint consistency and falls before handover.


### Porcelain tile installation

Identify porcelain type, dimensions, thickness, indoor or outdoor use and expected loads. Compare fixing method, compatible adhesive, joint size and cutting system; request a setting-out plan that avoids narrow slivers and coordinates finished levels at doors.


### Parquet flooring installation

Check substrate moisture and level, and specify parquet type, installation system and plank direction. Provide room areas and door positions; request underlay, perimeter gap and skirting details, including any manufacturer-required acclimatisation period.


### Vinyl flooring installation

Specify vinyl type, installation system, thickness and traffic class, and photograph the substrate and any joints or cracks. Require substrate preparation and seam and threshold details, then inspect adhesion and ensure there are no bubbles or raised edges.


### Interlock paving installation

Define paving area, intended use, gate and drain levels and vehicle movement, attaching a boundary plan. Specify sub-base, compaction, bedding sand, edging and pattern, then inspect unit stability, falls and drainage after installation.


### Wall cladding installation

Provide wall dimensions, substrate material, door and switch locations, and specify cladding type, weight and orientation. Require fixing, reinforcement, joint, corner and moisture-protection details, then inspect line alignment and edge junctions.


### Floor repair

Identify floor type, damage location and area, noting hollow sounds, settlement, cracking or detachment. Provide close and wide photographs; request cause assessment, matching tile availability, removal and reinstatement limits and substrate repair before closure.


## Electrical Works and Lighting


### Building electrical first fix

Provide architectural plans, furniture and appliance layouts, expected loads, building type and construction stage. Request point, outlet, panel and route drawings, separating first fix from supply, final installation and testing, and record cable routes before walls are closed.


### Wire and cable installation

Specify circuits, loads, distances, installation method and cable environment. Require cable sizes and specifications, containment routes, labelling and termination points, with continuity and insulation test results before energisation.


### Distribution board installation

Provide the load schedule, circuit diagram, board location and access and ventilation clearances. Specify board capacity, breakers, labels, protection and earthing, then verify circuit-to-schedule matching, wiring arrangement and inspection results.


### Socket and switch installation

Specify point count, mounting heights, intended use and furniture and appliance positions, with a marked plan or photographs. Compare product type, protection rating, finish and fixing, then test polarity, operation and faceplate stability.


### Electrical extensions for annexes

Provide the annex plan, connection distance, board location and new loads, noting any existing cable route. Request a review of supply capacity, protection and concealed or surface routing, with circuit testing before energisation.


### Electrical network rehabilitation

Describe recurring faults, network age and previous additions, and provide photographs of affected boards and circuits. Request an assessment of retained versus replaced components, with safe isolation, circuit labelling and load and insulation testing after rehabilitation.


### Electrical load assessment

Compile appliance ratings, operating periods, board schedule and any repeated breaker trips. Request documented load measurements and distribution review against circuit and supply capacity, with clear recommendations before adding equipment or circuits.


### Interior lighting installation

Specify room functions, ceiling heights, furniture layout and switch and control positions. Provide a luminaire schedule or product images; request circuit layout, colour temperature, glare and maintenance access, then test operation and lighting scenes.


### Exterior lighting installation

Provide façade, path and vehicle-entry plans and identify power points and exposure to water and dust. Compare fixtures, ingress protection, mounting, glare and beam direction, then inspect insulation, connections and night-time operation.


### Chandelier installation

Specify chandelier weight, diameter, ceiling height, suspension point and base type, providing product details and a ceiling photograph. Verify the support structure and suspension capacity before lifting, and agree access equipment, installation crew and fixing and connection tests.


### Concealed lighting installation

Provide ceiling or wall sections, recess dimensions and driver and control locations, defining the intended light effect. Require ventilation and maintenance access details and fixture distribution, then test uniformity and check for unintended hotspots or shadows.


### Garden lighting installation

Mark pedestrian routes, trees, seating areas and known irrigation or buried cable routes. Request circuit and luminaire locations with outdoor protection, and test insulation, earthing and any timers or sensors supplied.


### Motion sensor installation

State sensor purpose, coverage area, mounting height and connected lighting or system. Agree sensitivity, hold time and detection direction, then test under actual use conditions to reduce false activation and blind spots.


### Lighting fixture maintenance

List failed fixtures, quantities, heights and types, noting whether the fault appears to involve the lamp, driver or wiring. Provide label photographs if available; request compatible replacement parts and access arrangements, then test lighting after repair.


## Plumbing and Leak Detection


### Water supply pipework first fix

Provide bathroom, kitchen, tank and appliance layouts, identifying the supply source and proposed pipe routes. Specify materials, diameters, valves, isolation points and testing, and photograph routes before walls or floors are closed for future maintenance.


### Drainage pipework first fix

Mark sanitary fixtures, drains, outlet diameters, floor levels and connection routes. Request gradients, cleanouts and venting details, then test flow and leakage before concealing pipes and record their locations on the as-built plan.


### Sanitary fixture installation

List each fixture type and model, supply and waste points and installation heights, with photographs after initial finishing. Compare fixing, sealing, silicone, accessories and leak testing, not just the fixture price.


### Water tank installation

State tank capacity, material, location, height and delivery access, and describe the base and inlet, overflow and drain routes. Specify material, cover, float valve, connections and support, then inspect base stability, leakage and cleaning access.


### Water pump installation

Provide required flow and pressure, number of outlets, lift height and electrical supply, with photographs of the pump location. Specify duty curve, dry-run protection, vibration and noise isolation and valves, then test pressure and operation under a suitable load.


### Pipework rehabilitation

Identify pipe age, material, fault locations and previous repairs, providing plans or accessible-area photographs. Request an assessment distinguishing local replacement from full network renewal, with isolation, pressure testing and records of concealed joints before reinstatement.


### Drainage network maintenance

Describe blockages, odours or slow drainage, identifying affected outlets and recurrence. Request an inspection locating the obstruction and its cause, then test flow and ensure inspection covers are reinstated and the work area cleaned.


### Water leak detection

Record meter readings or changes if available, damp locations and when symptoms occur, noting whether leakage is continuous. Agree the detection method and inspection limits before opening finishes, and request a report identifying the likely area, confidence and next step.


### Pipe leak repair

Identify pipe material, leak location and access, providing photographs of the affected area and visible joints. Specify replacement length, fittings and materials, then pressure- or flow-test before closing the wall or floor and retain repair photographs.


### Tank leak detection

State tank type, material, capacity, location, water level and observed loss rate, distinguishing leakage from consumption. Request a suitable test of the tank body, fittings and supply lines, documenting findings before choosing a repair.


### Bathroom waterproofing

Provide bathroom dimensions, substrate photographs, drain and penetration locations, and state whether this is new work or remediation. Specify waterproofing system, substrate preparation, upstands, corners and penetrations, and the appropriate ponding or other test before tiling.


### Roof waterproofing

State roof area, falls, drains, existing membrane type, equipment and penetrations. Require substrate inspection, crack repair, edge and upstand details, membrane and protection layers, with a documented test before reinstating finishes.


### Water tank waterproofing

State tank type and substrate and whether it stores water for use, providing photographs of cracks, peeling or joints. Specify a waterproofing product suitable for the intended use with product documentation and curing instructions, then test filling and leakage before recommissioning.


### Moisture remediation caused by leaks

Do not repaint until the leak source is confirmed to have stopped. Record wet area, damage depth and affected materials, and request a drying plan, removal of deteriorated sections, treatment of salts or mould where present, and reinstatement after the substrate is verified dry.


## Air Conditioning and Refrigeration


### Split air-conditioning installation

State room area and height, solar exposure, occupancy, outdoor-unit location, pipe route and condensate drain. Request suitable capacity selection and details for insulation, bracket, power and commissioning, including condensate and cooling tests.


### Central air-conditioning installation

Provide architectural plans, zone areas, available heat-load data, equipment locations and service rooms. Request system capacity, air distribution, controls and maintenance design, separating equipment supply from ductwork, insulation, commissioning and testing.


### Concealed ducted air-conditioning installation

Specify ceiling void height, duct routes, service-unit positions, access panels and drainage. Request coordinated drawings for air-conditioning, lighting and gypsum, including air pressure, noise and access to filters and maintenance points.


### Air-conditioning refrigerant pipework

State system type, connection distance, height differences, pipe route and penetrations, providing a plan or route photographs. Specify pipe diameters, insulation, supports, physical protection and pressure and evacuation tests before charging the system.


### Air duct installation

Provide air-distribution plans, opening sizes, ceiling and service-clash locations, and specify duct material and insulation. Require suspension, joint, sealing and inspection-access details, then check leakage, vibration and air distribution during commissioning.


### Air-conditioning unit replacement

Provide existing unit data, capacity, electrical supply, installation-space dimensions and fault symptoms, with a nameplate photograph. Compare capacity, efficiency, pipe and control compatibility and warranty, and clarify removal and disposal of the old unit.


### Air-conditioning system commissioning and inspection

Provide unit and zone lists, system drawings and previous installation records if available. Request commissioning measurements for temperature, pressure, airflow, current and condensate drainage, with per-unit readings, defects and corrective actions.


### Air-conditioning unit cleaning

Identify unit type, location and quantity, noting weak airflow or odours and the last known cleaning. Specify cleaning of filters, coils, drain pan, fan and accessible components, protecting furnishings and electrical parts and testing operation afterward.


### Refrigerant recharge

Do not request a recharge without checking the cause of poor cooling or refrigerant loss; provide unit type, data and symptoms. Require leak inspection and repair where needed, using the refrigerant type and quantity specified for the unit, with post-charge readings recorded.


### Air-conditioning fault repair

Describe the fault, timing, error codes, unusual noise or leakage, and provide a nameplate photograph or short video. Request diagnosis identifying component and cause before replacement, followed by operational and performance tests.


### Air duct cleaning

State the number of units, ducts and zones with dust or odour concerns, and request an inspection of access openings and insulation. Agree cleaning and dust-containment methods, site protection, before-and-after photographs and a report of inaccessible sections.


### Central air-conditioning maintenance

Provide equipment inventory, operating hours, fault history and previous service results, stating whether work is urgent or scheduled. Request checks of filters, coils, fans, belts, pumps, controls and drainage, with readings and consumable parts recorded.


### Air-conditioning spare-parts replacement

Provide unit model and part number or a clear photograph, identifying the fault that prompted replacement. Confirm compatibility, part source and condition and warranty, retaining the removed part and recording the unit test after installation.


### Preventive air-conditioning maintenance contracts

Prepare a schedule of units, capacities, locations, operating hours and access conditions, and define visit frequency and response expectations. Compare inspections, cleaning, consumables, excluded parts, reporting and emergency coverage, including travel to the actual Al Dhafra sites.

## Request, comparison and handover

**Quotation and handover:** Provide the property's location within Al Dhafra, condition photographs, measurements, item list and preferred work window. Ask for labour, materials, transport, visit, testing and exclusions to be itemised. If no company is listed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive the same request; a published company is not a prerequisite. At handover, inspect completed items, record defects and photographs, and test operation before closing the request.
