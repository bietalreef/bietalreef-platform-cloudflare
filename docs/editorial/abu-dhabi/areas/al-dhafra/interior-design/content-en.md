# Engineering Consultancy, Design and Décor in Al Dhafra

This sector covers design and engineering consultancy enquiries across Al Dhafra, a geographically extensive region where the project location must be established before estimating site visits, surveys and coordination. Provide coordinates or a map pin, plot or building details, available drawings, current-condition photographs and the intended use and outcome. If documents are old or do not match the site, say so explicitly so design and measurement are not based on unverified information.

## Directory scope

Specialty and service names follow the source taxonomy. The professionals and tradespeople list is separate from this company and consultancy directory. Preparing a drawing, application or report does not mean an approval or licence has been issued; final requirements and decisions rest with the competent authority for the project and its location.


## Architectural Design and Drawings


### Villa design

Start with plot data, buildable limits, setbacks, levels and the required room, majlis and service programme. Provide the site plan, plot photographs and household or operational needs, then request comparable layout options showing areas, façades, ventilation and circulation.


### Residential building design

State unit count and types, service areas, parking and resident and visitor entrances. The proposal should explain space efficiency and relationships between units and services, identifying any site survey or multidisciplinary coordination needed before design freeze.


### Commercial building design

Provide the intended activity, operating hours, customer and delivery flows, storage and service needs. Compare options by spatial flexibility, loading access, circulation and service zones, and ask for the design assumptions before approving drawings.


### Architectural drawing preparation

Provide plot and existing-building measurements, property boundaries, levels and the functional brief. Agree the drawing register, scales, door and window schedules, sections and elevations, with a controlled record of revisions.


### Façade design development

Collect multi-angle photographs of existing façades and identify elements to retain or change, including available material preferences and budget range. Request material boards and details for openings, shading and lighting, checking that the design suits use, maintenance and construction.


### 3D modelling and visualisation

Supply current drawings or verified dimensions and define the required level of detail: exterior massing, interior spaces or close-up views. Agree views, materials, lighting, visualisation fidelity and revision rounds; a render is not a substitute for construction drawings.


### Existing drawing revisions

Provide the latest drawings, preferably in editable format, and mark each requested change on a copy or issue list. Request before-and-after comparisons and a revision log, explaining effects on areas, structure, services and façades.


### Permit drawing preparation

Identify project type, location, status and available documents, then ask the office to list the drawings and supporting documents it will prepare against current site requirements. Drawing preparation is separate from the authority's decision and does not guarantee acceptance or permit issuance.


### Building application submission support

Provide owner and site details, drawings, previous submissions and any available official comments. Define whether the office prepares, uploads, follows up or technically coordinates the application, and who handles fees and responses; the competent authority retains the decision.


### Approved drawing amendments

Do not revise from an obsolete copy. Supply the current approved drawings and identify the requested change, reason and location. Ask which drawings and review steps are affected, and verify the required project procedure before implementing any change that may affect an approval.


### Maintenance permit application assistance

Describe the maintenance work, location and any effect on structure, façade or services, attaching condition photographs and available drawings. The office may help prepare technical documents and follow up within its appointment, but permit issuance or exemption is determined by the competent authority, not promised by the service provider.


### Addition permit application assistance

Provide the as-built plan, proposed addition location, dimensions, intended use and connection to the existing building. Request an initial review of drawings, documents and site-specific approvals, separating professional fees and follow-up from authority fees and decisions.


### As-built drawing preparation

If old drawings do not match the building, define the areas to survey and arrange access to relevant rooms, façades and services. Request documented site measurements and drawings dated to the survey, with discrepancies noted instead of copying unverified legacy data.


### Government approval coordination

Provide the consultant with the authorities or reviews identified for the project and any available correspondence. Request a register showing each submitted document, status, comment and next action. This service must not be presented as guaranteed approval; requirements and decisions vary by project and location.


## Structural Design


### Reinforced concrete structure design

Provide architectural drawings, heights, occupancy, expected loads and available geotechnical information. Request the design assumptions, structural system, load paths and deliverable details, coordinating openings and services before issuing construction drawings.


### Foundation design

Foundation design needs ground investigation data, site levels, loads and column or load-bearing wall layouts. If a current report is unavailable, ask what information is required before pricing, and request the foundation type, assumptions and limits of the recommendation.


### Structural drawing preparation

State project stage, reference architectural revision and unresolved changes. Agree foundation, column, beam, slab, detail and schedule drawings, with revision numbering and a clear list of items requiring designer decisions before construction.


### Structural load review

Explain why the review is needed: an added floor, equipment, tank, change of use or visible deformation. Provide item location, weight, dimensions and available drawings; request a report stating assumed loads, data sources, limitations and any need for site inspection.


### Building structural safety assessment

Record symptoms, dates, locations and any previous incident or alteration rather than relying on a general façade photograph. Agree inspection scope and any non-destructive testing or sampling, and request a report separating observations, findings, urgent recommendations and assessment limitations.


### Structural strengthening design

Provide condition reports, original drawings, member dimensions, current loading and the proposed change. The design should specify strengthening method, anchorage, construction sequence and load monitoring, identifying any temporary support, evacuation or specialist supervision needed.


### Structural engineering reports

State the report purpose and the decision or recipient it is intended to support, and provide available documents, photographs and test results. Ask for inspection methodology, data sources, limitations, findings and recommendations, separating professional opinion from any formal approval required.


## Supervision and Engineering Consultancy


### Construction supervision

Define project duration and stages, required visit frequency, reporting format and the consultant's site authority. Agree hold points that must be inspected before concealment, and a process for recording and closing observations; supervision does not remove the contractor's responsibilities.


### Project management

Provide project scope, target budget, programme, existing contracts and stakeholders. Request management of time, cost, risk, changes, procurement and communication, with regular reporting on deviations and decisions needed rather than generic progress summaries.


### Work quality review

Identify items for review and provide specifications, samples, previous inspection records and construction photographs. The review should compare work with its reference documents, locate and grade defects, and define closure evidence; visual inspection alone does not prove concealed-work quality.


### Project stage inspections

Prepare the work list, stage drawings, test results and open-observation register. Request a documented inspection distinguishing accepted, rejected and reinspection items, with sign-off limited to the scope actually inspected.


### Progress reporting

Set reporting frequency, progress measurement rules and evidence sources such as completed quantities, photographs, programme and procurement. Reports should state completed and delayed work, causes, actions, owners and forecast dates rather than unsupported completion percentages.


### Quantity surveying and take-off

Provide current drawings, specifications and revision schedules, and state whether the take-off is for pricing, payment valuation or comparison. Require measurement rules, units, assumptions and unresolved items, linking each quantity to a drawing reference or location.


### Contractor quotation review

Submit quotations for the same scope with drawings, quantities, specifications and the enquiries sent to each contractor. Request a comparison of materials, labour, duration, exclusions, payments and warranties, identifying missing items instead of selecting the lowest headline total.


## Interior Design and Material Selection


### Majlis design

State occupancy, seating style, privacy needs, guest entrances, windows and air-conditioning positions. Provide wall and ceiling measurements and existing furniture photographs, then request a layout showing circulation, fabrics, lighting and power points before custom fabrication.


### Living room design

Explain daily room use, seating capacity, television and window positions and circulation routes. Request a layout testing furniture dimensions, viewing distance, door swings and movement, with a material palette suited to actual cleaning and use.


### Bedroom design

Provide room dimensions, doors, windows, power and air-conditioning points, and specify bed size and storage needs. The design should balance circulation around the bed, wardrobe access, lighting and privacy, with internal cabinet dimensions confirmed before fabrication.


### Kitchen design

Kitchen design requires accurate locations for water, drainage, gas or power, extraction and appliances. Provide corner and wall photographs with appliance dimensions, and request cabinet layouts, heights, worktop materials, ventilation and maintenance clearances before fabrication approval.


### Bathroom design

Record bathroom dimensions, drainage and supply points, doors, windows and floor levels. Request sanitaryware layout, door swings, waterproofing zones, falls and finishes, with access details for valves and traps to avoid unnecessary future demolition.


### Furniture layout

Provide a measured plan of doors, windows, columns and service outlets, with dimensions of existing or proposed furniture. Request circulation, use clearances and drawer and door swings rather than a plan that merely places furniture symbols in the room.


### Interior 3D visualisation

Specify rooms, views, camera angles, finishes and lighting to be shown, and provide plans and reference materials. Agree image count, detail level and revision rounds, checking dimensions against drawings because visualisation alone does not establish buildability.


### Office design

Provide staff numbers, work patterns, meeting, storage and equipment needs, and visitor circulation, identifying areas requiring privacy or acoustic separation. Request workstation, meeting-room, power, data, lighting and cooling layouts before purchasing furniture.


### Retail shop design

Define products, display method, customer journey, checkout, storage and receiving needs, and provide frontage, depth and height measurements. Compare display clarity, reconfigurable shelving, service routes and lighting, separating design fees from fixture fabrication.


### Restaurant and café design

Provide the operating zones—reception, seating, preparation, washing, storage and service—along with available utilities and extraction points. Request staff, goods and customer flow coordination, cleanable surface specifications and identification of drawings requiring competent-authority review before work.


### Salon design

State workstation count, washing, waiting, storage and privacy areas, and provide chair and equipment dimensions. Request a layout for water, drainage, power, lighting, ventilation and circulation, selecting surfaces that tolerate frequent cleaning.


### Clinic design

Start with service types and patient, staff, clean-supply and used-material flows, defining examination, reception and storage rooms. Ask the designer to document operational, privacy and cleanability requirements and coordinate with relevant specialists and authorities; a drawing alone does not establish licensing.


### Exhibition and showroom design

Define products, dimensions, display and demonstration methods, storage, visitor routes and loading access. Request a layout balancing entrance sightlines, circulation, lighting, signage and flexibility for changing displays, with fixture details.


### Reception area design

Provide entrance measurements, access routes, nearby lifts or corridors, reception point and adjacent services. Request a layout for visitor movement, waiting, privacy, counter position, lighting and materials suited to daily wear.


### Flooring selection

State room use, substrate condition, traffic, moisture and cleaning expectations, and provide photographs and area measurements. Compare samples for thickness, slip and wear performance, installation, joints and skirting, allowing for waste and spare stock.


### Paint colour selection

Photograph walls in daylight and under evening lighting, identifying furniture and floors to remain. Test sample patches on actual surfaces and review sheen, cleanability and coverage over the existing finish before confirming final quantities.


### Marble and ceramic selection

Specify application, required dimensions, veining or pattern, quantity and installation location. Compare samples and batches for colour, thickness, variation, finish, stain and slip performance, and inspect slabs or cartons from the same batch before purchase.


### Furniture selection

Provide room plans, entrance and lift dimensions and delivery routes, then state occupancy, use and budget. Compare frame, fillings, fabrics, maintenance and actual dimensions, confirming that pieces can reach the installation location before ordering.


### Lighting selection

Define each space's function, ceiling height, furniture positions and electrical circuits, attaching plan and ceiling photographs. Compare fixture type and distribution, colour temperature, controls, glare and maintenance access, requesting a trial or simulation for critical areas.


### Material board preparation

Bring proposed flooring, wall, worktop, timber, metal, fabric and lighting finishes into one room-linked material board. Request physical samples, product references, finishes and supply sources, documenting acceptable alternatives if a selected sample is unavailable.


### Finishes schedule preparation

Provide final drawings and room and elevation codes, defining the detail needed by contractors and suppliers. The schedule should record material, size, colour, finish, location, reference quantity and installation method, with revisions controlled between issues.


## Commissioning, comparison and handover

**Preparing a quotation request:** State the exact project location within Al Dhafra, project type and stage, requested services, available files and any known reviewing body. Ask for site-visit count, stage deliverables, file formats, revision rounds and excluded fees. If no suitable office is displayed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive the same information, complete the request and route it without requiring a published office. **Comparison and handover:** Compare scope, deliverables, assumptions, programme, coordination and revision responsibilities, then receive the drawing and report register, revision history and usable source files.
