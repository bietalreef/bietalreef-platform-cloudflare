# Metal, Aluminium, Glass and Joinery Workshops in Al Raha

## Scope of this guide

This sector covers made-to-measure fabrication, supply, installation and maintenance for joinery, aluminium, glass and metalwork. A reliable quotation depends on opening measurements taken after finishes, shop drawings, material and hardware specifications, fixing details and the route for bringing items into the property. In Al Raha, coordinate surveys, fabrication, delivery and installation with property management where necessary, particularly for oversized items, service-lift use or façade work; procedures should not be assumed to be the same in every building.

Company and establishment listings remain separate from individual tradespeople. If no suitable workshop is displayed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request containing item type, dimensions, photos, material and finish, quantity, drawings or sample, installation location, access and lifting requirements. A request does not confirm a workshop, price or appointment.

## Companies and establishments

### Wooden kitchens, cabinets and doors

#### 1. Wooden kitchen design
Design starts with measuring walls, corners, water and electrical points, extraction and appliances, then planning storage and door clearances. Provide photos, a plan and appliance dimensions. Request a layout showing materials, hardware and ventilation, and approve appliance openings before fabrication.
#### 2. Kitchen fabrication
Fabrication needs final shop drawings for each unit, board thickness, fronts, edges, hinges and runners. Provide approved measurements, colour sample and use requirements. Compare core and face materials, hardware and warranty; inspect dimensions, colour, edge quality and unit assembly before delivery.
#### 3. Kitchen installation
Installation follows checks of wall and floor levels, service locations and appliance heights. Provide unit drawings, site photos and the delivery route. Specify fixing, sealing and adjustments. Accept after checking alignment, doors and drawers, cabinet stability and access to service points.
#### 4. Wardrobe design
Design should account for room dimensions, ceiling height, door swing, circulation and actual storage needs. Provide wall measurements, obstacle photos and dimensions of larger clothing or luggage. Request an internal layout for shelves, hanging and drawers, and review drawings before cutting.
#### 5. Wardrobe fabrication
Fabrication requires a cut list defining dimensions, materials, fronts, edges, backs and hardware. Provide approved drawings, colour code and hinge type. Compare board thickness, sliding system and assembly method; inspect door alignment, even gaps and edge quality before dispatch.
#### 6. Walk-in wardrobe fit-out
Measure clear floor area, unit depth, circulation, lighting, ventilation and socket locations. Provide a plan, photos and storage requirements. Request drawings for shelves, drawers, hanging, mirrors and lighting. Accept after checking access, unit stability and door clearances.
#### 7. Kitchen and cabinet repair or renovation
Identify which hinges, runners and fronts can be repaired and which need replacement, and inspect moisture damage or swelling. Send close-up photos, hardware models and door dimensions. Separate repair from full renovation in the quote. Test drawer and door movement and front alignment after work.
#### 8. Wooden door fabrication
Specify opening size, wall thickness, handing, core, facing, frame and lock. Provide post-finish opening measurements, frame photos and a reference sample. Request a drawing showing hinges and edges; inspect dimensions, leaf straightness and finish before installation.
#### 9. Door installation
Check opening dimensions, frame alignment, finished floor level and clear swing path. Send door, frame and hardware photos and state whether the old door must be removed. Accept after testing opening, closing, lock, gaps and floor clearance.
#### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication
Request documented specifications for the door, components, size and intended rating; appearance alone does not establish fire performance. Provide the door schedule, openings and project requirements. Request product documents and compatible frame and hardware details, and verify compliance with the consultant or relevant authority before purchase and installation.
#### 11. Door-frame fabrication
Measure wall thickness, opening, finished floor level, door type and hinge requirements before making the frame. Provide wall sections and corner photos. Request fixing and packing details; check frame alignment, squareness and anchoring before hanging the leaf.
#### 12. Wooden partition fabrication
Define height, width, privacy, ventilation and whether the partition is fixed or demountable. Provide the space plan, floor and ceiling photos and service locations. Request base, connection and edge details; inspect stability, alignment and access and circulation clearances.
#### 13. Door maintenance
Identify whether the problem is at the hinges, lock, frame, swollen timber or floor clearance. Send a movement video and close-up photos. Request diagnosis before replacing parts; test closing, locking and smooth movement and confirm that rubbing does not return.
#### 14. Wood painting and refurbishment
The method depends on timber type, old coating, surface condition and whether the aim is a colour change or renewed protection. Provide close-up photos and a colour sample. Request a local trial and details of sanding, primer and coats; inspect colour consistency, edges and dryness before reuse.

## Requesting a quotation when no workshop is listed

Send Bait Al Reef customer service the item description, dimensions, photos, quantity, material, finish, drawings or sample, installation location, required date and access or lifting restrictions. State whether the request is for design, fabrication, supply, installation or maintenance. No supplier, price or appointment should be assumed until the request is checked.

## Individual tradespeople — separate

The source lists skills in joinery, iron and metalwork, aluminium and glass. Listing a skill does not establish that a tradesperson is available or that a workshop is approved; verify experience, identity and scope of responsibility before contracting.

## Questions before approving fabrication

- Are measurements final after tiling, painting and ceiling works?
- Do drawings show openings, hinges, handing, clearances and hardware?
- Does the price include lifting, transport, protection, installation and cleanup?
- What documents or samples support the material specification?
- What acceptance test, warranty and exclusions apply?

## Review boundaries

This guide does not certify products or workshops and does not interpret safety or permit requirements. Verify any special rating, mandatory specification or façade consent with the relevant authority, property management and consultant before fabrication or installation.

### Wood furniture and decor

#### 15. Custom furniture fabrication
Start with site dimensions, item function, expected load, timber or board type and finish. Provide a sketch or reference image and measurements of corridors and doors. Request shop drawings and a colour sample; compare joints, edges, hardware and warranty, then inspect dimensions and stability before handover.
#### 16. TV console fabrication
Define the console against screen and equipment dimensions, ventilation openings, cable routes and loads. Provide equipment sizes, socket locations and a wall photo. Request shelf and opening drawings; check stability, cable access and that ventilation is not blocked.
#### 17. Storage-unit fabrication
List stored items, their weights and access needs, and review door, drawer and opening clearances. Provide internal and external dimensions and site photos. Request a layout showing shelves and supports; test drawers, doors and shelf loading against the agreed use.
#### 18. Wooden wall cladding
Begin with a layout showing panel divisions, grain direction, edges, openings and electrical outlets. Provide wall dimensions, photos and service locations. Request a sample and framing and fixing details; inspect joint alignment, colour consistency and access to maintenance points.
#### 19. CNC decorative work
Digital cutting requires an executable design file defining material thickness, piece dimensions, edge treatment and fixing. Provide the available drawing file, board size and sample requirement. Request a prototype for complex patterns; inspect detail accuracy and edges for burning or chipping and verify piece fit.
#### 20. Counter fabrication
Measure counter area, height, depth, equipment and cable positions, storage needs and front treatment. Provide plans, photos and surface specification. Request joint and opening drawings; inspect level, frame stability, working edges and ease of cleaning.
#### 21. Shop and office furniture fabrication
Define function, user count, equipment dimensions, circulation and storage before fabrication. Provide the space plan, item schedule and brand references. Request material and colour samples; compare durability, maintenance and demountability, then inspect each item in its intended position.
#### 22. Interior-space design
Design coordinates furniture, cabinets, circulation, lighting and electrical points rather than treating each item separately. Provide a dimensioned plan, photos and user requirements. Request a layout and elevations or 3D model; review dimensions and openings before fabrication approval.
#### 23. Joinery fabrication and installation
This scope combines drawings, measurement, fabrication, transport and installation; responsibility for each stage should be stated. Provide an item list, installation location and samples. Separate material, workshop and installation costs. Accept against drawings and inspect joints, fixing and cleanup.
#### 24. Majlis fit-out
Measure walls and entrances and plan seating, storage, cladding and integrated lighting. Provide a plan, photos and required furniture dimensions. Request drawings showing dimensions and service openings; inspect symmetry, usability, cladding stability and maintenance access.
#### 25. Bedroom fit-out
Plan units around the bed, circulation, wardrobes, bedside tables, lighting and sockets. Provide room dimensions, bed model and door and window photos. Request a layout showing clearances and storage; check that drawers and doors do not clash and that units are stable.
#### 26. Office and retail fit-out
Inventory workstations, counters, display and storage units and customer or staff circulation. Provide an operational layout, equipment dimensions and user count. Request installation drawings and delivery sequence; inspect stability, safe edges and unobstructed circulation.
#### 27. Wooden decorative features
Translate the concept into details for cladding, battens, edges, joints and transitions to walls and ceilings. Provide a visual reference, site measurements and timber sample. Request a mock-up of repeated details; accept after checking alignment, fixing, colour and texture consistency.
#### 28. Interior joinery finishing and handover
Use this to close an installed joinery package against the item list, drawings and hardware schedule. Prepare room-by-room photos and snag notes. Request repair of scratches, joints and adjustments before handover; record accepted items, defects, warranties and open work.
### Aluminium doors and windows

#### 29. Aluminium window fabrication
Fabrication requires post-finish opening measurements and a defined profile system, glazing, colour, handing and divisions. Provide opening photos, dimensions and façade plans. Request shop drawings and gasket specifications; inspect dimensions, finish and sash movement before delivery.
#### 30. Aluminium window installation
Check opening alignment and level, fixing method and perimeter sealing before installation. Provide frame and glass details and site photos, and clarify removal of old units and making good. Test opening, closing, locks, drainage and perimeter joints.
#### 31. Aluminium door fabrication
Specify opening width and height, profile depth, infill or glass, lock and hinges. Provide handing, drawings and sample photos. Request leaf-weight and reinforcement details; inspect assembly or weld quality and door alignment before approval.
#### 32. Aluminium door installation
Installation requires checks of levels, swing path, fixing points, threshold and closing action. Provide opening, door and hardware photos. Specify sealing and insulation. Accept after testing movement, locking, friction, frame stability and edge sealing.
#### 33. Insect-screen installation
Measure each opening and define frame type, removal and cleaning method and mesh durability. Provide window photos and dimensions and state whether fixed or retractable screens are required. Check mesh tension, frame stability, ease of operation and gaps at edges.
#### 34. Door and window maintenance
Start by identifying track, hinge, lock, gasket or air and water ingress problems. Send a movement video and hardware photos. Request diagnosis distinguishing adjustment from part replacement; test operation, closure and sealing after service.
#### 35. Hardware and glass replacement
Identify profile system, hardware model, glass thickness and dimensions and fixing method before replacement. Provide label and damaged-part photos and opening measurements. Confirm replacement compatibility. Inspect glass fixing and edges and test door or window movement after work.
#### 36. Aluminium kitchen fabrication
Design around wall measurements, services, appliances, worktop heights and door finish. Provide a plan, photos and appliance models. Request cabinet layout, profile and hardware details. Compare profile thickness, closing system and durability, then inspect dimensions and assembly.
#### 37. Aluminium kitchen installation
Check floor and wall levels, water and electrical points and appliance openings before installation. Provide unit drawings and site photos. Specify fixing, sealing and adjustments. Inspect alignment, cabinet stability, doors and drawers and access to service points.
#### 38. Aluminium cabinet fabrication
Define use, dimensions, shelves, doors, ventilation, load and whether the cabinet is indoors or outdoors. Provide site photos and contents list. Request drawings showing profiles, back and edges; inspect door alignment, shelf stability and edge durability.
#### 39. Bathroom cabinet fabrication
Measure the space around the basin, drain pipes, moisture exposure and door swing. Provide photos, dimensions and basin model. Specify suitable material and edge treatment; check door clearance and access to isolation valves.
#### 40. Outdoor storage-unit fabrication
Define exposure to sun, water and dust, ventilation, load and fixing method. Provide site dimensions and floor and façade photos. Specify profiles, coating and hinges. Check base stability, door sealing, drainage and maintainability.
#### 41. Aluminium kitchen refurbishment
Inventory which frames, doors and worktops can be retained and which need replacement. Provide close-up photos, cabinet dimensions and fault notes. Separate door-front replacement from frame replacement in the quote; inspect hardware compatibility and final adjustment.
#### 42. Kitchen hinge and accessory maintenance
Identify hinge or runner type and model, quantity and closing or movement fault. Provide fitting and location photos. Request a compatible replacement rather than a merely similar size. Test door and drawer movement and adjustment and check screw security.
### Glass façades and glazing

#### 43. Glass façade works
The project needs elevations, panel dimensions, support and joint systems, drainage and movement details. Provide drawings, site photos and proposed glass type. Request fixing, loading and technical-review details. Fabrication should wait for drawing approval; accept after checking alignment, joints and weather sealing.
#### 44. Toughened-glass installation
Define application, panel size and thickness, processed edges, cut-outs and hinge or fixing positions. Provide opening measurements and frame photos. Request glass specification and product documents; inspect edges for damaging cracks or chips, panel stability and hardware compatibility.
#### 45. Shopfront façade works
A shopfront should account for opening width, door movement, signage, fixing points and adjacent finishes. Provide façade dimensions and the intended business layout. Request shop drawings and frame and glass materials. Check door operation, façade alignment and clearance from circulation routes.
#### 46. Glass-door installation
Select the door by glass thickness and weight, hinge type, floor or frame support and lock system. Provide opening dimensions and fixing-point photos. Specify hardware and safe clearances. Test door movement, return action, lock, hinge stability and edge clearance.
#### 47. Glass partition installation
Partitions need a layout showing height, divisions, doors, privacy level and glass type or treatment. Provide room dimensions and ceiling and floor photos. Request base, connection and gasket details. Inspect alignment, panel stability and the acoustic performance expected by the specification.
#### 48. Glass balustrade installation
Define the guarding line, height, panel lengths and whether fixing is through the floor or side-mounted. Provide site sections, edge and stair drawings. Request technical review of the support and glass system. Do not approve installation until design suitability for safety is checked; accept after testing stability and connections.
#### 49. Glass façade maintenance
Inspect panels, gaskets, sealant, supports, drainage and locations of leakage or vibration. Provide inside and outside photos, location and height. Specify safe access and repair scope. Accept with a record of treated areas, sealing checks and inspection of adjacent panels.
