# Metal, Aluminium, Glass and Joinery Workshops in Al Raha

## Scope of this guide

This sector covers made-to-measure fabrication, supply, installation and maintenance for joinery, aluminium, glass and metalwork. A reliable quotation depends on opening measurements taken after finishes, shop drawings, material and hardware specifications, fixing details and the route for bringing items into the property. In Al Raha, coordinate surveys, fabrication, delivery and installation with property management where necessary, particularly for oversized items, service-lift use or façade work; procedures should not be assumed to be the same in every building.

Company and establishment listings remain separate from individual tradespeople. If no suitable workshop is displayed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request containing item type, dimensions, photos, material and finish, quantity, drawings or sample, installation location, access and lifting requirements. A request does not confirm a workshop, price or appointment.

## Companies and establishments

### Wooden kitchens, cabinets and doors

#### 1. Wooden kitchen design
Design starts with measuring walls, corners, water and electrical points, extraction and appliances, then planning storage and door clearances. Provide photos, a plan and appliance dimensions. Request a layout showing materials, hardware and ventilation, and approve appliance openings before fabrication.
#### 2. Kitchen fabrication
Fabrication needs final shop drawings for each unit, board thickness, fronts, edges, hinges and runners. Provide approved measurements, colour sample and use requirements. Compare core and face materials, hardware and warranty; inspect dimensions, colour, edge quality and unit assembly before delivery.
#### 3. Kitchen installation
Installation follows checks of wall and floor levels, service locations and appliance heights. Provide unit drawings, site photos and the delivery route. Specify fixing, sealing and adjustments. Accept after checking alignment, doors and drawers, cabinet stability and access to service points.
#### 4. Wardrobe design
Design should account for room dimensions, ceiling height, door swing, circulation and actual storage needs. Provide wall measurements, obstacle photos and dimensions of larger clothing or luggage. Request an internal layout for shelves, hanging and drawers, and review drawings before cutting.
#### 5. Wardrobe fabrication
Fabrication requires a cut list defining dimensions, materials, fronts, edges, backs and hardware. Provide approved drawings, colour code and hinge type. Compare board thickness, sliding system and assembly method; inspect door alignment, even gaps and edge quality before dispatch.
#### 6. Walk-in wardrobe fit-out
Measure clear floor area, unit depth, circulation, lighting, ventilation and socket locations. Provide a plan, photos and storage requirements. Request drawings for shelves, drawers, hanging, mirrors and lighting. Accept after checking access, unit stability and door clearances.
#### 7. Kitchen and cabinet repair or renovation
Identify which hinges, runners and fronts can be repaired and which need replacement, and inspect moisture damage or swelling. Send close-up photos, hardware models and door dimensions. Separate repair from full renovation in the quote. Test drawer and door movement and front alignment after work.
#### 8. Wooden door fabrication
Specify opening size, wall thickness, handing, core, facing, frame and lock. Provide post-finish opening measurements, frame photos and a reference sample. Request a drawing showing hinges and edges; inspect dimensions, leaf straightness and finish before installation.
#### 9. Door installation
Check opening dimensions, frame alignment, finished floor level and clear swing path. Send door, frame and hardware photos and state whether the old door must be removed. Accept after testing opening, closing, lock, gaps and floor clearance.
#### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication
Request documented specifications for the door, components, size and intended rating; appearance alone does not establish fire performance. Provide the door schedule, openings and project requirements. Request product documents and compatible frame and hardware details, and verify compliance with the consultant or relevant authority before purchase and installation.
#### 11. Door-frame fabrication
Measure wall thickness, opening, finished floor level, door type and hinge requirements before making the frame. Provide wall sections and corner photos. Request fixing and packing details; check frame alignment, squareness and anchoring before hanging the leaf.
#### 12. Wooden partition fabrication
Define height, width, privacy, ventilation and whether the partition is fixed or demountable. Provide the space plan, floor and ceiling photos and service locations. Request base, connection and edge details; inspect stability, alignment and access and circulation clearances.
#### 13. Door maintenance
Identify whether the problem is at the hinges, lock, frame, swollen timber or floor clearance. Send a movement video and close-up photos. Request diagnosis before replacing parts; test closing, locking and smooth movement and confirm that rubbing does not return.
#### 14. Wood painting and refurbishment
The method depends on timber type, old coating, surface condition and whether the aim is a colour change or renewed protection. Provide close-up photos and a colour sample. Request a local trial and details of sanding, primer and coats; inspect colour consistency, edges and dryness before reuse.

## Requesting a quotation when no workshop is listed

Send Bait Al Reef customer service the item description, dimensions, photos, quantity, material, finish, drawings or sample, installation location, required date and access or lifting restrictions. State whether the request is for design, fabrication, supply, installation or maintenance. No supplier, price or appointment should be assumed until the request is checked.

## Individual tradespeople — separate

The source lists skills in joinery, iron and metalwork, aluminium and glass. Listing a skill does not establish that a tradesperson is available or that a workshop is approved; verify experience, identity and scope of responsibility before contracting.

## Questions before approving fabrication

- Are measurements final after tiling, painting and ceiling works?
- Do drawings show openings, hinges, handing, clearances and hardware?
- Does the price include lifting, transport, protection, installation and cleanup?
- What documents or samples support the material specification?
- What acceptance test, warranty and exclusions apply?

## Review boundaries

This guide does not certify products or workshops and does not interpret safety or permit requirements. Verify any special rating, mandatory specification or façade consent with the relevant authority, property management and consultant before fabrication or installation.
