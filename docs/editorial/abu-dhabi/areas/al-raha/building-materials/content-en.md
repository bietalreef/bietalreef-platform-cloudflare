# Building Materials & Supply in Al Raha

## Scope of this guide

This sector helps define construction and finishing materials for a property or project in Al Raha, from small purchases to phased deliveries tied to a work programme. Prepare one comparable bill of quantities stating specification, size, quantity, unit, delivery point and required date; attach drawings or product photos where a specific item is intended. Confirm vehicle access, unloading, storage and protection of common routes with property management or the site representative where necessary; restrictions should not be assumed to be identical across buildings.

Company and establishment listings are separate from individual tradespeople. If no suitable supplier is displayed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request without requiring a supplier to be published first. State the material, specification, quantity, acceptable alternatives, brand or origin if fixed, delivery point, unloading arrangements and required date. A request does not confirm price, stock or delivery availability.

## Companies and establishments

### Building and finishing materials
**Source note:** This specialty is marked as currently non-selectable in the taxonomy source. Its editorial content does not change or activate that classification.

#### 1. Cement supply
Specify cement type and grade, packaging, quantity and expected consumption, and whether bags or bulk delivery are appropriate for the site. Request production date, storage conditions and delivery timing. On arrival, check bag condition, product identification and delivered quantity.
#### 2. Sand supply
State intended use, sand type, cleanliness requirement, quantity and unit; mortar sand may not be interchangeable with fill or other applications. Attach the project specification or a sample photo where useful. Compare source, transport, measurement method and unloading arrangements.
#### 3. Aggregate supply
Define grading, intended use, quantity and delivery method; a generic “aggregate” description is insufficient where a specific size is required. Attach the specification clause or mix data if available, and agree whether loads are measured by weight or volume and where they will be tipped.
#### 4. Brick and block supply
Specify unit type, dimensions and required strength where stated, with quantities allowing for openings and waste. Provide wall plans or a take-off schedule. Compare dimensions, consistency, packaging and transport; inspect breakage, count and size on receipt.
#### 5. Steel supply
The request should state steel type, diameters, lengths, grade, quantity and any cutting schedule. Attach the reinforcement schedule or diameter list. Request product identification, weight information and transport protection; check markings, diameters and quantities before the material enters the works.
#### 6. Ready-mix concrete supply
Specify concrete strength, slump, pour volume, required time, pump location and truck access. Provide the mix specification, pour quantity and access plan. Agree truck sequence, sampling and required records; document arrival and pour times and the agreed test results.
#### 7. Project construction-material supply
Use a consolidated schedule linking each material to its work item, quantity, required date and storage point rather than a vague general request. Attach drawings or bills of quantities and list acceptable alternatives. Compare compliance, phased delivery, transport, unloading and returns policy.
#### 8. Gypsum supply
Clarify whether the order is for gypsum powder, boards, jointing or finishing materials, with quantity, application and storage conditions. Provide system details or board dimensions and thickness. Request compatible accessories; inspect dry packaging, board condition and unbroken edges.
#### 9. Paint-material supply
State substrate, interior or exterior use, primer and coat system, colour and quantity. Attach the finish schedule or colour code. Compare coverage, drying time, cleanability and layer compatibility; check batch numbers, expiry dates and container condition on delivery.
#### 10. Waterproofing-material supply
Define the location, purpose, system type, substrate and auxiliary layers; roof, bathroom and tank products are not automatically interchangeable. Provide specifications, work area and detail photos. Request product data sheets, storage and compatibility information, then check packaging, dates and quantities before use.
#### 11. Flooring supply
Specify flooring type, size, thickness, finish, grade and quantity including waste allowance and batch number if available. Provide a layout or sample photo. Compare grading, packaging, transport and availability of spare material from the same batch; inspect breakage and colour and size variation.
#### 12. Sanitaryware supply
Prepare a list of models, dimensions, connection points and accessories for each bathroom or kitchen. Attach product schedules and room photos. Compare warranty, accessories and compatibility with existing pipework. On receipt, check model, colour, quantity and ceramic condition.
#### 13. Electrical-supply materials
Identify cable, breaker, switch or accessory type, size and rating against the project design. Attach load schedules, drawings and product references. Do not substitute a component based only on appearance without technical approval. Check labels, dimensions, quantity and packaging.
#### 14. Plumbing-supply materials
Specify pipe material, diameter and class, fitting and valve types, quantities and the system in which they will be installed. Provide network drawings and a material schedule. Compare component compatibility, warranty and transport method; inspect sizes, markings and damage before installation.
#### 15. Building-material retail sales
For a store purchase, provide a defined list of materials, sizes and quantities rather than asking for “building materials” generally. Request unit prices, tax, delivery, exchange terms and batch availability, then reconcile the invoice and actual quantities on receipt.
#### 16. Sanitaryware retail sales
Identify the item, model, colour, connection dimensions and accessories, and provide a photo or product number to avoid unsuitable alternatives. Compare whether valves, hoses, fixings and warranty are included. Inspect the product before leaving the store or accepting delivery.
#### 17. Electrical-products retail sales
Define use, technical rating, power, dimensions and compatibility with the existing circuit or equipment. Provide a data-plate photo or model number when buying a replacement. Request product data and warranty; do not approve an alternative before confirming suitability. Check labels and packaging at delivery.
#### 18. Plumbing-products retail sales
Prepare a list of pipes, fittings, valves, diameters and compatible materials, and say whether the purchase is for a local repair or a new network. Attach an old-part photo or pipework plan. Compare fitting quality, compatibility and return terms for unused items; verify each size and marking before installation.
#### 19. Paint retail sales
Specify substrate, colour, sheen, quantity and primer system, and state whether an existing colour must be matched. Provide a colour code or sample and lighting photos. Request the mixed-colour record; check labels and shade before opening containers to reduce batch variation.
#### 20. Waterproofing-product retail sales
Choose products by application location, substrate and complete system rather than trade name alone. Provide the project specification or a photo of the previous product. Compare performance data, compatibility, accessories and shelf life; do not use a substitute without technical approval, and check batch references on receipt.
#### 21. Delivery of materials to the project
Delivery requires a material list, quantities, approximate weights, exact site location, unloading point and a time permitted by the site. Clarify whether lifting, unloading or internal movement is included, and agree responsibility for damage, delay and returns. Accept by checking quantities and packaging condition and recording shortages immediately.
