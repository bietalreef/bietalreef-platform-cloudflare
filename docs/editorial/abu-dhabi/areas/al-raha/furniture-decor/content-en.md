# Furniture, Furnishings and Fit-Out in Al Raha

## Scope of this guide

This sector helps define furniture, fabrication, upholstery, curtains, carpets and mattress requirements for residential or commercial property in Al Raha. Comparison should begin with verified room dimensions and the route for bringing items in, followed by daily use, materials, colours, hardware and installation needs. Coordinate oversized deliveries, lift use and common-area routes with property management where required; access and delivery windows should not be assumed to be the same at every property.

## Companies and establishments

### Furniture manufacturing
**Source note:** This specialty is marked as currently non-selectable. Editorial content does not change or activate the taxonomy status.

#### 1. Home furniture manufacturing
Begin with room dimensions, circulation, doors and windows and the function and user count for each item. Provide a sketch or photos, dimensions and finish sample, and request shop drawings showing materials and joints. Inspect stability, edges, colour and dimensions before handover.
#### 2. Office furniture manufacturing
Inventory workstations, chairs, storage, circulation and power and data outlets. Provide office layout, user count and screen and equipment dimensions. Specify materials and cable management; test stability, working heights and maintenance access.
#### 3. Hotel furniture manufacturing
Hotel furniture must account for intensive use, frequent cleaning, consistent dimensions and replaceable components. Provide room schedules, furniture standards, specifications and samples. Request a prototype before repeat production; inspect durability, finish, batch consistency and individual packaging.
#### 4. Restaurant furniture manufacturing
Define seating capacity, service routes, cleaning needs, use conditions and table and chair dimensions. Provide seating plan, aisle dimensions and surface requirements. Specify materials and cleanable edges; inspect table stability, consistent heights and safe edges.
#### 5. Custom furniture manufacturing
Describe function, dimensioned drawing, reference photos, material, colour and load. Agree on a prototype or sample and details of joints, hardware, delivery and installation. Compare quotations by specification and drawings rather than image alone, and inspect the item before acceptance.
#### 6. Furniture repair and refurbishment
Identify whether the fault is in the frame, hinges, fabric, foam or coating. Provide photos from several angles and use history. Separate repair from full remanufacture in the quote; inspect frame stability, upholstery quality and moving parts after refurbishment.
#### 7. Furniture supply for projects
Use one schedule stating model, quantity, dimensions, colour, materials and delivery batches. Attach room layouts and delivery routes. Compare supply, installation, packaging, warranty and spare parts; accept each batch against model numbers, quantities and packaging condition.

## Questions before approving an order

- Do measurements include the delivery route, lift and door clearances?
- Is the fabric, board or foam identified by sample and specification?
- Does the price include delivery, lifting, installation and packaging removal?
- How will colour, finish and stability be checked at handover?
- Are spare components or cleaning and care instructions included?

## Review boundaries

This guide helps describe needs and compare offers; it does not establish the existence of a supplier or certify a product. Confirm dimensions, samples, warranty and delivery terms with the supplier before payment or fabrication.
