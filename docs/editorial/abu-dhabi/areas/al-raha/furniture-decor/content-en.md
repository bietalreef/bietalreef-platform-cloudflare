# Furniture, Furnishings and Fit-Out in Al Raha

## Scope of this guide

This sector helps define furniture, fabrication, upholstery, curtains, carpets and mattress requirements for residential or commercial property in Al Raha. Comparison should begin with verified room dimensions and the route for bringing items in, followed by daily use, materials, colours, hardware and installation needs. Coordinate oversized deliveries, lift use and common-area routes with property management where required; access and delivery windows should not be assumed to be the same at every property.

Company and establishment listings remain separate from individual tradespeople. If no store or factory is displayed, Bait Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request with item type, dimensions, quantity, room photos, required material or fabric, an indicative budget if the customer wishes to provide one, delivery location, access route and installation needs. A request does not confirm a supplier, price or date.

## Companies and establishments

### Furniture manufacturing
**Source note:** This specialty is marked as currently non-selectable. Editorial content does not change or activate the taxonomy status.

#### 1. Home furniture manufacturing
Begin with room dimensions, circulation, doors and windows and the function and user count for each item. Provide a sketch or photos, dimensions and finish sample, and request shop drawings showing materials and joints. Inspect stability, edges, colour and dimensions before handover.
#### 2. Office furniture manufacturing
Inventory workstations, chairs, storage, circulation and power and data outlets. Provide office layout, user count and screen and equipment dimensions. Specify materials and cable management; test stability, working heights and maintenance access.
#### 3. Hotel furniture manufacturing
Hotel furniture must account for intensive use, frequent cleaning, consistent dimensions and replaceable components. Provide room schedules, furniture standards, specifications and samples. Request a prototype before repeat production; inspect durability, finish, batch consistency and individual packaging.
#### 4. Restaurant furniture manufacturing
Define seating capacity, service routes, cleaning needs, use conditions and table and chair dimensions. Provide seating plan, aisle dimensions and surface requirements. Specify materials and cleanable edges; inspect table stability, consistent heights and safe edges.
#### 5. Custom furniture manufacturing
Describe function, dimensioned drawing, reference photos, material, colour and load. Agree on a prototype or sample and details of joints, hardware, delivery and installation. Compare quotations by specification and drawings rather than image alone, and inspect the item before acceptance.
#### 6. Furniture repair and refurbishment
Identify whether the fault is in the frame, hinges, fabric, foam or coating. Provide photos from several angles and use history. Separate repair from full remanufacture in the quote; inspect frame stability, upholstery quality and moving parts after refurbishment.
#### 7. Furniture supply for projects
Use one schedule stating model, quantity, dimensions, colour, materials and delivery batches. Attach room layouts and delivery routes. Compare supply, installation, packaging, warranty and spare parts; accept each batch against model numbers, quantities and packaging condition.

Bait Al Reef customer service can receive the request without requiring a company listing. ## Questions before approving an order

- Do measurements include the delivery route, lift and door clearances?
- Is the fabric, board or foam identified by sample and specification?
- Does the price include delivery, lifting, installation and packaging removal?
- How will colour, finish and stability be checked at handover?
- Are spare components or cleaning and care instructions included?

## Review boundaries

This guide helps describe needs and compare offers; it does not establish the existence of a supplier or certify a product. Confirm dimensions, samples, warranty and delivery terms with the supplier before payment or fabrication.

### Furniture and furnishing stores

#### 8. Home furniture retail
Start with an item list, room and doorway dimensions and the delivery route, and define use, material and colour. Provide room photos and required item sizes. Compare model, materials, delivery, installation and warranty; inspect item condition and quantities before signing receipt.
#### 9. Office furniture retail
Define user count, work type, desk and chair sizes, storage and cable needs. Provide the office layout and use requirements. Request material, adjustment and warranty details; test chair height, desk stability and circulation space before accepting the order.
#### 10. Lighting retail
Choose fittings by application, brightness, colour, protection rating and mounting dimensions. Provide site photos, lighting plan and existing fitting details. Compare wattage, light colour, accessories and warranty; check product condition and compatibility with fixing and control points.
#### 11. Curtain retail
Selection requires window width and height, track position, fabric type and desired light control. Provide window photos and a reference sample or colour. Confirm whether track and accessories are included; check fabric length and cleaning instructions before purchase.
#### 12. Carpet retail
Define room area and shape, use location, furniture traffic, fibre type and cleaning method. Provide dimensions, floor photos and preferred sample. Compare pile density, backing and edge finish; inspect size, colour and pattern direction on delivery.
#### 13. Home-accessory retail
Prepare an item list with dimensions, materials, colours and intended location, checking compatibility with existing furniture. Provide reference photos. Compare finish quality, cleaning and exchange terms; inspect quantity and check for chips or scratches before acceptance.
#### 14. Furniture supply for projects
Use a schedule showing models, quantities, dimensions, colours, delivery batches and dates. Provide room plans and delivery route. Request an approved sample or mock-up and packaging plan. Compare supply, installation, warranty and returns, and accept each batch against the schedule.
### Majlis and sofa upholstery

#### 15. Arabic majlis tailoring
Start with wall and corner measurements, seat height and depth, access routes and the seating layout. Provide a plan, photos, seat count, fabric and filling preferences. Request drawings showing modules and joints; inspect seat level, base stability and fabric match before acceptance.
#### 16. Sofa tailoring
Define sofa length, depth, height, seat count, use and the doorway route for delivery. Provide a reference photo and room dimensions. Request fabric sample and foam and frame specifications; test seating comfort and inspect stitching, cushions and leg stability.
#### 17. Furniture upholstery
Inspect frame, springs, filling and old fabric to decide what can be retained and what must be replaced. Provide all-side photos and item dimensions. Separate frame repair from fabric and filling in the quote; accept after checking comfort, fabric tension and seam strength.
#### 18. Sofa fabric replacement
Identify sofa model, upholstery area, textile type, pattern direction and required durability. Provide photos, measurements and fabric sample. Clarify whether foam removal and repair are included. Inspect pattern alignment, even tension and corner finish after reupholstery.
#### 19. Foam and filling replacement
Select filling by seat dimensions, firmness and everyday use. Provide cushion measurements and condition photos. Specify foam type, density and filling layers. Compare a sample by actual seating feel and check shape recovery and even height after installation.
