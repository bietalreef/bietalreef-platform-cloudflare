# General Contracting, Building and Construction in Al Raha

## A practical guide to requesting contractor services

A well-scoped Al Raha contracting request begins with the property, the work boundaries and site access, followed by a clear decision on whether the need is construction, alteration, repair or external work. If the property is occupied or subject to building or community management, state the access, delivery and working arrangements confirmed with the responsible property manager; do not assume one procedure applies to every site.

This guide covers the **companies and establishments** list for general contracting, building and construction. It explains the services in the source taxonomy; it does not establish that a particular company exists, is approved, has availability or has quoted a price. Taxonomy entries marked non-selectable in the source remain non-selectable until reviewed outside this editorial task.

## Company and establishment services by specialty

### Building and villa construction

#### 1. Villa construction

A villa project should start with the plot boundaries, approved design information and available soil investigation, not an approximate floor area alone. Request a defined scope for civil, structural, MEP and finishing work, and state whether boundary walls, tanks and external works are included. Compare bids against the same bill of quantities and specifications; tie handover to stage inspections, test records, as-built information and stated warranties.

#### 2. Residential building construction

Define the number of units and floors, shared areas, parking provision and intended occupancy. The quotation should separate the structure, internal works and shared building systems, and explain coordination for lifts, utilities and fire-safety design against requirements confirmed by the competent authority. Ask for a phased programme identifying inspections before concealed work is closed.

#### 3. Commercial building construction

The intended commercial use affects circulation, loading, equipment loads and service capacity. Provide the anticipated use, site plan and known operating requirements. Ask the contractor to define responsibilities for façades, loading areas and mechanical and electrical systems. Do not compare headline prices until fit-out scope and completion obligations are aligned.

#### 4. Building works execution

This service suits a defined construction package within a new or existing project. Attach drawings, work boundaries and available quantities, and identify existing elements that must remain. The quotation should explain sequencing, included materials, interfaces with other trades, site protection and debris removal.

#### 5. Concrete works execution

Before pricing, identify the concrete element, location, dimensions and applicable structural drawings. The quotation should cover formwork, reinforcement, placement, curing and specified tests. Concrete grade or reinforcement details must not be changed informally. The responsible engineer should review test results and conformity before the stage is accepted.

#### 6. Structural frame execution

Request a structural-frame quotation that identifies excavation, foundations, columns, beams, slabs and stairs, including the formwork, steel and concrete included. Quantities should be taken from drawings, with site changes recorded in writing. Compare quality-control methods, pour planning and temporary works; accept each element after reinforcement, level and record checks.

#### 7. Turnkey project delivery

The phrase turnkey is not a scope by itself. Define whether design, procurement, construction, testing, commissioning, cleaning and handover are included. Attach a finishes schedule and acceptable brands or quality levels, and request an explicit exclusions and provisional-sums list. At handover, test systems, record defects and collect keys, manuals, warranties and as-built drawings.

#### 8. Building new villas

Where a new villa is to be built from an existing design, clarify the contractor’s responsibility for drawing review and trade coordination before site work begins. Supply drawings, specifications and door, window and finish schedules. Price unresolved items separately rather than silently substituting unapproved alternatives in the base offer.

#### 9. Residential annex construction

Survey the annex footprint, levels, heights and connection to the existing building, including access routes. State the intended use and whether bathrooms or kitchens are required, then define electrical, water, drainage and cooling connections. A qualified professional should assess the effect on the existing structure and confirm required approvals before work. Handover should include service, waterproofing and finish checks.

#### 10. Outdoor majlis construction

Start with the majlis location, its relationship to entrances and plot boundaries, circulation and privacy. Specify approximate occupancy, any washrooms, cooling, lighting and interior or exterior finishes. Request a drawing showing levels, rainwater drainage and service points, then inspect waterproofing, doors and system operation before acceptance.

#### 11. Villa extensions

An extension requires an accurate survey of the existing villa and a clear definition of how it connects to the current structure and services. Provide photographs and as-built information; obtain an engineering assessment of junctions, foundations and loads before final pricing. Separate protection, temporary support and service diversions from finishes, and document tests when systems are returned to service.

#### 12. Residential building alterations

Show the proposed change on a plan and photographs, identifying affected walls, openings and rooms. Distinguish non-load-bearing partitions from structural elements; no structural member should be cut or removed without specialist assessment, design and confirmation of required approvals. Ask the quotation to separate demolition, rebuilding, service repairs and finishes.

#### 13. Villa restoration

Begin with a condition survey that records defects, locations and likely causes rather than simply covering visible damage. Request a report separating crack, moisture, concrete, paint and service repairs, including areas that require opening or testing. Compare bids on cause treatment, materials, verification method and warranty, then obtain a record of completed work.

#### 14. Residential building refurbishment

For a full refurbishment, list the units, common areas and services affected, and state whether the building will remain occupied. Request a phased plan for protecting residents and property and coordinating access and deliveries. Separate unit work from common-area work. Handover should record service tests, defect closure and clean, safe work areas.

### Restoration and renovation

#### 15. Restoration of older buildings

Older buildings may contain undocumented layers or services that become visible only after opening-up. Request an initial survey, identify exploratory opening areas and agree how additional work will be approved. The quotation should distinguish essential repairs from optional upgrades and record replaced materials and elements retained after inspection.

#### 16. Villa renovation

List the rooms to be renovated and the intended outcome for each, stating whether the household will continue using parts of the villa. Provide photographs, approximate dimensions and preferred materials, and require protection for floors, furniture and circulation routes. Compare programme, payment stages and exclusions; accept each room after checking lighting, sockets, doors and finishes.

#### 17. Apartment renovation

Before renovating an apartment, record the area, bathrooms, kitchen and any material or debris movements. Ask how access, lift use and working times will be coordinated with the building management; do not assume identical rules across properties. The quotation should cover protection of corridors and shared areas and their reinstatement after the work.

#### 18. Building rehabilitation

Rehabilitation is appropriate when a building needs more than cosmetic renewal. Request an assessment of the structure, façades, services and finishes, with priorities based on safety and operation. The report should state inspection limits and areas not opened or tested, while pricing separates essential repairs from proposed upgrades.

#### 19. Structural crack treatment

Do not approve a repair price before understanding the crack pattern, location, width, direction and whether it changes over time. Send scaled photographs and building information; seek a structural engineer’s assessment where a load-bearing element may be affected. The quotation should explain the cause, repair method and monitoring, not merely fill and paint the crack.

#### 20. Façade renewal

A façade survey should identify cladding type, condition, working heights, access and fixing points. Provide overall and close-up photographs and state whether the scope is cleaning, repair, replacement or visual alteration. Compare bids on material samples, sealing, joints, drainage and safe access planning, then inspect fixing security and finish quality.

#### 21. Preparing a building for reuse

State the previous and proposed use, building area, service condition and spaces requiring removal or reconfiguration. Request an initial review of electrical, water, drainage, cooling and structural conditions as relevant to the project, and list matters requiring confirmation by competent authorities. Separate essential rehabilitation from fit-out for the new use.

### Demolition and concrete works

#### 22. Demolition works

Before pricing, mark demolition limits, retained elements and existing service conditions. Request a method statement covering isolation, disconnection, temporary support, neighbouring-property protection, dust and noise control and waste handling. Work should not start until site-specific permits and approvals have been confirmed. Handover follows debris removal and inspection of retained elements.

#### 23. Removal of buildings and annexes

Provide a plan marking the building or annex to be removed, its limits and adjacent elements to protect. The quotation should say whether it covers selective dismantling or full demolition, service disconnection, loading, transport and waste disposal. Compare trip allowances and quantity measurement, then accept the site clear of hazardous debris and within the agreed boundaries.

#### 24. Concrete cutting and coring

Specify the hole diameter or cut dimensions, member thickness and exact location, and attach available service drawings. Reinforcement and concealed utilities must be checked before work; a responsible engineer should review any structural element. The quotation should identify the cutting method, water and dust collection, site protection, edge treatment and removal of the cut section.

#### 25. Foundation and footing concrete placement

Price foundations from structural drawings, the soil report and site levels. Request a breakdown for excavation, replacement material, blinding, reinforcement, formwork, concrete placement, curing and tests, including responsibility for dewatering if required. Foundations should not be covered before dimensions, reinforcement and levels are inspected and concrete records documented.

#### 26. Concrete roof and floor slab casting

Define slab area and thickness, openings, beams and service zones, and review the formwork and temporary-propping plan. Request the pour sequence, pump location, sampling, curing and opening-protection method. Acceptance follows reinforcement and level checks and review of pour and test records, before loading the slab or removing props as authorised by the responsible professional.

#### 27. Concrete wall construction

Attach wall sections showing height, thickness, openings and embedded services. The quotation should identify reinforcement, formwork, joints, placement, curing and treatment of interfaces and waterproofing where needed. Check wall alignment, dimensions, opening locations and surface quality before proceeding to waterproofing or subsequent finishes.

#### 28. Demolition waste removal

Estimate waste type and volume, collection point and truck access, separating recoverable materials from mixed debris where required. Ask the quotation to state load count, vehicle capacity, loading, waiting time and charges at the authorised destination, with delivery evidence where requested. Waste must not be left at an unauthorised location.

### Landscaping and garden maintenance

#### 29. Home garden design

Begin with a survey of boundaries, levels, doors, windows and existing services, then define intended uses and seating, shade and planting zones. Send photographs from several angles and state the acceptable maintenance level. Request a coordinated plan for planting, materials, lighting, irrigation and drainage, clarifying whether the fee covers design only or implementation.

#### 30. Villa landscaping

To make a villa garden usable, identify circulation routes, seating, privacy needs and existing trees. The quotation should cover grading, soil, irrigation, lighting, edging and planting, with a bill of quantities separating supply from installation. At handover, check drainage falls, path stability and plant condition.

#### 31. Outdoor space design

Define the activities, approximate users, access routes and shaded areas for the outdoor space. Provide dimensions, photographs and water and power points, and request a layout showing paving, furniture, shades, planting and drainage. Compare designs for maintainability and safe circulation, not appearance alone.

#### 32. Lawn installation

Define the lawn area, soil condition, water source, irrigation method and expected use. Request details for ground preparation, grading, soil improvement, grass type, supply density and laying or seeding method. Check surface levels, drainage and root establishment or growth uniformity, and agree the initial care period.

#### 33. Tree and shrub planting

Provide a planting plan showing bed dimensions and distances from buildings, pipes and paths. The quotation should identify species, plant sizes and source, root condition, improved soil, staking and post-planting irrigation. Do not rely on a trade name alone; compare site suitability, water demand, mature size and maintenance needs.

#### 34. Natural grass installation

Specify the natural grass type, supply area, delivery method and laying window. Require soil preparation, grading and irrigation readiness before rolls or plugs arrive. Compare density, consistency, root condition and jointing method. At handover, inspect coverage, gaps and drainage, and obtain watering and mowing instructions for establishment.

#### 35. Artificial turf installation

Artificial turf quality varies with pile height and density, backing, infill and UV resistance. Request a sample and product data, and define the base, drainage, edge fixing and seam method. Inspect surface evenness, seam security and water run-off, and retain warranty and cleaning information.

#### 36. Plant and tree planting

This service focuses on supplying and planting specified plants rather than designing the entire garden. Attach a species and quantity list, or request recommendations after reviewing soil, light and water availability. The quotation should state plant size, root condition, planting mix, staking and irrigation; inspect plant health and delivery condition.

#### 37. Tree pruning and trimming

Identify the trees, photographs, height and proximity to buildings, cables or paths. Request a description of branches to be removed, access method and debris collection, including whether access equipment is needed. Avoid indiscriminate cutting or major removal without assessing whether the proposed pruning is suitable for the plant.

#### 38. Lawn maintenance

State lawn area, visit frequency, irrigation condition, weeds and damaged patches. The quotation should separate mowing, edging, cleaning, fertilising, patch repair and irrigation adjustment, and say whether materials and equipment are included. Review mowing consistency, grass health and clean edges at each contracted visit.

#### 39. Plant fertilisation and treatment

Do not select a fertiliser or treatment before identifying the plant, symptoms and relevant soil or irrigation conditions. Send clear photographs and note when the problem appeared and any products previously used. Request the product name, rate, application method, precautions and re-entry interval stated on its label, then monitor the plant response rather than repeating treatments blindly.

#### 40. Garden pest control

Identify the plant, affected location and photographs of the insect or symptoms where possible, and state treatment area and the presence of children or pets. The quotation should explain pest identification, control options, products and precautions, including care for edible plants. Request written prevention and follow-up instructions.

#### 41. Garden cleaning

List the areas to be cleaned, including paths, beds, seating areas and pruning debris. State whether the scope is leaf collection only or includes bulky waste removal and floor washing, and identify water and drainage points. Compare debris volume, loading and transport, and protection for plants and lighting; inspect that paths are clear on completion.

#### 42. Garden maintenance contracts

The contract should define area, visit frequency, routine and seasonal tasks, and what counts as extra work. Request a schedule for mowing, pruning, fertilising, irrigation checks and pest monitoring, with responsibility for materials, water and waste removal. Compare response arrangements and issue reporting, not visit count alone.

### Irrigation networks

#### 43. Irrigation network design

Design starts with plant types and zones, water source, and available pressure and flow. Request a zoning plan showing valves, filters, controller and pipe sizes, with calculations supporting pump selection where needed. The layout should allow isolation and maintenance, and water distribution should be tested before the final design is accepted.

#### 44. Drip irrigation installation

Specify drip-line lengths, plant count, soil type and water source. Ask for filter and pressure-regulator details, flushing points, emitter type and spacing. Each zone should be tested for blockage, leakage and even distribution. Obtain a simple route plan showing lines and valves, together with flushing instructions.

#### 45. Sprinkler installation

Select sprinklers according to coverage area and shape, water pressure, obstructions and wind exposure. Request a layout showing head positions, throw radius and overlap, while protecting walls and windows from unwanted spray. Test coverage for dry spots and ponding after commissioning.

#### 46. Irrigation tank and pump installation

Define the water source, required storage, demand, tank and pump locations and maintenance access. Request pump data and performance curve, electrical protection, base, piping and valves; do not rely on nominal power alone. At handover, test flow, pressure and safe operation, and confirm access for valve service and cleaning.

#### 47. Irrigation network maintenance

Attach the network plan if available and identify zones with no water, leaks or low pressure. The inspection should cover filters, valves, lines and controller, with replaced parts recorded. After repair, test each zone separately and confirm that distribution has returned to the required pattern.

#### 48. Automatic irrigation programming

State the controller model, number of zones and connected sensors, if any, and provide photographs of the controller and current settings. Request a schedule suited to plant types and seasonal use, with instructions for manual adjustment. Saving settings is not enough: operate each zone and verify available valves and sensors respond.

#### 49. Irrigation network leak detection

Record when the leak appears, the affected zone and whether it continues when irrigation is off. Request zone isolation and pressure, connection and valve checks before extensive excavation, with reinstatement defined. The report should identify the fault location and cause, the repaired section and the post-repair network test.

### Shades and external works

#### 50. Car shade installation

Start with vehicle count, dimensions, clearance, manoeuvring space and proposed post locations. Request drawings showing frame, canopy, foundations, anchorage and rainwater drainage, with loads and design reviewed by a qualified professional. Compare material durability, warranty and vehicle protection, and confirm site permissions before fabrication and installation.

#### 51. Pergola construction

Define the intended use, dimensions, sun orientation and roof covering, and whether the pergola attaches to a building or stands on independent foundations. The quotation should state materials, sections, joints, corrosion protection, covering fixings and drainage. Request a finish sample and inspect frame stability, joints and circulation after installation.

#### 52. Outdoor seating construction

Provide site dimensions and entrance photographs, and define seating capacity, intended use, privacy and shade. State whether the scope includes a base, paving, lighting or water connections. Request a layout and cleanable material specifications, then inspect seating levels, stability, access and surrounding drainage.

#### 53. External fence installation

Specify boundary length, fence height, material and gate locations, supported by a site survey showing corners and levels. Request details for foundations, posts, joints, finish and corrosion protection, and check buried utilities and site-specific requirements or approvals. At handover, inspect alignment, post stability and gate operation.

#### 54. Garden pathway construction

Define the route, width, expected use and connections to doors and parking. Provide levels and photographs, and request details of the sub-base, surface material, edging and drainage falls. Compare slip resistance, cleaning and maintenance; inspect evenness, edge stability and that water is not directed toward the building.

#### 55. Interlock paving installation

Pricing requires accurate area, paving pattern, block thickness and whether the surface carries pedestrians or vehicles. Request details for excavation, sub-base, compaction, bedding, edging and jointing sand. Inspect levels, falls, paving interlock, loose units and water ponding after completion.

#### 56. Waterfall and fountain construction

Define the feature location and size, water source, filling and drainage method, and access to the pump and filter. The quotation should cover waterproofing, basin, pipework, pump, any lighting and suitable electrical protection. Test leakage, circulation, noise and cleaning access, and obtain operating and maintenance instructions before use.

## Preparing a comparable quotation request

Prepare the property type and location within Al Raha, requested service and specialty, area or linear dimensions or quantities, recent wide and close-up photographs, available drawings or condition reports, required materials or finish level, existing service conditions, access and storage arrangements, whether occupants will remain during the work, dismantling and waste-removal needs, and a preferred timeframe that is not treated as a confirmed appointment. Note approvals already checked and those still requiring confirmation.

Ask the quotation to separate survey, design, supply, execution, testing, reinstatement and handover. It should state quantities and units, material specifications, brands or approved alternatives, approval method, programme, payment stages, exclusions and warranty. Compare quotations against the same scope rather than the total alone.

## Inspection and handover

Acceptance points depend on the service, but should document drawing and dimension conformity, material quality, site safety, relevant test results, defect closure, debris removal, and delivery of warranties, as-built information and operating or maintenance instructions. Concealed work should not be closed, and structural elements should not be loaded, before the appropriate inspection and approval.

## If no company is published

The absence of a visible company does not mean the service is unavailable. A request may be sent to **Bait Al Reef Customer Service** for intake and review and possible referral to a suitable provider, without inventing a company name, price, appointment or availability guarantee. The request form should capture property details, requested service, scope, quantities, photographs and drawings, materials, access conditions and preferred timing. Any quotation, contract or approval remains subject to direct verification with the provider and competent authorities where relevant.

## Practical questions before contracting

- Does the price include survey, measurement and design, or execution only?
- Who coordinates structural work, services and finishes?
- Which concealed works require inspection or testing before closure?
- How will additional quantities and variations discovered after commencement be priced?
- What records, tests and warranties will be handed over at each stage?

## Regulatory information limits

This guide does not determine specific licence or permit requirements. Confirm the relevant approval and competent authority for the property and work type before starting. This content is not a substitute for approved drawings, engineering advice or official requirements.
