# Metal, aluminium, glass and joinery workshops in Al Rahba

Made-to-measure joinery differs from buying ready-made furniture: site dimensions, shop drawings and materials should be agreed before fabrication. These are company and establishment services, separate from individual tradespeople. Where no provider is published, customers can submit a quotation enquiry to Biet Alreef customer service with the service, area, photographs, dimensions and preferred timing; no price or availability is guaranteed.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Measure the finished kitchen and locate water, drainage, power and extraction before designing cabinets. Request drawings showing drawer and appliance clearances and a schedule of board cores, finishes, edges and hardware. Compare storage capacity and maintainability before choosing colours.

### 2. Kitchen cabinet fabrication

Approve shop drawings against site dimensions and specify board core, facing, thickness and required moisture performance. Request samples of edge banding, hinges and runners, and clarify who supplies worktops and appliances. Check door alignment, consistent gaps and drawer movement at handover.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Check wall and floor levels and service outlets before fitting. Wall cabinets need fixings appropriate to the wall type and expected load; protect floors and appliances during installation. Test doors and drawers, surface levels and finishing around pipes before acceptance.

### 4. Wardrobe design

List hanging clothes, drawers, shoes and luggage before drawing a wardrobe: usable depth and door swing matter. Check switches, air-conditioning and ceiling bulkheads, and request an internal dimensioned layout and hardware specification.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

Specify unit dimensions, board material, facing, colour, edge thickness and hinge types. Compare quotations by drawer, runner and shelf quantities rather than frontage length alone. Inspect dimensions, squareness and packaging before installation.

### 6. Walk-in dressing room fit-out

Measure circulation space, door swings, lighting and ventilation before planning a dressing room. Agree on unit divisions, shelf and drawer heights, mirrors and electrical points. Test reach, illumination and secure fixing at handover.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe repair and refurbishment

Separate hinge or runner repairs from door-front replacement and full replacement of water-damaged boards. Send photographs and identify existing hardware where possible. The quote should distinguish repair from replacement; test all moving parts afterwards.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Provide final opening dimensions, swing direction, frame type, hinges and lock specification. Choose door core, facing, thickness and finish for indoor or outdoor use as appropriate. Inspect flatness, finish and edge protection before dispatch.

### 9. Door installation

Verify the opening, finished floor level and door swing before fixing. The quote should cover frame alignment, hinges, lock and any necessary making-good around the opening. Test closing without rubbing, consistent gaps and handle security.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door is not an ordinary timber door with special paint. Require a tested, approved door assembly specification for the intended application, including leaf, frame, hinges, closer, seals and compatible hardware. Verify project-specific authority requirements before ordering and inspect identification and installation against the assembly instructions; do not assume any product is approved.

### 11. Door frame fabrication and fitting

Measure opening size, wall thickness and finish levels, and specify timber or composite frame construction. Agree on anchoring, gap treatment and edge finish. Check plumb and square before hanging the leaf to avoid recurring rubbing.

### 12. Timber partition construction

Clarify whether the partition provides privacy, room division or supports shelving, with height, length and fixing points. Check electrical and air-conditioning routes before work, and specify panels, finish and maintenance access. Inspect stability and straight joints.

### 13. Door maintenance

Diagnose whether the problem is a hinge, lock, swollen leaf or moving frame; trimming without diagnosis may make matters worse. Photograph rubbing points and when they occur, agree on the repair scope and retest swing and locking.

### 14. Timber painting and refurbishment

Identify timber type, old coating, peeling and moisture before refinishing; not every product suits every substrate. Agree on preparation, sanding, filling, coats, colour sample and furniture protection. Inspect even colour and texture and ensure doors do not stick after curing.

## Wooden furniture and decorative joinery

### 15. Custom furniture fabrication

Start with a dimensioned drawing of each item's purpose and final position, checking lift and doorway access for delivery. Specify carcass materials, panels, edging, coatings and fixings. Compare finish samples and joint strength, then inspect stability and exposed edges at handover.

### 16. TV console fabrication

Confirm screen width, socket locations, cable routes and equipment ventilation before selecting a layout. Drawings should show height, depth, load assumptions and wall fixings where suspended. Check shelf stability, socket access and drawer operation after installation.

### 17. Storage unit fabrication

List what will be stored and its approximate weight before deciding clear depths, shelf spacing and door swing. Ask for shelf layouts, hinge and runner schedules, and anchoring details for tall units. Check suitable load support and aligned fronts at acceptance.

### 18. Timber wall cladding

Survey wall dimensions, outlets, maintenance access and any moisture concerns before agreeing panel divisions. Specify subframe, joints, movement allowance and removable access sections. Inspect straight lines and ensure ventilation and service access remain unobstructed.

### 19. CNC decorative woodwork

Provide an approved vector drawing or pattern with board material, thickness, overall dimensions and minimum cut detail. Request a physical sample to assess edges, openings and finish; agree on fixing for delicate elements. Inspect pattern accuracy and chipped edges before acceptance.

### 20. Counter fabrication

Define whether the counter serves reception, sales or service, including staff numbers and equipment, power and data positions. Drawings should cover working heights, knee clearance, storage and surface durability. Check level surfaces, structural stability and cleanable joints.

### 21. Retail and office furniture fabrication

Use a circulation plan and dimensions for desks, shelving and equipment, with a quantity and finish schedule for each item. Compare serviceable hardware, protected edges and surface durability rather than only lump-sum prices. Verify every unit against the agreed layout.

### 22. Interior space design

Collect as-built plans, photographs, user requirements and occupancy before proposing furniture arrangements. Coordinate door swings, lighting, cooling and electrical outlets; define deliverables such as drawings, samples and schedules. Do not release fabrication before final dimensions are approved.

### 23. Joinery fabrication and installation

Separate fabrication, transport, fitting and making-good in the quotation, with shop drawings identifying fixings and tolerances. Confirm site readiness and dry substrates before installation. Inspect joints, alignment, moving components and finish protection.

### 24. Majlis fit-out

Confirm seating capacity, entrances, sofa and table dimensions, lighting and power points. If wall cladding, cabinets or platforms are included, require individual drawings and fabric and timber samples. Test the seating layout and cleaning access before handover.

### 25. Bedroom fit-out

Measure finished room dimensions and locate the bed, wardrobe openings, circulation paths and switches. Clarify whether headboards, bedside units or integrated lighting are included. Check furniture stability and drawers opening without collisions.

### 26. Office and retail fit-out

Define customer, work and storage zones and circulation routes; distinguish joinery from electrical, air-conditioning and signage works. Request a bill of quantities, installation drawings and handover sequence. Check units in their actual positions against the approved layout.

### 27. Decorative timber installations

Clarify whether elements are decorative, conceal services or carry loads, as fixing methods differ. Approve colour, texture and joint samples before fabrication and detail corners and exposed edges. Inspect consistent finishes and maintenance access at handover.

### 28. Interior finishing and handover

Prepare a room-by-room snagging list covering coatings, cladding, doors, cabinets, joints and installation debris. Photograph defects, record locations and assign rectification responsibility. Retest hinges, drawers and integrated lighting after corrections; final cleaning does not replace technical defect repair.


## Aluminium doors and windows

### 29. Aluminium window fabrication

Prepare an opening schedule with width and height measured at several points, opening direction, glazing type and any insect screen. Agree the profile system, surface finish, drainage, gaskets and glass dimensions in shop drawings. Compare samples for weather sealing and replaceable hardware; inspect frame dimensions and corner joints before delivery.

### 30. Aluminium window installation

Check wall-opening alignment and sill level before fitting fabricated frames. Require details of anchors, movement gaps and sealants compatible with adjacent finishes, and protect glazing throughout the work. At handover operate the window and lock, inspect drainage and perimeter joints, rather than judging the silicone bead alone.

### 31. Aluminium door fabrication

Specify whether the door is internal or external, hinged or sliding, and record the clear opening, swing and threshold. Approve profile, reinforcement, infill or glazing, handles, hinges and locking hardware. Request a finish sample and clearance drawing; inspect leaf squareness and joint integrity before dispatch.

### 32. Aluminium door installation

Confirm finished-floor level, wall position and leaf-swing clearance; dimensions taken before finishing may be misleading. The quotation should distinguish frame fixing, hinge adjustment, perimeter sealing and making good surrounding finishes. Test gasket contact, locking and repeated opening without floor contact.

### 33. Insect screen installation

Identify the window type and opening path, plus safe access for future cleaning. Measure the actual fixing opening and select a removable frame and mesh suitable for ventilation and intended use. Compare how the mesh can be replaced and how the frame is secured; check edge fit and lock clearance.

### 34. Door and window maintenance

Record the fault on video: sticking movement, air or water leakage, rattling, or a failed lock. Check frame alignment, gaskets, rollers and hinges before recommending wholesale replacement. Request an itemised repair scope, parts and workmanship terms; operate the assembly repeatedly after adjustment.

### 35. Hardware and glass replacement

Photograph the damaged component with dimensions and door or window model, and distinguish cracked, scratched and failed-seal glazing. Replacement glass must match the required construction, thickness, size and safety performance for its location; matching colour alone is insufficient. Inspect glazing retention, gaskets and hardware operation after fitting.

### 36. Aluminium kitchen fabrication

Measure the kitchen after locating plumbing, drainage, power and appliances. Request unit drawings showing cabinet widths, depths and heights. Compare infill panels, profiles, hinges, drawer runners and cleanability under moisture exposure. Approve a colour, edge and appliance-junction sample before fabrication.

### 37. Aluminium kitchen installation

Verify finished floors, walls, service points and appliance dimensions before units arrive. Installation pricing should identify upper-cabinet fixings, levels, pipe and ventilation cut-outs and surface protection. Check aligned doors, smooth drawers, covered edges and continued access to service points.

### 38. Aluminium storage cabinet fabrication

Define the cabinet contents, expected loads and indoor or outdoor location, then obtain drawings with shelf dimensions, doors and fixing points. Review profiles, panels, edging, locks and hinges when comparing offers. Check frame alignment, shelf stability and accessible corners for cleaning at acceptance.

### 39. Bathroom cabinet fabrication

Measure around the basin, trap, isolation valves and door swing, leaving access to plumbing connections. Select panels and hardware appropriate for repeated moisture exposure and detail sealed edges and fixings. Test drawers and doors and check that the cabinet is not exposed to standing water at handover.

### 40. Outdoor storage unit fabrication

List stored items, dimensions and loading, plus exposure to sun, rain and dust and access space around the unit. Ask for drainage, ventilation, corrosion protection and anchorage details. Compare replaceability of panels and locks; inspect doors, edges and fixing after installation.

### 41. Aluminium kitchen refurbishment

Separate replacing cabinet fronts from structural repairs and full-unit replacement. Photograph moisture damage, drawers and hinges, and identify retained components to avoid mismatched dimensions or finishes. The quotation should cover removal, transport, alterations and finishing; inspect transitions between old and new work.

### 42. Kitchen hinge and hardware maintenance

Identify sagging doors and sticking drawers, photographing hinges, runners and part numbers where available. Inspect screw fixings and panel condition first: a new hinge will not repair a stripped substrate. Specify replacement type and quantities, then test opening and closing under normal use.
