# Building materials and supply in Al Rahba

Editorial content for companies and establishments, separate from the tradespeople directory. This text does not change the selection status of any restricted category in the taxonomy. Prepare drawings, a bill of quantities and delivery details before requesting supply. If no company is listed, Biet Alreef customer service can receive a quotation enquiry with the customer's name, contact number, material type, quantities, location, requested date and reference images or specifications; availability and pricing are not guaranteed.

## Building and finishing materials

### 1. Cement supply

Specify the cement grade from the project specification, bag or tonne quantity, production date and storage conditions. On delivery, inspect packaging and signs of caking; compare unloading and transport costs separately.

### 2. Sand supply

State whether the sand is for mortar or fill, the required cleanliness, grading and load volume. Inspect for visible contamination and agree how the delivered volume is measured before unloading.

### 3. Gravel supply

Give the aggregate size, grading, source and quantity for the concrete mix or base layer. Match the delivered load to the approved sample or specification and check the delivery note.

### 4. Blocks and masonry units

Specify block dimensions, type, thickness, required strength and piece count, allowing for cutting waste. Check visible breakage and dimensional variation before accepting the shipment.

### 5. Reinforcement steel

Attach the reinforcement schedule with diameters, lengths, weights and specified grades. Request product certificates where appropriate and reconcile bundle markings and quantities with the delivery record.

### 6. Ready-mix concrete

Provide the concrete grade, pour volume, pump location, casting time and required delivery rate. Agree on testing, sampling, truck tickets and the process for rejecting nonconforming loads.

### 7. Project building-material supply

Prepare a single bill of quantities identifying products, brands, permitted substitutions and delivery phases. Compare like-for-like offers and record shortages or damage with each shipment.

### 8. Gypsum products

Clarify whether the order covers boards, powder or accessories, with thickness and moisture or fire performance where applicable. Check board edges and dry storage conditions before installation.

### 9. Paint materials

List substrates, surface areas, primer and coat system, colour and sheen rather than colour alone. Request product data and inspect batch identifiers and sealed containers.

### 10. Insulation and waterproofing materials

Describe the waterproofing or insulation location, system, thickness and compatibility with the substrate and subsequent layers. Check shelf life, storage and required corner and joint accessories.

### 11. Flooring materials

Specify flooring type, net area, laying direction, tile or plank size and slip resistance where relevant. Inspect samples, batch consistency and broken pieces.

### 12. Sanitary fixtures

Send a schedule of basins, mixers and toilets with dimensions, finishes and connection types. Confirm compatibility with water and drainage points and inspect fittings and accessories before signing.

### 13. Electrical supplies

List breaker, switch, socket and cable quantities and ratings against the approved drawings. Compare brands, models and technical ratings, then inspect packaging and product markings.

### 14. Plumbing supplies

Identify pipe diameters, materials, operating pressures, fittings and valve types. Check system compatibility and do not accept material or size substitutions without technical approval.

### 15. Retail building materials

For counter purchases, bring a product list, quantities and reference photos. Separate unit price from delivery, loading and return terms, and retain invoices for reconciliation.

### 16. Retail sanitary fixtures

Compare actual dimensions, finishes and spare-part support before selecting fixtures. Inspect ceramic, glass and chrome for defects and confirm the installation kit is complete.

### 17. Retail electrical goods

State the intended use, voltage, power rating and installation environment of each electrical item. Check safety information, system compatibility and written warranty terms.

### 18. Retail plumbing goods

Bring a photo of the old fitting or measure its diameter and thread when replacing a component. Compare material and pressure suitability and avoid incompatible connections.

### 19. Retail paints

Test a colour sample on the actual surface before purchasing the full quantity and establish finish and theoretical coverage. Retain colour code and batch number for later touch-ups.

### 20. Retail waterproofing materials

Distinguish a local repair from a complete insulation or waterproofing system. Request compatible primers, reinforcement and protection materials rather than assuming one product is sufficient.

### 21. Delivery to project site

Provide the site address, unloading point, truck access restrictions and receiving hours. Agree who supplies lifting equipment or labour and photograph damage or shortages on the delivery note.
