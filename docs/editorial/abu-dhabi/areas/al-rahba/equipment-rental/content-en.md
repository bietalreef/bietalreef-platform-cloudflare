# Al Rahba — Factories and Equipment Hire

Equipment selection starts with the site and work requirements rather than the machine name alone. These business-service descriptions do not establish that a provider is available in Al Rahba.

## Construction and Lifting Equipment Hire

### 1. Scaffolding hire

State working height, facade length, ground conditions and intended loads, with photos of access and obstructions. Ask for separate pricing of design, erection, inspection, dismantling, hire and transport, including who certifies safe use. At handover have a competent person check bases, guardrails and inspection records.

### 2. Crane hire

Provide load weight and dimensions, pick-up and set-down points, travel path, reach and ground photographs. Clarify crane type, operator, rigging, lift plan, fuel and any site-specific approvals without assuming they apply universally. Compare booking and standby charges, and verify inspection records before operation.

### 3. Excavation equipment hire

Attach excavation limits, depth, width, known soil conditions and turning space, and flag underground services for verification. Compare excavator size, attachments, operator, transport, fuel and spoil disposal as separate items. Inspect equipment and confirm service locations before breaking ground.

### 4. Compaction equipment hire

Specify area, fill layer thickness, material and proximity to edges or sensitive services. Request suitable equipment for the working space and clarify operator, fuel, transport and any project-required compaction tests. Check consistent layers; machine passes alone do not prove adequate compaction.

### 5. Concrete mixer hire

State required batch volume, power or fuel availability and material access. Compare drum capacity, output, cleaning, transport and operator responsibility; do not confuse mixer hire with concrete supply or mix design. Check drum rotation and guards before accepting equipment.

### 6. Concrete cutting equipment hire

Identify member type, thickness, cut position, opening dimensions and potential reinforcement or concealed services. Clarify blade selection, dust or water control, waste removal and responsibility for structural assessment. Separate equipment hire from operator cost and inspect safety arrangements before use.

### 7. General project equipment hire

Prepare an equipment schedule with individual hire periods, locations and delivery/return dates. Request condition reports and clear breakdown, replacement, late-return, insurance and fuel terms; compare repeated transport costs. Record serial numbers and operating hours before return.
