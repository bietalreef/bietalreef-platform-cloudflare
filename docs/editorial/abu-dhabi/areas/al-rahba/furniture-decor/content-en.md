# Al Rahba — Furniture, Furnishings and Fit-out

This guide helps customers in Al Rahba define dimensions, materials, supply, installation and acceptance. A restricted or proposed category in the source taxonomy is not evidence of a selectable service or a published provider.

## Furniture manufacturing

### 1. Residential furniture manufacturing

List the required pieces, room plan and doorway clearances before selecting timber or upholstery. For storage, check shelf depth and hinge capacity; for seating, test seat height and cushioning. Request shop drawings and a finish sample, distinguish fabrication from delivery and installation, then inspect stability and edge quality.

### 2. Office furniture manufacturing

Start with staff numbers, workstation dimensions and power/data routes. Specify desk heights, knee clearance, drawer positions and board-edge protection. Compare samples, connectors and disassembly options; test drawer travel and desk stability at handover.

### 3. Hotel furniture manufacturing

Approve a schedule for each room type listing quantities, dimensions and finishes rather than a generic brief. Review corner durability, cleanability and replacement of damaged components. Sign off a sample room before batch production, then reconcile each delivery against the schedule.

### 4. Restaurant furniture manufacturing

Provide a table layout, service circulation widths and any outdoor seating locations. Specify stain-resistant surfaces, cleanable joints and stable legs on the actual floor. Compare table heights and chair dimensions using a sample and check for wobble before acceptance.

### 5. Custom furniture manufacturing

Attach dimensioned sketches, site photographs and switch or socket positions that may interfere with the piece. Agree material, thickness, door swing and colour before cutting, including who verifies final dimensions. Check the delivered item against signed drawings and finish samples.

### 6. Furniture repair and refurbishment

Photograph damage, joints and the frame, distinguishing structural repair from cosmetic renewal. Ask which components will be replaced or retained and whether transport is included. Test hinges, drawers and stability after repair, and inspect the finish for reasonable colour consistency.

### 7. Project furniture supply

Send a bill of quantities linking each item to its room, model and required delivery date. Clarify packing, storage, assembly and transit-damage remedies. Compare quotations against approved samples and phased delivery dates, recording shortages separately for each location.

## Furniture and furnishing retailers

### 8. Retail sale of home furniture

Measure the intended position, access route and drawer clearance before choosing showroom furniture. Confirm actual materials, set contents, returns and delivery terms. On arrival check the model, colour, fittings and assembly stability.

### 9. Retail sale of office furniture

Specify user numbers, seating patterns, chair adjustments and cable-routing needs. Compare dimensions, warranty and spare parts alongside bulk assembly charges. Test chair mechanisms and table balance on site.

### 10. Retail sale of lighting

Decide whether lighting is ambient, task or decorative, and review mounting dimensions, colour temperature and electrical requirements with a qualified installer. Obtain product specifications and required accessories; separate fixture cost from wiring. Test a sample before accepting the full order.

### 11. Retail sale of curtains

Provide window width, proposed track height and photographs of nearby AC units or furniture. Compare blackout performance, lining, pleat style and cleaning requirements; confirm whether track and fitting are included. Check drop length and smooth operation.

### 12. Retail sale of rugs and carpets

Specify area, door swings and footfall, then compare fibre, backing, cleaning method and edge finish. Ask about custom cutting and anti-slip underlay where needed. Verify colour, dimensions and flatness at delivery.

### 13. Retail sale of home accessories

List mirrors, shelves and holders with dimensions and intended mounting positions. Check fixings, wall substrate and stated load ratings, distinguishing supply from fitting. Inspect glass, coatings and accessory completeness.

### 14. Furniture supply for projects

For a multi-room retail procurement, approve item codes, quantities and delivery destinations by batch. Compare model availability, colour consistency and damage replacement terms; distinguish product warranty from installation. Sign delivery records only after counting.

## Majlis and sofa upholstery

### 15. Custom Arabic majlis seating

Draw the majlis perimeter, doors and tables, specifying each seating run, height and depth. Test foam and fabric samples, joint details and removable covers. Check alignment, pattern matching and seating comfort.

### 16. Custom sofa fabrication

Specify sofa configuration, seat count and access widths, especially for large sections. Compare frame, padding firmness and upholstery using physical samples. On delivery inspect cushion alignment, leg stability and dimensions.

### 17. Furniture reupholstery

Inspect frames, springs and padding first: new fabric cannot repair a broken base. Photograph the pieces and identify components to remove or replace, approving fabric and stitching samples. Test seating and corner tension after reupholstery.

### 18. Sofa fabric replacement

Count cushions, arms and back panels, and state whether existing foam is retained. Compare fabrics for wear, cleaning and pattern direction, including cutting waste. Inspect consistent colour, seams and zipper operation.

### 19. Foam and cushion filling replacement

Describe sagging by seat and specify dimensions, thickness and desired firmness after testing a sample. Establish whether the fault lies in foam, springs or the support base before replacement. Test support and even seat heights at handover.

## Curtains, carpets and bedding

### 20. Made-to-measure curtains

Measure opening width and finished track-to-floor height, allowing for overlap, pleat type and lining. View fabric samples in the room's lighting and compare blackout performance and sewing costs. Inspect hem length and pleat consistency.

### 21. Curtain installation

Identify wall or ceiling substrate and possible concealed services before drilling, then check fabric weight, track and fixing points. Separate fitting charges from track or motor supply. Test travel, end stops and secure anchorage.

### 22. Upholstery fabric supply

Supply item dimensions, usage conditions and colour references, requesting tactile and cleaning samples. Compare roll width, required metres, pattern direction and batch variation. Inspect lengths and visible flaws before cutting.

### 23. Rug and fitted-carpet supply

Provide floor plans, doors and thresholds, distinguishing loose rugs from fitted carpet. Compare pile, backing, adhesive or underlay and installation charges. Inspect pile direction, joins and edge flatness on acceptance.

### 24. Mattress supply

Confirm actual bed dimensions, mattress height, preferred firmness and number of sleepers. Compare layer construction, edge support, trial terms, warranty and delivery. Check model label, measurements and cover condition on receipt.
