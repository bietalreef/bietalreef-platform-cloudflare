# General Maintenance and Finishing in Al Rahba

This is editorial guidance for company and establishment services, not evidence of a listed provider. It helps clients define scope and compare quotations. Individual tradespeople remain in a separate taxonomy list.

## General Maintenance

### 1. الصيانة الدورية للفلل — Scheduled villa maintenance

Start with an inventory of AC units, pumps, distribution boards, doors and roof areas, supported by fault photographs and maintenance history. Request a preventive visit schedule distinguishing inspection from separately priced repairs, and define responsibility for parts. Accept the work against visit reports and test results, not attendance alone.

### 2. الصيانة الدورية للمباني — Scheduled building maintenance

For a multi-unit building, identify shared areas, assets and access responsibilities before pricing a maintenance contract. Record common lighting, pumps, leaks and any specialist systems within the provider's actual scope. Compare coverage, exclusions, agreed response targets and the method for tracking recurring defects.

### 3. إصلاح التلفيات الداخلية — Internal damage repairs

Photograph each defect, identify the substrate and damaged dimensions, and establish whether moisture, impact or building movement caused it. A quote should address the underlying cause, removal of unsound material and finish reinstatement rather than concealment. Inspect colour, texture and the repair after curing.

### 4. إصلاح الأبواب والنوافذ — Door and window repairs

Record frame type, hinges, locks and photographs of rubbing points or air leakage; measure sash clearances and opening dimensions. Ask whether adjustment, hardware replacement or a distorted component is required. Test smooth operation, locking, sealing and drainage before sign-off.

### 5. معالجة الرطوبة — Damp treatment

Distinguish condensation from plumbing leaks and failed waterproofing before specifying any damp-resistant coating. Provide dated stain photographs and moisture readings where available. Require source investigation, drying and layer-by-layer reinstatement; do not accept the cosmetic finish until the cause has been addressed and the substrate is dry.

### 6. معالجة الشقوق — Crack repairs

Mark crack location, direction, approximate width and first appearance; photograph it beside a scale. Plaster cracks differ from potential structural movement, which calls for competent engineering assessment when warning signs exist. The quotation should define repair materials, limitations and how recurrence will be monitored.

### 7. تجهيز المبنى قبل التسليم — Pre-handover snagging

Prepare a numbered, room-by-room snag list with photographs covering paint, doors, floors, sanitary fittings and lighting. Specify the correction and required test for each item, keeping post-construction cleaning separate from defect rectification. Reinspect jointly and record both closed and outstanding items.

### 8. أعمال الصيانة المنزلية البسيطة — Minor household maintenance

Group minor household tasks into a photo list showing locations, wall substrates and component types so the right tools can be prepared. Clarify the visit allowance, materials and labour, and route electrical or plumbing work to suitably qualified personnel. Test each repaired item and retain an itemised completion record.

### 9. تركيب الستائر — Curtain installation

Measure opening width and height, window swing, AC outlets and electrical points before choosing a pole or track. Confirm fabric weight, ceiling substrate, fixing method and clearance. Check track alignment, smooth travel and that curtains do not catch on handles.

### 10. تركيب الرفوف — Shelf installation

State the intended load, shelf depth and bracket spacing; photograph the wall and identify possible concealed services. Fixings for concrete, blockwork and plasterboard are not interchangeable. Verify level, anchorage and the agreed safe loading conditions before use.

### 11. تعليق الشاشات — TV mounting

Record screen size, weight, VESA pattern and wall construction; plan viewing height, sockets and cable routes. Require a compatible mount and substrate-specific fixings while avoiding concealed services. Check stability, tilt and the full movement range where an articulating bracket is used.

### 12. تركيب الإكسسوارات — Accessory installation

Mark each hook, holder or mirror position with photographs and dimensions, allowing clearance from doors and sanitary fittings. In wet areas, check waterproofing and concealed pipe locations before drilling, and choose moisture-resistant fixings. Inspect alignment, anchorage and exposed edges.

### 13. تجميع الأثاث — Furniture assembly

Provide package photographs, model number, parts list and assembly space. Clarify whether moving cartons, wall anchoring and packaging removal are included and how missing parts are handled. Test drawers, doors and stability, and anchor tip-prone furniture according to manufacturer instructions.

### 14. إصلاح الأعطال البسيطة — Minor fault repairs

Describe the symptom and when it occurs instead of requesting immediate replacement; attach a short video and model number where possible. Ask for diagnosis distinguishing adjustment, cleaning and part replacement, with approval before extra charges. Reproduce the original operating conditions during testing and record replaced parts.


## Integrated Finishing

### 15. تشطيب الفلل — Villa finishing

Break down a villa finishing quotation by floor, room, wet area and exterior scope. Use drawings to separate concealed services from final finishes, and approve material samples and a work sequence before covering waterproofing or pipework. At handover, inspect room by room and witness relevant system tests.

### 16. تشطيب الشقق — Apartment finishing

Survey the apartment and establish access, lift use, work-hour restrictions and protection of shared corridors. Distinguish surface renewals from kitchen or bathroom alterations involving hidden services. Compare quotations on protection, debris removal and junction details; inspect surfaces and doors after final cleaning.

### 17. تشطيب المحلات — Retail shop finishing

Begin with the shop's intended use, customer circulation, counters, lighting and service points. Separate tenant fit-out items from landlord-provided systems. Review shop drawings and durable finish samples before fabrication, then inspect doors, lighting, service outlets and exposed junctions at handover.

### 18. تشطيب المكاتب — Office finishing

Confirm workstation counts, meeting rooms, acoustic privacy and data/power routes before selecting partitions and ceilings. Compare bids against coordinated layouts, fixed joinery and future access above suspended ceilings. Test lighting, sockets and doors, and check that desk clearances match the approved plan.

### 19. تجديد المساحات الداخلية — Interior space refurbishment

Photograph retained and removed elements and assess substrates and services before setting the refurbishment budget. Separate demolition, remedial works and new materials in the bill of quantities, with protection for occupied areas. Check floor level transitions and interfaces between new and retained finishes.

### 20. تنفيذ أعمال الديكور — Decorative fit-out works

Supply reference images, wall dimensions and ceiling heights, noting concealed lighting, joinery or heavy cladding. Require fixing details, electrical coordination and physical finish samples rather than appearance alone. Inspect alignment, junctions, lighting performance and maintenance access.

### 21. تسليم التشطيبات المتكاملة — Integrated finishing handover

Request as-built information, material schedules, available warranties and test records, together with a dated snag list and assigned responsibility. Separate visual finish acceptance from concealed waterproofing and services requiring earlier inspection evidence. Close defects only after documented reinspection.

## Gypsum and Decorative Works

### 22. تركيب أسقف الجبس — Gypsum ceiling installation

Set the finished ceiling level and coordinate AC diffusers, sprinklers, lights and access panels before framing. Specify suspension details for the supporting structure and appropriate boards in humid locations. Check levelness, joints and cut-out positions after finishing.

### 23. تنفيذ الجدران الجبسية — Gypsum partition walls

Mark partition positions, door openings and intended wall-mounted loads. Specify stud dimensions, board layers and acoustic infill where needed, and coordinate electrical routes before closing the second face. Inspect plumb, door-frame anchorage, joint treatment and agreed performance.

### 24. تنفيذ بيت النور — Cove lighting construction

Define cove depth, wall offset, LED strip type and driver position before cutting boards. Approve a short mock-up to check light spread and avoid exposed LED dots. At handover inspect uniform illumination and access for replacing strips and drivers.

### 25. تنفيذ الديكورات الجبسية — Decorative gypsum detailing

Provide dimensioned drawings for cornices, frames and mouldings, including corner junctions, and approve a painted sample. Establish whether profiles are prefabricated or formed in situ and how they will be fixed. Inspect repeated patterns, corner symmetry and concealed joints.

### 26. تركيب ألواح بديل الخشب — Wood-look panel installation

Measure each wall, switch position and corner; confirm panel composition and suitability for expected moisture exposure. Specify substrate preparation, adhesive or mechanical fixing and joint orientation. Check batch colour consistency, edge adhesion and straight lines.

### 27. تركيب ألواح بديل الرخام — Marble-look panel installation

Map sheet dimensions and seams around basins and sockets. Distinguish decorative wall cladding from surfaces exposed to direct heat or water. Approve an edge and sealant sample and fixing method for the substrate; inspect voids, veining alignment and joints.

### 28. صيانة وإصلاح الجبس — Gypsum repair and maintenance

Photograph cracks, bulges and stains, and investigate movement, water ingress or loose framing before patching. Require removal of unsound board and inspection of hangers or nearby services where relevant. Accept after curing, checking flatness and absence of recurring symptoms.

## Flooring and Cladding

### 29. تركيب السيراميك — Ceramic tile installation

Calculate area and cutting waste for the chosen tile size and layout; check substrate flatness and falls to drains in wet areas. Specify adhesive, grout joints, skirting and corner treatment. Inspect hollow-sounding tiles, joint consistency, drainage falls and final cleaning.

### 30. تركيب البورسلان — Porcelain tile installation

Record large-format tile dimensions, thickness and movement joints, and request handling and cutting arrangements to limit breakage. The bid should specify substrate preparation, suitable adhesive coverage and joint continuity. Check lippage, alignment and voids beneath tiles.

### 31. تركيب الباركيه — Parquet flooring installation

Measure subfloor moisture and door clearances, and select solid or engineered boards and the installation system before ordering. Confirm underlay, acoustic treatment, expansion gaps and water protection. Inspect board flatness, movement noise and perimeter clearances.

### 32. تركيب الفينيل — Vinyl flooring installation

Confirm whether LVT planks or sheet vinyl suit the room's traffic and moisture exposure. Test subfloor smoothness and moisture because imperfections can telegraph through thin coverings; specify seams and skirting. Check adhesion, joints and cleanability at handover.

### 33. تركيب الإنترلوك — Interlocking paver installation

Survey entrance levels, drainage routes and area dimensions before selecting paver thickness and pattern. Require details of sub-base, compaction, bedding sand and edge restraints, not only paver supply. Inspect surface evenness, edge stability and drainage after testing.

### 34. تركيب كسوات الجدران — Wall cladding installation

Identify cladding material, weight, panel size, wall height and openings. Require a fixing approach suited to the substrate, particularly for heavy panels. Review joints and edges around sockets and doors; inspect alignment, anchorage and repair access.

### 35. إصلاح الأرضيات — Floor repair works

Determine whether the issue is a cracked tile, debonded adhesive, swollen timber or subsidence; photograph and measure the affected zone. Ask for cause diagnosis and material matching before local replacement. Check finished levels, joints and repair stability during trial use.

## Electrical Works and Lighting

### 36. تأسيس كهرباء المباني — Building electrical first fix

Before first fix, supply room layouts and proposed positions for lights, outlets and high-load appliances. The quotation should distinguish conduit routes, circuits, protective devices, earthing, wall chasing and reinstatement, with specialist design or approvals identified separately where applicable. At handover request as-built circuit information and insulation and protective-conductor continuity test results.

### 37. تمديد الأسلاك والكابلات — Wire and cable installation

Cable length alone is insufficient: specify the load, route length, installation method, ambient conditions, insulation type and termination points. Compare conductor sizes, identification, mechanical protection and how concealed joints are avoided or documented. Inspect installation photographs before routes are closed, followed by continuity and insulation testing.

### 38. تركيب لوحات التوزيع — Distribution board installation

Inventory existing circuits, foreseeable loads, available board space and surrounding conditions. The proposal should provide a circuit schedule, breaker ratings, protective devices, phase allocation where relevant, labels and maintenance access. Qualified personnel should check terminations against manufacturer requirements and test protection before acceptance.

### 39. تركيب المقابس والمفاتيح — Socket and switch installation

Mark each outlet's height and location against furniture and equipment, noting the wall construction and photographing damaged fittings. Confirm whether the price covers back boxes, wiring, faceplates and finish repairs, not merely the visible accessory. Test secure mounting, polarity, earthing and operation using suitable instruments.

### 40. تمديد كهرباء الملاحق — Electrical supply for extensions

Provide the extension layout, existing board location, cable route and expected loads such as air conditioning or kitchen equipment. Do not assume spare supply capacity; request an assessment of capacity, feeder routing, protection and associated civil work. Compare the scope of connection, tests, documentation and project-specific approvals that may need checking.

### 41. إعادة تأهيل الشبكات — Electrical network refurbishment

Recurring trips or hot connections call for documented symptoms and fault diagnosis, not wholesale rewiring by assumption. Have a qualified specialist assess circuits, boards, insulation and earthing before identifying sections to replace. Request updated circuit records, post-repair tests and a clear list of unresolved defects.

### 42. فحص الأحمال الكهربائية — Electrical load assessment

List appliances, rated power, usage patterns and previous faults; supply photographs of closed panels without opening them yourself. A qualified specialist should assess coincident demand, circuit distribution, measured values and overload risks. Require a report distinguishing observations from estimates and recommendations; a load survey is not automatic permission to increase supply.

### 43. تركيب الإنارة الداخلية — Indoor lighting installation

Define room use, ceiling height, furniture placement and the intended illumination before choosing fittings. Quotations should detail fixture quantities, wattage, colour temperature, controls, ceiling openings and reinstatement. Check light distribution, flicker and maintenance access after installation.

### 44. تركيب الإنارة الخارجية — Outdoor lighting installation

Photograph entrances, façades and paths and identify exposure to rain, dust and cable routes. Compare fixture suitability for outdoor conditions, sealed connections, secure mounting and time or photocell controls. Inspect the lighting at night and check for glare and water ingress risks.

### 45. تركيب الثريات — Chandelier installation

Before selecting a chandelier, verify its mass, ceiling height, structural support and supply point. The quotation should detail a load-appropriate fixing method, lifting, protection and assembly rather than relying on plasterboard alone. Inspect stability, alignment and safe access for lamp replacement.

### 46. تنفيذ إنارة مخفية — Concealed lighting installation

Supply the ceiling section, cove dimensions, strip lengths, driver positions and maintenance access points. Check strip power, heat dissipation, dimming compatibility and feed distribution to avoid uneven brightness. Run the system for a sustained test and inspect for exposed light points or abnormal heat.

### 47. تركيب إنارة الحدائق — Garden lighting installation

Identify trees, paths, irrigation lines and likely excavation areas before routing garden lighting cables. Require details of protected cable routes, junction locations and moisture-suitable fittings, avoiding roots and irrigation equipment. Check the result at night for stability and interference with sprinklers.

### 48. تركيب حساسات الحركة — Motion sensor installation

Describe the movement zone, mounting height and desired on-time. Compare detection angle, sensitivity, load compatibility and placement to limit nuisance switching. Test real approach directions, delay settings and daylight response as intended.

### 49. صيانة وحدات الإنارة — Lighting fixture maintenance

For flickering or failed lighting, record fixture models and when symptoms occur before replacing components. Qualified personnel should safely isolate and inspect supply, drivers, switches, connections and operating temperature. Record replaced parts and test repeated switching before handover.

## Plumbing and leak detection

### 50. تأسيس شبكات المياه — Water supply network installation

Start with a drawing of supply sources, fixture counts, expected pressure and pipe routes through walls and floors. Specify pipe material, diameters, accessible isolation valves and whether opening and reinstatement are included. Photograph concealed routes before closure and request pressure-test records and connection details.

### 51. تأسيس شبكات الصرف — Drainage network installation

Mark sanitary fixtures, invert levels, discharge connections, access points and venting. Compare pipe sizes, gradients, routes, odour control and clean-out access rather than counting outlets alone. Before covering pipework, request leakage and flow tests plus photographs of levels and joints.

### 52. تركيب الأدوات الصحية — Sanitary fixture installation

Provide a schedule of toilets, basins, mixers and showers with models, water and waste connections and room dimensions. Clarify whether valves, traps, sealing, fixing and removal of old fixtures are included. At handover test drainage, pressure, leakage, stability and service access.

### 53. تركيب الخزانات — Water tank installation

Define required capacity, installation-space dimensions, base loading, inlet, outlet, overflow and drain positions. Compare tank material and suitability for its intended water use, protected openings and cleaning access. Inspect support, float operation and leaks, and obtain operation and maintenance instructions.

### 54. تركيب مضخات المياه — Water pump installation

State storeys, outlets, tank level and low-pressure symptoms before selecting a pump. The quotation should address the appropriate duty curve, connections, dry-run protection, vibration, noise and maintenance access. Test pressure under simultaneous demand and automatic start-stop without excessive pressure.

### 55. إعادة تأهيل التمديدات — Plumbing pipework rehabilitation

For recurring leaks or poor flow, gather failure locations, photographs of exposed pipework and approximate age if known. Require a diagnosis distinguishing replacement from retained sections and consider routes that limit demolition, with finish repairs priced separately. Obtain pressure and flow tests and an as-built route record.

### 56. صيانة شبكات الصرف — Drainage system maintenance

Record blockage location, recurrence, usage, odours and any backflow. Compare mechanical cleaning with camera inspection where justified, identifying causes rather than merely clearing the blockage. Require a flow check and a record of defects needing later repair.

### 57. كشف تسربات المياه — Water leak detection

Document meter changes, damp patches, running-water sounds and when symptoms occur without opening walls at random. Ask which methods suit concealed or exposed pipes, their limitations and whether the price covers location only. Request findings, confidence level and a confirmation step before repairs.

### 58. إصلاح تسربات الأنابيب — Pipe leak repair

Once the leak is located, specify pipe material, failed section, joining or replacement method and likely cause. Compare access opening, isolation, protection, reinstatement and written repair warranty. Pressure-test the line and observe the joint before concealing it.

### 59. كشف تسربات الخزانات — Water tank leak detection

Distinguish losses from supply lines, overflow, tank body and connections. Photograph the base and surrounding area and monitor water level after safely isolating relevant routes. The report should identify the suspected source and verification method before proposing sealing or replacement.

### 60. عزل الحمامات — Bathroom waterproofing

Before retiling, identify wet zones, upstand heights, drain penetrations and wall-floor junctions. Compare a substrate- and tile-compatible system, curing time and protection during finishing. Require a documented flood test or other suitable verification before covering the membrane.

### 61. عزل الأسطح — Roof waterproofing

Inspect drainage falls, ponding, joints and equipment bases before choosing a membrane. The scope should cover substrate preparation, crack treatment, edge and penetration detailing and protection against exposure and traffic. Check outlets and suitable tests at handover, retaining photographs before layers are concealed.

### 62. عزل خزانات المياه — Water tank waterproofing

State whether the tank holds drinking water or serves another use, and document structural condition, joints and penetrations. Water resistance alone is insufficient: request evidence of suitability for the tank's use, substrate preparation, curing and rinsing instructions. Obtain a watertightness test, material records and a safe return-to-service plan.

### 63. معالجة الرطوبة الناتجة عن التسرب — Remediation of leak-related dampness

Do not paint over a damp patch before locating and fixing its water source and assessing moisture spread through the wall or ceiling. Compare removal of damaged finishes, drying, treatment of salts or mould where present, and reinstatement only after adequate drying. Request before-and-after photographs and evidence that the cause has stopped.
