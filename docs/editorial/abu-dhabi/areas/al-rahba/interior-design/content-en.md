# Engineering consultancy and design in Al Rahba — editorial draft

This draft describes company and consultancy services without implying a listed provider or official approval. Biet Al Reef customer service can receive quotation requests even when no company appears. Regulatory requirements depend on the project and competent authority; this is not permit advice.

## Architectural design and drawings

### 1. Villa design

Define household size, rooms, guest access and service circulation, and supply available plot dimensions and survey documents. Request plans, elevations, sections, an area schedule and early structural and HVAC coordination. Compare design alternatives, revision allowances and stage deliverables; check final drawings against the approved brief.

### 2. Residential building design

Specify unit mix, floors, parking and shared facilities rather than gross floor area alone. Review entrances, escape routes, lifts and service rooms with the relevant specialists, and request a unit-area schedule. Compare coordination responsibilities and receive plans with consistent dimensions and levels.

### 3. Commercial building design

Describe the intended use of each space, loading and deliveries, public entrances and utility requirements. Distinguish base-building design from tenant fit-out and confirm which service and facade drawings the consultant supplies. Review accessibility and maintenance routes before approving the layout.

### 4. Architectural drawing preparation

State whether drawings support concept design, pricing or construction, and provide available surveys and dimensions. Agree scales, levels, opening schedules and revision numbering. Cross-check plans, sections and elevations at handover, recording inconsistencies for correction before use.

### 5. Facade development

Provide facade photographs, drawings and opening dimensions, noting existing leaks or difficult maintenance access. Request cladding alternatives and details for fixings, window junctions and drainage. Compare quantities, installation methods and maintenance implications; a render does not replace technical details.

### 6. 3D model preparation

Define whether the model tests massing, room layouts or material options. Supply plans, levels and actual furniture dimensions, and agree viewpoints, detail level and revisions. Check the model against drawings; visualisation alone establishes neither structural adequacy nor regulatory approval.

### 7. Modification of existing drawings

Collect the latest approved drawings and document changes already built on site. Have a qualified professional identify structural elements before moving walls or openings. Request existing-versus-proposed drawings and a change register covering affected services before seeking construction quotes.

### 8. Permit drawing preparation

Identify the project type and competent authority before preparing an application package. Ask the consultant for a project-specific document checklist without assuming approval. Reconcile plot, ownership and area data across forms and drawings; handover should identify submitted versions and outstanding comments.

### 9. Building application submission

Clarify in writing whether the consultant only prepares documents or also submits and responds to authority comments. Arrange any required authorisation and supporting documents, and track the reference through the official channel. A submission receipt is not permission to build.

### 10. Amendment of approved drawings

Record the approved revision, proposed change, reason and effects on structure, services and floor areas. Request a marked comparison and an assessment of which authority reviews may need repeating. Do not execute a change requiring approval before that process is complete.

### 11. Maintenance permit assistance

Describe the work precisely—surface repair, building services alteration or structural intervention—because requirements may differ. Ask which authority and documents apply to the specific job. Separate consultant preparation and follow-up fees from authority charges; never assume a permit will be issued.

### 12. Extension permit assistance

Provide site drawings, existing and proposed areas, and photographs of connection points. Ask for an assessment of structural, service and access impacts, followed by a project-specific submission checklist. Compare the design and follow-up scope, not promises of approval.

### 13. As-built survey drawings

Where existing drawings differ from the building, commission a measured survey of dimensions, levels, openings and visible services. Agree what cannot be verified without intrusive inspection or testing. Receive dated drawings identifying measurement sources and unresolved uncertainties.

### 14. Coordination of authority reviews

Request a review matrix showing potentially relevant authorities, required inputs and responsibility for responses and revised drawings. Maintain a log of comments, versions and official correspondence, checking status through competent channels. Coordination cannot guarantee approval or waive requirements.

## Request a quotation through Biet Al Reef customer service

Even with no published company, submit your name, contact number, Al Rahba project location, property type, requested service, approximate area, available photos or drawings and target date. State whether you need a concept, drawings or application follow-up, and request itemised deliverables, revisions and exclusions. A published provider is not required to receive an enquiry.
