# Furniture, furnishings and fit-out on Al Reem Island

Custom fabrication, retail purchase, upholstery and supply require different scopes. Specify rooms, measurements, access and budget without assuming a provider or price. The following company and establishment services are separate from the tradespeople directory.

## Furniture manufacturing

### 1. Home furniture manufacturing

List furniture pieces room by room using finished dimensions, and check lift and doorway access. Compare frame materials, joints, edge finishes and warranties; inspect stability, drawer alignment and floor protection at handover.

### 2. Office furniture manufacturing

Start with headcount, circulation and power/display positions before approving desks and storage. Request layout drawings, dimensions, surface load information and cable management; test chair clearance, drawers and edges at handover.

### 3. Hotel furniture manufacturing

Provide a room schedule, unit types and expected duty levels, and approve a sample before batch production. Compare wear resistance, maintainability and dimensional consistency; check sample conformity, room allocation and fixings.

### 4. Restaurant furniture manufacturing

Document seating plans, service clearances, cleaning routines and surfaces exposed to spills. Request seat-fixing details, edge treatment and replaceable components; test stability, cleanability and unobstructed routes.

### 5. Bespoke furniture manufacturing

Send a reference image or sketch with site measurements, and establish whether design, fabrication, delivery and installation are included. Approve material samples, finishes and shop drawings before cutting; compare lead times and procedures for dimensional discrepancies.

### 6. Furniture repair and refurbishment

Photograph damage from several angles and distinguish frame, hardware, veneer and upholstery defects. Ask for a diagnosis separating repair from replacement and a finish-colour sample; inspect joint strength and colour consistency afterward.

### 7. Project furniture supply

Prepare an item-coded bill of quantities with locations, specifications and phased deliveries, assigning storage responsibility. Compare sample compliance, packaging, assembly and aftercare; sign off item by item rather than by shipment alone.

## Furniture and furnishings retailers

### 8. Home furniture retail

Measure the clear room, doorway and lift dimensions before choosing showroom sizes. Confirm frame and upholstery materials, return terms and installation; inspect each piece on delivery and record damage or missing parts.

### 9. Office furniture retail

Compare workstation dimensions against services, chair movement and storage needs, not appearance alone. Request surface and hardware specifications plus an assembly plan; check heights, stability and all accessories at handover.

### 10. Lighting retail

Define each luminaire's purpose, location and colour temperature, with ceiling and power-point photographs. Compare energy use, dimming, warranty and electrical compatibility; test actual light output before sign-off.

### 11. Curtain retail

Record opening width, mounting height, window swing and desired light control. Request fabric samples, operating mechanism and track details; verify drape alignment and free movement after installation.

### 12. Rug retail

Define floor area, surrounding furniture, substrate and foot traffic to select size and pile. Compare fibres, cleaning requirements, slip resistance and edge binding; inspect colour, dimensions and flatness on delivery.

### 13. Home accessories retail

Identify placement and shelf/wall dimensions before buying mirrors, brackets or decorative pieces. Compare fixing materials, item weight and maintenance; check edges and secure mounting without damaging finishes.

### 14. Project furniture supply from retailers

For multi-room retail procurement, require a consolidated item schedule, colour codes, quantities and delivery batches. Compare replacement policy and matching stock; reconcile shortage and damage records before final payment.

## Majlis and sofa upholstery

### 15. Traditional majlis tailoring

Specify seating capacity, seat depth, back height and corner configuration using a room plan. Request fabric and foam samples and removable-cover details; test comfort and seam alignment before acceptance.

### 16. Custom sofa fabrication

Photograph the space and measure doorways and lifts, then choose straight, corner or modular construction. Compare timber frames, cushion density and fabrics; inspect seat levels, leg stability and conformity to drawings.

### 17. Furniture reupholstery

Inspect the frame, springs and padding before stripping fabric, and identify replacement components. Request photographs, fabric swatches, stitching details and separate repair pricing; test tension, seating and edge finishing at handover.

### 18. Sofa fabric replacement

Count and measure pieces, photograph stains and tears, and select fabric for use and cleanability. Confirm fabric allowance, pattern direction, removal and transport scope; check colour matching and seams.

### 19. Foam and cushion replacement

Describe sagging or excessive firmness and identify cushions needing replacement rather than replacing the entire sofa. Compare foam thickness, density and feel using samples; check level seating and cushion recovery.

## Curtains, carpets and soft furnishings

### 20. Custom curtain tailoring

Measure wall width and ceiling height, specify pleat style and privacy needs, and photograph AC or other obstructions. Approve fabric, lining, track and sewing sample; test closure, alignment and floor clearance.

### 21. Curtain installation

Identify wall substrate, concealed electrical routes and fixing positions before drilling. Compare bracket types, track capacity and operation; test opening and closing without obstructing windows.

### 22. Upholstery fabric supply

Provide upholstery area, cushion count and pattern direction to estimate yardage and waste. Request batch numbers, colour swatches and care instructions; match delivered rolls to samples before cutting.

### 23. Carpet and fitted carpet supply

Measure each room, lay direction, seams and thresholds, noting usage. Compare pile thickness, underlay, fixing and cleaning methods; inspect colour consistency and straight seams after laying.

### 24. Mattress supply

Record bed-base dimensions, allowable height, firmness preference and ventilation needs. Compare mattress layers, warranty, trial and delivery terms; check labels, dimensions and packaging on arrival.
