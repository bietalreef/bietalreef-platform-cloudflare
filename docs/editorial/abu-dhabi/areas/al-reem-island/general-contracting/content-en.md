## Building and villa construction

### 1. Villa construction

A villa construction enquiry needs plot dimensions, floor count, room requirements, parking and service access. Provide project drawings or clarify the design stage before seeking comparable bids: a rate per square metre does not define foundations or finish quality. Compare itemised excavation, concrete, waterproofing and building-services schedules, inspection duties and interfaces. Accept work in documented stages against approved drawings, test records and defect lists.

### 2. Residential building construction

Multi-unit residential buildings require planning for shared circulation, vertical services and plant spaces. Provide unit counts, areas, drawings and lift requirements where relevant. Ask bidders to separate structural work, common systems, individual-unit finishes and connections, identifying commissioning responsibility. Handover must cover corridors, plant rooms and operating tests as well as the apartments.

### 3. Commercial building construction

The intended commercial use affects loading, delivery access, space division and utility provision. Supply drawings, a use brief and the boundary between landlord works and tenant fit-out rather than requesting a generic building price. Compare electrical, ventilation, cooling and façade specifications together with exclusions. Handover should check as-built documents, maintenance access and system commissioning.

### 4. Building works execution

Building works can mean blockwork, structural work or limited alterations, so the enquiry must break the scope into measurable tasks. Supply site photographs, dimensions and drawings; clarify demolition, waste removal and protection of occupied spaces. Compare quantities, units, materials and preparation allowances. Check alignment, dimensions and fixings before finishes conceal the work.

### 5. Concrete works execution

Identify the concrete element, location, dimensions and design-specified grade, and supply reinforcement, formwork and opening details. The quotation should cover supply, pumping, curing and agreed testing. Reinforcement changes require competent design review rather than assumptions from photographs. Inspection begins before the pour and continues through pour records, test results and checks after striking formwork.

### 6. Structural frame construction

The structural frame comprises foundations, columns, beams and slabs whose quantities cannot be inferred from floor area alone. Supply structural drawings, steel and concrete schedules and coordinated openings. Compare formwork, temporary supports, pour sequencing and testing allowances. Record inspections and resolve defects before structural elements are concealed.

### 7. Turnkey project delivery

Turnkey delivery must be defined through a responsibility schedule stating whether design, coordination, structure, building services, finishes, furnishings and external works are included. Provide drawings, expected finish standards and material schedules; compare exclusions, payment stages and approvals. Completion requires operating tests, warranties, documents and closure of defects—not merely finished paintwork.

### 8. New villa building

For a new villa, define future household needs, room sizes, privacy, circulation and equipment locations before freezing the design. Ask bidders to explain construction stages, material procurement, external works and service connections. Compare specifications and quantities rather than reference images alone. Inspect waterproofing, building systems and finishes progressively and collect final documentation.

### 9. Residential annex construction

An annex needs measured available space, its relationship to the existing building, access and drainage and electrical connection points. The design must address excavation effects, junctions, waterproofing and movement joints. Supply photographs, an existing-site plan and dimensions; price connections and reinstatement separately. Handover checks interfaces, leaks and finishes.

### 10. Outdoor majlis construction

An outdoor majlis brief should state seating capacity, floor area, entrances, washrooms and ancillary facilities before sizing the building. Review shade, insulation, cooling, ceiling heights and service circulation using measured drawings. Compare structure, finishes, systems and surrounding works separately. Handover checks cooling, drainage, lighting and potential water ingress.

### 11. Villa extensions

Before extending an existing villa, identify the elements being altered and compare existing and proposed plans. A qualified specialist must assess structural capacity and existing services. Document junctions, worker access and protection of occupied rooms. Price demolition, strengthening, new work and reinstatement separately. Inspect old-to-new connections, waterproofing, cracks and operation at handover.

### 12. Residential building alterations

Alterations may involve partition changes, new openings or a changed room function, each with different structural and service implications. Describe existing and proposed use and provide measured plans and photographs. Establish whether walls are load-bearing before removal and have the relevant professional check project-specific approval requirements. Compare reinstatement scope and test affected systems after completion.

### 13. Villa restoration

Villa restoration starts by diagnosing deterioration rather than covering it with new paint or cladding. Photograph damp patches, cracks and material decay; record when symptoms began and earlier repairs. Request a scope distinguishing structural treatment, waterproofing and finishes, with inspection criteria. At handover confirm the cause has been addressed and affected areas tested.

### 14. Residential building refurbishment

A residential refurbishment should inventory affected apartments, corridors and shared systems and define phasing if residents remain. Prepare a condition schedule for finishes, doors, electrical and plumbing works, and specify replacement materials, resident protection and waste handling. Compare the same items and service shutdown windows. Handover covers shared areas, tests and recorded defects.
