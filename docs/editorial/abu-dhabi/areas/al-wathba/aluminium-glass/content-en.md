# Metal, aluminium, glass and woodworking services in Al Wathba

This guide describes company and establishment services without claiming provider availability. Biet Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request with the customer's name, contact number, area, service, photographs, dimensions, specifications and preferred timing even when no provider is listed. Individual tradespeople are classified separately.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Start with finished-wall dimensions and the positions of water, drainage, power and extraction. Request an appliance layout with door and drawer clearances, board specifications, moisture exposure limits, edge finishes and hinge types. Compare the shop drawing with the cabinet schedule and approve fabrication only after site dimensions are checked.

### 2. Kitchen cabinet fabrication

After drawings are approved, specify board thickness, surface finish, cabinet dimensions and appliance cut-outs. Ask for a finish and edge sample plus shelf and back-panel fixing details; clarify whether worktops, sinks and accessories are included. Inspect completed units against the fabrication schedule before dispatch.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Installation starts by checking floor and wall levels and locating concealed services before drilling. Require aligned cabinets, consistent front gaps and correct worktop heights while protecting existing finishes. At handover, test every door and drawer, check anchorage and inspect appropriate junction seals.

### 4. Wardrobe design

Provide room dimensions, ceiling height, switch and air-conditioning locations, and the room-door swing. The design should allocate hanging space, shelves and drawers, confirm cabinet depth and consider ventilation where appropriate. Request internal elevations and sliding or hinged-door details, checking furniture clearances.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

A door count alone is insufficient: specify module widths, depth, board and edging, clothes-rail supports and drawer runners. Record door finish, handles and any integrated lighting. Before delivery, inspect dimensions, edging quality, panel flatness and surface condition.

### 6. Walk-in dressing room fit-out

Measure circulation routes and hanging heights before assigning space for long garments, shoes and bags. Coordinate lighting, mirrors and outlets before installation, without blocking service access. Check that drawers and shelves remain usable with doors fully open.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

Identify whether defects affect hinges, runners, swollen boards or only visible fronts. Photograph damage and ask for separate repair and replacement prices; water leaks or moisture sources must be resolved before replacing panels. Test repaired mechanisms and inspect colour and finish matching at handover.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Record the finished opening, handing, wall thickness, frame type and door construction, including any acoustic or moisture-resistance needs. Specify veneer or paint, lockset and hinges in the quotation. Inspect leaf straightness, edges and hardware preparations before installation.

### 9. Door installation

Check the opening and finished-floor level before fixing the frame. The frame must be plumb, with even leaf gaps and no rubbing against floor or jamb; fixing points should be made good without damaging finishes. Operate the latch, handle and closing action repeatedly at handover.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door is a tested assembly, not an ordinary door with special paint. Specify the required rating and request evidence covering the compatible leaf, frame, hardware and seals under the project's applicable authority requirements. Substitutions require technical review; acceptance should be checked by a qualified specialist.

### 11. Door-frame fabrication and fitting

Specify frame material, section dimensions and compatibility with wall thickness and door type. Check square corners, hinge positions, anchorage and finish junctions. At handover verify that the frame is not twisted, is secure and meets surrounding paint or cladding neatly.

### 12. Timber partition installation

Clarify whether the partition is decorative or divides usable space, then provide its height, length, fixing locations and concealed-service positions. Select an appropriate interior material and specify light, airflow or integrated-door requirements. Check stability, slat alignment and safe finished edges.

### 13. Door maintenance

Describe the actual fault: sagging leaf, sticking lock, noisy hinges or swollen timber. Hinge adjustment or hardware replacement may avoid a complete new door, but recurring moisture needs its source corrected. Test opening, closing and locking after repair and record replaced parts.

### 14. Timber refinishing and painting

Inspect the existing coating, veneer condition and scratches before selecting preparation, filler and topcoat. Request a small sample to approve colour and sheen, and protect floors and metal fittings while providing suitable ventilation. At handover inspect colour consistency, runs and visible application marks.

## Wooden furniture and decorative joinery

### 15. Custom furniture fabrication

For furniture that standard sizes cannot accommodate, supply overall dimensions, intended location and expected shelf loads. Agree board or timber type, edge treatment, joints, finish and colour sample, and whether delivery and assembly are included. Check dimensions, stability and safe edges at handover.

### 16. TV console fabrication

Confirm screen width, viewing height and power/data outlets before approving a TV console. Allow equipment ventilation and cable access; a wall-mounted unit needs a suitable supporting wall and fixing design. Compare surface materials and drawer hardware, then test anchorage and service access.

### 17. Storage-unit fabrication

List what the unit will hold—files, tools, shoes or household items—because depth, shelf spacing and loading differ. Supply wall width, clear height and photographs of obstructions, and specify locks or ventilation if needed. Test doors and shelf supports at handover.

### 18. Timber wall cladding

Measure wall area, sockets, door openings and corners, and inspect dampness and flatness before fitting timber cladding. Specify panel construction, grain direction, joints and access to services. Check alignment, edges and that ventilation openings remain unobstructed.

### 19. CNC decorative joinery

Provide approved artwork, dimensions, board thickness and the required cut-out pattern. Clarify whether the CNC panel is decorative or a screen needing structural support. Approve a cutting sample for detail and finish, then inspect edges and fixings after installation.

### 20. Reception and service counter fit-out

A reception desk and a retail counter differ in working heights, device space and staff circulation. Provide a layout, service points and user count, then specify a surface suited to wear and cleaning. Check front alignment, legroom and drawers at handover.

### 21. Retail and office furniture fabrication

Before fabricating desks or display shelving, confirm workstation count, circulation, equipment and merchandise weights. The quotation should list modules, cable provisions and floor protection during fitting. Inspect stability, suitable loading and access to outlets.

### 22. Interior space planning

This is a planning service, not automatically a fabrication contract. Supply existing plans, photos, dimensions and storage and circulation needs. Request a furniture layout, detailed elevations and material schedule before pricing works, checking conflicts with doors, AC and lighting.

### 23. Joinery fabrication and installation

For a combined joinery fabrication and fitting contract, request a component schedule, shop drawings and final site dimensions before cutting. Assign responsibility for transport, lifting, fixing and making good. Verify drawing compliance, finish protection and moving parts.

### 24. Majlis joinery fit-out

Plan seating, openings and access routes before selecting fixed majlis joinery, wall finishes and tables. Coordinate AC outlets, lighting and sockets, and distinguish upholstery if contracted separately. Inspect alignment, safe details and cleanability at handover.

### 25. Bedroom joinery fit-out

Start with bed size, circulation, wardrobe and drawer clearances and lighting positions. Coordinate headboard, bedside units and storage without blocking switches or AC outlets. Approve a finish sample, then check anchorage and full opening of each unit.

### 26. Office and shop joinery fit-out

Survey finished dimensions and map service, display, storage and customer/staff routes. Separate joinery from electrical work, signage and painting in the scope to avoid double pricing. Check furniture edges, shelf stability and as-built layout dimensions.

### 27. Decorative timber installations

Specify whether the work comprises wall slats, trims or ceiling features: each needs a different fixing approach and service coordination. Provide site photos, section details, joint spacing and colour, and approve a repeated-piece sample. Inspect secure fixing and finished ends.

### 28. Interior joinery finishing and handover

Prepare a room-by-room schedule of completed joinery and outstanding defects before handover. Inspect scratches, joints, colour consistency, locks, hinges and cleaning, then record remedial items and agreed close-out dates. Joinery acceptance is not acceptance of structural or electrical works.

## Aluminium doors and windows

### 29. Aluminium window fabrication

Fabrication should begin with a schedule of finished opening widths and heights, opening directions and frame fixing positions. Request shop drawings showing aluminium profiles, thicknesses, finishes, glazing, gaskets and drainage details; a frame-only price cannot be compared with a glazed, fully fitted unit. Check dimensions, corner alignment, finish and operation at the workshop before dispatch.

### 30. Aluminium window installation

Survey the actual openings, sill levels and surrounding finishes before installation, and protect adjacent floors and façade surfaces. Establish who removes the old unit, makes good the opening and seals the frame-to-wall junction, including the proposed fixing and sealing products. At handover, operate locks and sashes and inspect joints and drainage; a simple water spray is not a substitute for all performance checks.

### 31. Aluminium door fabrication

Specify whether the door is internal or external, the clear opening, handing and threshold level. Hinged and sliding doors require different profiles, rollers, hinges and locking arrangements, so ask for fabrication drawings, a finish sample and a hardware schedule, including any glazing. Compare quotes for the complete leaf, frame, seals and fittings, and test alignment and locking before delivery.

### 32. Aluminium door installation

Before fitting an aluminium door, verify that the substrate can accept the proposed fixings and that the swing or sliding track has sufficient clearance. Agree removal and disposal of the old door, protection of finishes and making good; concealed services must be checked before drilling. Test the door and lock repeatedly at handover and look for floor rubbing or gaps at the perimeter.

### 33. Screen fitting

Clarify whether the screen is intended to exclude insects or protect a ventilation opening, as mesh aperture, material and frame details differ. Send photographs and measurements of the operable window area and note any handles or sliding panels that obstruct fixing. Compare removable and cleanable mesh options, then inspect tension, secure edges and freedom of window movement.

### 34. Door and window maintenance

Describe the symptom rather than requesting a general repair: a dragging sash, failed roller, leaking joint, broken lock or cracked pane. Photograph the problem and the existing profile or component where possible; adjustment or gasket replacement may avoid replacing the entire assembly. Ask for diagnosis, parts and labour to be itemised, then verify operation and correction of the underlying leak or fault.

### 35. Hardware and glazing replacement

For hardware replacement, identify the handle, lock or hinge with photographs, dimensions and fixing pattern. Glazing replacement also requires pane dimensions, thickness, glass type and appropriate safety characteristics for its location, checked against the existing frame. Compare supply, fitting and broken-glass disposal separately, and inspect secure glazing, safe edges and correct operation.

### 36. Aluminium kitchen fabrication

Measure the finished kitchen walls and coordinate sink, drainage, electrical outlets and relevant appliance connections before fabrication. Ask for a cabinet and drawer layout, ventilation cut-outs, panel and profile specifications, surface finishes and edge details; confirm whether worktops and sinks are included. Compare hinges, runners and moisture-management details and approve finish samples before production.

### 37. Aluminium kitchen installation

Installation should start with checking floor levels, wall alignment, service positions and the approved cabinet layout. Define who modifies cut-outs, fixes cabinets and connects appliances: fitting cabinets does not automatically include plumbing or electrical work. At handover, open every drawer and door, check level and anchorage, and inspect joints near wet areas.

### 38. Aluminium cabinet fabrication

Aluminium storage cabinets must suit their intended contents, loading, exposure to moisture and available space. Supply overall dimensions and expected shelf loads, and request profile, panel, bracing, ventilation and lock specifications. Confirm shelf depths and internal divisions before fabrication, then check stability and smooth door and drawer travel.

### 39. Bathroom cabinet fabrication

Measure the space around the basin, wall and door, and locate waste pipes, water supplies and maintenance access points. Select finishes and components suitable for splashes and humidity without assuming every panel is fully waterproof. Compare shelves, hinges, pipe openings and whether the basin and top are included; verify valve access and protection against standing water.

### 40. Outdoor storage unit fabrication

For an outdoor unit, describe the stored items, exposure to sun, rain and dust, and the supporting surface. Request details of the roof, ventilation, drainage, locks and corrosion-resistant hardware, and measure the delivery route and door-opening clearance. Compare anchorage, finishes and maintenance needs, then check stability and that water cannot pool inside.

### 41. Aluminium kitchen refurbishment

Clarify whether refurbishment means replacing fronts, changing hinges, reorganising storage or repairing water damage. Photograph the existing modules and identify what is to remain; inspect the structural condition before covering it with new finishes. Ask for separate repair, replacement and waste-removal costs, and check colour matching, dimensions and door operation after completion.

### 42. Kitchen hinge and hardware maintenance

Photograph the failed hinges, drawer runners or handles and record unit counts, door type and approximate weight. Loose mounting screws or a distorted door may be the cause rather than a defective hinge, so inspect the fixing substrate before ordering parts. Compare compatible replacement hardware and warranties, then test each unit through its normal operating range.


## Glass façades and glazing work

### 43. Glass façade construction

Start with the façade area, height, supporting structure and exposure to sun and wind. Survey the openings and have a qualified designer assess movement, drainage and fixing details rather than choosing glass colour alone. Request shop drawings specifying glazing build-up, framing, seals and responsibility for access equipment and lifting. At handover inspect joint alignment, edge condition, anchorage and the performance tests agreed in the contract.

### 44. Toughened glass installation

Identify whether the toughened glass is for a door, partition or fixed pane. Measure the finished opening and finalise holes, hinges and handle positions before processing: toughened glass cannot be cut or drilled after tempering. Require the designer to specify suitable thickness, glass type and edge treatment; the word 'tempered' alone does not establish compliance. Inspect edge condition, alignment and hardware operation.

### 45. Shopfront construction

For a shopfront quote provide opening width and height, entrance arrangement, signage and lighting positions, and any existing emergency exits. Coordinate glazing, frames, thresholds and locks with access and maintenance requirements; check relevant approvals without assuming they have been issued. Compare like-for-like offers including removal, making good and sealing, then test door operation and agreed water-resistance details.

### 46. Glass door installation

Specify whether the door is hinged, sliding or automatic, its clear passage width, travel direction and finished threshold. Obtain a schedule for glass type, pivots or track, handles, locks and closers, with safety and accessibility reviewed by the responsible specialist. Check clearances, balance, smooth travel and freedom from collision at handover.

### 47. Glass partition construction

Provide a partition layout with lengths, heights, door positions and connections to suspended ceilings and concealed services. Define privacy and acoustic requirements, glazing specification and support method before pricing; assign responsibility for making good floors and ceilings. Compare glass build-up, framing and fittings, then inspect stability, joints and door alignment.

### 48. Glass balustrade installation

A glass balustrade is fall protection, not just decoration. The responsible designer must establish guard height, design loads, suitable safety glazing and anchorage into the actual concrete or steel substrate. Request reviewed drawings and calculations where required; do not select glass thickness by guesswork. Inspect base fixings, protected edges and movement against the project acceptance criteria.

### 49. Glass façade maintenance

Photograph and locate the defect: broken pane, leakage, failed gasket or loose fixing, including height and access constraints. Secure any hazardous area and plan safe access before work. Replacement glazing may need to match the original façade system rather than accept a patch repair. Request a cause report, parts schedule and test plan, then verify secure glazing and sealed joints.



## Steel and metal fabrication

### 50. Steel door fabrication

Supply finished opening dimensions, swing direction, exposure and security needs. Request fabrication drawings specifying steel sections, thickness, hinges, lock, reinforcement and corrosion protection. Compare supply, fitting and finishing scope, then inspect frame alignment, welds, coating and locking action.

### 51. Metal railing fabrication

Identify the railing location, length, stair or balcony levels and supporting substrate. A competent designer must establish height, spacing, loading and fixings; a decorative photograph is not a safe fabrication specification. Review weld details and coating sample, then check stability, spacing and safe edges.

### 52. Canopy fabrication

Record the covered area, vehicle or pedestrian headroom, columns and buried services. Review structural loads, wind resistance, anchors and drainage before fabrication, and specify covering and corrosion protection. Compare foundations, frame, covering and erection separately; inspect alignment, rainwater run-off and connections.

### 53. Structural steelwork

Start with drawings showing dimensions, loads, use and connections to existing structures. Require engineered member, weld and bolt details, temporary bracing, lifting plans and inspection requirements; visual judgement alone cannot size structural members. Compare fabrication, transport, erection and testing, then review alignment and connection inspection records.

### 54. Stainless steelwork

Specify whether the work is a handrail, worktop or cladding and describe humidity and cleaning chemicals. Request alloy grade, thickness, finish and welding details; shine alone does not guarantee corrosion resistance. Approve a polished sample, then inspect edges, finish consistency, joints and care instructions.

### 55. Welding services

Provide photographs, metal type, thickness, joint location and whether the task is structural or a non-structural repair. Critical welds need procedures and inspection defined by qualified personnel; covering a structural crack with a bead is insufficient. Itemise preparation, consumables, testing and protection, then review inspection records and finish.

### 56. Metalwork maintenance

Photograph corrosion, distortion, loose anchors or cracked welds and describe the component's function. Separate a coating defect from damage affecting a gate or guard's integrity; obtain engineering assessment before reuse where uncertain. Request itemised repairs, protection and final inspection, and check the cause rather than appearance alone.
