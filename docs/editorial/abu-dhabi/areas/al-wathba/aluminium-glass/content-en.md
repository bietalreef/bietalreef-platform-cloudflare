# Metal, aluminium, glass and woodworking services in Al Wathba

This guide describes company and establishment services without claiming provider availability. Biet Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request with the customer's name, contact number, area, service, photographs, dimensions, specifications and preferred timing even when no provider is listed. Individual tradespeople are classified separately.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Start with finished-wall dimensions and the positions of water, drainage, power and extraction. Request an appliance layout with door and drawer clearances, board specifications, moisture exposure limits, edge finishes and hinge types. Compare the shop drawing with the cabinet schedule and approve fabrication only after site dimensions are checked.

### 2. Kitchen cabinet fabrication

After drawings are approved, specify board thickness, surface finish, cabinet dimensions and appliance cut-outs. Ask for a finish and edge sample plus shelf and back-panel fixing details; clarify whether worktops, sinks and accessories are included. Inspect completed units against the fabrication schedule before dispatch.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Installation starts by checking floor and wall levels and locating concealed services before drilling. Require aligned cabinets, consistent front gaps and correct worktop heights while protecting existing finishes. At handover, test every door and drawer, check anchorage and inspect appropriate junction seals.

### 4. Wardrobe design

Provide room dimensions, ceiling height, switch and air-conditioning locations, and the room-door swing. The design should allocate hanging space, shelves and drawers, confirm cabinet depth and consider ventilation where appropriate. Request internal elevations and sliding or hinged-door details, checking furniture clearances.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

A door count alone is insufficient: specify module widths, depth, board and edging, clothes-rail supports and drawer runners. Record door finish, handles and any integrated lighting. Before delivery, inspect dimensions, edging quality, panel flatness and surface condition.

### 6. Walk-in dressing room fit-out

Measure circulation routes and hanging heights before assigning space for long garments, shoes and bags. Coordinate lighting, mirrors and outlets before installation, without blocking service access. Check that drawers and shelves remain usable with doors fully open.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

Identify whether defects affect hinges, runners, swollen boards or only visible fronts. Photograph damage and ask for separate repair and replacement prices; water leaks or moisture sources must be resolved before replacing panels. Test repaired mechanisms and inspect colour and finish matching at handover.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Record the finished opening, handing, wall thickness, frame type and door construction, including any acoustic or moisture-resistance needs. Specify veneer or paint, lockset and hinges in the quotation. Inspect leaf straightness, edges and hardware preparations before installation.

### 9. Door installation

Check the opening and finished-floor level before fixing the frame. The frame must be plumb, with even leaf gaps and no rubbing against floor or jamb; fixing points should be made good without damaging finishes. Operate the latch, handle and closing action repeatedly at handover.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door is a tested assembly, not an ordinary door with special paint. Specify the required rating and request evidence covering the compatible leaf, frame, hardware and seals under the project's applicable authority requirements. Substitutions require technical review; acceptance should be checked by a qualified specialist.

### 11. Door-frame fabrication and fitting

Specify frame material, section dimensions and compatibility with wall thickness and door type. Check square corners, hinge positions, anchorage and finish junctions. At handover verify that the frame is not twisted, is secure and meets surrounding paint or cladding neatly.

### 12. Timber partition installation

Clarify whether the partition is decorative or divides usable space, then provide its height, length, fixing locations and concealed-service positions. Select an appropriate interior material and specify light, airflow or integrated-door requirements. Check stability, slat alignment and safe finished edges.

### 13. Door maintenance

Describe the actual fault: sagging leaf, sticking lock, noisy hinges or swollen timber. Hinge adjustment or hardware replacement may avoid a complete new door, but recurring moisture needs its source corrected. Test opening, closing and locking after repair and record replaced parts.

### 14. Timber refinishing and painting

Inspect the existing coating, veneer condition and scratches before selecting preparation, filler and topcoat. Request a small sample to approve colour and sheen, and protect floors and metal fittings while providing suitable ventilation. At handover inspect colour consistency, runs and visible application marks.
