# Cleaning, disinfection and pest-control services in Al Wathba

This company and establishment service guide does not imply a listed provider or available appointment. Individual tradespeople are classified separately.

## Homes and building cleaning

### 1. Villa cleaning

List rooms, bathrooms, kitchens, glazing and floor types, with photos of stubborn deposits. Compare oven, cupboard and interior-window cleaning as explicit items; inspect corners, drains and surfaces at handover.

### 2. Apartment cleaning

Share apartment area, furnishing status and kitchen and bathroom photos. Agree interior windows, appliances and material-specific floor cleaning, then check wall marks and areas behind furniture.

### 3. Office cleaning

Specify workstations, meeting rooms, toilets and permitted access hours. Compare shared-surface cleaning, waste removal and protection of devices and documents; use a checklist that avoids disruption.

### 4. Building cleaning

Describe floors, corridors, lifts, entrances and common areas. Separate high-level facade work from routine floor cleaning; compare visit schedules, completion records and complaint handling.

### 5. Post-construction cleaning

Photograph gypsum dust, paint residues and adhesive, and identify tile and glass finishes. Require bulk debris removal first and protection of delicate finishes; check cement marks without indiscriminate acid use.

### 6. Pre-move cleaning

Document property condition before keys are handed over, including rooms, appliances and cupboards. Require cleaning and drying of interiors, touchpoints and bathrooms; photograph pre-existing damage.

### 7. Recurring cleaning contracts

Specify weekly visit count, hours, areas, consumables and who supplies them. Compare each visit's checklist, completion evidence and issue resolution rather than only a lump-sum monthly price.

## Upholstery cleaning

### 8. Steam sofa cleaning

Photograph stains and identify fabric, colour and care label: steam is unsuitable for some upholstery. Require a colourfastness test and drying estimate; check moisture and stain rings afterwards.

### 9. Rug cleaning

List rugs, dimensions, fibre types and whether off-site washing is possible. Compare dust removal, spot treatment, rinsing and drying; inspect fringes, colour and odour on return.

### 10. Carpet cleaning

State fixed-carpet area, pile type, stains and heavy-furniture locations. Clarify moving furniture, water extraction and reopening time; inspect heavily trafficked paths.

### 11. Arabic majlis cleaning

Photograph majlis seating, cushions and removable mattresses and list fabrics and quantities. Compare cleaning of seams and decorative elements and drying; check colour consistency and moisture inside filling.

### 12. Mattress cleaning

Provide mattress size, both surfaces, stain type, odour and care label. Request deep extraction and suitable spot treatment without soaking foam; check dryness before replacing bedding.

### 13. Curtain cleaning

State curtain drop, width, fabric and whether removal is needed. Compare on-site and off-site methods and shrinkage risks; inspect pleats, stitching and refitting after drying.

### 14. Upholstery sanitisation

Define the reason for sanitisation, fabric type, use and likely exposure. Request product name, contact and drying instructions and fabric compatibility; spraying does not replace soil removal.

## Facade and site cleaning

### 15. Glass facade cleaning

Provide facade height, area, glass type and safe access method. Require a work plan, pedestrian protection and a test on coated glass; inspect streaks from different viewing angles.

### 16. Building facade cleaning

Identify facade material, soiling, height and exposure. Compare access method, wash pressure, approved chemicals and drainage; inspect joints and cladding after cleaning.

### 17. Parking-area cleaning

Specify parking area, levels, oil stains, drains and vehicle movements. Agree work-zone isolation and liquid-waste handling; inspect floor and drainage before reopening.

### 18. Industrial site cleaning

Describe industrial processes, possible residues, production lines and prohibited contact points. Require risk assessment and equipment isolation where needed; compare waste collection and sign-off before restart.

## Tank and pool cleaning

### 19. Water tank cleaning

Provide tank volume, material, access hatch and sediment condition. Request safe draining and cleaning procedures and before-and-after evidence; confined-space entry requires specialist precautions.

### 20. Tank disinfection

Disinfection follows sediment removal and surface cleaning; specify tank volume and intended water use. Request a suitable agent, dose, contact time and any required flushing or testing, documenting recommissioning.

### 21. Swimming pool cleaning

Provide pool volume, filtration arrangement, algae or sediment condition and finish. Compare wall and floor cleaning, baskets, filters and water balancing; check clarity and test readings before use.

## Pest control

### 22. Cockroach control

Identify sightings, food and water sources and gaps, with kitchen and drain photos. Require source control and sealing plus appropriate bait or treatment; monitor activity after the visit.

### 23. Ant control

Photograph ant trails and note entry points, moisture and nearby planting. Compare colony treatment with superficial spraying; agree follow-up and keep chemicals away from food and children.

### 24. Rodent control

Identify gnawing, droppings, storage areas and service penetrations. Request exclusion, monitored traps and safe waste handling, documenting results and protecting occupants from bait.

### 25. Bedbug control

Document suspected bite locations and bed and upholstery crevices before treatment. Compare inspection, permitted heat or chemical methods, room preparation and repeat visits; avoid instant-eradication promises.

### 26. Recurring pest-control contracts

Specify pest species, history, treatment zones and inspection frequency. Compare visit reports, trap maps and escalation plans; check provider and product authorisation before contracting.

## Disinfection

### 27. Home spraying and disinfection

Define the reason for disinfection, target surfaces and whether occupants are present. Request product label, contact time, ventilation and protection measures; fogging is not automatically needed in every home.

### 28. Office and facility disinfection

Supply a map of work areas, occupancy hours, high-touch surfaces and sensitive equipment. Compare prior cleaning, disinfectant application and records, agreeing safe re-entry timing.

## Building operations and maintenance

### 29. Maintenance management

Provide fault history, priorities, asset inventory and owner/operator responsibilities. Require ticket logging, response targets and documented closure; compare performance against agreed indicators.

### 30. Facilities operations

Specify operating hours and HVAC, electrical, water and security systems within scope. Compare staffing, readings and emergency escalation; inspect reporting consistency, not attendance alone.

### 31. Preventive maintenance

Supply equipment inventories, required maintenance intervals and manufacturer guidance. Compare inspection procedures, consumables and planned shutdowns; request reports of completed work and outstanding issues.

### 32. Electrical systems monitoring

Identify panels, circuits, loads and recurring faults without asking unqualified people to open equipment. Require competent inspections, readings and corrective records, with safe isolation and re-energisation.

### 33. HVAC systems monitoring

List units, operating hours, temperatures, complaints and filter-cleaning history. Compare airflow, leak and drainage checks and settings; obtain before-and-after readings.

### 34. Plumbing and drainage monitoring

Photograph leaks, slow drains, valves and pumps. Require cause identification before demolition; compare suitable pressure or flow testing and documentation of repair and reinstatement.

### 35. Building condition reports

Define report purpose, floor count, assets and visible defects. Request dated photos, risk priorities and inspection limitations; a visual report is not a structural safety certificate.

## Staffing and operations

### 36. Cleaning staff supply

Specify staff numbers, locations, shifts, duties and cleaning supplies. Compare supervision, training, attendance, replacement and safety; labour supply does not automatically include materials.

### 37. Loading staff supply

State weights, sizes, floors, carrying distance and goods type. Request suitable crew size and handling equipment, protecting corridors; check counts and condition after loading.

### 38. Maintenance technician supply

Specify electrical, HVAC or plumbing trades, working hours and assets. Verify competence, responsibility and escalation; request work logs and closure testing.

### 39. Site supervisor supply

Define team count, supervisory duties, coverage hours and quality and safety reporting. Compare authority, nonconformance logging and client coordination; receive auditable periodic reports.

### 40. Residential compound operations

List buildings, units, shared facilities and service hours. Compare cleaning, maintenance, ticketing and resident communications, with trackable performance and complaints.

### 41. Commercial facilities operations

Specify commercial activity, peak hours, public areas and technical services. Compare operating coverage, breakdown response and tenant coordination; inspect response and continuity measures.

### 42. Project support services

Explain project stage, support duration, team size and logistics or administrative needs. Compare task boundaries, hours, attendance and reporting; accept work against documented deliverables.

## Request a quotation

Biet Al Reef customer service accepts requests even without a listed company. Include name, contact number, area, service, property type, floor area or item counts, photographs, safety notes and preferred timing. A request does not confirm price, availability or appointment.
