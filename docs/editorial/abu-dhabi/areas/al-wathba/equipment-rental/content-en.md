# Factory and equipment rental services in Al Wathba

This is an editorial guide to company and establishment services, not a claim that any supplier or machine is available. Individual tradespeople are listed separately.

## Construction and lifting equipment rental

### 1. Scaffolding rental

Specify working-platform height, façade length and the number of working levels, supported by photographs of the ground and access routes. Request an erection plan, edge protection and a pre-use inspection; price transport, assembly, dismantling and rental duration separately.

### 2. Crane rental

Provide the load weight and dimensions, pick-up and set-down positions, and lifting radius rather than weight alone. Compare rated capacity at the actual radius, outrigger footprint, lift plan, operator, insurance and mobilisation; lifting must await site-specific checks.

### 3. Excavation equipment rental

State excavation dimensions, depth, ground conditions, machine turning space and site access, with buried utilities identified before work. Compare excavator size, bucket type, operating hours, haulage and fuel; agree responsibility for spoil removal and edge protection.

### 4. Compaction equipment rental

Define layer area, thickness, fill type and access: a heavy roller is not automatically suitable beside walls or services. Agree the passes and density-testing requirement where specified, with operator, fuel and transport costs identified.

### 5. Concrete mixer rental

Share required batch volume, loading position, power or fuel supply and distance to placement. Compare usable drum capacity, cleaning arrangements and hire hours; a site mixer must not replace specified structural concrete without technical approval.

### 6. Concrete cutting equipment rental

Identify element thickness, cut length, reinforcement and concealed services, with access and slurry-drainage photos. Compare blade and saw selection, cutting depth, dust/noise control and waste capture; structural assessment is required before cutting a load-bearing element.

### 7. General project equipment rental

List equipment against project phases instead of requesting an unspecified package, including delivery dates and access constraints. Check inspection records, maintenance history, breakdown replacement and handover/return terms; separate daily hire from operation and consumables.

## Light tools and equipment rental

### 8. Generator rental

Calculate running loads, motor starting currents and operating hours, then identify location, ventilation and cable routes. Compare kVA rating, fuel autonomy, noise, earthing and protection; connections require competent electrical review.

### 9. Cleaning equipment rental

Describe floor material, area and sensitivity to water or chemicals, and whether washing, extraction or drying is needed. Compare working width, brushes, attachments, tank capacity and run time; test on a small area before acceptance.

### 10. Air compressor rental

Specify tool operating pressure and airflow, simultaneous tool count and hose length. Compare delivered compressor output, air quality, moisture, noise and safety devices; inspect hoses and couplings before operation.

### 11. Power tool rental

Describe the task, material, thickness and access constraints to select the appropriate drill, grinder or saw. Check cables, guards, batteries and bits; allocate consumables, damage and replacement responsibilities in the hire terms.

### 12. Welding equipment rental

State metal type, thickness, welding process, power source and available ventilation. Compare machine output, accessories, shielding gas or electrodes; require spark protection, connection checks and weld inspection before handover.

### 13. Water pump rental

State water source, suction depth, delivery head, required flow and liquid condition, with a photograph of the safe discharge point. Compare the pump performance curve, hose diameter and solids-handling capability; do not discharge to an unapproved location.

### 14. Garden equipment rental

Define working area, plant type, branch diameter and irrigation or cable positions before choosing a trimmer or mower. Compare noise, power source, guards and green-waste handling; inspect blades and safety devices at handover.
