# Al Wathba — Furniture and furnishings

This guide helps define furniture manufacture, retail, upholstery and furnishing requests in Al Wathba. It does not imply a listed supplier, confirmed price or selectable status for restricted or proposed taxonomy categories.

## Furniture manufacturing

### 1. Residential furniture manufacturing

Provide a room plan, access dimensions and a list of seating or bedroom pieces. Request samples of boards and finishes, joint and hinge details and shelf load assumptions. Compare quotes including delivery and assembly; inspect stability and edges at handover.

### 2. Office furniture manufacturing

Specify workstation count, monitor footprints, cable routing and chair clearance. Ask for desk material, thickness, cable ports and disassembly details. Test drawers, legroom and a representative unit before accepting a batch.

### 3. Hotel furniture manufacturing

Prepare a room-type schedule covering beds, wardrobes and tables with consistent finish drawings. Evaluate heavy-use durability, cleanability and replaceable hardware; distinguish supply from installation. Sign off a sample room and defect list before batch production.

### 4. Restaurant furniture manufacturing

Map tables, seats, service aisles and chair pull-back space; distinguish indoor from outdoor use. Request stain and moisture performance, base stability and repair options. Compare cleaning requirements and test table height and wobble before handover.

### 5. Bespoke furniture manufacturing

Supply a reference image and dimensioned drawing including sockets and adjacent door swings. Approve colour, cladding, fixing points and shop drawings before cutting. Compare permitted revisions and measure the finished piece against approved dimensions.

### 6. Furniture repair and refurbishment

Photograph broken joints, swelling or loose fittings from several angles and assess whether the frame can be safely repaired. Price hinge, veneer and fabric replacements separately from rebuilding. Check joint rigidity and finish matching after repair.

### 7. Project furniture supply

Issue a bill of quantities listing models, sizes, colours, destination rooms and delivery phases. Compare samples, substitutions, packaging, storage, assembly and damage procedures. Reconcile each delivery against a numbered inventory.

## Furniture and furnishing retail

### 8. Home furniture retail

Measure access routes, placement area and drawer clearance before ordering. Check frame material, upholstery grade, delivery, returns and assembly terms rather than relying on pictures. Confirm model, colour, movement and stability on receipt.

### 9. Office furniture retail

Define user count, desk footprints, chair adjustability and warranty needs. Request actual dimensions, spares and lead times; compare unit costs with volume assembly. Test chair adjustments and desk fixings at delivery.

### 10. Lighting retail

List each room's task, mounting type, ceiling cut-out and desired colour temperature. Compare declared light output, wattage and driver compatibility; separate fixture supply from electrical installation. Test a sample before bulk ordering.

### 11. Curtain retail

Provide opening width, track height and finished drop; choose blackout level, lining and pleat style. Compare fabric-by-metre prices separately from stitching, track and fitting. Inspect colour, smooth movement and fabric defects.

### 12. Carpet and rug retail

Measure the coverage area and door clearance, noting foot traffic and cleaning needs. Check fibre, backing and edge construction; distinguish ready-made rugs from cut carpet and fitting. Inspect dimensions, straight edges and safe placement.

### 13. Home accessories retail

List mirrors, brackets and handles with sizes, mounting locations and wall substrates. Verify included fixings and stated load ratings; compare any separate installation charge. Check for scratches and missing parts.

### 14. Project furniture supply

For commercial project supply, approve model schedules, quantities, room destinations and samples before ordering. Compare batch availability, finish consistency and replacement policies; itemise transport and installation. Record shortages at each delivery.

## Majlis and sofa upholstery

### 15. Traditional majlis seating

Draw the majlis perimeter, doors and tables; specify seat height, depth and module lengths. Request upholstery and foam samples and connection details; compare removable covers and cleaning. Test comfort and alignment at handover.

### 16. Custom sofa making

Specify sofa configuration, module count, seat depth and entry clearance. Compare frame construction, padding, fabric and wear performance; approve a drawing before cutting. Check dimensions, cushion consistency and leg stability.

### 17. Furniture reupholstery

Inspect frames, springs and padding before choosing fabric: a new cover will not correct a sagging seat. Photograph damage and count pieces; price structural repair, padding and stitching separately. Inspect seam tension and seating comfort.

### 18. Sofa fabric replacement

Record sofa dimensions, fabric repeat direction and cushion count; allow material for cutting waste and pattern matching. Compare cleanability, colourfastness and removal of old covers. Inspect straight seams and aligned patterns.

### 19. Foam and cushion replacement

Identify sagging areas, existing cushion thickness and desired firmness; check springs and support before replacing foam. Request a seating sample, material density and cut dimensions. Compare selective versus full-set replacement and check even support.

## Curtains, carpets and soft furnishings

### 20. Custom curtain tailoring

Measure window width and track-to-floor drop; define fullness, lining and blackout level. Compare fabric consumption, stitching, hems and lining, including shrinkage considerations. Check equal drops and consistent pleats.

### 21. Curtain installation

Identify wall or ceiling substrate, track length, curtain weight and fixing positions while allowing window and AC clearance. Confirm brackets, anchors and old-track removal scope. Test full travel and floor clearance.

### 22. Upholstery fabric supply

State intended use, colour, metres required, roll width and pattern direction. Compare abrasion performance, cleaning instructions, colourfastness and batch consistency; approve a swatch first. Inspect rolls for visible faults.

### 23. Carpet and fitted flooring supply

Supply a floor plan with square metres, laying direction, seams and cutting allowance. Compare pile, backing, adhesives and edge details, separating material from fitting charges. Check batch numbers and colours on delivery.

### 24. Mattress supply

Confirm bed size, mattress depth, preferred firmness and ventilation or cleaning needs. Compare spring versus foam construction, trial and warranty terms and delivery. Check dimensions, label and packaging condition before acceptance.
