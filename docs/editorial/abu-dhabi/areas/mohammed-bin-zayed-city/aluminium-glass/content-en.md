# Mohammed Bin Zayed City — Metal, aluminium, glass and joinery workshops

> **Area scope:** This guide addresses service enquiries in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi. Quote requests should specify the neighbourhood or zone, building or villa reference and site access. Provider availability, travel charges and permits must be confirmed for the actual location. Shared technical guidance is not evidence of previous local work.


Fabrication requests start with actual site measurements, photographs, material selection, expected loads and fixings. Approved shop drawings and samples are distinct from inspiration photos. Where no company is listed, customers may submit details through Biet Al Reef customer support's quotation form without an implied workshop, price or delivery date.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Before selecting a kitchen layout, provide wall-to-wall dimensions, ceiling height, window and door locations, and actual water, drainage and electrical points. Request a dimensioned layout showing the real appliance sizes, door and drawer clearances and work zones. Compare design quotations by construction drawings and included revisions, not renders alone. Check the final drawings against site measurements and approve finishes separately.

### 2. Kitchen cabinetry fabrication

Kitchen fabrication needs a cutting schedule for each carcass and door, specifying board thickness, moisture protection, edging and the exact hinge and runner models. Ask for samples of edge joints and front finishes; compare hardware, cutting, assembly and delivery as distinct quotation items. Before dispatch, inspect square carcasses, labelled units and appliance cut-outs against the approved drawings.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Check floor and wall levels and the intended worktop height before installers arrive; record conflicts with drainage pipes or sockets. The quote should allocate wall fixings, leg adjustment, joint sealing, sink-edge protection and responsibility for discrepancies from the approved drawings. At handover test every drawer and door at full travel, cabinet stability and access to service valves.

### 4. Wardrobe design

For a wardrobe, measure the finished wall width, height and usable depth without blocking room circulation. Count long garments, folded items, shoes and bags to determine the internal layout. Compare hinged and sliding doors for clearance and future maintenance, approve sectional drawings, and test drawer and door movement without rubbing at handover.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

Freeze the approved wardrobe schedule before production: unit dimensions, any ventilation openings, shelf arrangement and wall fixing positions. Compare quotations using board grade and thickness, edge treatment, hinges and runners, with lighting and accessories priced separately. At delivery inspect door alignment and shelf support; document and approve any dimensional deviation before acceptance.

### 6. Walk-in closet fit-out

Measure the clear walking aisle with drawers and doors open, and mark mirror, seating, lighting and power positions. Request a layout that allows access to high shelves, floor cleaning and suitable cabinet ventilation. Compare cleanable finishes and replaceable hardware. At handover test the lighting, drawer operation and the built dimensions against the plan.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

For an existing kitchen or wardrobe, photograph water-swollen panels, loose hinges and peeling edges; assess whether the cabinet carcasses can safely be retained. Request separate prices for doors, runners, shelves and moisture repairs versus full replacement. Check colour matching, even gaps, hinge security and drawer movement before accepting the refurbishment.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Specify whether the wooden door is for an internal room, entrance or moisture-exposed location; provide rough opening dimensions, wall thickness and swing direction. Request the core construction, face finish, edge treatment, hinges and lock specification rather than a generic premium-timber label. Inspect leaf straightness, hardware mortises and finish before installation.

### 9. Door installation

Measure the door opening after flooring and relevant finishes are complete, including threshold height and swing clearance. Compare offers that explicitly include the frame, hinges, lock and making good around the opening; assign responsibility for uneven openings. At handover cycle the door repeatedly and check latch engagement, even clearances and no floor contact.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door must be specified from the approved project schedule and opening requirements, not its appearance or timber thickness. Ask a competent supplier for evidence covering the complete rated assembly: leaf, frame, seals, hinges, closer and permitted installation details. At handover match labels to documents and test self-closing; avoid unapproved site modifications.

### 11. Doorframe construction

Before manufacturing a doorframe, measure finished wall thickness, threshold level and the opening at several points to identify out-of-square conditions. Specify frame material, section, anchorage, hinge positions and moisture exposure. Compare quotes including trims and wall-edge repairs. Check plumb, straightness and secure fixing before hanging the door.

### 12. Wooden partition fabrication

Define whether the timber divider provides visual screening or supports shelves or a screen; supply height, width, slat spacing and anchorage locations. Distinguish solid timber from faced boards in quotations and document edges, joints and access to concealed services. At handover check fixing rigidity, consistent slat spacing and the absence of sharp edges.

### 13. Door repairs

Identify the fault first: floor rubbing, lock failure, loose frame or moisture-swollen leaf. Supply photos of edges, hinges and both sides of the opening, and ask for separate adjustment, replacement-hardware and timber-repair prices. Assess repair before ordering a new door. After work, test closing, latch engagement and hinge security.

### 14. Timber refinishing

Identify the existing coating, peeling and scratched areas, and whether the goal is colour change or restoration. Request a discreet sample to confirm adhesion and colour, plus documented cleaning, sanding, filling, coat sequence and drying time. Inspect the finish in adequate light and check that paint has not clogged locks or impeded door movement.

## Custom furniture and decorative joinery

### 15. Bespoke furniture fabrication

For bespoke furniture, prepare an item-by-item schedule recording purpose, location, finished dimensions and anticipated use; a dining table has different demands from a heavily loaded filing cabinet. Provide site photos and delivery access dimensions, then select board or timber type, upholstery where relevant, and finish. Compare quotations that separately identify shop drawings, samples, hardware, transport and installation. Approve dimensions before fabrication; inspect stability, joints and safe edges at handover.

### 16. TV console fabrication

A TV console must be designed around viewing height, screen size, media-device depth, ventilation and accessible power and data outlets. Specify whether it stands on the floor or hangs from a wall, and establish that the fixing substrate can support the proposed assembly. Compare board construction, mounting system, surface finish and cable-management provision. Before acceptance check stability, drawer movement and service access without trapping equipment.

### 17. Storage unit fabrication

For a storage unit, list the actual contents—documents, shoes, tools or heavy objects—and the expected load on each shelf. Photograph the installation position and measure available depth, door clearance, nearby switches and possible moisture exposure. Ask for board thickness, shelf supports, hinge and runner specifications and appropriate wall anchorage. Do not compare a decorative shelf with a load-bearing storage solution; inspect stability and operation under an agreed representative load.

### 18. Timber wall cladding

Timber wall cladding may be decorative or conceal services, so define its purpose before choosing a pattern. Record wall flatness, damp areas, sockets and maintenance access, and show grain direction and joint spacing on an elevation drawing. Compare solid timber with faced boards, fixing methods, edge protection and allowance for movement. At handover check straight lines and ensure ventilation openings and access panels remain usable.

### 19. CNC decorative joinery

For CNC decorative work, supply a scaled vector drawing or dimensioned pattern specifying panel thickness, corner radii and the smallest cut-out. MDF, solid timber and composite boards behave differently, while sanding and coating alter fine details. Request a sample cut from the proposed material before releasing the whole batch. Compare cutting, finishing, wastage and installation costs; inspect repeat accuracy and smooth, splinter-free edges.

### 20. Custom counters

A reception or sales counter must support the daily workflow as well as the intended appearance. Measure the customer-facing length and height, worktop depth, staff circulation and locations for payment terminals, monitors, printers and cable routes. Specify surfaces exposed to impact or spills. Separate carcass, cladding, worktop, drawers and lighting in the quotation. At handover verify stability, cleanable joints and access to equipment connections.

### 21. Retail and office furniture fabrication

For shop or office furniture, supply a layout showing desks, cabinets, display shelving, aisle widths and anticipated loads rather than only a reference photograph. Distinguish repeatable modular units from site-specific pieces, and mark electrical and data interfaces. Compare workshop offers by samples, material thickness, assembly methods, item labelling, delivery and installation. Check each item against its schedule for stability, door operation and circulation clearance.

### 22. Interior space design

Interior space design within a joinery package should establish workable furniture, cladding and fixed-unit layouts; it does not automatically include engineering drawings or permit services. Provide measured room plans, door and window positions, lighting, air-conditioning and intended occupancy. Define deliverables such as layout, elevations, visuals, material board and fabrication drawings, pricing execution separately. Approve circulation, maintenance access and buildable dimensions before fabrication.

### 23. Joinery fabrication and installation

Distinguish preliminary estimating measurements from final site measurements taken after flooring and ceiling works. Request shop drawings for each joinery unit, showing edges, joints, hinges and anchor positions, and verify that large pieces can pass through doors or lifts. Compare whether trial assembly, protected transport, installation and making good are included. At handover check dimensions, clean joints, secure fixings and movement of all operable parts.

### 24. Majlis fit-out

Majlis fit-out starts with actual seating capacity, seating configuration and circulation around doors, not only decorative wall panels. Provide a room plan showing air-conditioning outlets, screens, curtains and lighting. Separate fixed joinery from sofas, upholstery and electrical work in the quotation. Compare practical layouts and cleanable material samples, with exclusions stated. At handover test seating use, door access and lighting in operation.

### 25. Bedroom fit-out

For a bedroom fit-out, measure the bed footprint, side passages, wardrobe and drawer opening clearance, power outlets, switches and windows. Check soffit heights and potential clashes with air-conditioning or curtain tracks before fabrication. Ask for separate pricing for cabinet carcasses, fronts, fittings and mattresses if included. Inspect bed alignment, wardrobe anchorage, movement around the room and continued window access at handover.

### 26. Office and retail fit-out

Office and retail fit-out often combines counters, fixed furniture and wall finishes, with electrical and lighting interfaces that need clearly assigned responsibility. Provide a plan for workstations or merchandise, storage, existing services and entry and exit routes. Compare shop drawings, quantities and staged delivery dates without assuming that engineering or regulatory approvals are included. Accept completed work zone by zone against a documented snag list.

### 27. Timber decorative features

Timber decorative features may comprise slatted walls, trims or ceiling details. Identify each position, dimension, cleaning method and exposure to moisture or heat. Approve a physical sample for colour, texture, edge profile and concealed fixing; a digital image alone cannot establish the final finish. Compare material, machining, coating, installation and replacement options. Inspect repeated patterns, alignment and fixing security.

### 28. Interior fit-out handover

Interior fit-out handover is a structured inspection, not simply a site clean. Prepare a schedule of cabinets, doors, drawers, cladding and lighting within the joinery contract, with approved drawings, samples and variations. Operate every door and drawer, check scratches, joints, anchors and maintenance access, and record defects, responsibility and reinspection dates. Obtain keys, agreed care instructions, warranty records and a signed list of outstanding items.

### 29. Aluminium window fabrication

Before fabricating windows, identify each opening separately and measure width, height and both diagonals after checking plaster and finished levels. Specify sash operation, drainage routes, glazing and whether the profile suits the dimensions and anticipated loads, with specialist design where needed. Obtain shop drawings showing frame sections, seals, reinforcement and finish. Compare complete systems rather than a rate per square metre alone; inspect squareness, dimensions and coating before dispatch.

### 30. Aluminium window installation

Window installation starts with checking the finished opening, fixing positions and movement clearances, especially where frames meet waterproofing and finishes. Agree how perimeter joints, sills, drainage and disturbed paint or tiles will be handled. Compare anchors, sealing materials and remedial obligations. At handover operate every sash and lock and check that water does not collect during an appropriate, safely performed test.

### 31. Aluminium door fabrication

For an aluminium door enquiry, supply the clear opening, swing direction, expected traffic and lock and handle positions. An exposed entrance and an internal partition have different reinforcement and sealing needs. Request an approved section showing frame, leaf, any glazing, hinges and threshold; compare quotes including all hardware. Inspect leaf alignment, predrilled holes and hinge security before installation.

### 32. Aluminium door installation

Before fitting an aluminium door, confirm finished floor height, threshold, opening direction and clearance from furniture or other obstacles. The quote should allocate frame anchoring, packers, perimeter sealing, lock adjustment and edge reinstatement. At handover cycle the door repeatedly, inspect even gaps and freedom from rubbing, and test handle and lock from both sides. Record adjustments still required.

### 33. Insect screen installation

For insect screens, identify the openings, operating style and access for removal and cleaning. Sliding, fixed and magnetic screens differ in sealing and upkeep, so request samples of mesh, frame, corners and handles. Compare a per-opening quote including measurement, fitting and accessories. Inspect mesh tension, tears and edge gaps, and demonstrate removal for maintenance.

### 34. Door and window maintenance

Photograph the door or window fault during operation and note whether rubbing occurs at the hinge, lock, sliding track or frame. Check alignment, seals, rollers and drainage before assuming complete replacement is necessary. Ask for a diagnosis distinguishing adjustment, parts replacement and leak remediation; compare each option's cost. Accept the work only after testing movement, locking and drainage and inspecting the cleaned repair.

### 35. Glass and hardware replacement

For glazing or hardware replacement, provide photographs, dimensions and any visible profile-system code, plus the cause of breakage or failure. Glass type, thickness and composition must suit the opening and safety function, not merely match its tint. Compare compatible hinges, rollers and locks, safe lifting arrangements and disposal of old glazing. Inspect panel edges, gasket seating and operation after replacement.

### 36. Aluminium kitchen fabrication

Aluminium kitchen fabrication needs a dimensioned plan showing cabinets, appliances, ventilation clearances and actual plumbing and electrical positions. Request details of frame profiles, door panels, shelves and loads, hinges and runners, with a finish sample suited to the room's moisture exposure. Compare quotes by units, fittings and cut-outs rather than overall length alone. Inspect assembled sizes and door alignment before delivery.

### 37. Aluminium kitchen installation

Before fitting an aluminium kitchen, remeasure finished walls, floor levels and actual service positions. Agree installation sequence, junction treatment at walls and worktops, protection of services during drilling and who connects appliances. At handover operate every drawer, hinge and closing mechanism; check front alignment and ensure shut-off valves and maintenance access remain reachable.

### 38. Aluminium cabinet fabrication

For aluminium storage cabinets, describe stored items, heavy or tall objects, shelf count and usable reach heights. Ask for drawings showing compartments, panel type, shelf reinforcement, backing and wall anchorage where required. Compare offers on load capacity, hardware quality and finish. Inspect cabinet stability, door alignment, secure shelves and the absence of sharp exposed edges.

### 39. Bathroom aluminium cabinet fabrication

Bathroom cabinets face splashes, humidity and frequent cleaning. Show basin, trap and valve positions, clearance above the floor and drawer opening space. Request materials and fittings suited to that environment and access for plumbing maintenance. Compare wall-hung and floor-standing options with regard to wall capacity and fixing method. Inspect penetrations, plumbing access and unobstructed doors.

### 40. Outdoor aluminium storage fabrication

For outdoor storage units, state their location, exposure to sun, rain and dust, and intended contents. Ventilation, drainage and anchorage must be appropriate; hazardous materials must not be stored in an unsuitable cabinet. Compare panel thickness, closures, exposure-resistant finish and base fixings. On handover check door alignment, water drainage and stability on the supporting surface.

### 41. Aluminium kitchen refurbishment

An aluminium kitchen refurbishment may replace fronts, drawers or worktops or rearrange selected units. Photograph every part and identify what remains and what is removed. Inspect the old frame and compatibility of replacement hardware before agreeing to cosmetic changes. Compare partial repair with complete renewal, including dismantling, transport and waste removal. Test all drawers and doors, finish matching and service integrity after work.

### 42. Kitchen hinges and accessory maintenance

For failed kitchen hinges or runners, list the number of components, fault symptoms and door or drawer loads, and photograph the existing fixing pattern. Check damaged screw holes and cabinet framing before fitting replacements to avoid repeat loosening. Require compatible component specifications, quantities and adjustment costs in the quote. Test each unit under ordinary use load, checking alignment and fastener security.

## Glass façades and installations

### 43. Glazed façade execution

Set façade height, environmental exposure, wind design and structural fixing points. Compare curtain-wall system, glass, joints and drainage against engineered design; inspect test evidence and anchors.

### 44. Toughened glass installation

Identify pane location, function, thickness, holes and edge profile before tempering. Compare fabrication details, handling and edge protection; reject chipped or cracked pieces.

### 45. Shopfront glazing

Measure retail opening, door position, visibility, security and closure. Compare glass, framing and hardware; inspect door action, reflections and weather seals.

### 46. Glass door installation

Specify panel dimensions, swing, weight, lock, hinges and fixing substrate. Compare load-rated hardware; test balanced closing without floor scraping.

### 47. Glass partition installation

Lay out rooms, glass heights, privacy, acoustic expectations and doors. Compare clear or obscured glass, anchoring and seals; check panel stability and ceiling junctions.

### 48. Glass balustrade installation

Identify guard height, stair or balcony geometry, glass sizes and fixing positions. Have a qualified designer establish load resistance; compare glazing build-up and fixings, then inspect for movement.

### 49. Façade glazing maintenance

Record leaks, condensation, failed opening hardware and seal faults, including elevated access. Compare joint, drainage and component repairs plus access equipment; verify repaired areas with a targeted test.

## Steel and metal fabrication

### 50. Steel door fabrication

Measure door opening, weight, hinge and lock positions and corrosion exposure. Compare metal gauge, coating and anchors; inspect flatness and functional locks.

### 51. Steel balustrade fabrication

Define guard lengths, heights, corner geometry, base fixings and users. Compare member sizes, joints and finish; inspect the full length for stability.

### 52. Steel shade fabrication

Measure vehicle envelope, traffic routes, post positions, wind and rain drainage. Compare steel sections, footings and covering; inspect secure anchorage and runoff.

### 53. Structural steelwork execution

Define structural function, spans, loads, approved drawings and lifting access. Obtain engineered connection details and shop drawings; inspect geometry, joints and corrosion protection.

### 54. Stainless-steel fabrication

Identify stainless grade, finish, cleaning exposure and piece dimensions. Compare weld preparation, grinding and finish; inspect smooth joins, safe edges and no surface staining.

### 55. Metal welding services

Record metal type, thickness, defect and whether the weld is structural or a non-load-bearing repair. Compare welding procedure, preparation, fire protection and inspection; check for cracking or distortion.

### 56. Metalwork maintenance

Photograph rust, distortion, failed hinges and weld damage, noting hazards. Compare repair, reinforcement and renewal for safety and cost; test function and coating after work.



## Workshop fabrication quotation brief

Provide site-verified dimensions for every unit, opening direction, wall condition, load points, glass/timber/metal type, finish and accessories. Ask for approved shop drawings and samples, and allocate lifting, transport, fitting, edges, anchors and reinstatement. Fall-protection glazing, structural metalwork and fire-rated assemblies require competent design and system review. Customer support can receive a quotation request even without a listed workshop.

**Common question:** Are dimensions estimated from photos enough? No; small measurement errors can make doors or glass unusable. Verify on site before final cutting.
