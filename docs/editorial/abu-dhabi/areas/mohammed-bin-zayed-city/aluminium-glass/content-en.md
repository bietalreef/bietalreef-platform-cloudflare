# Mohammed Bin Zayed City — Metal, aluminium, glass and joinery workshops

> **Area scope:** This guide addresses service enquiries in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi. Quote requests should specify the neighbourhood or zone, building or villa reference and site access. Provider availability, travel charges and permits must be confirmed for the actual location. Shared technical guidance is not evidence of previous local work.


Fabrication requests start with actual site measurements, photographs, material selection, expected loads and fixings. Approved shop drawings and samples are distinct from inspiration photos. Where no company is listed, customers may submit details through Biet Al Reef customer support's quotation form without an implied workshop, price or delivery date.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Before selecting a kitchen layout, provide wall-to-wall dimensions, ceiling height, window and door locations, and actual water, drainage and electrical points. Request a dimensioned layout showing the real appliance sizes, door and drawer clearances and work zones. Compare design quotations by construction drawings and included revisions, not renders alone. Check the final drawings against site measurements and approve finishes separately.

### 2. Kitchen cabinetry fabrication

Kitchen fabrication needs a cutting schedule for each carcass and door, specifying board thickness, moisture protection, edging and the exact hinge and runner models. Ask for samples of edge joints and front finishes; compare hardware, cutting, assembly and delivery as distinct quotation items. Before dispatch, inspect square carcasses, labelled units and appliance cut-outs against the approved drawings.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Check floor and wall levels and the intended worktop height before installers arrive; record conflicts with drainage pipes or sockets. The quote should allocate wall fixings, leg adjustment, joint sealing, sink-edge protection and responsibility for discrepancies from the approved drawings. At handover test every drawer and door at full travel, cabinet stability and access to service valves.

### 4. Wardrobe design

For a wardrobe, measure the finished wall width, height and usable depth without blocking room circulation. Count long garments, folded items, shoes and bags to determine the internal layout. Compare hinged and sliding doors for clearance and future maintenance, approve sectional drawings, and test drawer and door movement without rubbing at handover.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

Freeze the approved wardrobe schedule before production: unit dimensions, any ventilation openings, shelf arrangement and wall fixing positions. Compare quotations using board grade and thickness, edge treatment, hinges and runners, with lighting and accessories priced separately. At delivery inspect door alignment and shelf support; document and approve any dimensional deviation before acceptance.

### 6. Walk-in closet fit-out

Measure the clear walking aisle with drawers and doors open, and mark mirror, seating, lighting and power positions. Request a layout that allows access to high shelves, floor cleaning and suitable cabinet ventilation. Compare cleanable finishes and replaceable hardware. At handover test the lighting, drawer operation and the built dimensions against the plan.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

For an existing kitchen or wardrobe, photograph water-swollen panels, loose hinges and peeling edges; assess whether the cabinet carcasses can safely be retained. Request separate prices for doors, runners, shelves and moisture repairs versus full replacement. Check colour matching, even gaps, hinge security and drawer movement before accepting the refurbishment.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Specify whether the wooden door is for an internal room, entrance or moisture-exposed location; provide rough opening dimensions, wall thickness and swing direction. Request the core construction, face finish, edge treatment, hinges and lock specification rather than a generic premium-timber label. Inspect leaf straightness, hardware mortises and finish before installation.

### 9. Door installation

Measure the door opening after flooring and relevant finishes are complete, including threshold height and swing clearance. Compare offers that explicitly include the frame, hinges, lock and making good around the opening; assign responsibility for uneven openings. At handover cycle the door repeatedly and check latch engagement, even clearances and no floor contact.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door must be specified from the approved project schedule and opening requirements, not its appearance or timber thickness. Ask a competent supplier for evidence covering the complete rated assembly: leaf, frame, seals, hinges, closer and permitted installation details. At handover match labels to documents and test self-closing; avoid unapproved site modifications.

### 11. Doorframe construction

Before manufacturing a doorframe, measure finished wall thickness, threshold level and the opening at several points to identify out-of-square conditions. Specify frame material, section, anchorage, hinge positions and moisture exposure. Compare quotes including trims and wall-edge repairs. Check plumb, straightness and secure fixing before hanging the door.

### 12. Wooden partition fabrication

Define whether the timber divider provides visual screening or supports shelves or a screen; supply height, width, slat spacing and anchorage locations. Distinguish solid timber from faced boards in quotations and document edges, joints and access to concealed services. At handover check fixing rigidity, consistent slat spacing and the absence of sharp edges.

### 13. Door repairs

Identify the fault first: floor rubbing, lock failure, loose frame or moisture-swollen leaf. Supply photos of edges, hinges and both sides of the opening, and ask for separate adjustment, replacement-hardware and timber-repair prices. Assess repair before ordering a new door. After work, test closing, latch engagement and hinge security.

### 14. Timber refinishing

Identify the existing coating, peeling and scratched areas, and whether the goal is colour change or restoration. Request a discreet sample to confirm adhesion and colour, plus documented cleaning, sanding, filling, coat sequence and drying time. Inspect the finish in adequate light and check that paint has not clogged locks or impeded door movement.

## Custom furniture and decorative joinery

### 15. Bespoke furniture fabrication

Schedule each item, intended use, dimensions, circulation and expected loading. Compare materials, joints and fittings; approve finish samples and joinery details.

### 16. TV console fabrication

Provide screen width, bracket, sockets, data points, device depth and ventilation. Compare fixing and cable management; test stability and service access.

### 17. Storage unit fabrication

Inventory stored items, shelf loads, door sizes and proximity to moisture. Compare board thickness, anchoring and runners; inspect shelves under agreed loads.

### 18. Timber wall cladding

Measure wall, outlets, height, moisture and required grain direction. Compare panel finish, fixing and expansion details; inspect straight alignment and removable access panels.

### 19. CNC decorative joinery

Supply vector artwork or dimensioned pattern, board thickness and minimum cut detail. Compare CNC precision, edge quality and sanding; approve a sample before full production.

### 20. Custom counters

Set counter height, length, staff clearance, POS and data/power positions. Compare durability, surface finish and corner protection; inspect working clearances and connections.

### 21. Retail and office furniture fabrication

Schedule desks, storage and displays, power interfaces, loads and opening dates. Compare modular repeatability, production and labelled delivery; inspect stability and drawing compliance.

### 22. Interior space design

Map space function, circulation, lighting, freestanding and fixed furniture. Compare design deliverables with optional execution; approve materials before fabrication.

### 23. Joinery fabrication and installation

Record all dimensions, site walls, lifting access, doors and lifts. Compare workshop trial assembly, transport and installation; inspect safe fixings and unobtrusive joints.

### 24. Majlis fit-out

Define seating capacity, majlis dimensions, television, AC, curtains and upholstery. Compare fitted joinery separately from movable furniture; inspect circulation and lighting in use.

### 25. Bedroom fit-out

Measure beds, wardrobes, door swings, ceiling offsets and power points. Compare unit layouts, safe edges and anchorage; inspect window and door access after fitting.

### 26. Office and retail fit-out

Record retail stock or office use, storage, counters, circulation and existing service points. Compare joinery, electrical interfaces and finishes within a defined scope; hand over zone-by-zone snags.

### 27. Timber decorative features

Document motif, finish, panel position and heights on drawings. Compare mock-ups, edge treatment and cleanability; inspect consistent repeats and secure installation.

### 28. Interior fit-out handover

Use a handover checklist for doors, drawers, surfaces, lights, joints and site variations. Compare against approved drawings and materials; provide keys, maintenance notes and replaceable-part records.

## Aluminium windows, doors and fittings

### 29. Aluminium window fabrication

Provide opening dimensions, glass type, sash action and drainage requirements before profile selection. Compare frame system, thickness and gaskets; inspect corners and fabricated dimensions.

### 30. Aluminium window installation

Re-measure finished openings, sash clearance and exposure to wind and rain. Compare frame anchors, seals and perimeter silicone; test smooth operation and appropriate water resistance.

### 31. Aluminium door fabrication

Specify opening, swing, glazing, lock and required passage clearance. Compare profiles, reinforcement, hinges and security; inspect squareness and assembled hardware.

### 32. Aluminium door installation

Confirm flooring level, threshold, handles and swing. Compare anchors, packers and seals; check smooth closure and effective dust sealing.

### 33. Insect screen installation

Measure openings and choose removable or sliding mesh panels. Compare mesh aperture, frame design and cleaning access; inspect tension and side gaps.

### 34. Door and window maintenance

Map sash rubbing, frame movement, failed seals and water entry. Compare adjustments and gasket repairs against full replacement; test seals and square closure.

### 35. Glass and hardware replacement

Provide old hardware photos, aluminium-system code and glass size/thickness. Compare compatible components and safe glazing handling; inspect secure fit and unchipped edges.

### 36. Aluminium kitchen fabrication

Give kitchen plan, loads, appliance sizes and moisture requirements for cabinets. Compare profiles, panels, fronts and hardware; inspect assembled dimensions before delivery.

### 37. Aluminium kitchen installation

Measure walls, services and floor levels before fitting units. Compare installation, junctions and edge protection; test drawers, hinges and worktop alignment.

### 38. Aluminium cabinet fabrication

Specify cupboard dimensions, shelving and storage loads. Compare panel thickness, doors and fixing; inspect sturdy shelves and protected corners.

### 39. Bathroom aluminium cabinet fabrication

Locate under-basin or bathroom storage, splash zones, airflow and leak drainage. Compare moisture-resistant panels, seals and plumbing access; check vulnerable connections are protected.

### 40. Outdoor aluminium storage fabrication

Specify outdoor position, sun/rain/wind exposure and intended stored loads. Compare finish, ventilation, anchors and locks; inspect weather exposure and access.

### 41. Aluminium kitchen refurbishment

Photograph failing fronts, hinges and bent aluminium sections and identify reusable carcasses. Compare door/hardware renewal with full units; inspect colour matching and drawer action.

### 42. Kitchen hinges and accessory maintenance

Record hinge, runner and lock models, symptoms and movement lengths. Compare compatible replacements and adjustments; test even opening without sagging.

## Glass façades and installations

### 43. Glazed façade execution

Set façade height, environmental exposure, wind design and structural fixing points. Compare curtain-wall system, glass, joints and drainage against engineered design; inspect test evidence and anchors.

### 44. Toughened glass installation

Identify pane location, function, thickness, holes and edge profile before tempering. Compare fabrication details, handling and edge protection; reject chipped or cracked pieces.

### 45. Shopfront glazing

Measure retail opening, door position, visibility, security and closure. Compare glass, framing and hardware; inspect door action, reflections and weather seals.

### 46. Glass door installation

Specify panel dimensions, swing, weight, lock, hinges and fixing substrate. Compare load-rated hardware; test balanced closing without floor scraping.

### 47. Glass partition installation

Lay out rooms, glass heights, privacy, acoustic expectations and doors. Compare clear or obscured glass, anchoring and seals; check panel stability and ceiling junctions.

### 48. Glass balustrade installation

Identify guard height, stair or balcony geometry, glass sizes and fixing positions. Have a qualified designer establish load resistance; compare glazing build-up and fixings, then inspect for movement.

### 49. Façade glazing maintenance

Record leaks, condensation, failed opening hardware and seal faults, including elevated access. Compare joint, drainage and component repairs plus access equipment; verify repaired areas with a targeted test.

## Steel and metal fabrication

### 50. Steel door fabrication

Measure door opening, weight, hinge and lock positions and corrosion exposure. Compare metal gauge, coating and anchors; inspect flatness and functional locks.

### 51. Steel balustrade fabrication

Define guard lengths, heights, corner geometry, base fixings and users. Compare member sizes, joints and finish; inspect the full length for stability.

### 52. Steel shade fabrication

Measure vehicle envelope, traffic routes, post positions, wind and rain drainage. Compare steel sections, footings and covering; inspect secure anchorage and runoff.

### 53. Structural steelwork execution

Define structural function, spans, loads, approved drawings and lifting access. Obtain engineered connection details and shop drawings; inspect geometry, joints and corrosion protection.

### 54. Stainless-steel fabrication

Identify stainless grade, finish, cleaning exposure and piece dimensions. Compare weld preparation, grinding and finish; inspect smooth joins, safe edges and no surface staining.

### 55. Metal welding services

Record metal type, thickness, defect and whether the weld is structural or a non-load-bearing repair. Compare welding procedure, preparation, fire protection and inspection; check for cracking or distortion.

### 56. Metalwork maintenance

Photograph rust, distortion, failed hinges and weld damage, noting hazards. Compare repair, reinforcement and renewal for safety and cost; test function and coating after work.



## Workshop fabrication quotation brief

Provide site-verified dimensions for every unit, opening direction, wall condition, load points, glass/timber/metal type, finish and accessories. Ask for approved shop drawings and samples, and allocate lifting, transport, fitting, edges, anchors and reinstatement. Fall-protection glazing, structural metalwork and fire-rated assemblies require competent design and system review. Customer support can receive a quotation request even without a listed workshop.

**Common question:** Are dimensions estimated from photos enough? No; small measurement errors can make doors or glass unusable. Verify on site before final cutting.
