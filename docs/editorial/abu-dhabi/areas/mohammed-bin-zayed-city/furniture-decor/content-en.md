# Mohammed Bin Zayed City — Furniture and furnishing

> **Area scope:** For service enquiries in Mohammed Bin Zayed City, Abu Dhabi, specify the zone, villa or building and access route. No provider, price or permit is implied; shared technical guidance is not evidence of locally completed work.


Start with the dimensions of the space, delivery access and how the piece will be used, rather than choosing from a picture alone. ## Furniture manufacturing

### 1. Residential furniture manufacture

List furniture types, quantity, room size, access route and daily cleaning needs. Compare materials, finishes, fittings and actual samples; inspect stability and edges.

Request a layout showing drawer and door clearances and confirm whether surveying, fitting and removal of old units are included. Photograph outlets and irregular corners before final dimensions are approved.


### 2. Office furniture manufacture

Specify headcount, desks, seating, cable routes and existing outlets. Compare load capacity, ergonomics, adjustability and serviceability; test units in use.

Check worktop depth, chair movement and monitor placement, and request a cable-routing sample. Do not judge the order solely by desk count; test a representative chair and workstation.


### 3. Hotel furniture manufacture

List room types, beds, wardrobes, cleaning cycles and the hotel's own specification. Compare hardware durability, uniform finishes and replaceable parts; approve a sample room before volume production.

Approve a photographed, coded sample room before bulk manufacture, with a replacement process that preserves finish and dimensions. Price fixed joinery separately from loose furniture.


### 4. Restaurant furniture manufacture

Check seating count, staff routes, chair movements and moisture/heat exposure near tables. Compare cleanable materials, stable bases and wear resistance; verify service circulation.

Provide a seating and circulation plan and identify indoor versus outdoor use. Request a sample tabletop edge and leg joint, then assess repeated cleaning.


### 5. Bespoke furniture manufacture

Start with a scale drawing detailing sections, colour and precise dimensions of the item. Compare prototype, lead time, warranty and transport responsibility; inspect dimensions and assembly.

Include elevations, sections, joint details and outlets or ventilation where furniture is built in. Approve a finish sample before fabrication and record installation tolerances.


### 6. Furniture restoration

Photograph cracks, scratches, loose joints and underlying wood or fabric before selecting a repair. Compare selective replacements against rebuilding; inspect stability and consistent surfaces.

Diagnose looseness or swelling before agreeing to a treatment; moisture sources may need attention first. Record replacement components and repair limits so restoration is not priced as a complete rebuild.


### 7. Project furniture supply

Schedule furniture by room, quantities, models, delivery phase and storage space. Compare packing, installation, damage and delay terms; reconcile a numbered inventory with purchase orders.

Use a room- and floor-coded quantity schedule; assign responsibility for lifting, assembly and packaging disposal. Check delivery in two stages: quantities and then function and finish.


## Furniture and home furnishing stores

### 8. Home furniture sales

Measure doorways, stairways and rooms before choosing sofa or bed dimensions. Compare frame, cushion materials, upholstery and delivery terms; check dimensions and condition.

Ask for assembled dimensions, not just carton measurements, and verify lift and stair access. Record visible damage before signing the delivery note.


### 9. Office furniture sales

Plan workstation count, desk footprints, outlets, chair adjustment and load. Compare warranty, spares and returns; inspect assembly and operation.

Plan desks, storage and seating on a layout, with assembly and old-furniture removal separately priced. Test chair adjustment, drawer action and stability after fitting.


### 10. Lighting retail

Measure ceiling height, intended illuminance, colour temperature and lamp quantity. Compare photometric details, warranty and mounting kit; see an operating sample.

Define task, reading or ambient lighting and compare luminous output and beam distribution, not wattage alone. Check compatibility with any existing dimmers or controls.


### 11. Curtain retail

Measure window width, rail height, curtain drop, lining and cleaning requirements. Compare per-panel versus per-metre pricing and tracks/sewing; inspect fabric and finished length.

Photograph windows, curtain recesses and nearby openings, and specify privacy or blackout needs. Separate fabric, lining, track, stitching and installation costs.


### 12. Carpet retail

Record room size, rug dimensions, door swings and cleaning frequency. Compare pile density, backing and slip properties; inspect dimensions and edges.

Identify whether the rug sits under furniture or across a walkway and check edge thickness against door clearance. Inspect colour in the room's actual lighting.


### 13. Home accessories retail

List mirrors, handles and accessories with dimensions and fixing locations. Compare anchors, moisture suitability and weights; inspect for chips and missing hardware.

For mirrors and shelves, provide wall type, expected weight and mounting height; ask for anchor details. Test fixing security and inspect exposed edges.


### 14. Bulk project furniture supply

Define quantities, delivery rooms, project milestones and uniform finish samples. Compare substitution, packing, assembly and batch consistency; obtain a model-by-model receipt.

Prepare a delivery schedule identifying item code, location and quantity, with approved finish samples. Agree how missing pieces and replacements will be documented without disrupting handover.


## Majlis and sofa upholstery

### 15. Traditional majlis seating

Measure walls, seating capacity, seat height and clear door routes before designing a majlis. Compare frame, padding, textiles and joints; inspect comfort and even seating.

Specify seats along each wall, door positions, tables and nearby outlets; review backrest and cushion heights. Trial one seating section before commissioning the whole majlis.


### 16. Custom sofas

Specify straight, corner or modular forms, each section's size and seat depth. Compare upholstery performance, cushion fill, leg frame and transport; measure completed modules.

Measure each module's length, depth, back height and rear circulation, particularly for corner configurations. Price timber frame, foam, fabric and transport separately.


### 17. Furniture reupholstery

Photograph frame, arms and damaged textiles to identify spring, padding or upholstery faults. Compare stripping, frame repair, padding and covering as distinct costs; inspect rigidity and taut seams.

Where needed, open a small upholstery section to inspect springs and frame before quoting the whole job. Photograph damaged timber and seek approval for replacements.


### 18. Sofa fabric replacement

Record fabric area, pattern direction and extra material for cushions. Compare abrasion/cleaning suitability and stripping/sewing costs; inspect aligned patterns and seams.

Clarify whether arms, back and loose cushions are included; calculate fabric consumption allowing for nap and pattern direction. Keep a signed sample for handover comparison.


### 19. Cushion foam renewal

Specify foam density, seat thickness and collapsed zones while retaining finished dimensions. Compare firmness, fill options and warranty; test seating and upholstery closure.

Compare sample fills at two firmness levels before ordering every cushion. Check seat height after a short sitting test and inspect for lumpy edges.


## Curtains, rugs and soft furnishings

### 20. Made-to-measure curtains

Measure window width, track-to-floor drop, pleat style, lining and blackout needs. Compare fabric allowance, stitching and track loading; inspect finished drops and folds.

Measure each window individually from the actual rail location and confirm curtain opening direction. Review pleat fullness and uneven floor levels before cutting.


### 21. Curtain installation

Identify wall substrate, AC outlets and window operation before drilling rails. Compare bracket spacing and load rating; test smooth movement and window clearance.

Photograph nearby pipes and outlets and verify substrate and anchor suitability before drilling. Test the full curtain travel and clearance from AC units and window handles.


### 22. Furnishing fabrics supply

Specify sofa, curtain or bedding fabrics by use, sunlight and moisture exposure. Compare weave density, colourfastness and care; approve swatches and spare fabric if needed.

Request the fabric composition and care instructions plus a swatch from the actual batch. Allow for cutting loss and large-pattern repeats when calculating metres.


### 23. Carpets and fitted carpet supply

Measure each room and decide fitted carpet or loose rugs. Compare pile, underlay, seams and slip control; inspect grain direction and edge joins.

Request a cutting plan with pile direction and seam positions for fitted carpet; separate carpet, underlay, adhesive and labour. Inspect seams and secure edges.


### 24. Mattress supply

Measure bed base dimensions, sleepers, firmness preference and mattress depth. Compare construction, warranty and ventilation; check size and packaging at handover.

Verify bed-base dimensions and height against mattress thickness; clarify warranty conditions and base requirements. Inspect condition at delivery rather than assuming a showroom trial guarantees suitability.


## Furniture and furnishing quotation brief

Specify each item, quantity, width, depth and height, route into the room, intended users, colours and reference materials. Include inspiration photographs and fabric samples where possible. Request a bid separating frame, cushions, hardware, assembly, delivery, exchanges and any warranty. Where no company is published, Biet Al Reef customer support can log the request without confirming supply.

**Common question:** Is sofa pricing by linear metre alone comparable? No; construction, foam density, frame, textile quality, upholstery and stitching all affect cost and use.
