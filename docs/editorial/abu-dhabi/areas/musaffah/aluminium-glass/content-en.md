# Metal, aluminium, glass and wood workshops in Musaffah — company services

This is an editorial guide, not a listing of confirmed suppliers, prices or appointments. If no company is published, Biet Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request with the job type, location, photographs, dimensions, preferred materials and requested survey timing, without guaranteeing provider availability. Individual tradespeople are listed separately.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Begin with measured walls and the locations of plumbing, sockets, extraction and windows. Plan circulation and clearances for drawers and appliances. Request dimensioned upper and lower cabinet drawings, board and edge specifications, moisture protection, lighting and ventilation details. Compare shop drawings and hardware schedules before fabrication; at handover test door operation and appliance clearances.

### 2. Kitchen cabinet fabrication

Fabrication follows approved drawings and requires specified panel thicknesses, finishes, edge banding, hinges and drawer runners. Sink, oven and service openings must be coordinated, with moisture protection near wet areas. Ask for finish samples and a cutting schedule before production. Compare material and hardware specifications rather than price per metre alone; inspect square assemblies and clean edges before delivery.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Before fitting, verify floor and wall levels and locate concealed services so fixings are safe. Align base and wall cabinets, adjust joints and install worktops to their specified support details; qualified trades should connect appliances and services. Agree floor protection and waste removal. At handover operate every drawer and door and check the sink area for leaks.

### 4. Wardrobe design

Measure the wall, ceiling height, door swings, switches and AC outlets before planning a wardrobe. Allocate hanging space, shelves and drawers around actual clothing needs, and check clearances for hinged or sliding doors. Require an internal dimensioned elevation, panel thickness and lighting positions; compare usable storage capacity before choosing the finish.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

Fabrication covers panel cutting, edge finishing, joints and either hinges or sliding tracks to approved drawings. Durability depends on board grade, connections, shelf loads and runner quality. Ask for these specifications and a finish sample, confirm door orientation and handle positions, and inspect cutting accuracy and cabinet rigidity before fitting.

### 6. Walk-in wardrobe fit-out

A walk-in wardrobe needs a circulation plan that accommodates cabinet depth, drawer extension and ventilation. Define whether mirrors, lighting, sockets and seating are included, separating joinery from electrical costs. Work from verified site dimensions, not only concept drawings. Check cabinet anchoring and access to upper storage without obstructing the aisle.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

Inspect swelling near wet areas, damaged edging, misaligned doors, hinges and runners before specifying refurbishment. Sound cabinet carcasses may only need new fronts or hardware; severely damaged panels may require replacement. Ask for itemised repair, replacement and refinishing options with condition photographs. Test every unit after completion.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Measure the finished opening, swing direction and wall thickness before manufacturing. Specify solid or hollow construction, veneer or paint, frame, hinges and lockset according to exposure and use. Approve final dimensions and a door schedule before production. Inspect flatness, consistent edges and accurately positioned hardware.

### 9. Door installation

Check frame plumb, finished floor level and under-door clearance; fixings must suit the wall substrate. Protect floors and finishes, then adjust hinges and locks. Compare quotes by door count and inclusion of frames, hardware and old-door removal. At handover open, close and latch each door repeatedly.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door is a life-safety assembly, not merely a door described as fire-resistant on an invoice. Establish the required rating from the project design and relevant authority, and request test and approval documentation for the complete leaf, frame, seals, hinges and closer. Unauthorised site alterations may invalidate performance; handover must match labels and components to approved documents.

### 11. Door frame fabrication and fitting

Specify the frame to suit wall thickness, door type and adjacent finishes. Review anchoring, jamb width, hinge and strike positions, especially where floor levels vary. Request a frame section detail and material specification. Check square corners, alignment and secure fixing before hanging the leaf.

### 12. Wooden room partitions

Define whether the partition provides privacy, circulation separation or acoustic control: a decorative screen is not a sound-rated wall. Measure ceiling height, fixing points and nearby electrical and AC services. Approve panel spacing and finish drawings. Check stability, safe edges and unobstructed doors and escape routes.

### 13. Door maintenance

Diagnose rubbing or poor latching before replacing the door: possible causes include sagging hinges, timber movement, a distorted frame or lock failure. Request inspection and itemised adjustment, parts and refinishing. At handover verify smooth travel, secure hinges and reliable latching without excessive force.

### 14. Timber painting and refinishing

The preparation depends on timber condition and the existing coating. Clean, repair defects and test primer compatibility before applying paint or varnish. Decide whether the grain should remain visible or receive an opaque finish, and approve a small sample. Compare coat counts, application method and curing time; inspect colour and texture consistency after completion.

## Preparing a quotation request

Provide the locality and approximate address, unit types and quantities, measured dimensions or a request for a site survey, photos of openings and services, preferred materials and finishes, removal and installation scope, transport needs and desired timing. Request itemised materials, hardware, warranty and acceptance criteria. This guide does not certify a company's licence or product approval.

## Wooden furniture and interior joinery

### 15. Bespoke furniture fabrication

Custom furniture is useful when standard dimensions or layouts do not fit. Record length, depth, height and expected loads; request drawings showing joints, boards and veneer or paint. Quotes should separate fabrication, delivery and assembly. At handover check stability, balance and edge quality.

### 16. TV console fabrication

Measure the television, power and data outlets and ventilation clearances for equipment. The design should show support capacity, cable routes and drawer clearance, with suitable anchoring for floating units. Compare board thickness and hardware, then inspect level surfaces and access to connections.

### 17. Storage unit fabrication

Storage units must suit their contents, whether heavy files, tools or household items. Establish shelf loads, depth, reachable height and door type before choosing boards and supports. Require dimensioned internal layouts and load limits; test shelf stability and moving parts at handover.

### 18. Wooden wall cladding

Check wall flatness, possible moisture and electrical points before selecting a cladding fixing system. Specify board type, moisture suitability, joints and edge details, and approve a sample showing grain direction. Allow access to service points; inspect aligned joints and secure fixings.

### 19. CNC decorative panels

CNC-cut decoration requires an approved vector drawing with panel size, thickness, open-area ratio and minimum narrow-section width. Review a sample, fixing method and any backlighting rather than relying on a reference photo. Inspect cut edges, pattern consistency and panel security.

### 20. Counter fabrication

Define counter function, working height, staff and customer circulation, equipment and storage before fabrication. Drawings should locate power and data points, specify a worktop suited to use and protect exposed corners. Compare itemised units and finishes; verify heights, stability and cable access.

### 21. Retail and office furniture

Plan shop and office furniture around user counts, merchandise or file sizes and circulation. Request drawings for desks, shelving and displays with load ratings, finishes and cleaning access; separate electrical work from joinery. Test drawers and doors, stability and unobstructed walkways at handover.

### 22. Interior space design

Interior space design is distinct from fabrication. Survey dimensions, needs and budget, then develop a buildable furniture, cladding and lighting layout. Request a materials board and dimensioned details defining designer and fabricator responsibilities; renders do not replace shop drawings. Resolve door and HVAC conflicts before production.

### 23. Joinery fabrication and installation

For packages containing multiple joinery items, require a schedule for each door, cabinet and cladding panel showing dimensions, material, finish and installation stage. Assign responsibility for site measurements, protection and coordination with other trades. Inspect against the schedule item by item, not appearance alone.

### 24. Majlis fit-out

Majlis fit-out needs a seating plan, circulation widths, furniture dimensions and lighting and socket positions. Coordinate cladding, cabinets and tables with air conditioning and maintenance access; approve fabric and timber samples. Compare quotes by component rather than room area alone; inspect comfort, movement and stability.

### 25. Bedroom fit-out

Bedroom joinery starts with bed and wardrobe dimensions and clearances for doors and drawers, accounting for sockets, curtains and AC. Itemise headboard, bedside units, wardrobe, lighting and installation, specifying boards and edges. At handover inspect stability, safe corners and cleaning access.

### 26. Office and retail fit-out

A complete office or retail fit-out must coordinate joinery with services, shopfronts and circulation. Require an itemised schedule of counters, desks, displays and internal signage with dimensions and anchoring details; price non-joinery work separately. Test actual workflows and customer routes before acceptance.

### 27. Decorative woodwork

Specify whether the decoration uses slats, frames or raised panels and where movement joints and concealed-service access will be provided. Request section details, a finish sample, material grade and fixing method, particularly overhead. Check alignment, finish and secure installation before closing work.

### 28. Interior finishing and handover

Final interior handover needs an item-by-item checklist matching approved drawings to completed work, including coatings, edges, joints, doors and hardware. Record defects with photographs and locations, agree rectification responsibilities and obtain care instructions and agreed warranties. Snag closure does not replace specialist electrical or fire-safety inspections.


## Aluminium doors and windows

### 29. Aluminium window fabrication

Measure each finished opening at several points and confirm opening direction, curtain clearance and access. Require shop drawings identifying profile system, section thickness, finish, gaskets, glazing and rainwater drainage. Compare quotations by profiles and hardware rather than metre rate alone; inspect frame squareness, dimensions and corner joints before dispatch.

### 30. Aluminium window installation

Survey opening alignment, sill level and safe fixing points before work. The scope should specify anchors, perimeter seals and treatment of the frame-to-wall joint without blocking drainage slots. At handover operate each sash and lock, inspect gasket contact and agree a controlled water check that will not damage finishes.

### 31. Aluminium door fabrication

Record clear opening dimensions, swing direction, frequency of use, lock location and threshold requirements. Shop drawings should show reinforcement at hinges, infill or glass type and coating. Compare hinge and handle suitability and warranty terms; inspect sash squareness and joint integrity before transport.

### 32. Aluminium door installation

Check finished floor level, door swing and clashes with furniture or equipment. Agree frame anchorage, threshold treatment, perimeter sealing, lock adjustment and door stop. Accept only after checking even gaps, smooth closure, no floor rubbing and documenting any scratches or finish damage.

### 33. Screen installation

Distinguish removable insect mesh from a security grille: they require different materials, fixings and performance. Measure after windows are installed and plan cleaning access and safe opening where applicable; do not assume ordinary mesh provides security. Inspect mesh tension, edges, frame fixing and unobstructed window operation.

### 34. Door and window maintenance

Describe the symptom: sticking rollers, poor closure, dust or water ingress, or loose sashes. Ask for tracks, rollers, seals and hinges to be inspected before replacing an entire frame. The quote should separate cleaning, adjustment and new parts; test operation after repair and record the diagnosed cause.

### 35. Hardware and glazing replacement

Photograph the failed part and measure existing glass dimensions, thickness and location, retaining any manufacturer markings. Replacement glazing must have the appropriate safety characteristics for its application, while hardware must match the existing system. Itemise removal, handling and safe disposal; check glazing retention, locks and sash movement at handover.

### 36. Aluminium kitchen fabrication

Begin with a dimensioned layout showing appliances, sink, electrical and plumbing services and cabinet heights. Request finish and infill samples plus profile, hinge and drawer-runner specifications, with moisture-prone areas addressed. Compare itemised units, hardware and thicknesses; inspect doors, edges and alignment before delivery.

### 37. Aluminium kitchen installation

Verify as-built dimensions, floor levels and concealed utilities before drilling. Include cabinet fixing, jointing, drawer and door alignment, and appliance ventilation according to equipment instructions. Check stability, worktop level and opening clearances; have the relevant specialist test sink connections.

### 38. Aluminium cabinet fabrication

Specify storage purpose, expected loads, shelf spans, depth and whether the cabinet is indoors or exposed. Approve drawings showing door operation, anchorage, finish and appropriate environmental resistance. Inspect dimensions, shelf stability, hinge movement and sharp edges before acceptance.

### 39. Bathroom cabinet fabrication

Measure around the basin, trap, water connections and mirror, allowing adequate door clearance. Specify moisture-tolerant panels and edges, service openings and access for leak repairs. Compare infill and hardware specifications; at handover inspect water clearance and access to plumbing.

### 40. Outdoor storage unit fabrication

Identify contents, loads, sun and rain exposure, floor condition and fixing location. The design should address ventilation, drainage, corrosion resistance and locking; ordinary cabinets must not be assumed suitable for hazardous substances. Test anchorage, door operation and absence of standing water at the base.

### 41. Aluminium kitchen refurbishment

Survey which frames remain sound and which are distorted or moisture-damaged. Obtain an itemised scope separating replacement fronts, hinges and runners from structural cabinet replacement, with compatible finish samples. Compare refurbishment against replacement by component condition; test door and drawer alignment after work.

### 42. Kitchen hinge and hardware maintenance

Identify sagging doors and sticking drawers, photographing hinge or runner type and fixing dimensions. Inspect stripped screw holes, warped fronts and soft-close adjustments before ordering parts. New hardware alone cannot repair an unsound cabinet carcass; test repeated opening and closing and shelf stability after repair.


## Glass facades and glazing

### 43. Glass facade construction

Start with drawings, opening dimensions, design loads, heights and anchor locations, not glass tint alone. Require system details for supports, joints, weather sealing, drainage and maintenance access, with project-appropriate glazing specifications. Compare complete systems and required testing; specialist checks of anchorage, leakage and finishes precede handover.

### 44. Tempered glass installation

Confirm each pane's use, dimensions and hardware holes before tempering: tempered glass cannot be cut or drilled afterwards. Specify thickness, edge treatment, fittings and safe lifting arrangements. Verify suitability for its location and applicable safety requirements, then inspect edges, scratches and secure retention.

### 45. Shopfront construction

Survey shopfront width and height, entrance movement, signage and existing services. Request drawings for profiles, glass, locks, thresholds and fixing zones, pricing electrical work and signage separately. At acceptance check access, door alignment, edge protection and perimeter sealing.

### 46. Glass door installation

Measure the opening and finished floor, swing or sliding path, handles, hinges and closer positions. The quote should identify glazing type, fittings, edge protection and system limitations, with suitability checked for its intended use. Test repeated closing, floor clearance and absence of clashes at handover.

### 47. Glass partition construction

Define privacy, door positions, ceiling heights and conflicts with air-conditioning or lighting before setting out partitions. Specify glass, joints, supports, cleaning access and relevant acoustic or safety needs. Compare panel layout, doors and hardware; inspect plumb lines and joint stability.

### 48. Glass balustrade installation

A balustrade prevents falls, so its design and anchorage need appropriate engineering review for its position, height and loads. Require base and anchor drawings, glass composition, lamination and edge details before quoting. Appearance is insufficient: specialist inspection, documentation and any required tests must precede acceptance.

### 49. Glass facade maintenance

Photograph leaks, cracks or loose covers, noting floor level and safe access requirements. Diagnose gaskets, joints, anchors and glazing rather than assuming fresh sealant will solve every fault. Separate access-equipment costs from repair and testing; hand over a record of treated areas and safety checks.

## Steel and metal fabrication

### 50. Steel door fabrication

Measure the opening and finished level; establish use, swing, locks, hinges and expected weight. Request drawings specifying steel sections, thickness, reinforcement, weld treatment and suitable corrosion protection. Compare fabrication and fitting scopes, then inspect squareness, welds and hinge positions before delivery.

### 51. Metal railing fabrication

Stair and balcony guardrails differ from decorative barriers. Record location, height, run length and fixing points. Dimensions, loads, spacing and engineering details require review before fabrication, particularly for fall protection. Inspect welds, coating, anchors and technical records at handover.

### 52. Canopy fabrication

Define the shaded area, headroom, rainwater route, covering and column positions. Request frame, joint and foundation drawings, specialist assessment of site-appropriate loads and corrosion protection. Compare frame, cover and footing specifications; inspect slope, stability and drainage.

### 53. Structural steelwork

Steel structures require engineering drawings defining spans, loads, connections and foundations, together with quantities, steel grades and protection systems. Reference photographs alone cannot establish a buildable scope or price. Request fabrication and erection plans, bolt and weld inspections and relevant test records before handover.

### 54. Stainless steel fabrication

Specify whether the item is a handrail, preparation surface or cladding and its exposure to water and cleaning chemicals. Ask for stainless grade, finish, thickness, weld method and post-weld cleaning rather than a vague rustproof claim. Check edges, finish consistency, joint quality and secure installation.

### 55. Welding services

Describe base metal, thickness, joint location and whether the connection is structural or a minor repair. Specify welding process, surface preparation and protection against sparks and heat; structural connections need qualified specialist oversight. Compare preparation, inspection and finishing, then inspect weld quality and distortion.

### 56. Metalwork maintenance

Photograph rust, cracks and loose joints or bases before requesting a quote. Diagnose corrosion or vibration causes, then distinguish repairable components from those needing replacement or structural assessment. Itemise cleaning, corrosion treatment, recoating and parts; inspect stability and protection after repair.
