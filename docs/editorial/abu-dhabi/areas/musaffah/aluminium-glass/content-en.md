# Metal, aluminium, glass and wood workshops in Musaffah — company services

This is an editorial guide, not a listing of confirmed suppliers, prices or appointments. If no company is published, Biet Al Reef customer service can receive a quotation request with the job type, location, photographs, dimensions, preferred materials and requested survey timing, without guaranteeing provider availability. Individual tradespeople are listed separately.

## Wooden kitchens, wardrobes and doors

### 1. Wooden kitchen design

Begin with measured walls and the locations of plumbing, sockets, extraction and windows. Plan circulation and clearances for drawers and appliances. Request dimensioned upper and lower cabinet drawings, board and edge specifications, moisture protection, lighting and ventilation details. Compare shop drawings and hardware schedules before fabrication; at handover test door operation and appliance clearances.

### 2. Kitchen cabinet fabrication

Fabrication follows approved drawings and requires specified panel thicknesses, finishes, edge banding, hinges and drawer runners. Sink, oven and service openings must be coordinated, with moisture protection near wet areas. Ask for finish samples and a cutting schedule before production. Compare material and hardware specifications rather than price per metre alone; inspect square assemblies and clean edges before delivery.

### 3. Kitchen installation

Before fitting, verify floor and wall levels and locate concealed services so fixings are safe. Align base and wall cabinets, adjust joints and install worktops to their specified support details; qualified trades should connect appliances and services. Agree floor protection and waste removal. At handover operate every drawer and door and check the sink area for leaks.

### 4. Wardrobe design

Measure the wall, ceiling height, door swings, switches and AC outlets before planning a wardrobe. Allocate hanging space, shelves and drawers around actual clothing needs, and check clearances for hinged or sliding doors. Require an internal dimensioned elevation, panel thickness and lighting positions; compare usable storage capacity before choosing the finish.

### 5. Wardrobe fabrication

Fabrication covers panel cutting, edge finishing, joints and either hinges or sliding tracks to approved drawings. Durability depends on board grade, connections, shelf loads and runner quality. Ask for these specifications and a finish sample, confirm door orientation and handle positions, and inspect cutting accuracy and cabinet rigidity before fitting.

### 6. Walk-in wardrobe fit-out

A walk-in wardrobe needs a circulation plan that accommodates cabinet depth, drawer extension and ventilation. Define whether mirrors, lighting, sockets and seating are included, separating joinery from electrical costs. Work from verified site dimensions, not only concept drawings. Check cabinet anchoring and access to upper storage without obstructing the aisle.

### 7. Kitchen and wardrobe refurbishment

Inspect swelling near wet areas, damaged edging, misaligned doors, hinges and runners before specifying refurbishment. Sound cabinet carcasses may only need new fronts or hardware; severely damaged panels may require replacement. Ask for itemised repair, replacement and refinishing options with condition photographs. Test every unit after completion.

### 8. Wooden door fabrication

Measure the finished opening, swing direction and wall thickness before manufacturing. Specify solid or hollow construction, veneer or paint, frame, hinges and lockset according to exposure and use. Approve final dimensions and a door schedule before production. Inspect flatness, consistent edges and accurately positioned hardware.

### 9. Door installation

Check frame plumb, finished floor level and under-door clearance; fixings must suit the wall substrate. Protect floors and finishes, then adjust hinges and locks. Compare quotes by door count and inclusion of frames, hardware and old-door removal. At handover open, close and latch each door repeatedly.

### 10. Fire-rated door fabrication

A fire-rated door is a life-safety assembly, not merely a door described as fire-resistant on an invoice. Establish the required rating from the project design and relevant authority, and request test and approval documentation for the complete leaf, frame, seals, hinges and closer. Unauthorised site alterations may invalidate performance; handover must match labels and components to approved documents.

### 11. Door frame fabrication and fitting

Specify the frame to suit wall thickness, door type and adjacent finishes. Review anchoring, jamb width, hinge and strike positions, especially where floor levels vary. Request a frame section detail and material specification. Check square corners, alignment and secure fixing before hanging the leaf.

### 12. Wooden room partitions

Define whether the partition provides privacy, circulation separation or acoustic control: a decorative screen is not a sound-rated wall. Measure ceiling height, fixing points and nearby electrical and AC services. Approve panel spacing and finish drawings. Check stability, safe edges and unobstructed doors and escape routes.

### 13. Door maintenance

Diagnose rubbing or poor latching before replacing the door: possible causes include sagging hinges, timber movement, a distorted frame or lock failure. Request inspection and itemised adjustment, parts and refinishing. At handover verify smooth travel, secure hinges and reliable latching without excessive force.

### 14. Timber painting and refinishing

The preparation depends on timber condition and the existing coating. Clean, repair defects and test primer compatibility before applying paint or varnish. Decide whether the grain should remain visible or receive an opaque finish, and approve a small sample. Compare coat counts, application method and curing time; inspect colour and texture consistency after completion.

## Preparing a quotation request

Provide the locality and approximate address, unit types and quantities, measured dimensions or a request for a site survey, photos of openings and services, preferred materials and finishes, removal and installation scope, transport needs and desired timing. Request itemised materials, hardware, warranty and acceptance criteria. This guide does not certify a company's licence or product approval.
