# Cleaning, hygiene and facilities services in Musaffah

This editorial draft covers company and establishment services, without implying any listed provider, availability or price. Biet Alreef customer service can receive quotation enquiries containing the service, location, customer name, contact number, photographs, measurements, scope and preferred timing. Individual tradespeople are listed separately.

## Homes and buildings

### 1. Villa cleaning

State floors, bedrooms, bathrooms and outdoor areas, distinguishing routine cleaning from construction residue. Photos of kitchens and high-level areas clarify access equipment; inspect floors, glass and worktops before sign-off.

### 2. Apartment cleaning

Give the apartment size, occupancy and building lift/access arrangements. Specify whether cupboard interiors, appliances and balconies are included; inspect bathrooms and the kitchen at handover.

### 3. Office cleaning

Count workstations, meeting rooms and washrooms, and schedule work around occupancy. Set rules for electronics and confidential papers, then check the task log and high-touch surfaces.

### 4. Building cleaning

List floors, corridors, staircases, lifts, entrances and common-area responsibilities. Request a frequency plan and inspection checklist; compare bids by coverage and visits, not staffing alone.

### 5. Post-construction cleaning

Separate gypsum dust, adhesive marks and paint splashes from debris the contractor must remove. Trial chemicals on stone or timber finishes; inspect edges and ventilation grilles before acceptance.

### 6. Pre-move cleaning

Confirm whether the property is vacant and whether drawer, cabinet and appliance interiors need cleaning. Photograph pre-existing defects and verify surfaces are dry before belongings arrive.

### 7. Recurring cleaning contracts

Agree visit frequency, hours, consumables, absence cover and complaint handling. Require measurable task lists and visit records; compare base fees against excluded extras.

## Upholstery and textiles

### 8. Steam sofa cleaning

Count seats and identify upholstery fabric, backing and old stains; test colourfastness discreetly. Confirm moisture extraction and drying time, then inspect odours and marks.

### 9. Rug cleaning

Photograph both sides and provide dimensions, fibre type and collection requirements. Agree stain and fringe treatment and controlled drying; inspect pile and colour stability.

### 10. Fitted carpet cleaning

Provide square metres, fixed furniture locations and traffic stains. Clarify furniture movement and drying before reoccupation; assess any shade differences once dry.

### 11. Arabic majlis cleaning

Measure seating runs and count bolsters, cushions and associated rugs, noting fabric types. Test a hidden area and agree drying/reassembly; inspect seams and remaining stains.

### 12. Mattress cleaning

Give mattress dimensions, quantity, sides and stain locations. Avoid guarantees of total stain or allergen removal; confirm drying time and moisture condition before remaking beds.

### 13. Curtain cleaning

Provide drop, width, fabric and rail fixing details, including removal/reinstallation. Agree shrinkage and colour tests and on-site versus off-site cleaning; inspect folds after rehanging.

### 14. Upholstery disinfection

Explain the reason for treatment, fabric and occupants. Request product identity, label directions, surface compatibility and re-entry time; spraying alone does not substantiate a disinfection claim.

## Facades and sites

### 15. Glass façade cleaning

Estimate glazed area, height, access and operable windows. Require pedestrian protection and a suitable work-at-height method; check streaks and water marks after drying.

### 16. Building façade cleaning

Identify stone, aluminium or painted cladding and affected deposits. Request a small trial of pressure and chemicals before full treatment; inspect joints and finishes afterward.

### 17. Car park cleaning

State bay count, floor area, drainage and oil stains. Agree temporary closures and appropriate wastewater handling; compare results against a test section.

### 18. Industrial site cleaning

Describe processes, dust, oil and waste and define safe access zones. Separate routine surface cleaning from hazardous-material work requiring specialists; sign off by zone and safety checklist.

## Tanks and pools

### 19. Water tank cleaning

Provide capacity, material, location, access opening and last cleaning date. Request draining, sediment removal, rinsing and recommissioning steps; inspect cover condition and residue.

### 20. Water tank disinfection

Distinguish disinfection from sediment removal; require records of chemical, concentration, contact time and flushing under label instructions and applicable requirements. Clear water is not a substitute for required testing.

### 21. Swimming pool cleaning

Provide pool dimensions, volume, tile condition, filters and pump details. Separate brushing/vacuuming from water balancing and equipment repair; review clarity and agreed measurement records.

## Pest and rodent control

### 22. Cockroach control

Report sightings, timing, food sources and moisture; inspect cracks and drains before treatment. Record products, safety instructions and follow-up rather than promising instant eradication.

### 23. Ant control

Photograph trails, entry points and nearby vegetation or food. Ask about species identification, source treatment and child/pet precautions; assess activity at follow-up.

### 24. Rodent control

Record droppings, gnawing, entry routes and affected stores. Require proofing, secure monitoring stations and inspection records; bait alone does not resolve access causes.

### 25. Bed bug control

Identify affected rooms, signs and recently moved furniture. Request staged inspection/treatment, preparation instructions and follow-up; avoid discarding furniture without assessment.

### 26. Periodic pest-control contracts

Specify premises, risk areas, visit frequency and incident reporting. Compare monitoring, prevention, intervention and emergency response with product and outcome logs.

## Disinfection

### 27. Home spraying and disinfection

Identify target surfaces, purpose and children or pets. Require a product appropriate to the stated use, ventilation and contact-time directions; cleaning and disinfection are different tasks.

### 28. Workplace disinfection

List shared touchpoints, washrooms, occupancy hours and sensitive equipment. Require a treatment log, compatible products and re-entry instructions, without claims of lasting protection.

## Building operations and maintenance

### 29. Maintenance management

Compile asset and fault registers, priorities and approval authority. Require ticketing, response targets and closure reports; distinguish management fees from separately priced repairs.

### 30. Facilities operation

Define operating hours, shared systems and vendor responsibilities. Review emergency plans, shift handovers, performance measures and consumption logs before contracting.

### 31. Preventive maintenance

Supply equipment lists, manufacturer guidance and past inspection dates. Require asset-specific schedules, test evidence and spare-part terms; separate planned visits from breakdown repairs.

### 32. Electrical systems monitoring

Identify panels, loads, repeat faults and monitoring scope. Require measurement and inspection records from qualified personnel; live work needs appropriate safety controls.

### 33. HVAC systems monitoring

List units, capacities, temperature complaints and filter history. Review operating readings, airflow and coil-cleaning plans; distinguish monitoring from component replacement.

### 34. Plumbing and drainage monitoring

Identify leaks, pressure issues, blockages and available network drawings. Require fault diagnosis, repair logs and post-repair testing rather than merely concealing damp marks.

### 35. Building condition reports

Define report purpose, inspection limits and inaccessible areas. Request dated photos, risk grading and repair priorities, distinguishing visual observations from engineering tests.

## Workforce and operational support

### 36. Cleaning workforce provision

Define duties, shifts, location, tools and consumables. Clarify supervision, replacements and applicable employment obligations before comparing service fees.

### 37. Loading labour provision

Provide weights, dimensions, carrying distances, lift access and manual-handling risks. Agree aids and damage responsibilities; reconcile moved items at completion.

### 38. Maintenance technician provision

Specify trades, faults, tools and required competence. Check relevant qualifications, work approvals and reporting; separate parts and materials from technician time.

### 39. Site supervisor provision

Define crews, supervision scope, hours and instruction authority. Require daily progress and safety observation logs before acceptance.

### 40. Residential compound operation

List common facilities, buildings, response hours and security, cleaning and maintenance interfaces. Compare complaint handling, rounds and reporting, not headcount alone.

### 41. Commercial facilities operation

Define public hours, tenants, loading areas and shared systems. Agree off-peak fault handling, safety and cleaning reports and response-time measures.

### 42. Project support services

State project stage and tasks such as site organisation, logistics or records. Require a defined scope, contact person and weekly deliverables; do not confuse support with unagreed specialist works.
