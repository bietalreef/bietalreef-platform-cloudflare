# Public search content — approved direction

Public city/service copy targets search intent and brings visitors to the relevant companies, shops and suppliers. Use natural Arabic variants of service + location + question, with short direct answers, full server-rendered HTML and expandable presentation.

Do not expose editorial workflow or data-loading states in marketing copy: no statements about waiting for publication, missing approved data, indexing readiness or refusing to publish estimates. Keep those checks in the publishing workflow. Maintain pilot noindex until the final review is complete.

Use a helpful commercial next step: choose a service/district, prepare a quotation request, or contact Biet Alreef customer service if the visitor cannot find a suitable option. Do not invent prices, licenses, reviews, provider counts or coverage. Availability claims must follow the actual verified service/location records, while request language can positively describe what customers may ask for.

Do not create content visible only to crawlers; customers and search engines receive the same HTML.
